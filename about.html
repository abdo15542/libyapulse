About Libya Pulse

Libya Pulse is a tech news website from Libya to the whole world. We publish technology news, simple explainers, app coverage, and artificial intelligence stories — in clear, simple English that anyone can understand.

Every story includes a plain-language explanation, so you do not need a technical background to follow what is happening in tech.

Libya Pulse is the English sister of Wamda Libya (ومضة ليبية), the Arabic tech page from Libya to the whole Arab world. The two sites publish the same stories — one in Arabic, one in English.
