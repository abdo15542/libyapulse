⚖️ New York Creates an 'ASSET' Unit to Watch Over AI... and the 'RAISE' Law Takes Effect in January!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 17, New York Attorney General Letitia James announced the creation of a new unit called 'ASSET' — the AI Safety, Standards, Enforcement, and Transparency unit — to protect the state's residents from the harms of emerging technologies and oversee the laws regulating AI.

The new unit will lead the implementation of the 'RAISE' law — the Responsible AI Safety and Education law — which takes effect on January 1, 2027, and requires developers of advanced AI models to publicly disclose their safety procedures and report security and safety incidents quickly.

The unit will also focus on the technology's effects on data privacy, digital fraud, cybersecurity, and online safety, and will develop new policy proposals and provide its technical expertise to investigations across all divisions of the Attorney General's office, which also asked employees of AI companies to report, as whistleblowers, any unsafe development.

The unit operates within the Economic Justice division, in cooperation with the Bureau of Internet and Technology, and is led by Christie Phillips, who was a special counsel in the same division, while the team is now preparing to begin its work before the law takes effect.

The unit's creation follows other legislation in the state, including a law banning unsafe features in chatbots for children from June 2027, and a law requiring chatbots to disclose suicidal thoughts and warn users that they are not talking to a human.

Simply put: the state of New York is building a special team to hold AI companies accountable before its law takes effect; so does every country need a unit like this one?
