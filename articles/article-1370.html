📊 Gartner: Europe's Tech Spending Jumps 9.9% in 2027… and AI Is Driving the Growth!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Research firm Gartner predicted on November 24 that Europe's spending on information technology will reach $1.57 trillion in 2027, up 9.9% from 2026, with artificial intelligence remaining the top driver of spending.

Data center systems are leading growth among sectors, expected to jump from $129.2 billion in 2026 to $167.7 billion in 2027, a rise of 29.7%, driven by demand for high-performance computing and server prices that could reach three times their 2024 levels.

Spending on AI-optimized servers in Europe is expected to reach $79.7 billion, while spending on AI models is set to grow by 50%, the fastest growth rate of any software market.

European growth is slower than the global growth expected at 15%, taking worldwide spending to $7.35 trillion, and it is also a slowdown from Europe's own 12.1% in 2026, as the spending base widens and some investments cool after a strong year.

A Gartner analyst said building the computing capacity AI needs is still the largest infrastructure expansion in history, and that AI-optimized servers form the foundation of that infrastructure.
