📸 The Vivo S70 Series Launches Next Month.. and a Pro Version With a 200-Megapixel Camera!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Vivo has announced that the S70 phone series will launch in China next month — the series that follows the S60 line — and the company has already begun taking pre-orders in China.

The series is expected to include the S70 and S70 Pro, and leaks suggest the company will introduce a dedicated photography accessory alongside the series, a move aimed at phone-photography enthusiasts.

The Pro version, according to leaks, will carry a 200-megapixel main camera with support for an external telephoto lens that attaches to the phone, a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chip, and a 1.5K OLED display.

Advertisement

The Pro version is expected to reach global markets later under the name Vivo V90 Pro, meaning its specifications will reach a wider audience outside China.

A 200-megapixel camera means finer detail in photos and the ability to crop an image without losing quality, while the external telephoto lens allows long-range shots at higher quality than the usual digital zoom.

Simply put: a phone that turns into a professional camera with a small accessory.. Would a phone with a 200-megapixel camera replace a separate camera for you?

If your phone took photos as good as big cameras, would you still buy a separate camera?
