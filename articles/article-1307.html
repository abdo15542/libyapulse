🚁 Amazon Drone Crashes onto a Garage After Delivering Shampoo — and the FAA Is Investigating!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A delivery drone from Amazon's Prime Air service fell onto the garage of a home in the Detroit suburb of Huntington Woods, moments after delivering a bottle of shampoo to a neighbouring house.

The customer said the drone delivered the package to his back garden successfully, but during take-off it hit a fibre-optic cable and lost one of its propellers, then tangled with a power line and fell onto the neighbours' garage.

The crash caused minor damage to the garage roof, lifting some shingles out of place, with no one injured, while the company quickly removed the drone and contacted the homeowner.

Advertisement

The Federal Aviation Administration has opened an investigation into the incident, while the National Transportation Safety Board is gathering information to decide whether to open an independent investigation.

The drone is an MK30 model, weighs about 38 kilograms, and carries packages of up to 5 pounds, operating without a pilot. Amazon says its drones have delivered hundreds of thousands of packages this year, at about 6,000 flights a day, and that accidents are extremely rare.

Simply put: a successful air delivery that ended with a crash onto the neighbours' garage — even self-flying drones are still learning to dodge the neighbourhood's wires.
