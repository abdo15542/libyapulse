⌚ Honor Watch 6 Pro Arrives Globally with an 18-Day Battery!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Honor launched its “Watch 6 Pro” smartwatch globally, after its launch in China in September, reaching mainly European markets. The watch features a 1.5-inch dual OLED display, a battery lasting up to 18 days, high water and dust resistance rated IP69K, and Honor’s “Polaris” positioning system for stable signal in wild areas. The watch is available with two strap options.

Simply put: a battery that lasts 18 days means you charge your watch twice a month only, instead of every night.
