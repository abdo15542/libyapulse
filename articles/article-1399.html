⌚ ChatGPT Health Arrives on Android — Your Watch and Fitness Inside the Chat!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

OpenAI has announced that the health feature in ChatGPT is starting to roll out on Android, letting users connect fitness apps and wearables and understand their sleep and activity patterns.

The connection runs through Google's Health Connect platform, so the assistant receives selected data from compatible devices and services, including Garmin, Oura, and Whoop devices.

After syncing, users can ask about their sleep and exercise patterns, such as: does sleep quality improve on more active days? Or when do they walk longer distances?

Advertisement

Health data also reaches the proactive assistant Dots, which might, for example, suggest a different time to exercise if a meeting clashes with the usual gym time.

The feature is currently available in the United States only, for users aged 18 and over, on free and paid plans, with a gradual rollout that may reach some devices later.

Simply put: your smart assistant now knows your sleep and your steps — would you connect your health data to an AI assistant?
