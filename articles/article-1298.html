💬 Muse comes to Instagram DMs — it summarizes your chats and organizes your saved posts!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Meta announced on 24 November that its AI assistant Muse is now available directly inside private messages on Instagram, letting users chat with it in a message window without leaving the app or switching to the separate assistant app.

Users can ask Muse about a post, place or product they came across in the app, or ask it to summarize conversations they missed in their other messages, or send it a post and ask it to carry out a task, such as adding a café to a trip plan to Mexico City, or searching for a discount offer on shoes during sale season.

The rollout began for iOS and Android users in the United States, Canada, Mexico, Japan and Singapore, and conversations started in Instagram will also appear as a side conversation inside the main Muse app.

Advertisement

The company also announced new capabilities for saved content: Muse can organize saved posts and clips into custom folders called Collections, turn a recipe video into a shopping list, turn travel content from creators into a trip plan, and suggest outfits from saved fashion clips — with these features starting first in the United States and Canada.

The company confirmed that the user stays in control of their experience: nothing is published, sent or shared without their consent, and they can ask the assistant to forget a piece of information or undo an action at any time.

Simply put: the smart assistant is no longer a separate app you go to, but now lives inside your messages; it asks what you saved, organizes it, and turns it into ready-made plans, without anything moving without your permission.
