📱 HyperOS 4 Begins Rolling Out Globally.. Android 17 and a New Design for Xiaomi Phones!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Xiaomi began rolling out the stable HyperOS 4 update globally to its phones and tablets, as the company announced on 18 November. The update carries Android 17 features and a new design, and it arrives in batches starting with the newest devices before gradually expanding to the rest of the list.

The Xiaomi 17 Ultra will be among the first devices to receive the update, alongside the Xiaomi 17 and Xiaomi 15 series. In early November, the update will also reach POCO phones from the F8, X8, M8, C95 Pro, and C85 series, plus the Redmi Note 17 and Redmi Note 15 series and the Redmi 17 and Redmi 15 series, as well as the Xiaomi Pad 8 tablets. The first rollout thus covers dozens of devices from the flagship, mid-range, and budget categories together.

The company has not yet revealed all the details of the new design, but the update puts Xiaomi phones on the latest version of Android in the same year it was released, after major system updates used to lag by months. To install it, open Settings, then About Phone, then System Update, and the update will appear when your device's turn comes, as the rollout happens in batches and does not reach everyone on the same day.

Simply put: a brand-new system is knocking on the doors of tens of millions of phones at once; has the update reached your phone yet?
