An Electronic Nose That Smells Like a Human 🔬

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Osmo unveiled an AI-powered smelling system that it claims has reached the level of the human nose: it trained it on more than 10 million human smell ratings and 800 million smell data units, and says it beat human perfume experts last month in an internal test.

Simply put: the system recognizes and classifies smells the way a human nose does, and companies such as Procter & Gamble have already started using it to develop their products.

But some experts doubt these claims, saying they are not backed by published evidence.

Can you imagine a device that smells food to tell whether it has gone bad?
