🚀 “Shenzhou 24” launches to “Tiangong” — the first foreign astronaut visits the Chinese station!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

China’s crewed spacecraft “Shenzhou 24” launched on the morning of Wednesday 25 November aboard a “Long March 2F” rocket from the Jiuquan centre, heading to the “Tiangong” space station, in the first joint mission between Chinese and foreign crew members in the history of China’s crewed space programme.

The mission is commanded by Tang Hongbo on his third trip to the station, accompanied by flight engineer Wu Yi on his first trip, and Pakistani Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, aged 40, a Pakistan Air Force pilot working as a payload specialist — the first Pakistani to reach orbit and the first foreigner to visit “Tiangong”.

Ali will spend about a week on the station carrying out biological experiments, then return to Earth aboard “Shenzhou 23” with two colleagues, including Lai Ka Ying, the first female astronaut from Hong Kong.

The spacecraft docked with the station about three and a half hours after launch, and the new crew joined three crew members already there, so the station briefly holds six astronauts from China, Hong Kong and Pakistan.

Simply put: this mission is not just another supply trip to the station, but the start of a new chapter — “Tiangong” is no longer a purely Chinese station, but one that now welcomes astronauts from other countries.
