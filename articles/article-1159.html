🛰️ Ariane 6 Lifts Off Successfully! 36 New Satellites for Amazon Leo Internet!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The Ariane 6 rocket lifted off today, Tuesday, November 24, at 11:14 GMT from the European spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana, carrying 36 new satellites for Amazon's Leo satellite internet network, and the French space agency announced the launch was a success.

The countdown had been halted yesterday, Monday, before launch because of a technical problem in the rocket's ground support systems, so Arianespace postponed the attempt to today after checks and analysis, while the rocket and satellites remained safe and stable throughout.

The satellites are deployed at an altitude of about 465 kilometers, then Amazon raises them to their working orbit at 630 kilometers, bringing the total number of Leo satellites in orbit to about 430, including 136 launched by Ariane 6 alone in four flights. The network will eventually need 3,232 satellites to cover the world with satellite internet.

This launch comes just nine days after the Vulcan rocket failed on November 15, destroying 40 Leo satellites, at a time when Blue Origin's New Glenn rocket remains grounded after an explosion at the test stand.

Simply put: after losing 40 satellites in an explosion just days ago, Amazon is back in space on a European rocket… every new satellite brings satellite internet closer to your home.
