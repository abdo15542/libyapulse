🛡️ CrowdStrike Buys Zscaler for $44 Billion! The Biggest Cybersecurity Deal in History!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

CrowdStrike announced on Monday, November 23, the purchase of Zscaler in an all-stock deal worth $43.9 billion — the largest acquisition in the history of cybersecurity.

Zscaler shareholders get 1.135 shares of CrowdStrike for each share they own — about $260.41 per share, a premium of roughly 34% over Friday's closing price.

The two companies complement each other: CrowdStrike protects the devices themselves — computers, phones, and servers — while Zscaler secures the connections between users and networks with zero-trust technology, which requires identity verification every time an employee accesses an internal system.

Advertisement

The companies say the merger will help customers adopt AI safely, since AI agents now work alone inside companies and need broader protection than merely detecting breaches.

The merged company will start with annual recurring revenue exceeding $10 billion, about 8,000 enterprise customers, with CrowdStrike shareholders owning about 84% of it, while the deal aims to expand the target market by more than $185 billion through 2029.

The deal is expected to close in the second half of fiscal year 2028, after approval by regulators and Zscaler shareholders, with a $1.4 billion termination fee if Zscaler walks away from the agreement.

Simply put: a company that protects devices buys a company that protects networks, giving birth to a single security giant that wants to be the "cybersecurity operating system" in the age of AI.

Do you think merging security giants makes our data safer — or puts it all in one basket?
