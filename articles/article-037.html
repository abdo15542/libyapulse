You Gave Your AI Assistant Your Bank Password? Banks Say: The Loss Is Yours! 🏦

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

This week, Citi — the third-largest commercial bank in America — changed its terms of use, with a clear message: if you give an AI assistant your bank account login details and it makes a mistake or gets hacked, the loss is yours.

Under the new terms, any action taken by an AI agent — a program that carries out tasks on your behalf, like paying bills or transferring money — counts as if you did it yourself, even if your data was stolen.

Citi follows the same step already taken by JPMorgan and Bank of America in late October, while Wells Fargo's similar terms take effect on November 24.
