Is Your Phone Battery Draining Fast? Try These Three Steps:

18 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

1- Close the apps running in the background that you don't use.

2- Lower the screen brightness — it's the biggest battery drain.

3- Turn on power-saving mode when you need it.

Simple steps, and the difference is clear from day one. Try them and tell us how it went.
