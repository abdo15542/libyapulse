$4.5 Billion Deal: Cybersecurity Buys Into AI! 💰

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 18, Palo Alto Networks, one of the world's largest cybersecurity companies, announced it was buying Applied Compute for $4.5 billion, paid entirely in stock.

The acquired company develops a platform that helps businesses train and run AI models securely inside their own organizations. Open-weight models are models whose trained internal settings are published openly, so any developer can modify and run them on their own instead of renting a closed model.

The deal means security companies are no longer just protecting networks — they want to secure AI itself, because AI tools are now exposing vulnerabilities faster than companies can fix them.
