🛒 A Supply-Chain Attack Reaches Global Websites!

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Optimizely confirmed that attackers exploited a flaw in its platform to inject malicious code into the websites of 178 organizations, including news sites and major companies.

The malicious code replaced crypto wallet addresses and stole card details from visitors, without hacking those websites’ servers themselves.

Simply put, trust in a reliable third party was the vulnerability — the websites loaded the code from a provider they depend on.

Do you know how many third parties run on the websites you visit? And does that worry you?
