🎓 Carnegie Mellon Opens the Doors of One of the World's Top Computer Science Schools... Free on YouTube!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Carnegie Mellon University in the United States has launched a new platform called CS Learn, giving everyone free access to full lectures from its computer science school, via YouTube and the platform's own website, with no registration or fees.

The first batch includes eight courses shown during November, among them Principles of Computing, Generative AI, AI Agents, Machine Learning in Production, and Software Engineering, followed by five more courses in January 2027, with a bigger expansion in the spring.

What stands out is that these are not short edited recordings, but full semester lectures filmed inside the university's lecture halls, prepared by students using an AI-assisted system that moves a lecture from the hall to the internet within weeks of it being delivered, keeping real student questions and the professors' answers in the video.

Simply put: one of the best computer science schools in the world has opened its lecture halls to everyone. Would you watch a full AI lecture from a world-class university, or do you prefer short, fast courses?
