📱 Upcoming Motorola phones may come with Perplexity and Microsoft AI apps preinstalled!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A Google executive revealed during his testimony in the ongoing antitrust case against the company in the United States that upcoming Motorola phones may come with AI apps from Perplexity and Microsoft preinstalled.

Preinstalled apps are programs you find on your phone from the moment you buy it, without downloading them yourself, and they are usually part of commercial agreements between the phone maker and software companies.

Notably, the information surfaced in a trial in which the U.S. government accuses Google of illegally monopolizing the internet search market, making its executives' testimony an unexpected source of news about rival phones.

Advertisement

Neither Motorola nor Perplexity nor Microsoft has confirmed the news officially yet, and it is not known which models the deal will cover or when they will reach stores.

But if the news is true, it will be the first clear sign of fierce competition inside Android phones themselves: multiple AI assistants competing for your screen from the very first time you switch on the phone.

Simply put: your next phone may come with a ready AI assistant before you download any app... Would you prefer to choose your own assistant, or have it come ready?

If you found three AI assistants preinstalled on your new phone, which one would you try first?
