📱 Redmi Note 18 Appears in a Chinese Certification — with an 8888 mAh Battery!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The alleged Redmi Note 18 has appeared in China's 3C certification database under the model number M077CE, in a new sign that the launch of the Redmi Note 18 series is approaching.

The certification shows that the phone supports 45W wired charging, with two chargers listed under the model numbers MDY-17-EE and MDY-20-EM.

Earlier leaks say the phone will be an improved version of the Redmi Note 17 with a larger battery: 8888 mAh for the Chinese version, up from 8000 before, and 8100 mAh for the global version, up from 7700.

Advertisement

Charging speed, meanwhile, stays at 45W in both versions, unchanged from the current generation.

The series is expected to include three phones, among them the Note 18 Pro Max with a 10000 mAh battery, while Xiaomi has not yet announced a launch date.

Simply put: an official certification confirms the phone exists and hints at its huge battery — the new phone may be a copy of its predecessor with just a bigger battery.
