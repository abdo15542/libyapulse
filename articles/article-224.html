📟 Meta’s “Muse Charm” Device for $300 with Free Internet!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Meta plans to sell its new pocket device “Muse Charm” for $300, with free 5G data for up to 12 months from Verizon, according to press reports. The device is about the size of an earbud case, with a screen of about two inches, a fingerprint reader, and a built-in modem, and it lets you talk to the “Muse” AI assistant without needing your phone, with a launch expected in the December holiday season. Meta had revealed the device last September, while its own smartwatch was delayed to 2027.

Simply put: the device is like a small personal assistant in your pocket or on your keychain — it talks to you and answers you without you opening your phone or searching for a Wi-Fi network.

Would you pay $300 for a device that talks to an AI assistant?
