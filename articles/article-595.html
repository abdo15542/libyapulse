🧠 Microsoft open-sources its small model "Phi-4" — powerful AI for everyone, free!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft announced on Wednesday, November 18, that it has made its small AI model "Phi-4" open source, so anyone can now download and use it for free from the Hugging Face platform.

The model was released last month, but was available only via Microsoft's cloud platform Azure AI Foundry, and the company promised at the time that it would open-source it soon — which is what has happened now.

"Phi-4" is a small model but specialised in reasoning and logical thinking — it is designed to solve problems step by step instead of merely generating text, which makes it useful for tasks needing precision such as maths and programming.

Open-source models mean that developers and researchers can study the model, modify it and run it on their own devices, without relying on a particular company's servers or paying fees for every query.

Simply put: imagine a giant company giving you a small, smart artificial mind to put on your device and use as you like, free. That is what Microsoft did with "Phi-4".
