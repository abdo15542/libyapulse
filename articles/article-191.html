🎵 ElevenLabs and SoundCloud Let You Publish AI-Generated Music!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

“Eleven Music,” the AI music-generation platform from ElevenLabs, announced a partnership with SoundCloud that lets music makers publish their AI-generated work directly on SoundCloud, without leaving the generation platform or interrupting their creative workflow.

Simply put: you make music with AI on one platform, then publish it on the listening platform with one tap, instead of downloading files and uploading them manually.
