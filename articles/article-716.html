🔋 Leak: the Xiaomi 18 Max may come with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip and a battery over 8,000 mAh!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Famous leaker Digital Chat Station revealed on November 21 a large-screen flagship phone in development, running on the new 2-nanometre Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip, with a large main camera sensor and a battery over 8,000 mAh, with a simple design without decoration.

The leaker did not name the manufacturer, but Chinese media linked the specifications to the upcoming Xiaomi 18 Max, based on Xiaomi's history with the "Max" name for large phones. Xiaomi has not even confirmed the phone exists, so all the details so far are just unofficial leaks.

This leak comes months after the launch of the Xiaomi 17 Max in May, which came with a 6.9-inch screen, an 8,000 mAh battery, a Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chip and a 200MP main camera with Leica lenses, at a starting price of 4,799 yuan. The new generation seems set to continue the same bet: a large screen and an even bigger battery.

Advertisement

If the leak is correct, the phone may arrive around May 2027, before the Chinese "618" shopping festival, sitting between the standard phone and the Pro Max version: a large screen and a long-lasting battery without the most expensive chip in the series.

Simply put: a giant phone with a giant battery, but so far just a leak with no official confirmation. Do you trust leakers' reports months before launch, or do you wait for the official announcement?
