🎮 The GTA 6 Footage Thieves Finally Face Prison!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Two British hackers reached the end of their cases in the same week that “Grand Theft Auto 6” was released after a long wait. A US court sentenced Tyler Buchanan, 24, to 6 years in prison after his extradition to the United States, for stealing at least $11 million in crypto through phishing messages impersonating technical support departments. Arion Kurtaj, 21, pleaded guilty on November 16 to hacking Rockstar in 2022 and leaking 90 clips from the game and extorting the company, and he will be sentenced on November 23 — knowing that he carried out the hack from a hotel room where he was staying under protection, using an Amazon Fire TV stick.

Simply put: the two attackers did not rely on complex vulnerabilities, but on tricking employees with fake messages and calls; and caution toward “technical support” messages closes this door completely.

Would you trust any message claiming to be from technical support right away?
