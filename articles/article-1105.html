🍎 Apple Adds More Ads to App Store Search Results!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple has confirmed that it will begin showing additional ads in search results inside the App Store within two months, in a move that will change what you see when you search for a new app.

When you type an app's name or type into the search box, paid apps will appear among the regular results, meaning a larger part of the page will become ads instead of search results alone.

The new ads will start first in the UK and Japan, then expand to Apple's other ad markets before the end of March, and will work on devices running iOS and iPadOS 26.2 or later.

Advertisement

The move is part of Apple's expansion into advertising inside its stores, as app developers pay for their apps to appear in front of searchers, giving the company a new source of income from the store itself.

Simply put: searching for an app in Apple's store will soon show more ads... so do ads inside the store bother you, or do you accept them if they help you discover apps?
