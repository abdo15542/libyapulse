📍 Moto Tag 3 Appears on Amazon Before Its Announcement: A New Device or Just a New Colour?

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Motorola's Moto Tag 3 tracker suddenly appeared in Amazon listings, before the company officially announced it. It showed up in the UK Amazon store in 'Lucent White' for £24.96, and in the Italian Amazon in 'Indigo Blue', with a shipping date between one and four months away.

What stands out is that the listed specifications match the previous generation, the Moto Tag 2, exactly: ultra-wideband and Bluetooth technology, working with Google's Find Hub network to track belongings, IP68 water and dust resistance, a replaceable battery lasting more than 500 days, and a weight of 8.5 grams. Even the model number visible in the photos is the same as the previous generation, XT2561-1.

That raises the question: are we looking at a genuinely new generation, or just new colours for the previous generation registered under the wrong name? The second generation itself is relatively recent: it was announced in January 2026 and went on sale in America in June, less than six months ago. Motorola has not commented on the listings so far, so nobody knows for certain whether the Moto Tag 3 is a real device or a naming mistake.

Simply put: a 'new' device with the same number and specifications — do you buy the name or the device?
