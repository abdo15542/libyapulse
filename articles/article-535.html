🚗 Gemini Arrives in Apple Cars — a Full Voice Conversation While Driving!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google announced on November 11 the arrival of its Gemini assistant app directly in Apple's CarPlay system in cars, so iPhone users can hold natural, continuous conversations with Gemini Live from the car screen, without touching the phone, keeping their hands on the wheel and their eyes on the road. The global rollout covers all languages Gemini Live supports.

By tapping the Gemini icon on the car screen, the driver can resume a previous conversation or start a new one, ask for a summary of unread emails, review their calendar appointments, prepare for an upcoming meeting, and search for nearby restaurants and places. Because Gemini is connected to the Gmail and Google Calendar apps, conversations sync automatically with the Gemini app on the phone, so your summaries and ideas are waiting for you the moment the car stops.

To enable the feature, just update the Gemini app on iPhone to the latest version, enable microphone permission, and use Gemini Live on the phone at least once, then connect the phone to the car. Gemini was previously available in CarPlay only through the Google Maps app, but now it is a full standalone app. This brings Gemini in line with its rivals, as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, and Grok had already reached CarPlay before it.

Simply put: imagine your smart assistant riding in the car with you, reading your email, reminding you of your meeting, and suggesting a restaurant on your way — without you ever lifting your hands off the wheel; that is what Gemini now offers iPhone users.
