🧠 Ling 3.2 Air from Ant… A Huge Open Model with a License That Allows Commercial Use!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Ant Group launched its new Ling 3.2 Air model on November 23, a multimodal AI model that understands text, images, and video, with its weights released openly under the MIT license, which allows commercial use and modification.

The model uses a hybrid mixture-of-experts architecture, with 124 billion parameters in total and only 5.5 billion activated when processing each token, making it powerful without needing extreme hardware compared with its full size.

The model was designed specifically to automate professional work tasks, such as producing documents, working with office software, and browsing, and in the company's own task-automation test, task completion jumped from 15.7% in the previous generation to 61% in the new model.

It runs with a native context of up to 262,000 tokens, extendable to one million tokens, and comes with day-one support for the SGLang and vLLM inference engines; its weights total about 129 gigabytes spread across 13 files.

Running the model at full context requires four large graphics cards or eight 80-gigabyte cards, and it scored 23 points on an independent AI index, a strong result among open-weight models.

Simply put: a huge open Chinese model with a free license that reads your documents, images, and videos and handles office tasks; do you prefer open models you run yourself over closed ones?
