📷 November Update Breaks Pixel Cameras: A Black Screen with No Official Fix!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Pixel phone owners have reported a serious camera fault after installing the November 2026 update, which began rolling out on 10 November with the new Android 17 system. When you open the camera app, a black screen appears, or an error message saying 'Something went wrong... close the app and reopen it', and neither the front nor the rear camera works — and the fault extends to other camera apps too, not just Google's.

The complaints have centred on the Pixel 10 and Pixel 11 phones, including the Pixel 11 Pro XL, appearing in support forums and user discussions since 12 November. Some users also reported other issues after the same update: desktop mode does not send sound to the external display, the always-on display behaves strangely, and a foldable phone's screen sometimes stays black after opening it.

Google has not yet announced the cause of the problem or when it will be fixed, but some users have found a temporary workaround: switching the phone off completely and leaving it off for about five minutes before turning it on again, which brought the camera back for many — though the fix did not work for everyone. A factory reset fixed the issue for some but not others, so it is no guaranteed solution. The problem is precisely that a phone's camera is not a secondary feature: it is the daily means of documenting, working and communicating.

Simply put: an update meant to improve your phone breaks its camera — did you update your phone before reading this?
