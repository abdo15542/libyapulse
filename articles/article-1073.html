🛡️ Agon Raises $70 Million to Build Europe's Defense AI Lab... and a Virtual Battlefield!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Agon, a defense AI company based in London and Berlin, has announced it raised $70 million in a Series A funding round at a $450 million valuation, to build what it describes as Europe's first defense AI lab.

The round was led by investment firm Northzone, with participation from two British government funds — the Sovereign AI Fund and the National Security Strategic Investment Fund — in their first joint direct investment, alongside previous investors. This brings the company's total raised to $100 million, after an initial $30 million round earlier this year.

At the heart of the company's technology is a 'synthetic battlefield' powered by a proprietary engine, letting developers and defense agencies train autonomous systems and test them against thousands of virtual scenarios, from mass drone attacks to communications outages and degraded navigation, before deploying them in the real field.

The company is led by Tristam Constant, a former British Army officer who worked at Anduril and Applied Intuition, who says Europe is in an 'AI arms race' as it enters an era of what he calls 'proxy warfare', where intelligent systems need to understand their environment and make decisions at a speed humans alone cannot.

The company plans to use the new funding to expand its engineering and research teams and speed up development of its platform and its defense-specific core models, with a stated commitment to keeping human oversight over its systems.

Simply put: a lab that trains armies of drones in imaginary battles before they fly... will defense AI become an independent European industry?

If AI is going to lead defense systems in the future, do you trust that the final decision will stay in human hands?
