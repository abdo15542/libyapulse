📱 Nothing OS 5.0 Finally Reaches the Phone 2a.. Your Phone's Last Big Update!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The stable Nothing OS 5.0 update, based on Android 17, began rolling out to the Nothing Phone 2a and Phone 2a Plus on 19 November, in batches reaching users gradually. The update is 3.15 GB for those coming from the previous stable version, and only 361 MB for those who were trying the beta.

This update is the last major system upgrade for these two phones, launched in 2024, making it an important milestone for their owners. The update brings the new Glyph app with an improved interactive experience, separate Quick Settings and notification panels, arranging home screen icons in one go, a new three-color privacy indicator, a new transparent theme, wider lock screen clock customization, plus camera improvements, including seven adjustable tuning effects and H.265 video encoding.

The flagship Phone 3 was the first to receive the update on 12 October, followed by the Phone 3a and Phone 4a series on 30 October, and now it is the Phone 2a's turn. To install the update, open Settings, then System, then Nothing OS, and the download will appear when your turn comes, although the company has not officially announced the rollout for these two phones yet.

Simply put: your phone's last big update brings Android 17; has it reached you yet?
