🏛️ US Senate passes a law: AI data centers pay the grid bills!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US Senate passed the "American Affordability and Jobs Act" on November 19 by 62 votes to 33 — a law that speeds up energy projects and obliges data centers to pay for the electricity they consume.

Under the law, data centers pay their share of the cost of the current grid, plus the full cost of the new lines and stations needed to run them, instead of loading these costs onto household bills.

The law comes after AI data centers became one of the biggest drivers of rising electricity bills, to the point that dozens of US counties and cities imposed temporary bans on building new centers in this month's elections.

Supporters of the law see it as cutting through decades of complex procedures that make building anything in America difficult and expensive, while critics — including tribal groups and environmental organisations — say it weakens environmental protection laws and tribal rights.

The law still needs approval from the House of Representatives and the President's signature, before the current Congress ends its work on January 3, 2027 — otherwise it will be reintroduced in the next Congress.

The Rhodium research group estimates the law could cut the average American household's annual electricity bill by between $50 and $78 by 2040.

Simply put: whoever consumes electricity heavily pays for it. Will this principle ever reach our countries?
