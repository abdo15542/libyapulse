💰 A billionaire sells 'AMD' and 'Broadcom' entirely! And bets a billion dollars on 'Micron'!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

An official disclosure revealed that the 'Appaloosa' fund, run by billionaire David Tepper, sold its entire stakes in 'AMD' and 'Broadcom' during the third quarter of 2026, in a bold reshuffling of its bets on AI chips.

The changes appeared in the mandatory quarterly disclosure published on November 16, showing a snapshot of the fund's US stock holdings through the end of September, with its portfolio worth about $7.6 billion spread across just 24 companies, down slightly by 2.1% from the previous quarter.

The fund sold all its 'AMD' shares, numbering 197,500, and all its 'Broadcom' shares, numbering 150,000, in a complete exit from two companies that were among the most prominent names of the AI boom, without the disclosure explaining the reasons for the decision.

In return, Tepper put more money into two other companies: he raised his stake in 'Micron' by 28% to a value of about $1.33 billion, or 17.6% of the whole portfolio, making it his largest investment, and increased his stake in 'Nvidia' by 31% to reach two million shares worth about $457 million.

The rotation did not stop at chips: the fund cut its stake in 'Alphabet', the parent company of 'Google', by nearly half, reduced its stake in 'Amazon' by 10.5%, opened a new position in 'Oracle' with more than a million shares, and multiplied its investment in 'SpaceX' by more than 16 times to reach about $557 million.

Analysts see the move not as a withdrawal from AI, but as a careful choice of who owns the rarest parts of the supply chain: the memory 'Micron' makes has become the bottleneck in data centers, while 'Nvidia' faces pressure after its CEO's departure, making the bet on it a calculated risk.

Simply put: Tepper did not flee AI, he just changed horses... will he be right in his choice this time?

If you were in his place, would you have sold 'AMD' and 'Broadcom' or held on to them?
