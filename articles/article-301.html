🔑 Your wallet, keys, and bag now know where they are!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Nimble, a company specializing in sustainable accessories, has entered the tracker market with its new 'Trace' collection: 7 products that build tracking technology into everyday accessories, instead of a separate device you hang on your keys or slip into your bag.

Instead of a small tracking tag hung on keys or placed in a bag, the company built tracking into a wallet, a key chain, a slim wallet card, a luggage tag, and a passport cover. Prices start at $14.95 for the 'Trace' card with a replaceable battery, and reach $49.95 for the rechargeable 'Trace Folio' passport cover. The whole collection works with both Apple's 'Find My' and Google's 'Find Hub' networks, and several models support charging via USB-C or wirelessly, with the key chain's battery lasting about 6 months per charge. The products are made from recycled plastic and cactus-based vegan leather, and have undergone independent lab testing for chemicals. The collection is available now through the company's website and the Amazon store in five colors. Nimble was founded in 2018 by former managers at charger maker Mophie.

Simply put: imagine your wallet itself knowing where it is and telling your phone; you will never search for your keys again — you will just ask your phone where they are.

Would you buy a wallet that tracks itself, or stick with a small tracking tag?
