📉 The US securities regulator investigates Nvidia's accounting.. and the stock falls 13% in a single day!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US Securities and Exchange Commission is investigating Nvidia's accounting practices, according to reports on 20 November citing two sources familiar with the matter. The investigation comes as a growing crisis grips the company since its former CEO resigned on 11 November, and its stock fell about 13% on the day of the announcement alone, after already being down more than 6% before the news was published.

Investigators are focusing on how the company reports its revenue, including its change last year in how it reports revenue by geographic region: it stopped publishing Singapore revenue separately after it began relying on the customer's headquarters instead of the billing address, at a time when Singapore sales were about half of its global revenue. Circular financing deals are also raising growing concern, as the company finances its customers and partners and the money then returns to it as purchases of its chips, making it harder for investors to judge the quality of the real revenue.

Opening an investigation does not mean the company is guilty — many investigations close without any action — and the regulator declined to comment while the company did not respond immediately. But pressure is also mounting from other directions: the California Attorney General issued a subpoena on 20 November, and the Treasury Department asked the board to answer within ten days questions including whether questionable sales may have inflated profits.

Simply put: the world's biggest chip company is under the accounting microscope, and its stock is falling as the investigations widen — do you still trust the numbers of AI companies as you did before?
