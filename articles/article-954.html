📱 'iOS 27.3' Is Coming! Siri Controls Your Apps... Safari Transforms Completely... and Health Measures Your Health Age!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple's iOS 27.3 update is still in beta, and the public release is expected in December 2026, but its new details have begun to emerge — a wide package of changes touching the voice assistant, the browser, health and the watch together.

The highlight of the update is that the Siri assistant gains new actions inside Apple's own apps, such as Calendar, Mail, Messages, Photos and Safari, with a new API that lets it control apps through what is known as 'app intents'. You could, for example, ask it by voice to order dinner or book a table — but apps fully using this feature will not be accepted in the store before spring 2027. Siri also learns new languages: Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Turkish and Norwegian.

The Health app has been redesigned with three new tabs — Insights, Longevity and Browse — with a new measure called Health Age that estimates your overall health, noting that the Insights tab works only on the iPhone 15 Pro and later.

Safari gets the long-awaited vertical tabs, plus an adaptive dark mode that automatically converts sites that do not support dark mode, so night browsing becomes comfortable on every site without exception.

The redesigned StandBy mode reaches all supported phones starting with the iPhone 11, with one-handed access for the foldable phone, and the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 get what was called Voice Intelligence: a Live Replay feature that summarizes the last 15 seconds of your speech by double-pressing the crown, and a Siri Summary that summarizes conversations — optional, and deleted after a week.

Simply put: the December update makes Siri control your apps, Safari show vertical tabs and darken every site, and Health measure your health age... which feature are you waiting for most?

Would you prefer a voice assistant that controls your apps, or do you like controlling things with your own hand?
