🔍 Report: Chinese intelligence has run a secret campaign for two years to obtain AI chips!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Reports on 21 November revealed that Chinese intelligence has run a secret campaign for at least two years to bypass American export restrictions and obtain the advanced chips at the heart of artificial intelligence. According to current and former intelligence officials, the campaign is led by a special task force formed by State Security Minister Chen Yixin and reporting directly to him, whose mission is to help Chinese companies buy chips and smuggle them into China, and to gather information about the semiconductor industry and its supply chains around the world.

The minister had announced this new mission publicly in an article in the Communist Party's magazine last April, calling for "deepening the struggle against the blockade" and solving the problem of critical technologies that "cannot be obtained by asking, buying, or begging". In 2023, the Chinese president also created a central science and technology commission to narrow the gap with the United States in the computing power needed for AI, the commission that leads the vision of achieving technological superiority.

The campaign intersects with the case of businesswoman Alice Huang, whom American officials allege cooperated with Chinese intelligence to gather information about Nvidia's former chief executive, while China denies these accusations and calls them fabricated. These developments come after the United States banned the direct export of advanced chips to China in late 2022, prompting Beijing to turn to roundabout routes through intermediaries and data centres in other countries.

Simply put: the chip war is no longer just trade and politics — intelligence agencies have entered it; will chips become the great battleground between the superpowers?
