💵 The 'USDS' Coin Loses $2 Billion in One Day — but Keeps Its Price!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The "Sky" protocol had an exceptional day on November 19, processing more than $2 billion in redemptions of its "USDS" stablecoin for "USDC", through the price-stability module that swaps the two coins at a fixed rate. The coin's circulating supply fell by about 40% in a single day to about $3.2 billion, yet the coin kept its price of one dollar throughout the wave.

The wave came on the same day an investigative report published research by an analytics firm tracking $518 million in transactions through the stability module to wallets linked to hackers and sanctioned entities over five years, including about $101.9 million linked to North Korea, among them wallets linked to the "Bybit" platform theft in February 2025. The protocol responded that the mentioned amount represents less than 0.06% of more than $960 billion the module has processed since its creation, and that two-thirds of the cited activity occurred between 2021 and mid-2024.

The protocol confirmed that the stablecoin reserve inside the module never fell below $800 million at the peak, and returned above $1.1 billion by Friday, and that it holds about $9.3 billion in backing assets against about $6.11 billion of circulating "USDS" and "DAI", plus about $93 million as a reserve to absorb losses. It also noted that most of the cited transactions passed through intermediary trading services that automatically choose trade routes.

Simply put: a stablecoin lost two-fifths of its supply in one day and didn't lose its price — the real test of a stablecoin isn't in calm times, but in a moment of panic.
