🏛️ The White House fires the cybersecurity director after a wave of outages!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The White House has dismissed National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross, after a week of widespread technical outages across the United States, in the first major leadership change since the wave began. Kirsten Davies, the Defense Department's chief information officer, took the post in an acting capacity. The decision came after Microsoft services were down for about a full day, affecting hundreds of millions around the world, a nationwide flight stoppage ordered by the Federal Aviation Administration that disrupted more than 18,000 flights, plus outages at major banks, Apple, and internal systems of the National Security Agency. A source close to the White House said the president fully backed the dismissal, because Cairncross 'lacks the strong cyber qualifications needed for the post', while officials said the outages were linked to maintenance and security updates, not a coordinated attack, and there is no evidence of hackers or foreign parties. Cairncross had held the post since August 2025.

Simply put: the national cyber director is the coordinator of America's digital protection; and when banks, aviation, and mail all fail in a single week, the White House looks for new leadership it trusts for its expertise.

Is firing one official enough to restore trust after outages on this scale?
