🎮 Qwen 4 Max: A Model That Builds Complete Games and Tests Them Itself in Unreal and Unity!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Alibaba announced on November 16 its new flagship model, Qwen 4 Max, a huge model with 2.4 trillion parameters, of which only 71 billion are activated at each token, with a context window of up to a million tokens, and it understands text, images, and video together.

Its standout ability is that it does not just write code; it builds a complete playable game and tests it itself: it works inside the Unity, Unreal Engine, and Godot engines, running the game, watching it, and improving the visuals, camera, and movement from its observations, then testing again - in other words, it plays its own game before handing it to you. Among its samples is a game called Mew of Magic about a wizard cat, built inside Unreal Engine. It also builds 3D models inside Blender.

As for prices, they are much cheaper than rival flagship models: the API costs $1.50 per million input tokens and $4.50 per million output tokens. The model is available through the company's cloud services and developer tools, but it is a proprietary model with closed weights - the company promised to open the weights of smaller models from the same family later.

Simply put: when the model builds the game, plays it, and fixes it itself, the developer's role becomes directing the idea, not writing every line. Do you think small game studios will explode with these models, or will big games remain a human craft?
