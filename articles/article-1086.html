💾 Galaxy S27: The Fastest Memory Reserved for the Priciest Models.. A Leak Reveals the Details!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new leak has revealed the memory and storage options for Samsung's upcoming Galaxy S27 series, and the standout detail is that the fastest types of memory and storage will be reserved for the most expensive models only.

According to the leak, both the Galaxy S27 Pro and Galaxy S27 Ultra will come in three options: 12 GB of memory with 256 GB of storage, 12 GB with 512 GB, and 16 GB with 1 TB.

As for the faster LPDDR6 memory and the latest UFS 5.0 storage, on the Pro model only the 16 GB with 1 TB version will get them, while on the Ultra model the 12 GB with 512 GB and 16 GB with 1 TB versions will get them — the cheaper versions will stay on the previous generation of memory and storage.

Advertisement

That means a buyer of the cheapest version could pay flagship-phone money without getting the fastest available memory, while a buyer of the most expensive version gets higher speed when opening apps, transferring files, and running AI tasks.

The likely reason is the global rise in memory prices, as companies try to cut costs in the cheaper versions, while other reports suggest the series itself may become more expensive than its predecessor for the same reason.

Samsung has not confirmed any of these details officially, and the leak should be treated with caution until the official announcement.

Simply put: you might pay flagship money and still not get the fastest memory unless you choose the most expensive version.. Would you accept this split, or would you rather all versions were equal in speed?

If the only difference between the two versions is memory speed, would you pay the difference or settle for the cheaper one?
