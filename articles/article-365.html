🤖 Humanoid Robot 'Atlas' Enters Chip Factories for the First Time in Early 2027!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Boston Dynamics" and "GlobalFoundries" have announced an expanded robotics partnership, making "GlobalFoundries" the first semiconductor maker to test the humanoid robot "Atlas" under an early-adopter program, with trials inside its factories starting in early 2027.

The partnership will also expand the use of the four-legged robot "Spot" for equipment inspections across the company's plants in Malta, New York, Burlington in Vermont, Dresden in Germany, and Singapore. The company has used "Spot" in Burlington for more than four years, and in Singapore two robots have covered about 900 kilometers and completed more than 90,000 inspections since April, with a 99% success rate.

An accompanying skills-development workshop aims to identify tasks the humanoid robot can perform inside the factory, assess the readiness of facilities and staff, and start early training on specific tasks, as part of the company's global program to qualify its employees in AI. The first tasks, the number of robots, and the factories that will host them have not yet been revealed.
