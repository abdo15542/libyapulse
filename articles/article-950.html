💻 Asus's Two New Laptops Run AI Offline! Memory Up to 128 GB... and a Cinematic Screen!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Asus launched two new laptops in India on 3 November for creators and AI developers — the ProArt P16 and ProArt P14 — the first creator laptops powered by the Nvidia RTX Spark N1X platform, designed to run large AI models on the device itself, without needing the cloud.

The ProArt P16 carries a 16-inch 4K OLED touchscreen with a 120 Hz refresh rate and unified memory of up to 128 GB, while the ProArt P14 has a 14-inch 3K OLED touchscreen and 24 GB of memory. Both have Pantone-validated color screens with brightness of up to 1,600 nits on the larger model, a battery of up to 99.9 Wh with 140 W charging over USB-C, and Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.4.

The platform pairs Nvidia's Grace processor with a Blackwell graphics card, so the processor and graphics share one unified memory. The laptop can run huge AI models and agents that work around the clock inside your own device, keeping your files private with no cloud fees per query.

Advertisement

Prices in India start at 4,99,990 rupees for the larger model with 64 GB of memory, and 6,49,990 rupees for the 128 GB version, while the smaller model costs 3,29,990 rupees. Sales began on 3 November through Asus stores, Reliance Digital, Croma, Flipkart and Amazon, with launch offers including interest-free installments and an extended warranty until 30 November. The company also launched the ProArt MD301 mouse and the ProArt KD300 keyboard alongside them.

Simply put: a laptop that puts the power to run AI models in your pocket, but at a price starting from around 3,29,990 rupees... so is it worth it?
