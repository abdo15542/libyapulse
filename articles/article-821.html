📱 A screenless device from Nothing — AI that listens to you and remembers you, on 8 December!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On 20 November, Nothing confirmed that it will officially unveil its first AI device on 8 December — a screenless device offering an experience that cannot be built as an ordinary phone app. The confirmation came after musician Sebastian Ingrosso published a photo on 19 November showing a small screenless device among headphones and a laptop.

The company revealed some design details: a metal case with a textured body, two pill-shaped lights in red and green, a white button with a red dot that appears to be for recording, plus at least one camera. Company founder Carl Pei says the idea rests on the notion that "context doesn't live in a chat box" — the device remembers what is happening around you and gives answers based on your context, instead of you opening a chat app every time.

The launch comes at a difficult time for standalone AI devices, after Humane shut down in early 2025 and momentum faded around the Rabbit R1. Will Nothing's device succeed where others failed, or will the phone remain the king of AI devices?

Simply put: a small screenless device that is supposed to understand the context of your life and remember what you do — would you trust a device that listens to you all the time?
