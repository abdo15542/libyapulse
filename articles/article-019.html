Dubai Launches Its 'Smart City Agent'! 🏙️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Dubai has adopted an initiative that makes AI agents a core part of government work: 93 specialized agents in human resources, finance and procurement, expected to carry out more than 34 million tasks a year, save more than 812,000 working hours and cut costs by more than 151 million dirhams.
