📶 vivo T6x Phone Appears in India Certifications Before Launch!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

vivo’s new “T6x 5G” phone appeared in the database of India’s standards authority, signaling that its launch in India is near, and it had previously appeared in Bluetooth certifications. The phone is the successor to the vivo T5x, and is expected to be released soon in the Indian market, without the company revealing its specs or price yet.

Simply put: appearing in official certification records means the company has completed its basic tests — usually the last step before the official announcement.
