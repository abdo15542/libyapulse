💻 Copilot Finally Comes to Mac! A Native App for Mac Devices — Summoned with a Keyboard Shortcut!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Microsoft has finally launched a native Copilot app for Mac, after it was previously available only on iPhone and iPad — so Mac users can now reach the AI assistant directly from their computers.

The new app works like the ChatGPT app on Mac: it can be summoned with a keyboard shortcut at any moment, without opening the browser or hunting for it among windows.

The iPhone and iPad apps also got updates at the same time, so users of all Apple devices get a consistent experience with the same assistant.

Advertisement

The launch comes amid a fierce race between AI assistants on the desktop: in the same days, Google expanded Gemini's abilities on Mac to run desktop apps and fill in forms on the user's behalf.

Simply put: Microsoft's smart assistant now lives inside Mac like any native app — one click summons it whenever you want.
