🪙 Bitcoin Breaks Below $60,000 — Its Lowest Since September 2024!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Bitcoin, the world's largest cryptocurrency, fell below $60,000 on Friday, for the first time since last July, amid growing concern about overvalued AI stocks — a worry that hit all high-risk assets.

The coin continued falling overnight to about $55,830 late Friday evening — its lowest level since September 2024 — after hitting an intraday low of $57,716. Bitcoin has now lost more than half its value from its all-time high above $126,000, set in October 2025.

The drop triggered the liquidation of about $2 billion in leveraged positions within 24 hours, including about $94.6 million in a single hour across Binance, Bybit, and OKX as the $60,000 level broke. The total crypto market value also fell about 5.7% to about $2.03 trillion, and the Fear and Greed Index dropped into extreme fear territory.

Simply put: imagine a cryptocurrency priced above $126,000 just a few weeks ago, then collapsing below $56,000 in a single wave of fear — that is what Bitcoin's latest drop looks like, wiping out all post-US-election gains.
