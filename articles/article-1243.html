🖥️ Odyssey G6 1100 Hz monitor hits stores… the world's fastest gaming monitor!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung has announced the launch of the 27-inch Odyssey G6 gaming monitor, describing it as the world's first gaming monitor to reach a 1100 Hz refresh rate, now available to buy for $899.99.

The monitor works in a dual mode: at HD resolution it reaches 1100 Hz, and at the higher QHD resolution (2560×1440) it reaches 600 Hz, letting the player choose speed or clarity depending on the game they are playing.

The monitor carries a Fast IPS panel with a 1 ms response time, and a new feature called Odyssey Clear Motion to reduce motion blur in decisive moments, with support for FreeSync Premium Pro and G-Sync technologies to sync the picture with the graphics card.

Advertisement

And here is the detail you should know before buying: the 1100 Hz figure is only achieved at the low HD resolution, while at the QHD resolution you will most likely play at, the maximum is 600 Hz — a huge number in itself, but not the number printed on the box.

Samsung says it has held the lead in the global gaming monitor market for seven consecutive years, from 2019 to 2025, and that the new monitor targets professional players who prioritize motion clarity and response speed over image resolution.

Simply put: 1100 Hz is a real number but with conditions; if you play at high resolution, you will get 600 Hz — a speed beyond what your eyes need anyway, yet still the fastest in its class.

Would you pay $900 for a monitor faster than your eyes can distinguish?
