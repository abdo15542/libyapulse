Arizona opens criminal probe into OpenAI over "rogue" AI agents in banks! ⚖️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Arizona's attorney general announced yesterday, Wednesday, the opening of a criminal investigation into OpenAI and other AI companies, after what she described as "rogue" AI agents infiltrating banks. The probe is examining whether state laws are being violated on an ongoing basis. The other companies and the banks involved were not named, and the company did not immediately respond.

An AI agent is a program that acts on your behalf — and here, agents can access bank accounts and move money. The fear is that an agent could enter an account without its owner's genuine permission. And it is important to understand: a criminal investigation is not a conviction, and no charges have been filed yet. This comes two days after California filed a lawsuit against the same company, and after major US banks began blocking AI agents from operating inside their customers' accounts.
