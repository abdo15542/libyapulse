⚖️ A European Court Suspends a Lawyer for Two Years... Because AI Invented Fake Rulings for Her!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 13, the president of the European Court of Human Rights decided to suspend a Romanian lawyer from representing applicants before the court for two years, after it turned out that her briefs, drafted by AI, cited rulings that do not exist at all, invented paragraphs attributed to real rulings, and real precedents that had nothing to do with her case or contradicted her argument.

The lawyer admitted using AI tools, and described the errors as 'inaccuracies' and 'citation errors', but the court found a pattern of many, repeated, and material errors, not just isolated slips, and that she had not checked her references in the court's official database. The press release about the decision was published on November 18.

The suspension applies to the lawyer alone, not to the second representative in the case, and she may later request to be readmitted, and her name was not revealed.

Simply put: AI writes with confidence even when it invents, and here the price was a lawyer's career; so is it enough to admit using AI, or is checking every reference a duty that allows no shortcuts?
