🖥️ 'Vise' Raises $8.75 Million — Shared Workstations for Humans and AI Agents!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Startup "Vise" has raised $8.75 million in a seed round led by "Andreessen Horowitz", with other investors participating, to solve a problem anyone working with AI agents knows: computer-using agents take over an employee's screen while automating tasks, stopping the human from working. The new company was founded by the "Parsec" team — the company "Unity" acquired for $320 million in 2021 — and is led by its founder Benji Boxer.

The platform turns powerful workstations — which can cost more than $10,000 — into flexible shared resources used by humans and agents together. Instead of every employee getting a powerful machine that sits idle during meetings and breaks, the machines pool together and are allocated when needed, to whichever employees or agents need them, via low-latency streaming technology that makes remote work feel like working on the machine itself.

Agents can control the workstations remotely through a dedicated server using the Model Context Protocol, logging in and running applications exactly as an employee would, but with the user's own permissions and no more. In a demonstration, a tech-support bot handled an employee's request via "Slack" by logging into their computer and diagnosing the problem, then smoothly handed the session to a human engineer when the fix needed finer intervention.

Simply put: the powerful computer on your desk is no longer owned by one person, but a shared resource where human and agent work together — without one stealing the other's screen.
