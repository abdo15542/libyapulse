Gemini Comes to Budget Phones: AI for Everyone! 📱

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 18, Google announced that the Gemini app is coming to low-memory phones. Android Go is a lightweight version of Android made for cheap phones with small memory — phones used by hundreds of millions of people around the world, especially in developing countries.

Google says it rebuilt the app to run smoothly on these devices, enabling voice conversations and interview practice in more than 45 languages through Gemini Live, pointing the camera at notes or models to learn and translate, and creating custom images through Nano Banana.

The app can be downloaded from Google Play, or by long-pressing the power button on supported phones — or it will come preinstalled on upcoming devices from partners like Transsion.
