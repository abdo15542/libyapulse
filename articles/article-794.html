🛰️ Blue Origin Withdraws From Orbital Reef Station and Turns to the Moon!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Blue Origin has announced that it will not apply for funding from the American space agency for its commercial space station Orbital Reef, which it was developing to replace the International Space Station in the 2030s, and will focus instead on Moon projects.

The agency had granted the company $130 million in the first phase of the commercial low Earth orbit destinations program, and Orbital Reef was designed as a mixed-use business complex in orbit, developed with partners including Thales Alenia Space, Sierra Space, and Redwire.

The company itself is deeply involved in the Moon program: it is developing Blue Moon landers to carry crews and cargo to the lunar surface, as part of the agency's plans for an ambitious series of Moon missions ending with a permanent base around 2033.

The decision narrows the number of companies eligible to advance to the program's second phase to three: Axiom Space, Starlab Space, and Vast Space. The agency issued its request for proposals on October 9, with full proposals due on December 8 and the winner to be announced in spring 2027.

Simply put: the race for commercial space stations narrows to three competitors, while Blue Origin chooses the Moon; so does humanity's future lie in orbit or on the Moon's surface first?
