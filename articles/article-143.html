From Song to Music Video in Minutes 🎵

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Suno launched its new “Visuals” feature: turn any AI-generated song into images and short video clips up to 15 seconds long, with lip movement synced to the song’s lyrics.

The feature runs on a new model called H3 Move and uses a separate credit balance from the music balance, with filters that prevent imitating the faces of real artists.

Simply put: anyone can now make a music video for their song without a camera or a studio — but the platform still faces lawsuits from artists over music rights.
