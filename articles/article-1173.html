🤖 "Superintelligence Is Coming"! A Shocking Warning from Meta's AI Chief — and Can We Control It?

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Alexander Wang, Meta's chief AI officer, published an essay on Sunday, November 22, titled "Superintelligence Is Coming", warning that the decisions humanity makes over the next year could shape the world future generations live in.

Wang leads Meta's superintelligence research labs, and he believes the hardest part of building systems smarter than humans is largely behind us — what remains is the specialized expertise and the infrastructure needed to run huge experiments.

What worries him most is that models are now improving themselves. He wrote that it is "hard to imagine a world in which humanity stays in control with a superintelligence a thousand times smarter than it and more capable in every field", and he called for treating safety with the same seriousness as launching stronger models.

The warning came after an Anthropic researcher announced on November 20 that he had found a proof for a 167-year-old math problem with the help of the model Claude — which Wang described as the "pinnacle of intellectual achievement" for anyone who has studied math.

In Washington, Representative Yasmine Ansari called on the new Congress to put AI at the top of its agenda from day one: pausing the development of advanced models, regulating the industry, and banning artificial superintelligence.

She is a sponsor of a bill introduced in September that freezes training of models whose computing power exceeds a set threshold until a new Department of AI is created and sets safety rules, and bans superintelligence outright, with penalties of up to 20 years for serious violations. But the bill is still in committee, and the new Congress starts work in January 2027.

Simply put: Meta's AI chief says intelligence smarter than humans is now close, and the question is no longer "is it coming?" but "can we control it?"
