🗳️ The US Senate Passes a Law Banning Bets on Wars and Assassinations!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US Senate passed by voice vote on November 19 a bill known as the "Death Bets Act", banning platforms registered with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission from offering betting contracts on terrorism, assassinations, wars, US military operations, and the death of any person — and the bill has now been referred to the House of Representatives.

The bill was introduced in March 2026 by Senator Adam Schiff, co-sponsored by Senator John Curtis, and came after six wallets on the "Polymarket" platform earned about $1.2 million betting on the timing of US strikes against Iran in February, raising suspicions of insider trading, without the traders' identities being known.

The law imposes no new criminal penalties, but relies on existing federal commodities laws and the commission's enforcement, noting that these contracts are already banned under federal regulations. The "Kalshi" platform had previously fined two traders, one of them more than $20,000, for trading on confidential information.

Simply put: betting on someone's death or a war breaking out opens the door to profiting from secret information, and the new law closes that door on licensed platforms.
