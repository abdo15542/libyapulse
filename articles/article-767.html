💰 StepSecurity Raises $20 Million to Protect Developer Tools from Hackers!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 18, StepSecurity announced it had raised $20 million in a new funding round led by Elephant, bringing its total raised to $23 million. The company was founded in 2021 by engineers who worked at Microsoft for nearly two decades, after witnessing famous supply-chain attacks, and it built tools to protect the computers developers write code on, code repositories, software packages, and automated build and delivery systems.

The announcement came in the same week that the company's researchers investigated two malicious packages from Microsoft's Agent Framework library, published on the PyPI repository on November 17, which stole cloud credentials and build systems as soon as Python ran. The company says it documented 56 confirmed compromises of trusted software components between August 2025 and August 2026, and that its annual revenue has multiplied fivefold in each of the past three years.

The company is expanding its protection to isolated environments where AI agents run, because an agent that writes code might download software packages without anyone noticing. It also made its package-scanning service free for open-source projects.

Simply put: today's attack does not start at your front door, but from a software library you trust and download every day; do you think scanning every package before downloading it has become essential, or is speed more important than caution?
