Britain's Financial Watchdog Launches the World's First Platform for Tracking AI Use in the Financial Sector. The new platform is called the 'AI Observatory' and brings together more than 200 institutions working in around 150 different fields, from banks to insurance companies. Simply put: regulators will know where AI is actually being used, and what risks might arise from it, before they turn into a problem. That way, financial institutions can innovate with confidence, while people's money and data stay better protected.
