🛰️ 36 "Amazon Leo" satellites on the launch pad — and the countdown starts Monday!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Amazon Leo" announced on November 21 that its packaged payload of 36 satellites is on the launch pad in preparation for integration with the "Ariane 6" rocket, and that the launch is proceeding as planned on Monday, November 23, from the European spaceport in Kourou, French Guiana, with the launch window opening at 11:09 a.m. Greenwich time.

The mission is called "LE-04", the fourth European flight of "Leo" satellites — the satellite internet network formerly known as "Project Kuiper". Amazon has so far 396 satellites in orbit across 14 missions since April 2025, and needs thousands of satellites for the network to be complete.

The launch comes days after the failure of the "Vulcan" rocket on November 15, which was carrying 40 of the same "Leo" satellites, when fuel did not reach its engines in sufficient quantities after about 10 minutes of flight. The US aviation authority opened an investigation into the incident, while Amazon prepares to continue deploying its network via "Ariane 6".

The rocket launches from Kourou near the equator, where rockets benefit from the Earth's extra rotation speed to save fuel. If the launch succeeds, the number of "Leo" satellites in orbit will rise to 432 — a new step in the satellite-internet race against competing networks.

Simply put: imagine a space train preparing 36 carriages at once for launch from the closest point to the equator. Every new satellite brings satellite internet closer to your home.
