⚡ Inside OpenAI: Codex Agents Chase Down ChatGPT’s Slowness!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI revealed an internal system called the “Performance Factory” that uses “Codex” agents to investigate ChatGPT performance regressions. The system ran more than 1,700 investigations in over three months, at a rate of about 17 investigations a day, found the root cause in more than 600 cases, proposed about 400 fixes, and around 50 of them were merged — including 15 fixes with code written verbatim by Codex. A second Codex session also reviews the diagnoses adversarially and sends back about 25% of them, and the system reached 70% accuracy across one hundred manual reviews, while the final merge decision stays with the human engineer.

Simply put: AI fixes itself; agents search for the cause of the slowness and suggest the fix, and a human engineer reviews and decides.

Would you trust a fix written by AI without human review?
