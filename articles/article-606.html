🕵️ "MoonLock" comes to iPhone — an app that checks scam messages before you fall for them!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The company MacPaw launched its security app "MoonLock" on iPhone phones, to protect users from the scam messages that reach them daily.

The app checks suspicious text messages, screenshots and QR codes, then explains to the user why the message looks like a scam, before he presses any link or sends any data.

Scam messages are messages pretending to be from a bank, a shipping company or an official body, aiming to steal your password or money, and they have become so polished that it is hard to tell them apart by eye.

Advertisement

A QR code is that dotted square you open with your phone's camera to reach a site or service; scammers may use it to take you to a fake site resembling the real one.

The idea is that the app works as a guard reading the message before you, warning you of the danger before you get involved.

Simply put: imagine an expert friend sitting beside you reading every suspicious message, saying "Careful, this is a trick" before you press. That is what "MoonLock" does.
