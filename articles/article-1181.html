💹 Supertech Raises $20 Million! AI That Turns Your View of the Market Into a Stock Portfolio!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Startup Supertech has raised $20 million in a seed funding round, to build a service that turns people’s views on the market into real investment portfolios using artificial intelligence.

The round was led by Adjacent, with participation from Coinbase Ventures and Union Square Ventures, and well-known investors joined in, including Nat Friedman, Guillermo Rauch, Nikita Bier, and Scott Belsky, after an earlier round worth $2 million.

The idea looks simple on the surface: you explain to the service in your own everyday language how you see the world changing, the AI talks it through with you back and forth, then turns your view into an investment “position” — a real stock portfolio executed through your account.

Advertisement

Users’ ideas are shown on a public leaderboard, so anyone who agrees with you can invest with you in the same idea — like the idea that interest in appearance and longevity will grow, so the portfolio holds drug and beauty companies.

The service currently works with the Coinbase and Robinhood platforms, and plans to expand to other brokers, and to operations like short selling, derivatives, and prediction markets through partners.

To reassure users, the company put strict controls in place: the AI cannot deposit or withdraw money without your explicit permission, it cannot see your bank account login details, and there are limits on drifting away from the portfolio, allow and block lists, limits on trade sizes, and real-time monitoring of unusual activity.

The company is led by Lincoln Moore as CEO and John Peterson as CTO, both from Coinbase, while Michael Mignano, the former founder of Anchor, oversees the board of directors.

Simply put: instead of picking stocks yourself, you tell the AI your view of the future, and it builds you a complete portfolio — but your money stays in your account and only moves with your permission.

Would you trust an AI to manage your money and buy stocks on your behalf?
