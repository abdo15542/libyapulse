📱 iQOO 16 Appears on Amazon.. A 2-Nanometer Chip With Speeds Up to 5.01 GHz!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The iQOO 16 phone appeared briefly on Amazon's site in India on a dedicated page for it, before the page was quickly pulled — a strong sign that the phone's launch in India is now very close.

The page showed that the phone runs OriginOS 7, built on Android 17 — the latest version of Google's system — meaning a new interface and modern features from day one.

But the most important thing about the phone is its Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip from Qualcomm, built on 2-nanometer technology, with a top speed of 5.01 GHz — one of the fastest chips in any smartphone yet.

Advertisement

The head of iQOO in India has announced that the phone will launch in the country soon, without giving an exact date, and it is expected to carry the same design as the Chinese version of the phone.

The 2-nanometer technology means the chip's components are smaller than ever before, so the phone runs faster and uses less power — which shows in gaming and AI performance and in battery life.

Simply put: a page that appeared and then vanished has revealed an upcoming phone with the fastest chip on the market.. Would you buy a new phone for the chip alone?

If your current phone works fine, what would push you to switch: speed, the camera, or the battery?
