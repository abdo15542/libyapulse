💻 Huawei Unveils the MateBook Fold S — a Foldable Laptop with Three Screens!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Huawei has unveiled its new MateBook Fold S, a foldable laptop with three screens, in a bold move to rethink the very shape of the computer.

The main display is a 14.7-inch foldable OLED screen with high resolution and a 120Hz refresh rate; folded in half it becomes a 10.2-inch screen, while a small 3.5-inch square screen on the outer cover adds an unprecedented touch: answering messages, starting a meeting recording or checking notifications without opening the device at all.

The device runs on Huawei's Kirin X90 processor and the company's own HarmonyOS 7, with up to 32GB of memory and up to 1TB of storage, plus a 6,021 mAh battery supporting 66W charging.

Advertisement

Despite all that, the device weighs just 639 grams without the keyboard and is only 6.49 mm thick when open, making it one of the lightest foldable laptops ever.

The specs also include a 50-megapixel rear camera with a spectral lens for better colours, a 12-megapixel front camera for video calls and an infrared camera for face recognition, plus Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0 support.

Prices start at 14,999 Chinese yuan (around $2,250) and reach 24,999 yuan for the limited edition, with a Master Edition at 29,999 yuan; sales begin in China first on November 27, with no announced date for global markets.

Simply put: Huawei is not just folding the screen — it is adding a third one on the cover; a laptop you once had to open to see what was inside now tells you from the outside, and the question is whether the third screen is a practical idea or just a showpiece.

Would you buy a foldable laptop with three screens, or do you prefer a traditional laptop?
