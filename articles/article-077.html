Apple Announces the Top 45 Apps and Games of 2026! 🏆

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple announced yesterday 45 finalists for the 2026 App Store Awards, chosen by App Store editors around the world based on user experience, design, innovation, and cultural impact. The categories include iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro, Apple Watch, and Apple TV apps and games, plus Apple Arcade, as well as a Cultural Impact category.

Apple's annual awards for the best apps are like the Oscars for apps — and the shortlist is a great place to discover new apps worth trying. Among the nominees for iPhone App of the Year: ElevenReader, Not Boring Camera, and Reilly; and for Game of the Year: Pull X Beat, Dave the Diver, and Gambonanza. The winners will be announced in the coming weeks, and there is no public vote — the editors choose.
