Science & Innovation

A new economic report in Sweden has found that the office jobs most exposed to AI fell by 13% over two years, from around 114,000 jobs in 2023 to around 98,500 in 2025. The affected jobs include customer service, accounting assistants, human resources and procurement. The survey also found that 83% of the union branches surveyed said their employers had introduced AI, compared with only 29% three years ago. But the report itself cautions that the figures do not prove AI is the only cause — economic conditions may also play a part. Do you think AI will create as many new jobs as it eliminates? 💼
