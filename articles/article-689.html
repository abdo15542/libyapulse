💻 Microsoft launches two new Surface devices with a battery lasting more than 22 hours!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft has expanded its Surface lineup with two new devices running Windows: the 12-inch Surface Pro tablet and the 13-inch Surface Laptop, plus a new wireless mouse.

Both devices run on Qualcomm's Snapdragon X2 Plus platform, with up to 24GB of memory and up to 512GB of storage. The Surface Pro supports a detachable keyboard and the Slim Pen, while the Surface Laptop carries a full backlit keyboard and a precise touchpad. Both devices have touch screens with 500 nits of brightness, support Wi-Fi 7, and run Windows 11 Home.

The company says the Surface Laptop's battery reaches 22.5 hours of video playback — enough for a full working day and more without a charger, thanks to the efficiency of Snapdragon processors built on ARM architecture. The company has not yet announced the official prices of the two devices in all markets.

Simply put: a laptop that works a full day without a charger. Has battery life become the first standard when buying a new computer?
