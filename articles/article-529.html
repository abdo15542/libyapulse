📅 Google Sets Its Developer Conference Date: May 19–20, 2026 — and AI Takes Center Stage!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Google announced that its annual developer conference, Google I/O 2026, will be held on May 19 and 20, 2026. The date was revealed after users solved an interactive online puzzle on a 'Save the Date' page featuring mini-games powered by the Gemini AI.

The conference is expected to focus heavily on AI, with updates across Gemini, the Android system, Search, the Chrome browser, the Workspace suite, and Pixel devices. The company will likely stream the keynote sessions live online, while the in-person venue has not yet been confirmed.

Simply put: imagine the world's biggest tech company turning the date of its own conference into a puzzle for developers to solve — that is how Google announced its next conference, six months before it happens.
