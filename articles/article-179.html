🔋 Lava Launches the Smart 4 Pro Phone with a 7000 mAh Battery and No Ads!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Lava launched its new “Smart 4 Pro” phone in India, the third phone in the Smart 4 series, running on Android 16, and the company says it has no bloatware and no ads. The phone carries a huge 7000 mAh battery, a 13-megapixel rear camera, a 6.75-inch LCD display with HD resolution and a 90Hz refresh rate, and military-grade drop and shock resistance certification MIL-STD-810H. It comes in two colors, the gold “Konark Gold” and the blue “Neelam Blue,” and is sold in India through direct-sale stores only, with free home repair service.

Simply put: a 7000 mAh battery means two days or more of use without charging for most people; and the phone is aimed at anyone who wants a cheap, clean phone with no ads.
