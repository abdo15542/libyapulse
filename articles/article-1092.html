🛡️ CrowdStrike Officially Buys Zscaler for $43.9 Billion.. The Biggest Cybersecurity Deal in History!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Cybersecurity companies CrowdStrike and Zscaler announced on Monday a final agreement in which the first will acquire the second in an all-stock deal worth $43.9 billion, after weeks of reports about negotiations between them whose outcome was uncertain.

The two companies say the deal will create the 'largest single market' in cybersecurity, combining CrowdStrike's endpoint protection software — for computers, phones, and devices connected to a company's network — with Zscaler's zero-trust platform, which serves around 8,000 companies.

Zero trust is a simple security principle: the system does not automatically trust any user or device; instead, it asks for identity verification every time someone tries to access an internal system or resource — even if they are inside the company's own building — making it harder for hackers to steal a password and then roam freely inside the network.

The two companies added that merging the two products will give customers 'superior protection' against data leaks and help them adopt AI safely, as AI attacks have become faster and harder to detect.

The deal still needs regulatory approval, and it is expected to close around this time next year — so it will not complete immediately despite the announcement.
