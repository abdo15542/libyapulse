🚁 Your Food Arrives by Drone! Uber Eats Starts Aerial Delivery in Houston!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On Tuesday, November 17, Uber Eats began commercial drone delivery in the city of Spring, Texas, near Houston, in the first commercial launch within its partnership with Zipline announced in August. All the customer has to do is choose the 'by drone' option in the app, and the meal arrives within minutes.

Zipline's drones fly at speeds of up to 70 miles per hour, carry up to 8 pounds over a distance of 10 miles, and drop the food from a height of about 330 feet on a tether without landing, operating daily from 8 in the morning until 10 at night, with no extra fees and no tip because the delivery is fully automated.

National restaurants such as Jersey Mike's and Zaxby's took part in the launch, alongside local restaurants, and the two companies plan to expand to Dallas and then dozens of American cities, aiming for one million deliveries a day by the end of 2029.

Simply put: your food drops from the sky instead of arriving on a bike; so would you order your meal by drone if it came to your city?
