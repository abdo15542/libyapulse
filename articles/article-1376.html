📱 HMD Launches 'Rise' for Under 10,000 Rupees… AI That Speaks 22 Indian Languages!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

HMD has launched its new phone 'Rise' in India, a budget 4G phone with a special first-sale-day price of 9,999 Indian rupees, with sales starting on December 2 through the Flipkart store.

The phone runs on the octa-core Unisoc 9863A processor, with 4 GB of memory and 64 GB of storage expandable to 128 GB with a memory card, a 6.517-inch display, and a 5,000 mAh battery with a charger in the box.

The rear camera has a 13-megapixel main sensor within a dual unit, and a 5-megapixel front camera, with a USB-C port and a 3.5 mm headphone jack, and splash resistance rated IP52.

The phone runs Android 16, the lightweight Go edition, with quarterly security updates for two years, and comes in three colors: silvery blue, dark gold, and emerald gold, in a dual-tone design.

What stands out is that the phone carries the 'Indus' AI platform from Sarvam, which supports 22 Indian languages, with optional integration with the government's 'Sanchar Saathi' phone-safety platform, in an attempt to make AI understandable in the user's own language.

Simply put: a cheap phone with a big battery and AI that understands India's local languages; is the low price alone enough to convince you to buy a budget phone?
