🛰️ A Chinese Rocket Launches 9 New Internet Satellites — the Guowang Constellation Approaches 250 Satellites!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

China successfully launched 9 new satellites for the Guowang space internet network, also known as SatNet, aboard a Long March 8A rocket from the coastal Wenchang spaceport on November 20.

The rocket lifted off at exactly 11:59 GMT, carrying the constellation's thirtieth batch of satellites into low Earth orbit, raising the number of the network's satellites in orbit to between 231 and 247, depending on the source, out of 13,000 satellites planned in the end.

China aims to launch 310 satellites for the constellation this year, then 900 satellites in 2027, and 3,600 satellites a year after that, in a direct race with other space internet networks building similarly huge constellations.

This launch was the thirteenth for the Long March 8A rocket overall, and its seventh during 2026, while SpaceX leads the world's launch race this year with 125 launches, followed by China with 77 launches, then Rocket Lab with 19 launches and Russia with 14.

Simply put: China is building a giant space internet network of thousands of satellites, and each new launch adds 9 satellites at once, with the goal of reaching the network everywhere on Earth.
