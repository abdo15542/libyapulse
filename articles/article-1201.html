⌨️ A Tiny Desktop Device Turns Your Whisper Into Text! "VoicePad" With Five Buttons Launches Your AI Assistants With One Press!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A crowdfunding campaign launched today, November 24, for a new desktop device called "VoicePad" from Second Brain — a small tool that sits on your desk and combines a programmable button pad with a whisper-powered voice assistant.

The device carries five fully customizable buttons: you program each button in the companion app to instantly open an app such as ChatGPT, Claude, Notion or YouTube, run a familiar shortcut, or start an AI agent such as a meeting-summarizing agent.

To dictate text, you just pick up the device or tap it and then speak, and it types your words as clean, punctuated text inside whatever app you're working in, automatically removing filler words like "um" and false starts.

Advertisement

The standout feature is "whisper voice": the device carries a dual-microphone array designed to pick up your voice as you speak at a naturally low volume, making voice commands practical in busy open-plan offices, where ordinary laptop dictation fails.

The device is deeply integrated into the Genspark ecosystem — its app and workspace — so your notes, meetings and commands go there for it to learn how you work and take routine admin tasks off your hands.

As for price, it's $169 for general sale, and between $119 and $149 in the crowdfunding campaign running until December. Every copy includes unlimited dictation for a year, 300 minutes a month of transcription and summarization for life, and 1,000 credits for the Genspark Super Agent.

Simply put: instead of hunting for the right tab and typing what you're thinking, you whisper what you want and the device writes it for you and starts the right assistant.

Would you rather whisper to your computer than type?
