🚀 Europe's Hera Approaches the Asteroid NASA Struck... and Reveals the Secrets of Planetary Defense!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The European spacecraft Hera has begun a close survey of the Didymos binary asteroid system, which the US space agency deliberately struck four years ago, in the first mission in history to return to the site of a designed impact meant to protect Earth.

The European Space Agency announced on November 12 that the spacecraft is now studying the large asteroid and its small moon Dimorphos from just 15 to 18 miles away — around 25 to 30 kilometers — after reaching its target in early November, following a journey of more than two years since its launch from Florida in October 2024.

The agency published its first close image on November 5 from around 60 kilometers away, then a second image on November 12 from around 56 kilometers, showing huge boulders, smooth areas and a sharp ridge at the large asteroid's equator — a side the DART spacecraft's camera could not see before the impact.

The large asteroid is around 780 meters across, and its small moon around 151 meters, and the DART spacecraft had struck the small moon in September 2022 at around 6.1 kilometers per second, shortening its orbit around the large asteroid by 33 minutes and nudging it onto a slightly different path around the Sun, with a debris cloud stretching thousands of kilometers.

Hera carries 25 scientific instruments and two shoebox-sized mini-satellites, one of which will attempt to land on the small moon, and detailed images are expected on November 25, before the spacecraft moves closer in December to a distance of 20 kilometers to measure the crater's size and the asteroid's internal structure.

Simply put: DART proved we can push an asteroid off its path, and Hera is now returning to the scene of the impact to understand exactly what the collision did... Will this survey become our first guide to defending our planet?

If a dangerous asteroid ever approached Earth, would it reassure you to know that humans have already tried changing the path of one?
