Meta Is Testing a "Direct" Messaging App That Works Without an Instagram Account! 💬

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Meta is testing a new standalone app called "Direct", dedicated solely to Instagram messages — much like it once split Messenger off from Facebook.

The idea is to let you message your friends without opening Instagram and losing time scrolling through posts.

And the surprise: the app works even without an Instagram account at all — signing up with an email address or phone number is enough, and only your contacts can message you.

Advertisement

The app is currently available for testing in Turkey and Thailand only, on Android and iPhone, and includes tools such as in-chat drawing, custom stickers, and sound effects. Meta had announced the test on 16 November.
