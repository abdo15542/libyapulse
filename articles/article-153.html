🎮 Beware Before Buying GTA 6: Thousands of Fake Coins and Sites That Steal Wallets!

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

With the launch of the long-awaited game approaching, a security company spotted more than 8,800 fake cryptocurrencies carrying the game’s name on the Solana network alone, out of more than 20,000 similar coins, in addition to hundreds of scam promotional posts. The most dangerous was a fake site imitating the PlayStation Store, offering a copy of the game for $89 — and when you click the buy button, it asks you to connect your crypto wallet instead of paying normally, stealing whatever is in it. Remember: there is no official coin for the game and no free giveaway, and official stores do not accept crypto payments.

Simply put: any offer for the game outside the official stores is a scam.
