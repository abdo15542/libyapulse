💻 Intel signs its first external customer for the 18A process — and the yield of good chips reaches 70%!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Intel has announced that it has signed its first external customer to manufacture chips with its advanced 18A process, in a speech by CEO Lip-Bu Tan during the Tomorrow X summit in Austin on November 18 — a long-awaited step for the company's ambitious project to compete with Taiwanese manufacturing giant TSMC.

The company's chief technology officer later explained that the customer will use the improved 18A-P version, which entered pilot production last June, without revealing the customer's identity. The CEO also said the yield of good chips in this process has reached 70%, after it was described as very poor when he took office in March 2025.

Reports speculate about the customer's identity, among major companies that design their own chips such as Apple and Nvidia and cloud computing companies, while earlier reports suggested that Microsoft may manufacture its new Maia 2 AI chip with this process. Intel did not reveal the size of the order, the value of the contract, or when mass production for the new customer will begin.

Simply put: after years of promises, Intel has finally found an outside customer for its newest process. Is this the start of the company's return to the top of the chip industry, or just a first step on a long road?
