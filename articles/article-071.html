WhatsApp Tests One Username Across Your Facebook and Instagram Accounts! 👤

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

WhatsApp is testing a new feature that lets you use the same username you use in Meta's other apps, such as Facebook and Instagram, for your WhatsApp account. In recent weeks, the app had also been testing the ability to reserve usernames ahead of the full launch of the username system.

A username is a name like "Ahmed" instead of your phone number — so people can find you on WhatsApp without seeing your number. Using the same name across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp makes you easy to reach everywhere. One important note: the feature is still only in development and testing, and hasn't launched yet, so don't go looking for it in your app right now.

Would you rather people know you by a username instead of your phone number? 🤔
