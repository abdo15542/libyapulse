📱 Gemini calls, books, and pays for you... and Tasks become free for millions of users!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google has announced that the 'Tasks' feature in its 'Gemini' assistant has become free for hundreds of millions of users in six countries — the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India — after being limited to paid-plan subscribers. The feature runs on the new 'Gemini 4 Hydrogen' model, and turns the assistant from a chatbot answering questions into a personal assistant that actually carries out tasks.

Just flip a switch inside the app from 'Chat' mode to 'Tasks' mode, then ask in plain words: 'Gemini' calls shops and books appointments after reviewing your calendar, compares products and completes purchases through 'Google Pay', logs in to websites through the password manager, and even sends text messages or forwards emails to itself to carry out what is in them. It uses a secure payment protocol with digital tokens with 'Mastercard' and 'Visa' cards, you can set a spending cap it will not exceed, it asks for your confirmation before any purchase, and your receipts and tracking information reach Google Wallet. Advanced features, such as calling businesses, paying, and logging in, start in the United States first, while the basic feature is available in all six countries.

Simply put: instead of asking the assistant 'What is the best car workshop?', you ask it 'Book me a maintenance appointment on Friday morning' — it calls, waits on the line, books, and adds the appointment to your calendar while you drink your coffee.
