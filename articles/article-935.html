🎮 The critics have spoken: 'GTA 6' scores 99 out of 100! The highest rating in gaming history... but players are less excited!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Review sites lifted the embargo on reviews of 'Grand Theft Auto 6' on November 18, one day before its launch, and the result was exceptional: the game scored 99 out of 100 on Metacritic, matching the highest score in the site's history — the figure held by 'Zelda: Ocarina of Time' since 1998.

On the PlayStation 5 version alone, the score was based on 152 critical reviews, all positive with no mixed or negative reviews, while 118 critics gave the game a perfect score, including reviewers from IGN, GameSpot, and Eurogamer, and 99% of critics recommended it on OpenCritic — a rare consensus in the gaming world.

But the picture among players themselves is completely different: the user score on Metacritic was only 5.4 out of 10 from more than 30,000 ratings — a gap of 45 points from the critics' score — a major split that the site itself does not explain, and which cannot be taken as conclusive evidence of an organized campaign, but it shows that the critics' enthusiasm did not fully reach the player audience.

Advertisement

The parent company had announced on November 20 that first-day sales reached $3.79 billion, with 32.5 million copies sold before launch, making the game the biggest entertainment launch in history — meaning the game succeeded commercially and critically at the same time, even as some players held back in their ratings.

The question remains open: do critics' reviews really reflect the average player's experience, or have perfect scores become part of the launch noise? A game that scores 99 from critics and 5.4 from players puts the credibility of reviews themselves on trial.

Simply put: the critics say it is the best game in history, and the players say 'not quite that good'... who do you believe?

Do you rely on critics' reviews before buying a game, or do you wait for players' opinions first?
