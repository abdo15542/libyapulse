🏭 "Nestlé" turns to "Microsoft" and "Boston Dynamics"! AI and robots in food factories!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Swiss food company "Nestlé" has announced a partnership with "Microsoft" and "Boston Dynamics" to expand the use of AI, robots and smart sensors in its factories and consumer products.

The company signed agreements with both companies to develop AI-powered engineering solutions at its new deep-tech centre in the Swiss city of Orbe, which will serve as a testing and development hub for technologies before they are rolled out across the company's operations worldwide.

"Microsoft" and "Boston Dynamics" will contribute AI capabilities, hardware and engineering expertise to improve manufacturing operations and consumer-facing products, including coffee machines.

Advertisement

The long-term goal is more autonomous production lines, where smart systems monitor operations themselves, adapt to variations in raw materials and maintain consistent quality — but the company stresses that fully autonomous production is a future goal, and human expertise will remain present.

The company is also in advanced talks with "Google" about a possible partnership, and is exploring spatial computing applications from "Apple"; it also plans to use AI to understand consumers and speed up research and development, including personalized coffee offerings and digital tools that help pet owners manage their pets' nutrition and health.
