🔐 Apple Appeals the UK “Backdoor” Order for Encrypted Data!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Apple is appealing a British order asking it to open a “backdoor” to access users’ encrypted cloud storage data, and a British court has confirmed it will hear the case. Apple insists that encrypting user data is a red line, and that any deliberate weakness in encryption would put everyone’s privacy at risk, while the authorities see access as necessary for security investigations.

Simply put: encryption is a lock whose key only the data owner holds; and a “backdoor” means a copy of the key held by another party — and that weakens the lock for everyone.
