📱 Huawei launches the 'Aosta' phone brand six years after selling Honor!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Huawei has launched a new phone brand today called 'Aosta', the first phone sub-brand the company has launched since selling the 'Honor' brand in 2020, in a move aimed at reaching new customers by focusing on AI.

The brand name stands for four principles: authenticity, intelligence, technology, and accessibility. The company showed the first two phones under the new brand, the 'Aosta O1' and 'Aosta O1 Pro', with the second carrying a wide 6.39-inch screen at a 16:9.5 ratio, the company's own 'Kirin 9030S' processor, a 200-megapixel main camera, and a 7,000 mAh battery.

The phone's price starts at 5,699 yuan (about $855), at a reduced introductory price until the end of December. It runs 'HarmonyOS 7' in a special AI edition, where the smart assistant's screen appears as soon as the phone is opened, letting tasks be done through natural conversation without searching for apps. The brand also signed an agreement with the 'JD' store targeting sales worth 70 billion yuan over three years.

Simply put: after Huawei sold its previous brand six years ago, it returns now with a new brand betting that AI is what makes people buy the phone.
