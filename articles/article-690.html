🍎 Apple confirms officially: the new Siri will run on Google's Gemini models!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple has confirmed that the new generation of its voice assistant Siri will run on Google's Gemini models, during its earnings call for the first quarter of 2026 — official confirmation of a partnership that has been the talk of the tech world all week.

The company explained that the new Siri will run on the device itself and through its own Private Cloud Compute service, confirming that Apple will keep control over the assistant's responses, and that its internal AI work will continue alongside the partnership. The two companies did not reveal the financial details of the deal, nor Apple's Intelligence usage figures or its effect on sales.

The two companies said the Gemini-powered Siri will arrive later this year, offering a more personalised assistant experience, after long delays to the advanced version of Siri that Apple had previously promised.

Simply put: two historic rivals are joining forces in a single phone. Would you accept your phone's assistant running on a rival company's brain, as long as it works with greater privacy?
