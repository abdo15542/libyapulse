📧 'Mailchimp' sold for $3.68 billion after Intuit bought it for $12 billion!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Italian company 'Bending Spoons' has announced the purchase of the email-marketing platform 'Mailchimp' from 'Intuit' for $3.68 billion, in a deal expected to close in early 2027 after regulatory approvals.

The deal consists of $3.31 billion in cash and $368 million in 'Bending Spoons' shares that Intuit will buy within the same deal. Notably, Intuit bought 'Mailchimp' in 2021 for about $12 billion, so it is now selling it for less than a third of what it paid, a big loss reflecting AI pressure on traditional software companies, as Intuit's shares lost more than half their value within a year. Intuit said the sale lets it focus on AI tools for small businesses and use the cash to buy back its shares. As for the buyer, an Italian company that listed its shares in an IPO last July, it bought over the past year famous internet companies such as 'AOL', 'Eventbrite', 'Airtable', and 'Miro', and before them 'Evernote', 'Meetup', and 'Vimeo', and its strategy is to keep beloved brands for the long term, integrate them into its systems, and cut their costs.

Simply put: imagine buying a house for 12 million then selling it after five years for just 3.6 million; that is how Intuit lost on 'Mailchimp', while the buyer sees it as a winning deal at a reduced price.
