⚡ 'Google' Launches 'Gemini Decisions' — AI That Answers 1,000 Questions in a Quarter of a Second!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Google" announced on November 11 a new API called "Gemini Decisions", powered by the "Gemini Decision 1" model, designed for a completely different task from chatbots: it doesn't write long texts, but answers closed questions — yes or no, multiple choice, or graded ratings — with numbers and probabilities. The API can evaluate up to 1,000 questions at once about the same input, whether text, image, audio, video, or a "PDF" file.

Speed is the heart of the idea: instead of generating the answer word by word, the model evaluates all options in a single pass, returning the result in just 150 to 250 milliseconds — faster than the blink of an eye. The cost is just 10 cents per million input tokens, with no charges on outputs, making it about five times cheaper than the previous model. Developers can try it for free through "Google AI Studio".

"Google" itself already uses it inside its products: in "Gemini Notebook" it routes every question to the right model and optimal thinking level, cutting response time for everyday questions by up to 40%; in the "Stitch" design tool it generates interface layouts in seconds; in Search it detects attempts to misuse the tools; and in the "CC" app it picks the right emoji from more than 200 options.

Simply put: most AI models write you an essay when you ask a simple question; this model settles for a fast numerical answer — yes or no, and with what confidence — which is exactly what apps that make thousands of small decisions every second need.

Do you prefer AI that explains its answer to you, or AI that decides quickly without talking?
