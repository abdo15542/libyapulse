🍎 iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2 in March 2027... and production starts soon!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Samsung and LG are preparing to begin mass production of the OLED screens for the iPhone 18 and iPhone Air 2, ahead of their launch in March 2027, according to a South Korean report. LG starts production in late December, while Samsung starts in January, and is the sole supplier of the iPhone Air 2 screen. The launch is part of Apple's new strategy of splitting iPhone launches, after the Pro models launched last September. The iPhone 18 is expected to carry the A20 chip built at 2 nanometers with 9 or 12 gigabytes of memory, while the iPhone Air 2 gets a second ultra-wide camera and a bigger battery. Apple has not confirmed any of these details officially.

Simply put: Apple has split the iPhone launch into two seasons: the pricier models in the autumn, the regular models in the spring — so waiting for the regular iPhone now takes six months longer.
