🎮 Lenovo Readies the 'Legion Go Beam': A Cheaper Handheld Gaming Device with Fixed Arms and a White-and-Green Design!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Leaked images revealed that Lenovo is preparing to expand its "Legion Go" handheld gaming series with a new budget model called the "Legion Go Beam", seen in three leaked images published by the site "Windows Latest". The device features a white body with bright green accents around the joysticks, and controller arms built into the body that don't detach — unlike the premium "Legion Go 2" with its OLED screen and detachable arms.

The images reveal noticeably thick screen bezels, a 16:9 aspect ratio instead of 16:10, and the disappearance of the small touchpad below the right joystick — one of the most notable cost-cutting steps. The device has front-facing speakers at the bottom of each grip, four buttons matching the "Legion Go S" (Legion Space, Display, Quick Settings, and Menu), LB/LT and RB/RT shoulder buttons, and two extra Y1 and Y2 buttons on the back, along with rough grips and a large ventilation opening.

The top edge has a power button with a green ring, volume buttons, just one USB-C port, a 3.5mm headphone jack, a microSD card slot, and two ventilation openings. The leaks did not reveal the processor, price, or operating system yet — those details remain unknown until Lenovo's official announcement. The report suggests the company may unveil the device at its "Tech World" event during CES 2027 on January 6 in Las Vegas.

Simply put: Lenovo is trimming its premium device to offer a cheaper version — fixed arms, thicker bezels, one port — so will gamers accept giving up luxury for a lower price?
