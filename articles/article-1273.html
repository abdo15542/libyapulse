🎵 Tidal deletes AI-generated music — 27 million songs disappear in December!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Music platform Tidal announced on 24 November that it will begin on 9 December banning and deleting all fully AI-generated songs from its platform, in an escalating step to protect real music from the automated flood.

The company said that more than 43% of the songs uploaded to the platform over the past ninety days were AI-generated, peaking on 6 November when they exceeded 60% of uploads in a single day, which led it to say that previous measures were no longer enough.

The decision will lead to the deletion of about 27 million songs — 6.3% of the platform's entire library — rising to about 29 million songs by the end of the year, while rejecting between 80,000 and 85,000 new songs every day, and barring distributors from delivering any fully AI-generated song.

Strikingly, 96% of those songs had never been listened to by anyone at all over the past three months, and the platform also deleted more than 65,000 songs suspected of impersonating real artists' names to steal listens and revenue.

Music in which the artist uses AI as an assisting tool in production remains allowed; the ban targets only works generated entirely by AI without human involvement.

Simply put: the platform is not fighting AI as a tool, but automated content that floods the library and that nobody listens to; the huge volume has begun hiding real music from listeners.
