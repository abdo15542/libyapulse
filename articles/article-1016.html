📱 Leak reveals the Vivo S70 Pro.. a 200MP camera and an external zoom lens!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new leak has revealed the expected specs of the Vivo S70 Pro, a phone the company has not announced yet, led by a 200MP main camera, with support for an external zoom accessory once reserved for flagship phones.

According to the leak, the phone carries a triple rear camera: a 200MP main camera, a 64MP zoom camera with a Sony sensor and optical stabilization, and an 8MP wide camera, plus a 50MP front camera that may support autofocus.

Notably, the phone may support an external zoom lens with a 200mm diameter, an addition previously limited to the company's flagship X series, which would bring it to a mid-range class for the first time.

Advertisement

The phone is expected to come with a flat 6.59-inch 1.5K display, a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 processor, and a battery of 7000mAh or more with 90W fast charging, a metal frame and IP69 dust and water resistance.

The phone may launch globally as the Vivo V90 Pro, alongside the standard S70, but the company has not yet confirmed the phone's existence, its specs or its launch date, so all these details remain leaks until an official announcement.

Simply put: a mid-range phone camera with specs once reserved for flagships.. so would you buy a phone for its camera alone?
