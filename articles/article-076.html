WhatsApp Tests Sharing Your Status to Facebook and Instagram in One Tap! 📤

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

WhatsApp is developing new quick-share options for Status updates in its iPhone app, allowing you to share a Status to Facebook and Instagram without navigating through a separate interface. Users may need to link their WhatsApp account to Meta Accounts Center to use them.

Status is the photos and videos that last 24 hours on WhatsApp. Soon you may post once and your post will also appear on Facebook and Instagram, instead of posting the same thing three times. One important note: the feature is still in development and available only to beta testers for now — it hasn't launched yet.
