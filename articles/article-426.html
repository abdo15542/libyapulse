☁️ 'OVHcloud' Launches 55 Cloud and AI Products in One Go!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

French cloud-computing company "OVHcloud" unveiled 55 new cloud and AI products, at a summit it held on November 19, spanning from infrastructure to applications, through inference, agent building, and AI-enhanced platforms.

Among the highlights announced by the company was the addition of new "Nvidia" processors — "RTX 6000 Pro", "Blackwell B300", and "Rubin R200" — to its cloud and bare-metal services, with its data centers able to host up to 60,000 "B300" processors or 35,000 "R200" processors — figures describing potential capacity, not equipment actually installed.

It also added about a hundred new models to its inference service, including voice and image models, an agent service that connects to company tools through about 480 connectors, a search service that explores hundreds of millions of documents, a speech and voice-transcription partnership in more than 100 languages, plus a workspace bringing together email, documents, and video conferencing with smart assistants.

But mind the details: of the 55 products, only 21 are actually available today, while 7 are in beta, 20 in preview, and 7 more coming soon. The company also announced a European alliance with "Quant", "Shadow", and the "European Perspective on Research".

Simply put: the French company wants to be a European alternative hosted in Europe to the American cloud giants, betting customers will choose it because their data stays under European law.
