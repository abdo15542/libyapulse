📧 A New Email Service Takes on Gmail! 'Kagi Mail' Opens Its Doors to Everyone — No Ads, No Tracking!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Kagi has announced that it is opening the doors for everyone to try its new email service, Kagi Mail, in its public beta, adding email to its products after its paid search engine and the Orion browser — a direct challenge to free, ad-supported email services.

The service offers its own email and calendar, with an address ending in @kagimail.com or your own domain if you prefer, plus 50 GB of storage, and the company promises a service with no surveillance, no ads, and no lock-in, built on open standards that let you move your email whenever you want.

The beta includes built-in translation, a mail assistant that answers simple-language questions about your inbox, a dedicated view for newsletters, and highlighting for messages awaiting your reply — and you can switch off the AI layer entirely if you do not want it.

Advertisement

The price is $99 a year after a 60-day free trial, or included in the Kagi Ultimate monthly subscription at $25, with contacts, address book, and full assistant integration coming in the first quarter of 2027, while native phone apps will not arrive before the third quarter of 2027.

Until then, the service can be used through the web app on phones, or through any mail client that supports the IMAP and JMAP protocols, such as Apple Mail and Thunderbird.

Simply put: a paid email service that promises you are not the product — two months free, then $99 a year — would you pay for email with no ads?
