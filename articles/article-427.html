🏛️ Spain Imposes Strict Rules on Data Centers: Renewable Energy and Less Water!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Spain has passed a royal decree imposing new strict rules on large data centers, in a move aimed at controlling the rapid expansion of these facilities, which consume huge amounts of electricity and water. The rules apply to facilities exceeding 10 megawatts in capacity.

Among the key conditions, data centers must obtain 80% of their electricity from renewable sources, with hour-by-hour matching of consumption, and achieve high water efficiency equivalent to the European "A" rating — about 0.1 liters per kilowatt-hour. The rules also include requirements for cybersecurity and digital sovereignty.

The Spanish Data Center Association announced it will challenge the decree in court, while the opposition promised to repeal it if it wins the elections scheduled for November 29. The government says investing 17 billion euros in the electricity grid through 2030 will save consumers about 5 billion euros a year.

Simply put: Spain is telling data-center companies: if you want to build giant centers here, the electricity must be clean and the water must be little — otherwise, no license.
