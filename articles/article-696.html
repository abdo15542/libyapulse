🖱️ Logitech launches the MX Master 4 in a creators' edition — a touchpad, haptics and incredible speed!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Logitech has unveiled the MX Master 4 mouse in a special creators' edition, in India, with a new design focused on shortcuts and precise control for those who work in design, editing and programming.

The new mouse carries a "Haptic Sense" pad that gives customisable touch feedback, and an "Actions Ring" button that gives quick shortcuts to different apps' tools, while the MagSpeed scroll wheel supports speeds of up to 1,000 lines per second.

The mouse connects via Bluetooth or a USB-C receiver, and the company has updated its wireless system with a new radio chip and an improved antenna for a more stable connection. The edition is limited, and is currently on sale at a reduced price in Indian stores.

Simply put: a mouse that turns into a small control panel for creators. Is a professional mouse worth its price when it saves hours of daily work?
