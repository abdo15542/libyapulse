🛒 Amazon and Meta negotiate to bring Muse back to the Amazon store.. before Black Friday!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Amazon and Meta are negotiating a deal that would bring the Muse shopping agent back to the Amazon store, after Amazon banned it last September, claiming it enters its store without permission and violates the terms of use.

The negotiations, held over multiple rounds, focus on two thorny points: how search results will be displayed inside the store, and how advertising will work, as Amazon fears the agent will bypass its paid ads that humans see, a business that earns it $68.6 billion a year.

Any final deal needs the approval of the two companies' CEOs, and there is no guarantee it will be completed, as the talks are still ongoing and could fail, but their timing before Black Friday and the most important shopping season of the year gives them special momentum.

Advertisement

Muse has become Meta's most successful consumer product so far, used by more than 10 million people in the United States and Canada, with shopping making up a quarter of its activity, an average purchase of over $200, and it captured 51% of shopping agent orders in the week ending 22 November.

In the same context, Walmart announced this week a partnership allowing Muse users to shop Black Friday deals and complete purchases inside the agent, a sign that major retailers have begun opening their doors to AI agents instead of banning them.

Simply put: an AI agent that shops for you, and a giant store afraid it will steal its ads and customers; will AI soon buy everything for us?
