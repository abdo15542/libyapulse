Cybersecurity

A new security report published today has revealed a major breach affecting more than 2,500 companies, after attackers in March managed to hack a popular AI tool that companies use to connect AI models to their systems. The breach exposed data and software development code belonging to major companies, including well-known names in the tech world. Notably, the vulnerability the attackers exploited had been confirmed by a US security authority just one day before the report was published. Simply put: the tools that help companies use AI can themselves become a gateway for hackers if they are not properly secured. Do you think companies are rushing to adopt AI tools without securing them enough? 🛡️
