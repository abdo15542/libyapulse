🎁 Google's Black Friday deals: Pixel 11 Pro for $749!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Black Friday 2026 deals have officially begun in the American Google Store, with big discounts on Pixel 11 phones. The Pixel 11 is now $699 instead of $899, the Pixel 11 Pro $749 instead of $1,099, and the Pixel 11 Pro XL $899 instead of $1,299 — a discount of up to $400 — while the 'Pixel 11 Pro Fold' has dropped to $1,649. Phone deals run until December 5, while watch deals end on December 1.

The discounts did not stop at phones: the 'Pixel Watch 5' is $100 cheaper, 'Pixel Buds Pro 2' are $169 instead of $229, the 'Google Home' speaker is $79.99, and the fourth-generation 'Nest' thermostat is $219.99. Store mailing-list subscribers also get an extra $50 discount code for carrier-unlocked phones, to be used before December 1.

The same deals are also available at Amazon and 'Best Buy', with a trade-in bonus of up to $600 when buying a 'Pro' or 'Pro XL' with an old phone, and up to $900 with a 'Pro Fold'. The deals also include refurbished devices: the 'Pixel 9 Pro' for $574, the 'Pixel 9' for $449, and the 'Pixel 8 Pro' for $499.

Simply put: if you have been waiting for the moment to buy a Pixel 11, these are the deepest direct discounts on the series yet — but note: the biggest discount requires the carrier-unlocked version.
