Urgent Warning for Everyone Who Runs Servers or Tech Systems 🚨

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency added four serious vulnerabilities to its list of known exploited flaws, after confirming they are actually being exploited in real attacks.

The flaws affect Synology file-storage systems, the LiteLLM tool for connecting AI models, the Apache Axis server, and the RedCap research system. Three of them scored 9.8 out of 10 in severity.

Simply put: these are vulnerabilities that let an attacker take control of a system remotely, updates have been released to fix them, and US government agencies are required to apply them before November 21 and December 2.
