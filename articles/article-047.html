Rollme PulseFit Band With No Screen: Tracks Your Pulse and Sleep and Lasts 15 Days! ⌚

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Rollme has launched a new health band called PulseFit with no screen at all, for people who want to track their health without distracting notifications.

The band only vibrates for alerts, and it measures heart rate, blood oxygen, sleep, and heart rate variability — an indicator of heart and nervous system health — plus a touch area for ECG, an electrical tracing of heart activity.

Its 200mAh battery lasts up to 15 days of normal use and more than 30 days on standby, and it is water-resistant to 5ATM, meaning it can handle swimming.

Advertisement

It costs $59.99 and comes in three colors: Phantom Black, Titanium Grey, and Quartz Pink.

The company also claims to measure blood pressure, body composition, blood sugar, uric acid, and blood lipids — but these are the company's claims only, not confirmed medical facts.

Would you give up the screen for a battery that lasts 15 days? 🔋
