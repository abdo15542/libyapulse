🧠 Anthropic Says It Has Crossed the AI Self-Acceleration Threshold — and an Independent Auditor Says Progress Has Doubled!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Anthropic, the maker of the Claude models, told the US Securities and Exchange Commission on November 10 that it has decided it has crossed an important threshold in its own safety policy — the threshold of 'large acceleration in progress due to AI' in AI development research itself. The announcement came in a second amendment to its IPO filing, after it said in a first amendment on November 2 that it 'may be approaching' the same threshold.

The company's policy states that the threshold is met if models become able to replace an entire research team at a competitive cost, or if the rate of progress in AI capabilities doubles because research automation itself. The company explained that its decision rests on its expectations for future models and the general pace of progress, not on observed behavior in its current models alone, and that it is considering updating its policy to keep humans in control of advanced model development, including human review of certain research decisions.

In an independent assessment published on November 5, the research organization METR estimated, after about eleven weeks of work inside the company, that Anthropic now improves its models at roughly double the pace it had between March 2024 and October 2025, and that most of this acceleration — perhaps about 80% of it — comes from using AI in research itself. But the organization stressed that its estimate is uncertain, ranging from 1.3 to 3 times, and that the company withheld most of the detailed evidence behind it.

Simply put: imagine a factory making faster machines, and suddenly the machines themselves start designing machines faster than themselves; that is what Anthropic says has become close or already achieved in its research, and what an independent auditor is trying to measure in numbers.
