🖥️ Washington Approved Selling Over 879,000 AI Chips to Chinese Companies!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Documents published by the U.S. House Foreign Affairs Committee on 19 November revealed that the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security had approved, by May 2026, the sale of more than 879,000 advanced AI chips to Chinese companies, including Alibaba, Tencent, ByteDance and Baidu. The companies had applied to buy 3.4 million chips between December and May, and the department approved only about a quarter of them. The chips in question are Nvidia's H200 and its AMD equivalents, and some approvals covered companies the U.S. Department of Defense has classified as Chinese military companies.

The irony is that approval does not mean the chips actually arrive: Nvidia confirmed it has stopped shipping the H200 to China despite holding the licences, and Beijing itself has restricted imports of the chip to support its domestic manufacturers. Licensed Hopper sales to China brought in $2.4 billion in the fiscal quarter ending in late October — about 2% of the company's total revenue.

Simply put: America bans chips to China on one hand, and approves the sale of hundreds of thousands of them on the other — so can export controls succeed when exceptions are granted on this scale?
