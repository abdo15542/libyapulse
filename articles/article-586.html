🤖 "Step X Neo" — the first "AI agent" phone for everyone that works without internet!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Chinese company StepFun revealed its "Step X Neo" phone on Monday, describing it as the first "agent" smartphone aimed at ordinary users.

The company, founded by former Microsoft executives in 2023, says its new phone includes a built-in AI assistant that can carry out complex multi-step tasks across apps, with minimal input from the user.

What sets the phone apart is that its assistant works without an internet connection, so it can carry out tasks even in places with no network.

Advertisement

The phone runs on the "Step Edge" model the company developed itself, and runs its own operating system called "Step AOS".

This is the first consumer hardware device the company has launched, after focusing since its founding on developing AI models.

The idea, simply, is that the phone doesn't just answer questions — it carries out tasks itself: it books, arranges and coordinates between different apps without asking you to carry out each step separately.

And if the idea succeeds, the shape of our relationship with the phone could change: from a device we use step by step, to an assistant that carries out tasks on our behalf while we simply review the result.

Simply put: imagine asking your phone to arrange a whole trip, and it books, compares and arranges everything on its own without you opening one app after another. That is what the "agent phone" means.

Do you trust a phone that carries out tasks on your behalf, or do you prefer to carry out every step yourself?
