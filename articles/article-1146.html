☁️ Google Denies Mass Layoffs in India! Only 30 Jobs... and the Rumours Set the Internet Alight!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google has denied rumours of mass layoffs in its operations in India, confirming that the change was limited to about 30 jobs only in the Google Cloud division, as part of a small reorganisation and reordering of priorities.

The story began with anonymous posts on the professional platform Blind claiming wide layoffs in Google Cloud teams in India, which then spread on X and worried employees and tech experts, before the official Google Cloud account in India responded with a firm clarification.

The company said in its reply: 'These rumours are completely untrue. Google Cloud last week made a small organisational change in India, and about 30 jobs were eliminated as part of reordering work priorities,' without revealing the affected teams or roles, or whether further steps are coming.

The clarification comes at a time when Google Cloud in India is going through a leadership transition, with Amit Kumar appointed as interim head for India in October, succeeding Sashikumar Sridharan, who moved to the AI startup Sarvam.

Despite this small step, the company continues hiring in India in engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud infrastructure, and sales, while Google Cloud's global revenues doubled to $30.7 billion in the quarter ending in September 2026, and the company is investing $15 billion to build a huge AI centre in the city of Visakhapatnam.
