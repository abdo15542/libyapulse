🛡️ America adds 4 critical flaws to the "actually exploited" list — and the deadline is November 23!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US Cybersecurity Agency "CISA" on Friday added four flaws to its official list of vulnerabilities known to be actually exploited, and gave federal agencies until November 23 to fix them, in an urgent move revealing that attackers had already beaten protection teams to all four flaws.

The list includes a flaw in "Apache Roller" rated 9.8 out of 10, allowing an attacker without an account to execute commands on the server via an endpoint, and "Apache" itself acknowledged this week that it is actually exploited. A flaw in "Cisco Expressway" with the same rating, allowing command execution with root privileges via hard-coded credentials built into the device, which attackers used to breach a Russian organisation after a proof-of-concept exploit code was published. And a flaw in "VMware vCenter" also with the same rating, allowing authentication bypass and system takeover without a password.

The fourth flaw is the least documented: a flaw in the "SAP NetWeaver" gateway rated 6.5, allowing information disclosure or modification via data injection, fixed in November 2025, but with no public reports so far documenting its actual exploitation.

Investigations revealed that the "vCenter" flaw was used by a spy group called "Armory Castle", suspected of links to China, along with another flaw in the "BeyondTrust" remote-support tool, to plant custom backdoors and steal data silently since September. The list includes flaws fixed in October and November, but many organisations have not updated yet — and that is the real danger.

Simply put: imagine the police publishing a list of four open doors in your city, telling building owners: close them within two days. The thieves already know these doors, and the deadline is not a suggestion but an urgent repair order.
