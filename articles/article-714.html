🎬 Bing gives everyone video generation for free — a new tool powered by the Sora model!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft has added a new tool to the Bing app on phones called "Bing Video Creator" — a tool that turns written text into a video clip, powered by OpenAI's Sora video-generation model. Most importantly, the company is offering the tool to all users for free — the first time Sora's video-generation power has been available at no cost.

The idea of the tool is simple: you write a sentence describing the scene you want, such as "a sunset over the sea", and the model turns it into a short video clip ready to share. Until now, AI video generation was a paid feature in most services, or limited to waiting lists — but now it is within reach of anyone with the Bing app on their phone.

This free access opens a wide door for creativity, but it also opens difficult questions: when anyone can produce a realistic video in minutes, how do we tell the real from the made-up? The ease of producing video also means a flood of clips on platforms, and it may become hard to reach good content amid the crowd.

Simply put: write a sentence and get a video for free — the first time Sora has been available at no cost. Will you try generating a video yourself today, or do you fear the internet will drown in fake clips that are hard to tell apart?
