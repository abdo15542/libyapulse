📄 Gemini summarizes any page in Chrome with one tap... without copying links!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google has added a new option to the Chrome browser on Android called 'Summarize pages', which summarizes any web page for you instantly through the Gemini assistant, without you copying its link or pasting it anywhere.

The new option appears above the Gemini command bar, between the 'Share screen with Live' and 'Ask about this page' options, and when you tap it a floating window appears with the page summary, which you can expand with follow-up questions or listen to read aloud.

The summarizing works with the Gemini 2.5 Flash model, and comes after the feature launched last month in Chrome on Mac and Windows for users in the United States, reaching Android phones now.

The option is not limited to Chrome pages alone, but also works through custom tabs, Search, Discover and Google News, saving you from opening the page and reading it fully when you only want its main idea quickly.
