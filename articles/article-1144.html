🏭 Giant Satellite Factory in Bengaluru! Pixel to Build 100 Satellites at Once!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Indian startup Pixel has chosen the city of Bengaluru for its new satellite factory, 'Giga Pixel', in a step that expands its ambitions from a small-satellite company into a huge space factory.

The factory sits near Kempegowda International Airport on 250,000 square feet, and will initially allow around 50 satellites to be built in parallel, with a plan to expand to 100 satellites at the same time, opening its doors in March or April 2027.

The decision came after fierce competition between the states of southern India: the states of Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu tried to attract the company with offers of land and incentives, after its chief technology officer publicly complained about the difficulty of obtaining land in Bengaluru, before the government of Karnataka intervened and contacted the company directly.

Advertisement

Pixel was founded in 2019 by two students, and has become one of the leading space-imaging companies today, building satellites that see Earth in hundreds of wavelengths, and has raised $195 million in funding, including a $100 million round last September led by Temasek.

The competition for the factory reflects a wider race between Indian states for space and defence investments, with Karnataka alone accounting for 65% of India's space and defence industry, aiming to raise its share to 75%, with investments worth 60,000 crore rupees and 60,000 jobs by 2031.

Simply put: one factory building 50 satellites at a time... and India is turning into a global space workshop... Will space become a mass industry soon?
