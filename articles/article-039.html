Samsung Opens One UI 9.5 Beta for Galaxy S26 Phones in 6 Countries! 📱

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Samsung has started testing the beta version of its new One UI 9.5 interface — the layer that changes the look and menus of your phone on top of Android.

The beta is now available only to owners of the Galaxy S26, S26+, and S26 Ultra in 6 countries: Germany, India, Poland, South Korea, the UK, and the US, and registration is done through the Samsung Members app. This is the first time Samsung has opened the beta to all markets at the same time.

The standout new feature is 'App Lock': you can lock any app with your fingerprint or PIN, hiding its notifications and widgets from the screen. The Health app also added new features, such as sleep training, global medication tracking, and a 'Mindful Moment' feature.
