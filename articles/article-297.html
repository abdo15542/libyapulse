🚗 Italy approves Tesla's supervised driving system before all of Europe!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Italy has granted preliminary approval to Tesla's 'supervised driving' system, becoming the first large country in the European Union to allow the system, ahead of the expected European vote next December.

Transport Minister Matteo Salvini announced the decision on Friday after an in-depth technical review of its feasibility, and the company said the rollout in Italy starts soon, publishing a clip of a car driving itself through the streets of Rome. The system is Level 2 automated driving: it steers the car, brakes, accelerates, and handles intersections and lane changes, but the driver remains supervising and responsible for the car at all times. Italy thus becomes the twelfth European country to approve the system, after the Netherlands, which was first in April, then Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, Belgium, Slovakia, and Finland, while Germany and France still refuse to approve it. The service will be available in Italy for a monthly subscription of 99 euros.

Simply put: imagine your car driving itself through the streets of Rome while you just sit watching the road; that is what Italy has approved, but your eyes and hands must stay ready to intervene at any moment.
