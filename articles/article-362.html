🌏 Trump Rejects Any Global Rules for AI at the Asia-Pacific Summit!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

US President Donald Trump said at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in the Chinese city of Shenzhen on Thursday that the United States opposes "any form of global regulation" of AI, and that he "will not allow it to happen." He described the technology, which he calls "superintelligence", as "almost entirely good", but with bad elements that must be handled with great caution.

The remarks came on the same day Trump met Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in the first trilateral meeting between the leaders of the three countries — a meeting that lasted 81 minutes with no major agreements announced. Xi had called at the same summit for closer cooperation between countries to keep AI "under human control", while Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney warned that the absence of global governance poses a "huge risk".

The disagreement reflects two opposing directions: Washington sees global rules as potentially stifling innovation and slowing its race with China, while Beijing sees international cooperation as necessary to ensure the technology's safety. Trump had agreed with Xi in September to create an urgent communication channel for exchanging information when AI incidents occur, with its details to be completed at this meeting.
