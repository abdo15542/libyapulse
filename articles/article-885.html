📰 New York Times: Huang Threatened Nvidia Employees With Ripping Off Their Arms... and an HR Employee Was Fired After Objecting!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new investigation revealed that more than 12 current and former Nvidia employees described years of verbal abuse from former CEO Jensen Huang, saying public humiliation was a normal part of working inside the company.

According to what employees recounted, Huang would scold those present in meetings with harsh words, including threats to rip off their arms and tear out their hearts, and on one occasion he went around a room full of managers saying he would kill them, then told an older employee that everyone wished him dead.

As for the human resources employee who objected to this treatment, she was fired after Huang said she was not "the right person," and she later reached a settlement with the company worth more than one million dollars, according to the report.

These testimonies came after Huang resigned earlier this month, against a background of accusations of extramarital affairs, while the company's board confirmed it does not intend to bring him back as CEO, even though more than 1,600 internal comments demanded his return.

Simply put: the man who built the artificial intelligence empire ran it, according to his employees, with shouting and threats — was that price acceptable in exchange for success?

Do you think a harsh management style like this could stay hidden for 33 years inside one of the biggest tech companies?
