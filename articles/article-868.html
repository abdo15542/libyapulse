📱 Telegram is changing completely! A news feed and AI agents inside the app!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On 20 November, Telegram launched a major update that changes the look of the app: a new news feed that gathers posts from the channels you follow in a separate tab, plus a second tab for personalised recommendations based on your interests, with search and the ability to mark items as read.

The biggest new feature is that you can now connect AI agents from inside the app using your own keys, choosing between ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, and OpenRouter, and switch between them whenever you like.

For Premium subscribers, an AI editor corrects mistakes, changes the style of a text, and translates messages instantly, even after they have been sent, without the other person seeing that the message was edited. The update also added smart dictation: while recording a voice message, swipe your finger left and your speech turns into text instantly. Plus redesigned profiles on iPhone with circular buttons and musical effects, gifts sold through Fragment and bought with a Gram wallet, and new bot capabilities such as interactive tools inside profiles and inviting users to bots.

Simply put: Telegram is no longer just a messaging app; it now reads the news for you, talks to AI models, and corrects your messages before they arrive — would you rather a messaging app stay simple, or do these additions make it your first app?
