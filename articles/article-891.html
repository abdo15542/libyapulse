🤖 DeepSeek Raises $15 Billion... and Its Valuation Jumps to $92 Billion While Its Founder Keeps Control!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Chinese AI company DeepSeek has closed a second external funding round worth about 100 billion yuan, roughly $15 billion, according to corporate registration records, making it the largest funding round in the history of Chinese AI.

Commercial registration changes, completed on 17 November, showed that a new investment partnership of 31 partners now owns about 17.3% of the company, and that the company's registered capital rose by about 21%.

Among the largest investors are Tencent and funds linked to battery maker CATL, with a 9.57% stake each, in addition to China Mobile and CITIC Securities with a 7.65% stake each, and other companies such as Vivo, NetEase, and Meituan.

Advertisement

The company's value after the round is estimated at about 615.5 billion yuan, roughly $92 billion, up about 75% from its first round, when it was valued at about 350 billion yuan last July.

Despite the size of the money, founder Liang Wenfeng keeps full control, as he owns 99% of the company that manages the investment partnership, while external investors have no voting rights and cannot sell their stakes for five years.

The company is preparing to list its shares on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, and it has appointed CITIC Securities to prepare the listing, while reports indicate that most of the new money will go toward buying computing power, after the company began restricting use of its free app and website due to a shortage of computing capacity.

Simply put: An AI company raised $15 billion in a single round, yet all the decisions stay in the hands of one founder — do you see a founder's full control as a strength for the company or a risk to it?
