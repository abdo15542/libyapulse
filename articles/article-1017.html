⌚ Samsung may cancel a new watch in 2027.. and settle for software updates!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A South Korean report says Samsung may not launch any new smartwatch in 2027, and is considering moving to a two-year launch cycle for its watches, ending nine consecutive years of launching a new watch every year since 2018.

The report links the decision to financial pressure on the company's phone division, which recorded losses for two consecutive quarters, alongside rising prices for key components such as memory and processors, plus a lukewarm reception for the current Galaxy Watch 9, whose price rose this year.

If the report is accurate, the new generation Galaxy Watch 10 will not arrive before 2028, and the Galaxy Watch 9 and Galaxy Watch Ultra 2 will remain the company's latest watches throughout next year.

Instead of a new device, the company plans to focus in 2027 on software updates and additional health features for current watches, alongside launching a new budget fitness band from the Galaxy Fit series to fill the gap.

The report also says the company may cut production of its budget Galaxy A series phones by around 20%, as part of a broader plan to cut costs and improve profitability, and Samsung has not confirmed any of these plans yet.

Simply put: this year's watch may stay the latest for two full years.. so do you prefer software updates or a new device every year?
