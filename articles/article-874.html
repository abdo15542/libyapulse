🏛️ America Demands Answers from Nvidia Over Its Sales to China! Who Was in Charge of Compliance?

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said on Sunday, November 22, that the United States is asking Nvidia for answers about its chip sales to China, and wants to know who was responsible for export-rule compliance inside the company.

Bessent added in a television interview that the US administration is in contact with the company's interim management, its directors, and its lawyers, and that the company must move quickly, because Washington wants to know "what the culture was" inside it.

He said the company's board is working to determine what happened, and that he is confident the board will provide "full and strong answers." He added: "We want to see who was responsible for compliance there... Was it at the lower levels? Or the middle? Or did it reach the top?"

The remarks came days after the company's former CEO resigned, and after the US administration issued a new rule tightening restrictions on China's access to advanced AI chips, ahead of the US president's visit to China and his meeting with the Chinese president.

The US president said on Friday that he would like the former CEO to apologize and return to the company, while Nvidia's chairman said the board will not reappoint him.

Simply put: Washington is not only asking about the chips, but about who allowed them to be sold and who knew — will the investigations reveal it was a decision from the top, or a violation from below?
