🔀 Microsoft Merges Its Two Copilot Apps into One! A Personal Account and a Work One — Together!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Microsoft has announced the merger of its AI assistant's two apps into a single one: Copilot for individuals and Microsoft 365 Copilot for businesses, after each was a separate app.

Users will soon be able to sign in to the unified app with a personal account, a work account, or both together, and switch between the two depending on the work or the question, with both apps' capabilities moving into one place.

In exchange, the company will remove some features from the app during the merger, in a step aimed at simplifying the experience instead of splitting it across two apps.

Advertisement

The merger comes after the company launched a new Copilot design this month that brings chat and teamwork together in one place, as part of a broad reorganization of its AI assistant.

Simply put: instead of two apps for one assistant, there is now one app with two accounts — a personal one for home, a work one for the office, and you switch between them with a tap.
