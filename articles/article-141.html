New Leak About the OnePlus 17 📱

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New reports reveal that the upcoming phone will carry a huge 6.89-inch 2K display from BOE, with a refresh rate being re-evaluated to reach 240Hz, ultra-thin bezels on all sides, and a wide color range under the BT.2020 standard.

Simply put: the refresh rate is how many times the image on the screen renews every second — the higher it is, the smoother scrolling and gaming feel. And thin bezels mean a bigger screen in a smaller body.

The leaker did not name the phone explicitly, and the expected launch is in October 2027, so this is very early news.

Does a high refresh rate matter to you in a phone, or are battery and camera more important?
