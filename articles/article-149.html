💼 Microsoft Says Its AI Assistant Has Become a “New Operating System for Work”!

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

At its annual conference, the company unveiled a new vision: the assistant is no longer just a tool for answering questions, but now works in four modes: chat, collaboration, coding, and an “autopilot” mode that carries out long tasks on its own, with its own identity, memory, and tools. Word, Excel, and PowerPoint now also run directly inside the assistant, and it can understand the context of your work, files, and data to give more accurate answers. More than 50 million AI agents have been registered on the platform since last May.

Simply put: AI is shifting from an assistant that answers to a colleague that works with you.

Would you trust AI to complete your work tasks entirely on its own?
