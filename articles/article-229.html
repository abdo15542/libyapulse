🔊 Amazon Polly clones your voice from minutes of recording!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Amazon has launched voice cloning in its 'Polly' text-to-speech service, as a restricted general release, letting companies create a custom voice from recordings of a single consenting speaker. Just one to ten recordings are enough, up to 15 minutes in total, for the system to build a new voice within 5 to 10 minutes. The feature supports seven languages — English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, and Hindi — and creating the voice is free while usage is billed by consumption, with a watermark embedded in the generated voice, a maximum of 10 voices per account per region, and the original recordings are not used to train models.

Simply put: instead of recording every ad or voice message again, you record your voice once, and the system reads any new text in your own voice, with a watermark proving it is a generated voice.

Would you trust a cloned voice when you cannot tell if it is real?
