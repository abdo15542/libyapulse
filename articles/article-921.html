💹 Leveraged Funds on "Anthropic" Shares Start Trading Today! A Doubled Bet on the Share Rising or Falling — and Options Trading Launches Too!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Trading begins today on the Chicago exchange in two new funds that bet on the share price of AI company "Anthropic" in a leveraged way: one doubles your gains if the share rises, the other doubles your gains if it falls — a step that reflects the huge excitement around the company after its historic listing.

The first fund carries the ticker "ANTT" and gives you twice the share's daily movement: if the share rises 5% in a single day, the fund rises about 10%. The second fund carries the ticker "ANTZ" and moves in the opposite direction: if the share falls 5%, the fund rises about 10%.

The funds are issued by "Tuttle", a firm specialising in leveraged funds. Trading starts today, Monday 23 November, and on the same day options contracts on "Anthropic" shares also launch — contracts that let traders bet on the share rising or falling over a set period.

This move comes after a stormy week for the share: "Anthropic" listed on 19 November at $83 a share, in the largest IPO in history, then the share fell 13.5% on its second day, making extreme volatility the defining feature of its first days of trading.

But these tools are not for beginners: leverage works in both directions, and losses are doubled just like profits. These funds are also designed for short-term daily trading, not holding for the long term, because the effect of leverage changes over time.

Simply put: a doubled bet on the world's most famous AI stock — would you dare double the risk along with the reward?
