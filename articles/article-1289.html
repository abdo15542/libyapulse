📵 Sudden Outage Stops TikTok for 25,000 Users — Videos Won't Load and Profiles Are Empty!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

TikTok went down on the afternoon of Tuesday, November 24, as videos stopped loading on the 'For You' page, search results failed to appear, some profiles opened to blank pages, and video links stopped working — on iOS and Android phones and on the website.

Reports began climbing toward 3 p.m. Eastern Time, passing 25,000 reports on Downdetector, the platform that tracks outages from user reports — a sign that the problem was widespread, not an isolated case.

The company acknowledged the outage, and its spokesperson said the app had suffered a brief technical issue affecting access to the platform, and that the problem had been fixed, apologizing to users. Service returned to normal about 45 minutes after the outage began.

The outage is part of a wave of successive technical failures hitting major companies in recent days: on November 12 and 13, Microsoft 365 services went down, including email and Teams, then US flights were halted after a fault in communications systems, and iCloud and Apple Pay services went down on November 17 — with no link proven so far between these incidents.

Simply put: an app used by hundreds of millions suddenly stopped without warning, then came back as if nothing had happened — but repeated outages at giant companies within a single week raise a question about the fragility of the services we rely on every day.

What do you do when your favorite app suddenly stops: wait for it to come back, or move to another app?
