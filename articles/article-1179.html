🧩 Claude Turns into an App Store! More than 10,000 Add-ons — Even for Free Users!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Anthropic announced on Monday, November 23, the merging of "connectors" and "skills" in Claude under a single name: "plugins", making the AI assistant closer to an app store.

The add-ons used to be spread across three separate places; now a single "plugins" page gathers them, each card showing the add-on's type, with the option to filter the list to show only skills or only connectors.

Connectors link Claude to external services such as email, calendars, and company databases, while skills are instruction packages that teach it a specific task, such as building a spreadsheet in a specific format.

Advertisement

The new store holds more than 10,000 add-ons to browse, and public add-ons carry a "verified" or "community" tag, while your company's or colleagues' add-ons appear first under "Your plugins".

The feature is available starting today on the Free, Pro, Max, and Ultra plans, and it will reach the Team and Enterprise plans soon, starting November 30 gradually.

And there is an important warning: deleting a plugin does not cut off the connector built into it — if you remove a plugin that accesses your email, the connection stays active until you disconnect it yourself from the connector settings.

Simply put: Claude is no longer just a smart conversation — it is a platform that learns new professions and reaches your tools, and plugins are the way to get there.
