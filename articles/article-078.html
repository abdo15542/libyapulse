Voyager 1 Reaches a Full Light-Day Away From Earth! 🚀

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

NASA's Voyager 1 probe reached a full light-day from Earth yesterday, Wednesday — the distance light travels in 24 hours, about 16 billion miles — becoming the first human-made object ever to reach this distance. Now a radio signal takes about 24 hours in one direction, so a message and its reply together take about 48 hours.

A "light-day" is a distance, not a time: sunlight reaches Earth in 8 minutes, but to reach Voyager 1 it needs a full day. The probe has been flying since 1977 and entered interstellar space in 2012, and two of its scientific instruments are still working; the current energy-saving measures may be enough to keep it in contact with Earth for three or four more years.

Can you imagine that a message from Earth takes a whole day to reach a spacecraft? ✨
