🚗 Tesla plans a Model Y with no steering wheel and no pedals — a robotaxi that seats seven!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Tesla CEO Elon Musk said at the DealBook Summit on 18 November that the company plans to produce a fully self-driving version of its Model Y car with no steering wheel and no pedals, to complement the Cybercab robotaxi. The goal is for robotaxi passengers to have a wider option that seats seven people — a whole family or a group of friends — while the Cybercab seats only two.

No specifications or production details have been revealed yet, but the company may build the new car on the basis of the Model Y L, a longer version that appeared in China in 2025 and then reached America this year, with a starting price of $61,990, sold with six seats. Removing the steering wheel and pedals could make room for a seventh passenger, in the same way the Cybercab was built.

Musk defended the idea of the two-seat car, saying it covers about 90% of passenger trips, and that a Model Y with no controls would complement it. But the road is not easy: in September 2026, the US safety authority opened an investigation into whether Tesla properly certified the Cybercab under federal safety standards, and the larger car could face similar regulatory questions, with no clear timeline yet.

Simply put: a driverless taxi with no steering wheel that seats your whole family — would you ride in it with your children?
