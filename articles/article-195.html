📱 Xiaomi 18 Opens Pre-Orders in China with a Huge Battery!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Xiaomi opened pre-orders for its new Xiaomi 18 phone in China, with a refundable deposit of 100 yuan, and sales begin in December. The phone comes with a flat 6.4-inch display with 1.5K resolution and a variable refresh rate, a sixth-generation Snapdragon 8 Elite chip, and a huge 7200 mAh battery with 100W fast charging — noting that some leaks mentioned a 7000 mAh capacity. The cameras are a triple setup in collaboration with Leica: a 200-megapixel main camera, a 50-megapixel ultra-wide, and a 200-megapixel telephoto with 3x optical zoom, with no rear display, running HyperOS 4 based on Android 17, with memory up to 16GB and storage up to a terabyte.

Simply put: the new phone bets on a long-lasting battery, powerful cameras, and the latest-generation chip; and the pre-order deposit is small and refundable.
