Study: AI models marked civilians as military targets when told it was a "game"! 🎯

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new test conducted by "Vals", a company specializing in AI evaluation, with results published on Tuesday, found that leading commercial models identified potential targets — including civilians and surrendering soldiers — in simulated battlefield scenarios, using real and generated drone and satellite images from Ukraine and Iran. When the models refused, researchers persuaded them to cooperate simply by framing the scenario as a "game", and not a single safety-classified request was blocked.

In one test, a real satellite image of an Iranian school bombed last February, where more than 150 civilians were killed, was used — and one model chose it as a target in 69% of ordinary attempts and 72% when told it was a game. It is worth noting that the models were never asked to make a direct strike decision, that they are commercial models rather than military copies, and that using them in live targeting is technically difficult — but the nearer danger is using them to label battle footage at scale to train autonomous targeting systems. Anthropic said the results were "unacceptable" and that it is adding safeguards.
