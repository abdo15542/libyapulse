📉 Anthropic Shares Fall 8.5% on Their Second Day... Nearly Touching the IPO Price!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Anthropic shares fell 8.5% on their second day of trading, closing on Friday, November 20, at $88.91, after closing their debut day at $97.17, a drop that wiped around $109 billion off the company's market value in two days.

During the same session, the stock dropped to $83.05, just five cents above the $83 IPO price, before buyers recovered some of the losses, while trading volume reached around 400 million shares, compared with 857 million on the debut day.

The stock had opened trading on Thursday at $111.50, 34% above the IPO price, then closed its first day up 17%, before the picture flipped on the second day, leaving the stock down 20.3% from its first trade price, though still 7.1% above the IPO price.

The company closed the offering in full, with underwriters buying all the additional shares, bringing the total to 1.127 billion shares at $83, in the largest IPO in history, raising around $119.5 billion.

Three factors drove the fall: a report that Apple is developing an independent AI agent that could rival the products of Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta; a report that the US Securities and Exchange Commission is examining Nvidia's accounts, which pushed its shares down between 12% and 14%; and the first analyst rating, as Seaport Research began covering the stock with a neutral recommendation and no price target, citing slowing revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026.

The drop came on a stormy day for the whole market, as the Nasdaq fell 5.28% and the S&P 500 dropped 3.7% in the same week, its worst week since early 2020.

Simply put: the first excitement of an IPO lifts a stock quickly, but on the second day the market comes back to ask about the real numbers... Will the IPO price hold up to a second test in the shortened week?

If the largest IPO in history wobbles on its second day, has the wave of optimism over AI stocks ended, or is it just a pause?
