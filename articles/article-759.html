🤖 Xiaomi Robots Work 8 Hours Straight in a Car Factory... Without Stopping!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 3, Xiaomi published a video showing two humanoid robots working a full shift at its electric car factory in Beijing: the two robots, CyberOne 02 models, worked for 8 hours and 5 minutes at a station sorting center console covers, covering the station's production needs for a full day, while detecting and correcting errors on their own and coordinating with the production line control system.

But the full picture is more nuanced than the headline: the two robots stopped twice to charge during the shift (after about 3 hours and 6 hours), and the company did not announce how many parts they sorted, their success rate, or how many times humans intervened, and the video is sped up 100 times. The step is still important: the same robots previously achieved a 90.2% success rate over 3 hours fitting nuts in March, then 98% in July.

And the message the company wants to send is clear: the humanoid robot is no longer a showpiece at an exhibition, but a worker covering a full shift in a real factory, even if that is at a single specific station, with charging breaks, and under human supervision.

Simply put: a full robot shift in a real factory is a step from demonstration to real work, but it is still one station, not a whole factory; do you think humanoid robots will enter production lines within a few years, or will charging and maintenance remain a long-standing obstacle?
