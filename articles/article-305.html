🔌 Nvidia secures its chips: huge expansion at its Taiwanese supplier!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nvidia has announced new supply agreements with its partners in Taiwan and South Korea, led by its supplier Kinsus's plan to expand production capacity for the chip-packaging substrates made for it by more than 3.5 times.

Under the new agreement, Kinsus will expand manufacturing of advanced packaging substrates at its complex in Taoyuan, Taiwan, with production starting in the second half of 2027 and the additional capacity completed in the third quarter of 2028. Nvidia also amended its agreement with 'Samsung Electro-Mechanics' to improve its existing factories in South Korea, and expanded advanced packaging operations with Taiwanese company 'Siliconware' with funding worth billions of dollars, to increase contracted capacity by mid-2029. The agreements came as shipments of the new generation of 'Vera Rubin' chips began in large volumes in the third quarter, using 5 and 3 nanometer manufacturing technologies.

Simply put: imagine the maker of the world's most powerful chips buying extra production capacity years before it needs it; because the substrate that carries the chip has become the new bottleneck in the AI race, and whoever owns it holds the keys to production.
