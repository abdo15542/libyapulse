📱 Galaxy S27 Pro Spotted in Benchmarks with a 2nm Chip and 12GB of RAM!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Samsung’s Galaxy S27 Pro appeared on the “Geekbench” benchmarking platform under model number SM-S957B, believed to be the India version of the phone. The phone runs on Samsung’s S5E9975 chip, likely the new Exynos 2700 with ten cores built on 2-nanometer technology, with its main core reaching speeds of 4.24 GHz. The phone also appeared with 10.6GB of memory, expected to be marketed as 12GB at launch, and it may run One UI 9 based on Android 17.

Simply put: appearing in benchmark tests means Samsung is actually testing the phone; and the new 2nm chip is smaller, more powerful, and uses less energy.
