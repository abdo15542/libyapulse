🔍 AI answers are eating into Google search profits.. and Apple is paying the price!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Financial results revealed that Google search revenue grew by only 12% in the third quarter of 2026, reaching $63.35 billion, compared with 17% growth in the previous quarter, even though the number of searches is growing at its fastest pace in five years.

The reason is that users rely more on AI answers, such as smart summaries and AI Mode in search, and these answers generate far fewer ads than traditional search results, so each search earns less than it used to.

That also hit Apple, as growth in its services division slowed from 12% to 10% year on year, and the company expects further slowdown next quarter, because Google pays it a share of search advertising revenue that passes through the Safari browser.

Advertisement

Google pays Apple around 36% of search advertising revenue through Safari, under a default search deal estimated at around $20 billion a year, so if advertising revenue falls, what Apple receives falls with it.

Google shares have fallen by around 18% since the results were announced, while the company is trying new ways to show ads inside AI answers, without scaring users off with too many ads or changing the look of the search they are used to.

Simply put: we search more than ever, but each search earns less.. so do you prefer a ready AI answer or traditional search results?

Do you still click on search result links, or do you settle for the AI answer?
