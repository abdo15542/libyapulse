🎧 New "Nothing" Earbuds Record Your Talk and Turn It Into Text.. From the Charging Case Itself!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The company "Nothing" confirmed that its mysterious product in the world of artificial intelligence is wireless earbuds called "Ear (Note)", and it will reveal them in full on December 8, in the company's first AI device.

The idea is that the charging case itself has become a recorder: it carries microphones and a "Talk" button and a record button, so it records meetings and face-to-face conversations and turns them into text, while the earbuds themselves record phone calls, and a companion app then manages the recordings, texts, and summaries generated by AI.

The earbuds also support voice interaction with several AI agents, including a simple voice-control system the company itself developed that allows back-and-forth conversation, with programmable buttons that can be used to control other programs.

Advertisement

The company has not announced the price yet, and has not settled the availability details, but its CEO said he does not expect an imminent "iPhone moment" for consumer AI devices, and that the company's medium-term ambition is to become a major supplier of hardware supporting a wide range of AI agents, not a single model.

Simply put: your earbuds have become a smart meeting recorder in your pocket.. they listen, write, and summarize for you.. so would you wear earbuds that record everything you say?
