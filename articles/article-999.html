🍎 Apple sells AI by the piece: a $49.99 usage pack... and new limits in 2027!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple has launched what it calls 'AI usage packs' — a one-time purchase that lets users of its Logic Pro music app get extra use of AI features after they exhaust their monthly allowance, priced at $49.99 in the United States.

The same packs are expected to extend to other apps such as Final Cut Pro, Pixelmator Pro and the iWork suite, a sign that the AI features built into Apple's apps may not stay unlimited and free.

The company also announced it will impose usage limits in 2027 on some server-powered Apple Intelligence features, including the Siri assistant and the Image Playground tool, in a clear shift from free built-in AI to a model that measures usage and charges for it.

That means a user who relies on AI in their daily work may face two choices: ration their usage, or pay for extra packs whenever their allowance runs out.

Simply put: the free AI in your devices may soon come with a meter and a price... will you pay for extra usage or settle for the free allowance?
