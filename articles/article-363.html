💶 Lagarde Calls on Europe to Bear the First Losses in Chip Factories and Data Centers!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde has called on the European Union to use public money, including EU budget funds, to bear the first losses in major chip-factory and data-center projects, so private investors are encouraged to fund them. The call came in her speech on November 20 at the European Banking Congress in Frankfurt — her eighth and final address to the congress as the bank's president.

Lagarde said raising Europe's share of global AI computing capacity from about 5% to 15% by 2030 could require investment of nearly 1.3 trillion euros, according to an independent estimate she cited. The idea is Europe-wide public-private partnerships, in which public money absorbs the first loss, so the private investor enters with less exposure to early losses.

Lagarde stressed that the proposal is not an approved funding program or a budget allocation, but a policy recommendation: investment is needed now, its benefits are far off, and the cost of inaction accumulates silently. She warned that continued reliance on foreign computing providers leaves Europe exposed, and that industries once lost are hard to recover.

Simply put: building chip factories and data centers is expensive and risky, and Lagarde is saying: let governments pay the price of the first risk, so investors follow with their money.
