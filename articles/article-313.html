⚛️ Spotify founder bets on nuclear fusion: an AI researcher builds a small sun!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Daniel Ek, founder of Spotify, is backing a British startup called 'Europa Fusion' that works on generating electricity from nuclear fusion — the same reaction that lights the sun. The company was founded last February by researcher Nantas Nardelli, who previously worked at 'DeepMind' and in AI research at Facebook and Microsoft, and until recently was head of machine learning at an American fusion company.

The company relies on what is called 'pulsed power': huge, precisely synchronized electrical pulses that compress fuel until its atoms fuse and release energy. The different idea here is that Nardelli wants to rely on simulation and data more than giant machines, unlike most fusion companies that spend fortunes building huge equipment to test their theories.

Nuclear fusion could one day become a huge source of clean electricity from nearly endless fuel, and could also ease worries about AI's enormous energy consumption. But the truth today is that no one has yet built a fusion plant that feeds electricity to the grid, so the bet remains a long-term scientific one. Nardelli says his background is in electronics and control engineering, that he used to build robots, 'but the time has come to fix climate change for good'.

Simply put: imagine building a small sun in a lab to generate endless clean electricity; that is what the company is trying to do — instead of building giant machines first, it relies on computers and data to design the reactor.
