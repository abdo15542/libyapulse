🤖 Musk settles it: the AI5 chip goes to the Optimus robot... and Tesla cars stay on AI4!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

In a reply on November 22 to a Tesla owner who asked whether he should wait for the AI5 chip before upgrading his old car, Elon Musk said the new chip would go to the humanoid robot Optimus, and that cars would stay on the current AI4 computer — because, in his words, it is enough to achieve driving better than a human.

Musk described the new chip as a 'massive improvement', with computing power of about 12 times more, and said a single AI5 chip is worth roughly 5 times a dual AI4 computer. The chip's design was completed last April, and the goal is to put it in Optimus around next April.

The decision came as Musk explained the new 'reality view' feature in the FSD 15 self-driving system, which generates a live AI rendering of the road. He explained that generating this view consumes only half of one accelerator of the current computer, suggesting that AI4 still has enough capacity.

In the same replies, Musk revealed that the cost of the AI4 computer has now doubled due to rising memory and storage prices, and spoke of a coming upgrade to the current computer that raises its memory from 16 to 32 GB per chip, with an increase of about 10% in power and bandwidth, with timing depending on Samsung, which manufactures the revisions.

Musk did not explain how long cars will stay on AI4, nor whether the decision covers the self-driving taxi Cybercab, leaving the bigger question: will owners of older cars accept that the company's most powerful chip is reserved for a robot, not their cars?

Simply put: the most powerful chip Tesla makes will run in a robot that walks on two legs, not in a car... so is the current computer really enough to drive better than a human?

Do you agree with Musk that AI4 is enough for self-driving, or were you waiting for AI5 in your car?
