🖥️ A 1250 Hz Display — TCL Shows the World's Fastest Gaming Monitor!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

TCL CSOT showed at the DTC 2026 conference a prototype gaming monitor with a 1250 Hz refresh rate, the highest ever, beating LG's UltraGear monitor, which runs at 1000 Hz.

The new display is 27 inches with QHD resolution, higher than full HD, and the company claims its response time is just 1 millisecond, that it suffers no motion blur at all, and that colors look good enough even though it is not an OLED screen.

The monitor is not for sale yet — it is still just a display prototype — but the company confirmed it is working with partners to bring it to market soon, without setting a date or a price.

Advertisement

At the same conference, the company also showed another prototype of an inkjet-printed OLED monitor, also 27 inches and QHD, but at 500 Hz, with full coverage of the DCI-P3 color spectrum — a technology that promises more stable colors and higher brightness than traditional OLED screens.

These shows come at a time when LG is considering using the same inkjet-printed OLED screens in 5,000- and 6,000-pixel resolution monitors during 2028 and 2029, suggesting the monitor race has moved from resolution alone to speed and color quality together.

Simply put: the refresh rate is the number of times the screen redraws the image each second, and the higher the number, the smoother the motion — and now an experimental screen has reached 1,250 times per second, faster than any gaming monitor on the market.
