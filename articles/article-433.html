🇮🇳 India Opens Registration for 'Consent Managers' — but the Data Protection Board Has No Chair!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

India has launched a registration framework for "consent managers" under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act of 2023, and the framework took effect on November 13, 2026, with the launch of a new online portal to receive applications.

A "consent manager" is a registered entity that lets people give, manage, review, and withdraw consent for the use of their personal data through a transparent platform, instead of dealing with each company separately. Registration requires the company to be incorporated in India, with a net worth of at least 20 million Indian rupees, plus technical, operational, and financial capacity.

But the irony is that India's Data Protection Board — the body that must decide on applications — still has no chair and no members so far, so the door is open for applications, but no one can decide on them yet. The main rights and obligations under the law begin on May 13, 2027.

Simply put: India built the portal and opened registration, but the committee that grants approvals hasn't been formed yet, so applications wait for someone to read them.

Do you trust a consent system run by a body that isn't complete yet?
