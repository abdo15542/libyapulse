⌚ Honor Watch 6 Pro Arrives in Europe... a Bright Screen and an 18-Day Battery!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

After launching in China in September, the Honor Watch 6 Pro has arrived in Europe and the UK, with pre-orders starting on November 19 at a price from 379 euros, with a 100-euro discount for a limited time until the end of the year.

The watch carries a dual-layer AMOLED screen measuring 1.5 inches, with peak brightness reaching 5,500 nits, making it one of the brightest smartwatch screens, staying clear under direct sunlight. It is surrounded by a stainless steel frame and nano-ceramic, and weighs 50 grams without the strap.

It runs on a 515 mAh battery that lasts up to 18 days in long battery mode, 10 days in normal use, and 7 days with the always-on display turned on. It supports more than 120 sport modes, including a professional running coach, a football mode, and a badminton mode, plus dual-frequency GPS that tracks you accurately in the wild.

The watch works with Android phones and iPhones alike, and supports cardiac arrest detection in the European version, though the company is not promoting it while it waits to complete a European medical certification.

Simply put: a screen that sees the sun and does not fear it, and a battery that makes you forget the charger for two weeks; so would you buy a watch with a long battery and a bright screen, or is the price what decides?
