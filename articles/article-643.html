☀️ Tesla wants to build a $3.1 billion silicon plant in Texas — but on one condition!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Tesla has applied to the state of Texas to build a polysilicon refining plant worth $3.1 billion near the city of Robstown in Nueces County, in a move the company describes as the first part of an integrated American solar manufacturing platform.

But the project is conditional: the company said in its application that it will not build the plant in Texas without a property tax exemption, and that it is also considering sites in other states — tax incentives are a decisive factor in its final decision.

The material the plant will make is ultra-pure polysilicon, the raw material for solar cells, and China controls more than 90% of its global production, so Tesla aims to produce it locally instead of importing it.

According to the timeline, construction begins between 2027 and 2030, operations start in 2030, with 260 workers at first, then 515 by 2032, at an average annual wage of $130,000, and the investment is split across $1.5 billion for construction, $1.4 billion for equipment and $183 million for infrastructure.

The project follows another, larger Tesla project in Texas: a $10.1 billion solar cell and module plant in Fort Bend County, which cleared an important tax hurdle in September when the local school board approved the exemption.

The move aligns with new US tariffs taking effect on December 4 on imported polysilicon, with a minimum import price and 15% duties on imported ingots, wafers, cells and modules — measures that raise the value of domestic production.

Simply put: Tesla wants to make American solar from raw material to finished panel — but it sets one condition: cut the tax first, or it will look elsewhere.
