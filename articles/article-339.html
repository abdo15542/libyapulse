🔋 Vivo Launches the 'V80 Lite' with a Giant 10,000mAh Battery!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Vivo has launched the "Vivo V80 Lite 5G" in selected global markets, and its standout feature is a giant 10,000mAh battery — a capacity rarely seen in an ordinary phone. The phone comes in three memory and storage versions: 6GB with 128GB, 6GB with 256GB, and 8GB with 256GB, and in three colors: black, green, and pink, sold through Vivo's store in Malaysia.

The phone carries a 6.83-inch AMOLED screen with 1.5K resolution and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, a 50MP main rear camera, and runs on MediaTek's "Dimensity 7300e" chip made with 6nm technology. The company has not yet revealed the price or the exact sale date in other markets.

This huge battery arrives as companies race to increase battery capacities, after 7,000mAh batteries became common in mid-range phones. The real question is not battery size alone, but the phone's weight and charging speed — two details Vivo has not yet revealed for this model.

Simply put: 10,000mAh should mean days without a charger in theory — but until Vivo announces the weight and charging speed, the final verdict remains on hold.

Would you buy a heavier phone in exchange for a battery that lasts for days?
