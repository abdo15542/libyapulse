New Supply Mission to the International Space Station Launches November 29! 🛰️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The supply mission "CRS-25" is targeting launch no earlier than Sunday morning, November 29, aboard a Falcon 9 rocket from Cape Canaveral. The "Cygnus XL" cargo spacecraft carries about 11,000 pounds — about five tons — of science, supplies, and equipment to the International Space Station, and astronauts Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney will capture it with the robotic arm on Tuesday, December 1, and it will remain docked until May 2027.

The space station needs regular "delivery trucks" from Earth, and this flying truck is a warehouse carrying food, equipment, and science experiments — including research to improve delivery of cancer and arthritis drugs, predict the spread of cancer, treat muscle loss, and produce food for long journeys. The spacecraft was named "S.S. James Lovell" in honor of the astronaut who commanded the Gemini, Apollo 8, and Apollo 13 missions.

Did you know that cancer research works better in zero gravity? 🔬
