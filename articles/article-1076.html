🍏 Apple prepares a wave of cheap devices in 2027 — iPhone 18e and MacBook Neo in February!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple is preparing to focus on cheap devices in early 2027, with the wave expected to begin in February or March with the budget iPhone 18e, alongside the release of iOS 27.4, according to a new report based on specialist journalist Mark Gurman.

In the same batch comes a new cheap iPad and the second-generation MacBook Neo, which runs on the A19 Pro chip coming from the iPhone 17 Pro generation, while the current first generation runs on the A18 Pro chip. The new iPad, meanwhile, carries the A19 chip and 8 GB of memory, plus the N1 chip for Wi-Fi 7, with an option for the C1X chip for 5G connectivity in cellular models.

The current iPad is Apple's last device without Apple Intelligence support, as it has not been updated since early 2025, when it got the A16 chip and only 6 GB of memory.

Advertisement

Around the same time, though likely at a separate event, the company will launch the regular iPhone 18 with the A20 chip and a bigger battery, along with a possible $100 price increase, plus a new MacBook Air with the M7 chip, fitted with stronger AI hardware.

The first half of 2027 does not stop there: also expected are an iPad Air with an OLED screen, new iPad Pro models with the M6 chip, and the slim iPhone Air 2 with a second camera, fixing the first generation's biggest flaw. The rest of the year will bring the iPhone 20 generation, a new Apple Watch, smart glasses, and AirPods with cameras.

This shift comes after last September's event was limited to the iPhone 18 Pro and the foldable iPhone Fold, while the new CEO, John Ternus, wants to launch products throughout the year instead of one big September event.

Simply put: Apple is splitting 2027 into waves, and starting with the cheapest — will you wait for the iPhone 18e or buy a phone that is available now?

If the iPhone 18e arrives in February at a lower price with a modern chip, will you wait months or buy your phone today?
