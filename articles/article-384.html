🏆 'Google Play' Announces the Best Apps and Games of 2026 — 'Adobe Premiere' Tops the List!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Google" announced on November 17 the "Google Play" list of the best apps and games in India for 2026. The app "Adobe Premiere" won best app, for bringing professional editing with a multi-track timeline and audio enhancement to the phone, while the game "Arknights: Endfield" won best game and best story, for its fantasy world combining exploration and factory building.

The list included winners in creativity, AI, and everyday life: the app "ibis Paint X" won best creative expression for drawing and animation, "Wispr Flow" won best innovative app for turning speech into formatted text across apps, the Indian app "Pronto" won best productivity partner for booking cleaning and laundry services, "Pocket FM" for audio entertainment, and "FanCode" for sports fans.

In games, "Monument Valley 3" won best indie game for its calm architectural puzzles, the Indian game "ScarFall 2.0" won best game made in India for its battles in Mumbai streets and trains, "Pokémon Champions" won best multiplayer game, and "eFootball" won best ongoing game. The annual list is a chance to discover new apps that might not reach you on their own.
