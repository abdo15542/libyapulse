📡 China builds more than 26,000 industrial 5G networks — and 6G senses bodies like radar!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

China has announced that it has built more than 26,000 private fifth-generation networks inside factories and industrial facilities, in a rapid expansion that makes the whole factory connected to the internet, while tests of the sixth generation — which can sense the movement of bodies like radar — are advancing.

The announcement came during the "5G + Industrial Internet" conference held in the city of Wuhan between November 18 and 20, where officials from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology confirmed that the country has also built 1,260 factories running on 5G, and that the industrial internet now covers all 41 major industrial categories, from cars to mining.

"5G" is the fastest phone network today, but its industrial version is different: a private network inside a single factory that connects robots, sensors and production lines to each other with almost no delay, so mining trucks drive without drivers and robots work together on a single assembly line. As for the coming "6G", its signals do not only carry data — they sense the movement of bodies around them, which could help self-driving cars in future.

A 6G pilot project was also launched during the conference in Hubei province, where 6G trial stations were shown, along with robots that move moulds and sort parts inside factories.

Simply put: the factory has become a single network in which machines move in real-time coordination, and the next generation will make the network itself see what is around it. Do you expect connected smart factories to reach our countries soon?
