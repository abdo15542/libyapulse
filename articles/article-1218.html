📱 One Screen Unites the Rivals… Samsung to Supply Its All-New OLED Displays for Pixel 11, iPhone 18 Pro, and iPhone Fold!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung will supply the new generation of OLED displays for Google's and Apple's upcoming phones, according to a report citing industry sources.

The new display uses a new light-emitting material called M16, the latest generation of Samsung's display materials.

Among the first phones to carry it: the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro XL, and the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and iPhone Fold, Apple's first foldable phone.

Advertisement

Samsung's display division will also supply the reported M16 display to other flagship phones, making it the most widespread display among upcoming top phones.

The news is still in the realm of leaks and reports, as none of the companies has officially confirmed it yet — not Samsung, not Google, and not Apple.

Simply put: the two biggest rivals in the phone world will buy their screens from the same company, and from the same material; competition in the next phones may be more about the camera and software than about the display.
