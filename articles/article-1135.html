🛒 AI That Shops for You at Walmart! Muse Buys Black Friday Deals with a Spending Limit You Choose!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Walmart has switched on the live version of its own portal for AI agents, so Meta's Muse agent can now search its store, build a shopping cart, and complete payment right inside the chat, just as the Black Friday deals begin.

All you have to do is tell the agent what you want, like a TV deal or delivery offers, and it searches, suggests, and builds the cart. When you add the first item, it takes you to Walmart to sign in and create an "agent profile", a file dedicated to the agent's activity inside your account.

That profile gives you full control: you pick a spending limit, like $50, $100, or $200, decide which purchases need your approval before they go through, review a record of everything the agent bought, and delete the profile at any time. The agent sees the same prices shown on the website, and the same return policy applies.

Advertisement

The company says the portal helps agents find products without sharing your purchase history, and that it runs on Model Context Protocol technology, which connects the agent directly to the store's systems instead of browsing the website.

The service covers the Walmart website and Sam's Club, with no need for a Walmart+ membership, and the company says support for Muse is only the beginning, with more agents and apps on the way.

Simply put: tell the AI what you want, and buy while it shops for you, with a spending limit you choose. Would you let a smart agent shop in your place?
