💼 OpenAI Will Pay $8,000 a Month to Anyone Who Teaches Small Shops AI!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

OpenAI has launched a new program called Open Works that recruits local AI experts to help small shop owners in America use ChatGPT in their businesses, in exchange for a monthly reward of up to $8,000.

The chosen partner works four full days each month inside the shops for free, plus a monthly community workshop, helping restaurant, store, and workshop owners find new customers, manage their operations, and build simple tools. Participating shops get three free months of ChatGPT Business.

The pilot program started in 20 American cities, including New York, Chicago, Los Angeles, and Fargo, and targets independent shops only, excluding venture capital-backed startups and software companies. Applicants were asked to describe a real problem they solved with AI and what changed afterward.

Simply put: instead of selling its subscriptions from afar, OpenAI pays people who know the neighborhood to teach the restaurant owner and the shop owner how to use AI in their daily work.
