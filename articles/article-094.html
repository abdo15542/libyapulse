Siemens Launches an AI Agent That Programs Factories.. and Targets Small Businesses! 🏭

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Siemens launched yesterday in India its engineering agent "Eign", an AI designed for automation engineering. The agent uses multi-step reasoning and self-correction to carry out tasks on its own, inside real engineering systems, such as programming programmable logic controllers, designing operator interfaces, and configuring devices — while observing standards of accuracy, safety, and reliability. The agent was successfully tested with more than 16 clients in India, including a printing and packaging machinery company.

A controller is the small computer that controls a factory's machines; an operator interface is the screen the operator uses. The new agent writes the programming for these systems instead of merely suggesting it. Siemens is targeting especially small and medium-sized manufacturing companies with lean engineering teams. An important note: the company's figures — two to five times faster, up to 80% higher solution quality, and 50% greater efficiency — are Siemens' own figures and have not yet been verified by an independent party.
