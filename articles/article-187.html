🎧 Samsung Prepares the Premium Over-Ear Galaxy Studio Headphones!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New reports suggest that Samsung is preparing to enter the premium over-ear headphone category with a new product called “Galaxy Studio.” The headphones’ specs appeared in the code of the One UI 9.5 beta, including active noise cancellation, spatial audio with head tracking, wear detection, wired and wireless connectivity together, and an 870 mAh battery. Samsung has not confirmed the headphones or their launch date yet.

Simply put: over-ear headphones mean large cushions that cover your whole ear, with better noise isolation and sound that surrounds you from all directions.
