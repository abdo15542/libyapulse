🛰️ A faulty robotic arm joint returns from space! Dragon carries a Canadarm2 part to Earth after 5 months!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A rare piece from the International Space Station has returned to Earth: a faulty wrist joint from the Canadian robotic arm Canadarm2, carried back by the Dragon cargo spacecraft on its return on Saturday, November 21, landing in the Pacific Ocean near Los Angeles.

The joint failed on May 27 during routine work, when its motor drew more current than expected and did not move as it should. Astronauts Chris Williams and Jessica Meir went on a spacewalk on June 30 lasting seven hours and twenty minutes, replacing the faulty part with a healthy one, and the arm returned to work.

The space agency will examine the returned joint in the lab to find the exact cause of the failure, and may repair it for reuse if possible, as Canadarm2 is the station's main arm that moves equipment and catches visiting spacecraft.

The joint was not the only new return: Dragon also carried a spacesuit and protein crystals prepared by Canadian astronaut Joshua Kutryk for a Japanese study on drug development, whose research will continue on Earth.

With this, Dragon completed its 35th commercial resupply mission, after undocking from the station on Friday morning, November 20, following a delay from Wednesday due to bad weather at the landing sites.

Simply put: a giant robotic arm in space had its joint fail, so two astronauts went out to fix it in open space, and the faulty joint came back to Earth for scientists to examine... will the part be repaired and return to space again?
