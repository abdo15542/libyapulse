📱 Redmi R80 Appears in Xiaomi's China Store — Huge Screen and a 6,000 Battery!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Xiaomi has listed the "Redmi R80 5G" and "Redmi R80M 5G" on its store in China, revealing their prices and full specs before announcing a sale date. The "R80" starts at 1,899 Chinese yuan for the 4GB RAM and 128GB storage version, while the "R80M" starts at 2,199 yuan.

Both phones run on MediaTek's "Dimensity 6300" chip, with a huge 6.9-inch LCD screen at HD+ resolution and a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, and touch response up to 240Hz. They carry a 13MP rear camera and a 5MP front camera, a 6,000mAh battery with 33W wired charging, and run "HyperOS 3".

Both versions come in four colors, including white, black, leather brown, and blue, and the phone weighs 205 grams and is about 8mm thick. Xiaomi has not yet announced when sales begin, or whether the phones will reach markets outside China.
