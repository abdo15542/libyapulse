⚠️ Urgent Warning: Serious WhatsApp Vulnerabilities — Update Your App Now!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

India's computer emergency response team has issued a high-severity warning about multiple vulnerabilities in WhatsApp that could allow a remote attacker to run malicious commands and access sensitive information.

The flaws include a bug in WhatsApp for Android that could allow stored files to be deleted when a rigged message about "order details", sent from an unofficial client, is tapped.

They also include a certificate-validation flaw inside the verification system linked to the AI service, and a third flaw that could expose the user's internet address through rigged advertising links.

Advertisement

The warning covers WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business on Android and iPhone, as well as the desktop versions for Mac and Windows, all in specific versions older than the latest update.

The fix is simple and straightforward: update the app from the app store to the latest version, as the company has already fixed these flaws in its recent updates.

Simply put: a single message could be enough to hack your phone if your app is old; do you update your apps as soon as updates arrive?
