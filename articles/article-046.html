Apple Watch: The New Siri Modular Face Summarizes What Was Said 15 Seconds Ago! ⌚

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The upcoming watchOS 27.3 update adds two new designs for the "Siri Modular" watch face, bringing the total to 5 designs.

The new face places Siri in a pill-shaped module with three buttons: Siri conversations, Siri intelligence, and "Siri Summaries".

The "Summary" feature turns audio into written text, so you read a summary of what was said instead of listening to it again.

Advertisement

And with the "Live Playback" feature, you can see a transcript of the last 15 seconds of spoken words on your watch screen.

The update is in beta, with the official release expected in December, and it works with Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4.
