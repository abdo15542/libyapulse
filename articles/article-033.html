California Wants an Emergency Kill Switch for AI! 🛑

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 18, California Governor Gavin Newsom issued new recommendations to tighten oversight of AI. Note: these recommendations, in an 85-page report titled "AI in 2026 and Beyond," are not yet law — they are proposals for the legislature to discuss when it returns in December.

The highlights: safety standards set by the state, not by companies; ongoing oversight by licensed independent verification bodies; the state's power to issue an emergency stop order for a dangerous model if the company refuses to contain the risk; shortening the deadline for reporting critical incidents from 15 days to 48 hours; and the power to cap repeated self-improvement — that is, AI designing a smarter version of itself without human intervention.
