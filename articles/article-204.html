🤖 Meta’s Muse Mixes Up Chats and Exposes Private Data!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New reports stirred controversy around Meta’s AI assistant “Muse,” after cases were observed in which it mixes up conversations and shows private data to other users, with examples circulating of bank balances appearing in chats — though it has not yet been confirmed whether the data was real or hallucinations from the model. Meta described the cases as “isolated incidents” and said many of them were hallucinations that have been handled. Other cases were also observed, including a non-refundable booking made without the user’s permission, automatic updates to crypto balances, and a reminder for a messaging service through a notification permission on Android and accessibility and screenshot access on macOS — a path Apple closed on iPhone. The app has passed 10 million downloads since its launch in September, and about a quarter of users return to it daily.

Simply put: the smart assistant may act on its own or mix up conversations; so don’t share sensitive data with it, and review any action it takes in your name.
