🎮 'GTA 6' Makes $3.79 Billion on Its First Day — A New Record!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Rockstar has confirmed that its game "Grand Theft Auto 6" made $3.79 billion in retail sales worldwide on its launch day, November 19, setting a new record for any entertainment release in history. The figure beat the estimate from research firm "Sensor Tower", which had predicted only about $2.36 billion, and 32.5 million copies were sold before the release itself — a new record for pre-orders across platforms.

The game takes players back to the city of "Vice City", a fictional version of Miami, with two protagonists, Lucia and Jason, in a crime story and a relationship that brings them together — 13 years after the last main entry in the series. For comparison, "GTA 5", released in 2013, made about a billion dollars in its first three days and sold more than 230 million copies over its lifetime, making the new entry's launch far stronger.

This opening reflects the enormous commercial power of major game franchises, and it could affect console sales, digital distribution, streaming, and gaming accessories in the coming months, while the biggest opportunity for parent company "Take-Two" remains ongoing sales and online multiplayer services.
