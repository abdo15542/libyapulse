🛒 Black Friday 2026: the first time AI shops for you and pays on your behalf!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Black Friday on 27 November will be different from all that came before it: it is the first time an AI agent can search for a product, buy it and pay for it on your behalf. On 13 November, OpenAI launched "ChatGPT Wallet", which lets its agent pay on the store's own website with a one-time payment token, after you review the products, the total and the card and approve with your face or fingerprint, without the agent or the store ever seeing your real card number.

Google was not slow to join the race: on 17 November it published a guide for stores explaining how to let its agent pass through protection systems, and Gemini became able to complete purchases through built-in payment, although it may hand the screen back to you for the final click on some sites. Adobe expects visits coming from AI to American shopping sites to jump 159% above last Thanksgiving's level.

But the road is not paved with roses: some stores may block agents unintentionally through anti-bot systems, fraud detection systems may reject legitimate orders placed by an agent, and airline United changed its terms on 11 November to require written permission before any agent searches, books or buys through its website. Bank of America also warned its customers against handing their bank account username and password to any AI agent, stressing that any loss would be their own responsibility.

Simply put: this year the robot may buy the holiday gifts instead of you while you approve with your fingerprint — do you trust an agent that pays with your money, or do you prefer to keep your finger on the buy button?
