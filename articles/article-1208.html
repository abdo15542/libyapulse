💰 "Google" Models Outspend "OpenAI" for the First Time in Months

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The platform "OpenRouter", which lets developers reach multiple AI models through a single interface, revealed that its users spent more on "Google" models last week than on "OpenAI" models.

This is the first time "Google" has beaten its rival in weekly spending on the platform since the week of June 8 — more than five months ago.

According to the platform, "Google"'s share passed 50% in the head-to-head comparison between the two companies, thanks to strong demand for its newest models, led by "Gemini 4 Neon", released on November 18, and "Gemini 4 Carbon".

Advertisement

The platform did not reveal exact dollar amounts, or how much each model contributed to the increase.

But the full picture matters: these figures cover spending inside "OpenRouter" alone, not the whole market — they exclude direct purchases from "OpenAI" or "Google Cloud" or "Azure", and they exclude subscriptions from regular users.

Simply put: imagine a single market bringing together models from several companies, where developers this week spent more on "Google" models than on any other; that is an important sign of how strong the new models are, but it does not mean "Google" has overtaken "OpenAI" across the whole market.

Will "Google" hold on to this lead, or is it just temporary excitement around a new launch?
