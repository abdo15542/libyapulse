📈 The biggest IPO in history: Anthropic goes public and its founders pocket about $2 billion!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Anthropic, the AI company behind the 'Claude' models, has entered the Nasdaq stock exchange under the ticker 'ANT', in the largest initial public offering in history. The share price was set at $83, trading began on Thursday, November 19, at $111.50, then closed the first day at $97.17, up 17.1% from the offering price, giving the company a market value of about $1.3 trillion.

The core offering raised about $81.3 billion, rising to about $93.5 billion after banks exercised the option to buy additional shares, plus a private placement worth $26 billion to investors including Google, Nvidia, and Microsoft. The seven founders sold about 3.38 million shares each, about $280 million per founder, totaling nearly $2 billion before fees and taxes, while each pledged to donate about 80% of their stake in the company to charity.

The company plans to use the money for computing infrastructure and hiring, as its contractual obligations amount to about $70.7 billion due within one year, mostly for computing capacity to train and run its models. About 600 non-founder employees now hold stakes worth more than $100 million each.
