🤖 Leaked 'Bixby 5.0': Samsung's assistant finally remembers you!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new leak has revealed an unreleased version of Samsung's 'Bixby 5.0' assistant, obtained and tried by a tech site before its official release with the 'One UI 9.5' update next year. Most notable is a cleaner new interface, with a floating bubble suggesting you try 'actions' and 'things' you can ask the assistant to do, such as repeated and scheduled tasks.

The biggest change, though, is the 'Memory' feature: a new button in the assistant's settings under 'Advanced features' enables a built-in memory, so Bixby remembers the details and preferences you tell it during conversations. You can say 'remember' or 'save' to store a piece of information, or let it learn automatically from your conversations, so its replies become more personalized to you. Memories can be saved on the device itself without the internet, or online according to your preference, noting that memories relying on cloud processing do not work when offline processing is enabled.

Testing of 'One UI 9.5' is expected to begin this month or in December, starting with 'Galaxy S26' phones, with the final version arriving with the launch of the 'Galaxy S27' in early 2027. All these details remain an unofficial leak until Samsung announces officially.

Simply put: imagine an assistant that does not ask you the same things every time, but remembers what you like and what you asked it before, like a friend who knows you well.
