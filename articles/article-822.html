🛡️ Samsung hides your screen from prying eyes — a new privacy layer with the Galaxy S26!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On Wednesday, Samsung announced a new privacy layer coming soon to Galaxy phones, to fight what is known as screen snooping — someone next to you reading what is on your screen in public places. The company says the feature makes it harder for others to see your screen, and gives you a higher level of security on public transport and in crowded places.

The company has not yet revealed how the feature works technically, but it said it will come with multiple settings: you can adjust the screen clarity level yourself, choose specific apps the privacy layer applies to, such as banking and messaging apps, and customise other parts of your phone experience. The feature is expected to appear first with the upcoming Galaxy S26 series.

This step comes after a series of leaks that revealed the feature before the official announcement, at a time when companies are competing to protect users' privacy in public places. The idea is simple but practical: your screen is for you alone, even when you are sitting among hundreds of people.

Simply put: a screen its owner sees clearly, but the person sitting next to them struggles to read — have you ever been a victim of someone peeking at your screen?
