📊 US Phone Shipments Fall 9%… and Only Apple Grows!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Data from research firm Counterpoint showed that smartphone shipments in the United States fell 9% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to last year, with rising memory costs pressuring Android manufacturers. Apple kept the lead with a 57% market share and was the only company to achieve growth during the quarter, while Samsung came second with a 25% share.

Simply put: the US market is selling fewer phones this year, but Apple is selling more than everyone else, while Android manufacturers struggle with the rising cost of phone components.
