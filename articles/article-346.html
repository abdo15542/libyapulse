📱 Vivo 'X500' Series Prices Leak Before Its India Launch: The Top Model at 179,999 Rupees!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Vivo is preparing to launch its flagship "X500" series in India next week. The series includes three phones — the "X500", "X500 Pro", and "X500 Pro Max" — alongside the "Watch GT 2" smartwatch and a "Zeiss" telephoto lens. Sale prices in India have leaked, along with the memory and storage versions, ahead of the official announcement.

According to the leaks, the "X500" will sell for 119,999 Indian rupees for its only version with 12GB of memory and 256GB of storage, while the "X500 Pro" will cost 149,999 rupees for the same version. The "X500 Pro Max" starts at 159,999 rupees for the 12GB and 256GB version, and reaches 179,999 rupees for the 12GB and 512GB version, with an optional professional photography kit that could cost an extra 50,000 rupees. The "Watch GT 2" will sell for 14,999 rupees.

The announced specs indicate the "X500 Pro Max" carries a triple rear camera tuned by "Zeiss", including two 200MP cameras, a 50MP main camera with optical stabilization, and a 200MP telephoto camera with a large sensor, plus an octa-core "MediaTek Dimensity 9600" processor. The phones will be available in "Apex Orange" and "Eternal Black" for the higher models, and "Ember Red" and "Titanium Black" for the standard model, through Vivo's store, Amazon, and "Flipkart".

Simply put: Vivo is betting on the camera first — two 200MP cameras in one phone, at a price approaching two thousand dollars for the top version.
