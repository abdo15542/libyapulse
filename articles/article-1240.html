🎵 Spotify kicks thousands of users out of their accounts… a sudden outage stops the music!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Thousands of Spotify users woke up this morning to an unwelcome surprise: the app logged them out of their accounts suddenly and refused to let them back in, so the music stopped mid-journey.

Reports began climbing around 6:30 AM Eastern time, passing 14,000 reports on outage-tracking site Downdetector, reaching more than 16,000 reports in the United States alone, and around 8,000 in Britain.

Most of the complaints were about logging in: users found themselves outside their accounts without doing anything, and when they tried to return they saw error messages, some were asked to create a new account, while login codes sent by email never reached others.

Advertisement

The company acknowledged the problem through its status account on X, saying it was aware of the issue and investigating it, then announced after about 40 minutes that things were looking "much better now", and service returned for most users.

By 7:17 AM, reports had fallen to only around 600, a sign that the outage ended quickly, but the company has not yet disclosed its cause.

There is nothing to suggest the accounts were hacked; the sudden logout was a symptom of the outage itself, not account theft, and songs downloaded for offline listening did not work either during the outage.

Simply put: a short outage logged you out of your account and refused to let you back in, then everything returned within an hour; the lesson: don't reset your password or create a new account during a public outage — your account and playlists never disappeared.
