📱 Leaks: the OnePlus Ace 6 Pro Max with 16 GB of memory and an 8,000 mAh battery before the end of November!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New leaks suggest the OnePlus Ace 6 Pro Max will launch in China before the end of November, and may arrive globally later under the name OnePlus 15R.

According to the well-known leaker "Smart Pikachu", the phone will run on the new Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 processor, with 12 or 16 GB of LPDDR5X Ultra RAM.

Storage options will be 256 or 512 GB or one terabyte, with fast UFS 4.1 technology.

Advertisement

The phone will come with a 6.7-inch OLED display at 1.5K resolution, and a huge 8,000 mAh battery with 100W fast charging.

The cameras include two 50-megapixel rear cameras and a 32-megapixel front camera for selfies.

This phone is part of the Ace series, which focuses on strong performance and a competitive price, combining a flagship processor, a large battery and fast charging.

All of the above remains unconfirmed leaks, as the company has not yet announced the launch date or final specs.

Simply put: imagine a phone with giant memory, a battery that lasts two days, and charging that fills it fast. That is what the leaks promise about the new phone — if they are true.
