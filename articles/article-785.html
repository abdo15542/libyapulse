🏛️ The 'AI Audit Act'... Congress Wants to Know Where the AI Trillions Are Going!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 19, four members of the U.S. Senate, from the Democratic and Republican parties, introduced a new bill called the 'AI Audit Act', aimed at increasing transparency about how publicly listed companies invest in AI and manage it.

The bill requires public companies to disclose whether they have an AI governance policy and a dedicated AI committee on the board of directors, publishing the policy publicly, or explaining why it is absent, and it also requires them to disclose the financing of their AI infrastructure, including joint projects, special purpose entities, and related debts.

The bill also asks for a unified risk section in annual reports, disclosing reliance on third-party models, concentration of computing providers, cost volatility, and data licensing risks, in addition to a study by the Securities and Exchange Commission on how companies account for AI research, revenues, and capital spending.

The bill comes amid growing concern about the scale of investment, as one study estimates that building AI infrastructure could need $10.3 trillion between 2025 and 2032, about 3.6% of U.S. GDP each year, while the ways these investments are financed remain unclear to investors and regulators.

Simply put: trillions of dollars are flowing into AI without anyone knowing exactly how they are financed; so do investors have the right to know where their money is going?
