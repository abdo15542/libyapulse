📉 Anthropic Shares Fall 13.5% on Their Second Day — and Approach the IPO Price!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Shares of Anthropic, the company behind the "Claude" AI models, fell about 13.5% in Friday midday trading, approaching $84 — very close to the initial offering price of $83 — just one day after the company went public. The shares began trading on Thursday at $111.50 and closed the first day at $97.17, up 17.1% from the offering price.

In contrast, the company announced the completion of its huge financing: the public offering raised about $93.5 billion, plus a $26 billion private placement for investors including Google, Nvidia, and Microsoft, bringing total financing to about $119.5 billion. The company will use the money to expand its computing infrastructure, develop stronger models, and compete for enterprise customers.

This sharp volatility reflects how hard it is to value advanced AI companies: the company is growing fast, but developing and running models requires huge ongoing investment, and investors want stronger proof that revenue growth can support this enormous market value over the long term.

Simply put: first-day excitement doesn't guarantee second-day support; the market is now asking: can the company turn huge sums of money into real profits?
