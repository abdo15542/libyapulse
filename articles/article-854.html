📉 Worst Week Since 1991: Taiwan Index Plunges 6.23% in a Single Day Amid AI Fears!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Taiwan's "Taiex" index plunged 6.23% on Friday 20 November, closing at 36,033 points — its third-largest single-day drop in history, and its lowest close in about seven months. That brought the index's losses to about 17.5% in a single week, its worst week since 1991. The drop came amid a global sell-off: South Korea's index fell 5.21% and Japan's 5.06%, while "TSMC" lost 4.76%, "MediaTek" about 10%, and "SoftBank" shares plunged 14.48%.

The reason, according to analysts, is that investors have started doubting whether the billions being spent on AI data centres will return enough profit to justify them, amid uncertainty over the future of "Nvidia" and the US tightening restrictions on chip exports to China. Taiwan's authorities responded quickly: the Financial Supervisory Commission announced a ban on short selling below the previous day's closing price, starting Monday 23 November — the first comprehensive ban of its kind since 2015.

Simply put: Asia makes the world's AI hardware, so when investors doubt its profits, its markets fall first and deepest — have investors started doubting that AI investments will pay back?
