📱 ColorOS 17 Reaches More OPPO, OnePlus, and Realme Phones in November!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OPPO announced the rollout schedule for the ColorOS 17 update, based on Android 17, during November, covering its phones as well as OnePlus and Realme phones and tablets. The rollout began on 5 November with the first batch, which includes the Reno 15 series, flagship OnePlus phones and their tablets, plus Realme phones from the GT and Number series and the P series.

The rollout did not stop at the announced schedule: on 19 November, the OnePlus Nord 6 and Nord CE 6 in India began receiving the stable version of the update, including system-level AI features and other updates. The update carries version number CPH2793_17.0.0.100 for the Nord 6, and CPH2805_17.0.0.101 for the Nord CE 6, which means the update has actually reached real devices in users' hands, not just promises on paper.

ColorOS 17 arrives at a time when all Chinese companies are competing over Android 17 updates, with each company wanting to prove that its phones do not lag far behind Pixel. To install it, open Settings, then About Device, then Software Update, and if the update has not appeared for you yet, wait your turn in the gradual rollout, as it reaches regions and models in batches during the month.

Simply put: the Android 17 update is knocking on the doors of three brands at once; is your phone on November's list?
