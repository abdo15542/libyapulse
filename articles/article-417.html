🤖 A Home Robot from 'Apple' with a Screen That Turns Toward You — Launch in 2027, Priced Around $1,000!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Apple" is working on a tabletop home robot, with a screen of about 9 inches on a rotating arm that turns 360 degrees toward whoever is speaking, in an internal project codenamed "J595". The project began in 2019 as a video-calling device, then turned into a home robot, and the company targets a launch in 2027, with a possible delay to 2028.

The hardware has been largely ready for about two years, but the software is what's delaying the launch. The software team is now narrowing the device's initial scope to "FaceTime" calls and smart-home control, like turning on lights when someone enters the room, after the original plans included a conversational voice assistant. The device is expected to run a new operating system called "Charismatic", combining elements of the "Apple TV" and "Apple Watch" interfaces and relying heavily on widgets.

"Apple" has not officially announced the device yet — neither the final name nor the price — but former employees expect the name "Home View Ultra", and the target price is around $1,000, which could rise as component costs increase. Meanwhile, the smaller 7-inch "Home View" screen reaches customers on November 13, starting at $299. The project is driven by new CEO John Ternus, who wants to make robotics a new field for the company.

Simply put: instead of a fixed device on the table, this is a screen that moves and turns toward you wherever you are in the room, as if following you with its eyes.
