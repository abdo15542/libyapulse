💳 ChatGPT Wallet: Your AI Agent Pays for You... and Your Fingerprint Is the Approval!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI has launched a new feature called ChatGPT Wallet, letting its always-on agent Dots help you choose what to buy and then complete the purchase on your behalf, in a step that moves the smart assistant from giving advice to completing the entire transaction.

The feature is initially available to a limited number of Pro plan subscribers in the United States, aged 18 or older, who hold a US card from Visa, Mastercard, or American Express. The user adds their card once, then reviews before each purchase: the items, the total amount, and the card being used, and approves with a security check such as a face or fingerprint scan, creating a clear record of every purchase they approved.

Most importantly, neither the agent nor the store ever sees your real card number: after your approval, the payment network issues a substitute token carrying a card number, security code, and expiry date specific to that transaction alone, and the amount is charged to your card while reward points and purchase dispute protection stay as they are. The company later plans to allow pre-approved purchases within limits set by the user, such as a spending cap or specific product categories.

Simply put: your agent can now pay, but the key to your card is still your fingerprint. Are you comfortable with the idea of AI buying for you with a single approval, or do you prefer to always be the one who presses the buy button?
