💼 CDW launches 3 AI tools… from experiment to production without waste!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

CDW has unveiled three new AI capabilities at its 2026 Partner Summit, with one stated goal: turning companies' AI investments into measurable results, not experiments that stay trapped in the lab.

The first tool is AI Control Plane, a platform that helps companies evaluate, deploy, govern, and improve AI models across different platforms, based on benchmarking and implementation expertise, with capabilities for model routing and cost monitoring to be added later.

The second is AI Elevate, a service extending the company's field engineering model to help companies overcome the biggest obstacle they face today: a shortage of specialized engineers, by identifying the highest-impact opportunities, setting deployment roadmaps, and integrating solutions into existing workflows.

The third is Build IQ, an AI software delivery platform that speeds up building production-ready applications with a governed approach in which AI assists across the full lifecycle, from requirements and design to development, testing, security, and release.

The company says the three tools address the problems that stall most AI projects: rising costs, a skills shortage, governance concerns, and the difficulty of moving from experiment to real production.

The step comes as companies shift from experimenting with AI to actually running it, at a time when the company's shares have risen 4.1% over the past month.

Simply put: many companies buy AI and don't know whether it earns them anything; these tools try to answer the hardest question: how much does the model cost, and how much does it return to you?
