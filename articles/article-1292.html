📱 iQOO 16 Arrives in Malaysia on December 8 — 2nm Chip and a 165Hz Display!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

iQOO confirmed that the iQOO 16 series will launch in Malaysia on December 8, 2026, including the iQOO 16 and iQOO 16s phones, and pre-orders opened with a symbolic deposit of one Malaysian ringgit from November 24 to December 7, with a chance to win prizes worth up to 16,000 ringgit.

The main phone, the iQOO 16, runs on the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chip built on 2nm technology, alongside a dedicated gaming chip from the company called the Q4, a Samsung 2K display with a 165Hz refresh rate, and an 8,400 mAh battery with 100W wired charging, according to the Chinese version of the phone launched in September.

The smaller version, the 16s, comes with a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chip and a 7,650 mAh battery, according to reports the company has not yet confirmed, and the iQOO 16 will be available in Matrix and Alpha colors, while the 16s comes in Vector Grey and Shadow Black.

Advertisement

The company has not yet announced prices in Malaysia, nor set a date for the series launch in India despite confirming the phone is coming there, while unofficial leaks suggest the Indian launch may be delayed until January.

Simply put: a phone with the most powerful chip in its class, the fastest display, and a huge battery, but its price is still unknown — anyone who wants to buy it will have to wait until December 8 to learn the price first.

Would you buy a phone with a huge battery and an ultra-fast display even if its price was not known yet?
