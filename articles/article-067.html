Apple tests a daily limit for Siri AI use — with more for iCloud+ subscribers! 📱

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple is testing a new idea in the beta of "iOS 27.3": a daily limit on using Siri AI and AI features that rely on server-side processing. Each device gets a free daily allowance, and once it runs out, usage can be extended via an iCloud+ subscription. Simple commands like setting a timer stay free and unlimited even after the limit is reached.

The idea is like a data plan: simple questions are always free, while smart multi-step requests are the ones that count. The allowance grows with paid iCloud+ plans — up to twenty times more with the largest plan — with no change in prices. And crucially: this is only a test in a beta version, Apple has not announced a public release date, and the limits may change before the final version, so don't take it as settled yet.
