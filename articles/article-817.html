📅 Google buys Howie — an AI assistant that arranges your meetings from your email!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On 16 November, Google Labs announced the purchase of Howie, a Seattle startup whose AI scheduling assistant works from inside email, without disclosing the value of the deal. Howie's team will join Google's experimental labs to build the next generation of AI tools for small businesses.

Howie works simply: you copy it into an email, and it coordinates meeting times, adds them to the calendar, follows up on replies, and alerts you to conflicts, following rules you set yourself, such as the meeting length and your preferred calling app. The company was founded by Austin Petersmith and Dave Newman around 2023, raised a seed round of $6 million in September 2025, has more than 1,000 paying customers, and schedules more than 5,000 meetings a week.

The deal comes amid a heated race between Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta to build AI agents that carry out tasks on users' behalf. Howie said it will keep serving its current customers, and that joining Google is natural because its tool is already built on Gmail and Google Calendar.

Simply put: copy a smart assistant into an email and it books your meeting and sends it to your calendar — will scheduling become the first office task people hand over to AI completely?
