🚚 Electric Trucks with $100 Million More in New Funding — "Harbinger" Expands with Financing from "Palmer Luckey"'s Bank

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

American electric truck company "Harbinger" announced on Tuesday that it has raised more than $100 million in new funding, to fuel its growth and expand products that include self-driving vehicles, energy storage and defense.

The funding includes a convertible note from "Pinegrove Venture Partners" and debt from "Erebor" bank, the bank founded by "Palmer Luckey", founder of virtual reality headsets, which launched last February and whose loans have passed $1 billion.

The new funding comes less than three weeks after a previous $240 million round led by "Tiger Global" and other funds, in which "FedEx" took part and ordered 2,000 trucks from the company.

The company's founder and CEO said building an industrial company of this size needs a different kind of funding: the money needed to scale up production and deliver trucks to customers should not come only from selling shares, as the biggest car and airline companies have done for a long time.

"Erebor" is now the company's main banking partner, and "Luckey" said traditional banks find it hard to support companies that build real technology and provide American jobs, and that his company was built specifically to support this kind of business.

Simply put: a small electric truck company raising hundreds of millions in a matter of weeks, because it has real orders from a shipping giant, and a new bank willing to lend to tech companies that old banks ignore.
