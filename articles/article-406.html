🍏 Apple Cancels the 'Siri AI' Waitlist — the New Assistant Is Available Immediately!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Apple" has removed the mandatory waitlist for the new "Siri AI" assistant in "iOS 27", so eligible users can now download and use the new assistant immediately without waiting their turn. The company updated the assistant's support page in early November, so the text now says the required software "will start downloading automatically" when activated, after previously saying that pressing the button "will add you to a waitlist". The list remains only during times of high demand, and the wait time may vary then.

The new assistant launched with "iOS 27" on September 14 — the biggest upgrade to the assistant in years: a conversational assistant that understands personal context and performs multi-step tasks across apps. But "Apple" placed it behind a waitlist from day one, likely to manage server load, so users waited weeks for their turn before the download began.

There are still conditions: the assistant is still in beta, works on iPhone 15 Pro and newer, and supports six languages — English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish — with more languages arriving later this year. It remains unavailable on iPhone and iPad in the European Union for regulatory reasons, doesn't work with "Apple" accounts in mainland China, or with users under thirteen. To activate it, update your system, then open "Settings", tap "Siri", and choose "Siri AI (Beta)".

Simply put: the queue is over, and the new assistant is one tap away — but only if your phone is new enough, and you're outside Europe.
