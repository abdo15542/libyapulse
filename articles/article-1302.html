💶 Dutch company Accelera negotiates $400 million to compete with Nvidia!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Dutch chip company Accelera AI is in talks to raise an additional $400 million to expand development of its AI chips, according to a report published on 24 November citing people familiar with the private talks, and the company is also seeking hundreds of millions of euros in grants from the European Union.

The company's chips target running AI models, a process called inference, aiming to make it cheaper and less energy-hungry, with data processed locally instead of relying on the cloud, in sectors such as manufacturing, robotics, retail, security, defence and agriculture.

The company, founded in 2021 in Eindhoven, raised more than $250 million in February 2026 led by the Innovation Industries fund, with participation from BlackRock and SiteGround, bringing its total funding to more than $450 million across equity, grants and debt.

The company unveiled in November its third-generation Titania architecture, aimed at data centres and supercomputers, and says it has more than $300 million in confirmed customer commitments, targeting production to start in the third quarter of 2027, and it works with Dell on European AI infrastructure projects.

The talks are still ongoing, with no disclosed valuation or new investors in the proposed round, and the company confirmed the talks are ongoing without announcing an amount or a timeline, and they may not end in a deal.

The move comes as part of a broader European race to reduce reliance on American and Asian chip suppliers, as other European startups raised hundreds of millions in recent months to build their own inference processors.

Simply put: a small European company wants $400 million to compete with the chip giant in its own field; money alone is not enough — it must prove its chips compete on performance, cost and software.
