💰 Sierra Raises $1.75 Billion! Its Value Doubles to $30 Billion in Just Six Months!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Sierra, the company building AI agents for customer service, has announced a new funding round of $1.75 billion, lifting its valuation to $30 billion, double what it was worth just six months ago.

The round was led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, and every previous investor who led its earlier rounds took part, including Benchmark, Sequoia, GV, and Tiger Global, a sign of their growing confidence in the company.

The company was founded by Bret Taylor, the former co-CEO of Salesforce, and Clay Bavor, and it builds AI agents that answer customers and get their tasks done through chat, voice, text messages, WhatsApp, and email, such as refunds, claims, payments, and renewals.

The company says its annual recurring revenue doubled from $100 million to $200 million in six months, then from $200 million to $400 million in just five months, and that it now works with nearly half of the top 50 companies on the Fortune list.

Its previous round in May raised $950 million at a valuation above $15 billion, so the new valuation is double that in less than seven months, at a time when competition over customer-service agents is intensifying.

Simply put: a customer-service AI company doubles its value twice in less than a year. Do investors really believe agents will replace customer-service staff?
