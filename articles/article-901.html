🚗 A Record in China: Two Out of Every Three New Cars Have a 'Plug'!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Plug-in cars set a new record in China during October, making up about 68% of all cars sold in the world's biggest car market.

The share is made up of 45% fully electric cars and 23% plug-in hybrids, and the record came despite the whole market falling 27% compared with last year, to about 1.6 million cars, because gasoline car sales alone collapsed by 44%, while plug-in car sales fell by only 14%.

With this performance, the share of plug-in cars across all of 2026 rose to 59%, five points higher than the result for the whole of 2025, while fully electric cars alone reached 40%, compared with 33% in 2025.

The clearest scene of gasoline cars going extinct was in the best-sellers list: the top ten cars are all electric, led by the small Geely Xingyuan with 50,190 registered cars, up 14% on last year, at a price of only about $10,000, followed by the BYD Song with 30,772 cars, then the BYD Yuan Up with 28,514 cars.

In contrast, Tesla had a difficult month in China: sales of the Model Y fell 60% to just 7,807 cars, and the Model 3 fell 85% to 1,010 cars, while the Volkswagen ID. Aura T6, made and developed in China, overtook it for the first time.

What stands out is that cheap small cars are the stars of this phase: three of the top four cars in the small-car category are electric, and most of the best-selling small cars are priced between $10,000 and $11,000, while companies keep launching huge sports cars longer than 5 meters that most buyers do not want.

Simply put: the Chinese market is shrinking, but what remains of it is switching to electricity fast, until two out of every three new cars now run on a plug. Will every car in China become electric before 2030?
