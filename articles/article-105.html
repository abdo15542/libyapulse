Phones & Devices

OnePlus has started rolling out its new operating system update, ColorOS 17, based on Android 17, to the Nord 6 and Nord CE 6 phones in India, eight days ahead of the previously announced date. The update is arriving gradually over the air and is about 3.25 GB in size. It brings a smoother new design, along with AI features such as smart suggestions, live alerts and photo enhancement, plus a new translation app. Users can check whether the update has arrived from their phone's settings.
