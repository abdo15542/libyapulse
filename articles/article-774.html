📱 The Galaxy S28 Could Bring Back the Variable Aperture... a Feature Gone Since 2019!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new leak says Samsung could bring back the variable aperture feature to the expected Galaxy S28 Pro and Galaxy S28 Ultra phones in 2028. The leak came from leaker Yogesh Brar, who explained that the feature "could" return, without firm confirmation, and that Samsung is not the only company considering this technology in its flagship phones.

Samsung had introduced a mechanical aperture in the Galaxy S9 and S10 phones, then abandoned it with the S20 in favor of computational photography. Reports indicate the Galaxy S27 series coming in early 2027 will not carry this feature because of cost and camera thickness, making the S28 the first realistic chance for its return.

A variable aperture lets the camera physically change the size of its lens opening: opening it wide to let in more light for night photos with natural background blur, and narrowing it in bright scenes to preserve details. Apple introduced the same feature in the iPhone 18 Pro this year, which explains Samsung's renewed interest in it.

Simply put: your phone's camera could once again open and close its aperture like professional cameras; would you prefer this mechanical feature, or is computational photography enough for you?
