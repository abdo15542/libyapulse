🚨 Allstate Outage Cripples Its Website and App for a Third Day — Customers Left Without Claims Before Thanksgiving!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

US insurer Allstate has been hit by a technical outage since the morning of Monday 23 November, leaving customers across the country unable to log in to their accounts, review their insurance documents, file claims or pay their bills — just before the Thanksgiving holiday.

Checks confirmed the outage affects both the company's website and its mobile app: visitors to the site see a message saying services are unavailable and that the company is "working to restore access as soon as possible", while the company replies to customer complaints with automated messages asking them to contact it by private message.

According to an outage-tracking site, user reports began on Monday morning, and services were still down today, with the company issuing no official statement explaining the cause or when it will be fixed, while local agents receive calls and messages from customers who cannot use the digital services.

The outage follows a report published earlier this month saying the company plans to lay off up to 10% of its workforce as part of an AI efficiency plan — a report the company has not confirmed and which was attributed to unnamed sources.

The timing is especially bad: Thanksgiving is one of the busiest travel weeks in the United States, which means car accidents happen, while customers find themselves with no way to reach their insurer to file a claim or request roadside help through the app.

The Allstate outage is the latest in a series of disruptions that have hit mail, meetings, airlines, electronic payments and banks. Officials have said these outages are not cyberattacks, but the result of recent updates to old systems that need repeated security fixes, with AI making it easier to discover vulnerabilities.

Simply put: a giant insurer with no website and no app in America's biggest travel week; do you still trust that your essential services will work when you really need them?

Do you keep a paper copy of your insurance policy in case of an outage like this, or do you rely entirely on the app?
