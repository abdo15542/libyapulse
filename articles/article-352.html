🏛️ Trump Threatens Taxes on Companies Building AI Data Centers Outside America!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

US President Donald Trump said on Friday, at a White House press conference, that his administration will seek to punish American tech companies that build data centers outside the United States, by imposing heavy taxes or restrictions on them. He justified it by saying that America's AI infrastructure must stay inside the country, and must not give Chinese competitors access to advanced computing capabilities.

The remarks came after the president's diplomatic tour of Asia, and following new US export restrictions on semiconductors targeting data-center arrangements abroad. But Trump did not specify tax rates, enforcement mechanisms, proposed legislation, or a timeline, and acknowledged that administration officials had not been informed of the proposal in advance.

Companies like Amazon, Microsoft, Google, "Equinix", and "Digital Realty" run infrastructure in many countries to serve local customers, reduce response times, and comply with data-residency laws, and broad restrictions could complicate their investment plans. Data-center projects inside America itself also face opposition over electricity and water consumption and rising utility bills. It remains unclear whether the administration can impose these penalties by executive authority or needs Congress.
