📉 Nasdaq Wipes Out 2026 Gains in One Day — and 'Nvidia' Falls 14% Amid AI Panic!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Tech stocks fell sharply on Friday, as the Nasdaq index lost 5.3% of its value in a single session, erasing all its gains during 2026. The index recorded its second-biggest one-day point drop in history, losing more than 1,200 points, while the "S&P 500" and "Dow Jones" indexes entered correction territory after falling 3.7% and 1.9% respectively, and the fear index "VIX" jumped 33% to 37 points.

Leading the fall was "Nvidia" stock, which dropped 14% in one day — its worst day in about two years — losing 23% during the week and 47.5% since its peak on October 6, shedding about $2.7 trillion in market value. The drop came after reports of a US Securities and Exchange Commission investigation into the company's accounts, plus continuing concern about its future after its CEO's departure.

The biggest IPO in history didn't escape the wave either: "Anthropic" shares fell about 9% on their second day of trading, closing near $88, after jumping 17% on their first day. The semiconductor index fell 11%, while the Nasdaq's weekly drop of 10% was the biggest since 2020.

Investors attribute the wave to growing fears of an AI bubble: high valuations, huge spending on data centers without clear returns yet, and a possible slowdown in construction projects. Still, many investors say they still believe in the AI trade for the long term, seeing the drop as a painful repricing, not a bubble bursting.

Simply put: one day erased a full year's gains for the tech index, and the market is asking one question: will the huge spending on AI turn into profits soon, or have valuations run ahead of reality?
