🏭 Australian Residents Overwhelmingly Reject an Anthropic Data Center — 595 Objections Against Just Two Supporters!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

An official file for a planned data center project in a rural area of Australia, leased by Anthropic as the main tenant, revealed overwhelming opposition from residents: of about 600 public submissions about the project, only two expressed support and one expressed neutrality, while the rest opposed the project or asked for it to be postponed until the company meets environmental conditions.

The project, called Western Downs Digital Park, lies about 250 km west of the city of Brisbane, with a planned computing capacity of 1.44 GW, making it one of the largest data centers in the country. About a third of the objectors noted that they live less than 200 km from the site, and their main concerns were water and electricity consumption, rising prices, noise, road damage from construction trucks, and pressure on housing.

The project's developer, Xira DC, said a large number of submissions raise legitimate issues about water, energy, noise, roads, housing, and the project's size, and that it takes these observations seriously and is expanding its engagement with the community. Anthropic declined to comment.

Notably, the project received only five objections in a full month before it was announced on September 15 that Anthropic was the tenant — then the rest of the objections poured in until the local government closed submissions on November 11. This opposition comes in the same week Anthropic entered the US stock market in the largest IPO in history, with a market value of about $1.5 trillion.

The case reflects a growing tension worldwide between local communities and the 'AI factories' being built at an accelerating pace, in a boom estimated at about $7 trillion by 2030. In Australia itself, Goodman canceled a data center worth 1.2 billion Australian dollars in Sydney in September after residents' protests, and NextDC postponed a project in Melbourne over energy-consumption concerns.

Simply put: residents of a rural area telling the AI giant: we will not build your future at the expense of our water, electricity, and homes.
