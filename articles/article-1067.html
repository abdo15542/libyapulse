💾 Samsung's memory is fully booked until 2029 — with long contracts stretching to 2031!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A Korean industry report has revealed that most of Samsung's memory chip production capacity between 2027 and 2029 is already booked under signed contracts, and some customers are asking for longer contracts stretching to 2031, in a frantic race to secure memory before it runs out.

The report builds on the company's announcement in its third-quarter 2026 results, where it confirmed signing long-term supply contracts with 10 major customers and negotiating with 10 more, with some of their deals close to completion. The company aims to allocate about 60 to 70 percent of its medium- and long-term capacity to multi-year contracts by 2030, giving it clearer visibility over its revenues and securing supplies for its big customers.

The crisis runs deeper than AI memory alone: supplies of high-bandwidth HBM memory for 2027 have sold out completely, and capacity for traditional DRAM chips for the same year is effectively exhausted too. The problem is that producing HBM consumes large amounts of DRAM resources, limiting manufacturers' ability to increase traditional memory output even with new investment.

Industry sources estimate that the big memory makers currently meet only 60 to 70 percent of demand, and the shortage could worsen in 2027. The company also warned that building new factories takes years before they enter service, so no quick improvement is in sight, even if investment continues at its current pace.

NAND memory is a less certain picture: research firm TrendForce expects its contract prices to start falling in the first quarter of 2027, as storage orders from Chinese cloud companies decline and production rises. But the report's overall message is clear: the AI boom has reshaped the entire memory market, and whoever wants memory must book it years ahead.

Simply put: the memory of the next three years was sold before it was even made — will the shortage reach the price of your phone and computer?

If the biggest companies are booking memory until 2031, do you expect device prices to fall soon, or rise?
