🎮 The Famous "GTA 6" Hacker Walks Out of Prison on Sentencing Day.. and the Judge Warns Him!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The hacker who leaked footage of the game "GTA 6" walked out of prison on the very day he was sentenced, at the end of one of the most famous hacking cases in gaming history.

On November 23, a British court sentenced Arion Kurtaj, 21 years old, to seven years in prison after he admitted to hacking the company "Rockstar" in 2022 and stealing 90 clips of the unfinished game and blackmailing the company, in addition to other hacks that targeted companies including "Uber" and "Revolut".

But Kurtaj walked free the same day, because he had already spent most of the sentence in custody, since his arrest as a teenager until his transfer to a regular prison in January of this year, after he was deemed fit to stand trial, so he was released on conditions instead of serving the rest of the term behind bars.

With his release, a five-year serious crime prevention order was imposed on him, restricting his online activity and banning him from using "Telegram" and "Discord", and the judge warned him, saying: "Don't let this happen again".

Kurtaj had carried out the famous hack from a hotel room where he was staying under police protection, using a TV streaming stick, a television, and a wireless keyboard, and "Rockstar" says the hacks cost it around $5 million and thousands of hours of employee work.

His release comes just days after the game was finally released on November 19, selling 32.5 million copies on launch day alone, with revenue approaching $3.79 billion.

Simply put: he leaked the most famous game in the world from a hotel room.. was sentenced to seven years.. then walked out the same day.. so are five years of internet restrictions enough to prevent a repeat?
