🤖 Korea Holds a Hearing on a Defence AI Law!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The defence committee of the South Korean parliament has held a public hearing on two draft laws to speed up the introduction of artificial intelligence into the military, in a move that could change how fast the country arms itself.

The two bills seek to create a fast track for systems proven in trials — such as drones, robots and smart combat vehicles — to reach the military quickly, instead of waiting years in the usual procurement procedures.

The lawmakers drew inspiration from Ukraine, saying after field visits that its sense of needing to survive had produced world-class drones and anti-drone technology, and that Korea needs the same speed.

The plan proposes an approved list of systems that have already passed testing, deploying early versions of them and then improving them while in use, and among the candidate systems are reconnaissance drones, engineering vehicles that work alongside humans, and smart thermal glasses for snipers.

The next step will move to a parliamentary subcommittee to review the two bills, in a week lawmakers described as decisive for the future of the country's advanced defence capabilities.

Simply put: Korea wants an army that moves at the speed of AI... so does AI make defence stronger, or the arms race more dangerous?
