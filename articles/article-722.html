💰 Amazon Nears $7.5 Billion Purchase of Descartes, One of the Biggest AI Deals of the Year!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Press reports say Amazon is in the final stages of negotiations to acquire AI startup Descartes in a deal that could be worth about $7.5 billion, and that the agreement could be settled early next week, with the possibility of the talks collapsing still open, since neither company has officially confirmed anything yet.

Descartes, founded by three engineers in 2023, builds software that helps developers use different AI chips instead of being tied to a single supplier, along with 'world' models that generate interactive scenes. The company raised $300 million in a funding round in May at a valuation of about $4 billion, meaning the price now on the table is almost double its valuation just six months ago. Its three founders have also agreed to stay with Amazon for five years, with a large part of the amount deferred until the end of that period.

Earlier negotiations had stalled: Anthropic walked away from purchase talks in August, and Nvidia declined to reopen talks, before Amazon entered with an initial offer of about $5.5 billion in October, after which the price gradually rose above $7 billion. Reports suggest the announcement could coincide with Amazon's annual cloud computing conference on November 30.

Simply put: a company only three years old could be sold for almost double its May valuation, because whoever owns the tools to run AI on any chip owns the key to the whole market; so is a startup really worth all that money, or has the AI race pushed prices above their real value?
