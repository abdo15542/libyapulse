🎮 Valve's 'Steam Machine' gaming device now sells with no waitlist!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Valve has announced that the 'Steam Machine' gaming device with 512 gigabytes is now available for direct purchase from the 'Steam' store without reservation or a waitlist, after fulfilling all previous reservations for this model.

The 2-terabyte model is a different story: the company has closed its waitlist to new registrants, because the number of people waiting exceeds the number of devices it can produce, and the page's button will change from 'Add to waitlist' to 'Out of stock' when inventory runs out.

The smaller model costs $1,049 and the larger $1,349, with delivery estimates of 3 to 5 business days for the available model. The company attributed the tight supply to memory and storage prices rising at an unprecedented pace, and said it will share more information about additional supply in January.

Simply put: the smaller device has become like any product on the shelf you buy immediately; the bigger one has become like a sold-out concert ticket, and those waiting must follow January's news.
