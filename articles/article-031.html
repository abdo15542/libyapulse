Sovereign AI from the UAE! 🇦🇪

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 18, Etisalat UAE launched a sovereign AI platform called etisalat AIO. Sovereign AI means the models and data run and are stored inside the country and are not transferred abroad — something that matters to governments, banks, and hospitals.

The platform brings together 10 technology pillars in a single system: sovereign and mobile data centers, on-demand GPU services, on-device AI that works even when the connection drops, digital twins that simulate operations — including the Smart Wadi platform for early flood warnings.

The group plans to increase its international connectivity capacity 25-fold to 500 terabits per second, and to double its revenue from corporate and government services.

Would you rather your country's data stay inside its borders? 🛡️
