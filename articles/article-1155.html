📱 Faster Memory for the Priciest Only! Leak: Base 'Galaxy S27 Ultra' Stays on Old-Generation Memory!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new leak published by SamMobile on Monday, November 24, revealed that Samsung may reserve the faster LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage for only the top tiers of the upcoming Galaxy S27 series, while the rest of the lineup stays on older-generation memory.

According to the leak, all variants of the Galaxy S27 and S27 Plus come with 12GB of LPDDR5X memory and UFS 4.1 storage, as do the 256GB and 512GB variants of the S27 Pro, while only the 1TB Pro variant gets 16GB of LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage.

In the S27 Ultra, the 256GB variant stays on 12GB of LPDDR5X memory and UFS 4.1 storage, while the 512GB variant gets 12GB of LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage, and the 1TB variant gets 16GB of LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage — meaning only 3 of 10 variants get the new memory.

Advertisement

The report suggests the reason is sharply rising memory chip prices, as AI data centers buy up most of the production, so Samsung limits the rise in phone prices — noting that UFS 5.0 storage reaches read speeds of 10.8 gigabytes per second.

These details remain a leak Samsung has not yet confirmed, apply to the Korean versions of the phones, and may change at the expected launch in early 2027.

Simply put: you pay for an Ultra but get old-generation memory unless you buy the highest storage tier... Would you buy the most expensive phone for faster memory?
