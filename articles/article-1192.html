💰 Anthropic Becomes xAI's Biggest Customer: $3.1 Billion a Month!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new report revealed that Anthropic has become the biggest customer of Elon Musk's xAI, with a contract worth about $3.1 billion a month to rent its computing power, up to $120.5 billion through 2029.

The Anthropic deal alone makes up more than half of all of xAI's revenue, while Google also rents computing from the same company for about $800 million, with the full capacity of the contract to be completed in December 2026, and either side able to cancel with 90 days' notice after commitment periods ending in January and March 2027.

The shift came after Musk pressed his engineers in August to focus on "revenue per graphics card", even renaming an internal chat to "sell compute because we can't compete", frustrated that his own company's models could barely compete.

And xAI is shrinking its own projects to keep up with the shift: in November it dissolved the 60-person "Grok Imagine" team for images and video, and stopped AI avatars in September, while OpenAI cancelled the "Sora" video model for the same reason: tight computing capacity.

xAI has been renting computing to competitors since May 2026, and its public offering in June increased the pressure on revenue, to the point that new data centers must be completed by October to meet Anthropic's orders, which wants to rent even more.

Simply put: a company that built data centers to compete in AI ended up renting them to its competitors, because renting pays better than competing.
