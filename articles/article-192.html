🚀 Terran R Rocket Passes Its Tests and Books a Mission to the Moon!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Relativity Space announced the completion of structural qualification for all major structures of the first flight of its “Terran R” rocket, after the interstage section withstood compression loads of millions of pounds, and loads higher than flight levels. The first-stage thrust structure also completed horizontal rotation with all thirteen “Aeon R” engines, fuel lines, and flight systems installed, and the second stage completed a full ignition test lasting more than 400 seconds. At the same time, NASA assigned the rocket a lunar mission through “Impulse Space,” which will begin launching lunar landers from 2028.

Simply put: the new rocket passed the strength tests proving its structure can withstand launch pressures; passing these tests means its first flight is near, and booking a mission to the Moon gives it great confidence.
