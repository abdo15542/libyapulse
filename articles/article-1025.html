💰 ABB acquires German Ziehl-Abegg for $2.65 billion! A giant ventilation and motors deal!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The Swiss engineering group ABB announced on Monday that it will acquire the German company Ziehl-Abegg, which specializes in ventilation technologies and motors, for $2.65 billion.

The group said the deal values the German company at around 1.9 times its expected sales for 2026, and around 18.6 times its earnings before interest, taxes and depreciation, figures that reflect the buyer's confidence in the strength of its business.

ABB expects to complete the acquisition in the second half of 2027, after obtaining regulatory approvals and meeting the usual closing conditions.

The deal comes at a time when major engineering companies are racing for ventilation technologies and energy efficiency, as data centers and factories expand and need advanced cooling and ventilation.
