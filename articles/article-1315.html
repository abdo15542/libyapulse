📉 Anthropic Shares Close Below IPO Price for the First Time — and Its CEO Sold $280 Million in Stock!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Anthropic shares closed on Tuesday 24 November at $81.91, down 6.5% in a single day, recording their first close below the initial offering price of $83 — less than a week after their historic listing on Nasdaq.

During the session, the stock fell to $75.61, wiping out about $200 billion of market value, after opening trading on 19 November at $111.50 and peaking at $115.10, when its value approached two trillion dollars.

An official disclosure revealed that CEO Dario Amodei sold more than 3.37 million shares worth over $280 million the day after the listing, while the seven founders together sold shares worth about $1.96 billion as part of the offering itself.

The decline came as the company missed an AI-stock rally led by Nvidia, alongside a broad sell-off in tech shares and fears of inflated AI valuations and huge infrastructure costs.

Despite the decline, analysts expect support from index funds that may buy up to 30% of the floated shares before the end of the year, and the company will join the MSCI global indexes at the close of 30 November, announcing its first quarterly results as a public company on 24 February.

Simply put: the biggest offering in history went within days from record excitement to a close below the offering price — the market is now testing whether AI valuations are overblown.
