🛡️ 'F5' Warns of 6 Critical Flaws in 'BIG-IP' Devices That Could Allow Remote Code Execution!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"F5" has issued emergency fixes for six critical flaws in its "BIG-IP" networking products, warning that some configurations could let attackers disrupt services or execute unauthorized commands. "NHS England Digital" published an alert on Thursday detailing the security issues and identifying the affected devices, including cloud products and products linked to "Kubernetes" systems.

Some of the flaws relate to memory handling in the components that manage network traffic, where an unauthenticated attacker could send specially crafted requests that might crash network operations or execute commands under certain conditions. The company has provided security updates and engineering fixes for supported versions, and organizations must review their versions carefully to determine whether they are at risk.

The danger of these flaws is that "BIG-IP" devices are widely deployed to manage application traffic, distribute loads, and support access to important services — so one successful attack could affect many applications even if their core systems stay intact. Fixing them requires careful coordination, especially in hospitals, banks, and government agencies where network outages can be dangerous.
