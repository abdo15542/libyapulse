⚡ Nvidia Launches cuDSS 1.0! Solving Engineering Equations 151 Times Faster than the CPU!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nvidia has announced version 1.0 of the cuDSS library, a library that accelerates solving the sparse linear systems at the heart of almost every engineering and scientific simulation, from structural mechanics to fluid dynamics, circuit simulation, and power grids.

The announced numbers are striking: compared with the Intel MKL library on a B200 card, the analysis phase became 2.5 times faster, the numerical factorization phase 5.9 times faster, while the triangular solve phase jumped to 151 times faster.

The improvements were not only about speed: the new version enables by default symmetry-preserving matching and Bunch-Kaufman pivoting, which improved the accuracy of results by up to 10 decimal places in some difficult matrices.

It also added a hybrid memory mode that coordinates data between the card's memory and the computer's memory, cutting the required memory by about 30% in large design examples, so problems that once needed several cards can now be solved with a single card.

The company says the library is now ready for commercial production, and developers get these gains simply by upgrading, without changing their applications' code.

Simply put: equations that once took hours on the CPU are now solved in minutes on the graphics card — and the engineer tries more designs in less time.
