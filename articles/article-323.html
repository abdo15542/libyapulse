📱 ChatGPT gets a new look on your phone... and 'Dot' gets its own tab!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI has begun rolling out a new design for the ChatGPT app on phones, bringing your favorite features closer and easier to reach. The first wave reaches 'Pro' plan subscribers who have 'Dot' — a persistent personal assistant that follows your work — or are eligible to create one, and the new design can be turned on or off from the settings.

The highlight of the design is a new bottom bar: a home screen that greets you with a morning message and an 'Ask anything' field, a dedicated tab for 'Dot' that takes you to a continuous conversation with your personal assistant, and a third tab showing a grid of tools such as 'Codex', projects, images, and appointments, and you can pin any tool to the bar by long-pressing it. The rollout currently excludes the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and South Korea, while the design reaches a small group of 'Plus' subscribers, and 'Dot' expands to all business plan subscribers in the United States.

Simply put: the app has become just like your phone: what you use often gets pinned to the bar, and your personal assistant now has a fixed place that never gets lost among conversations.
