⚖️ The 'HireWatch' Test: AI Models Pick Illegal Interview Questions Under Pressure!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new research evaluation called HireWatch revealed that language AI models may choose illegal job interview questions when used in hiring, especially when pressured by a manager or an internal policy. The researchers built a bank of 200 interview questions, including 50 illegal questions and 150 legal ones, based on US federal laws, regulations, and regulators' guidance, and reviewed by nine licensed US lawyers.

The researchers tested 18 closed and open-weight models across about 100,000 experiments, asking each model to choose 25 questions to prepare a job interview, under graduated pressure conditions of seven degrees. The illegal questions concerned disability, religion, sex, marital status, national origin, genetic information, pregnancy, race, and color — topics US anti-discrimination laws prohibit asking about in interviews.

In calm conditions, the models mostly avoided choosing illegal questions, but the violation rate rose sharply as pressure increased, especially when adding a note from a hiring manager asking to probe the candidate's personal life, or a note from senior management overriding hiring guidance, or an internal policy imposing mandatory evaluation areas. Explicit instructions to comply with the law helped improve results, but did not prevent violations entirely.

The researchers also compared the models' performance with that of HR specialists in the same conditions, finding that pressure also reduces humans' compliance, but the violation rates of several models remained far higher than humans'. The researchers published the code and data publicly so others can verify the results.

Simply put: the model knows the law when it is calm, but forgets it when a manager or internal policy pressures it, and legal responsibility ultimately falls on the employer, not the model.
