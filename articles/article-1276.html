💰 JPMorgan raises its forecast for AI data centre spending — $1.5 trillion in 2027!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

JPMorgan raised its forecasts for data centre spending in a report issued on 23 November, lifting spending growth in 2026 to 105%, an increase of more than $425 billion in a single year — the largest annual increase ever, after the previous record increase was around $190 billion in 2025.

The bank also raised its 2027 forecasts, expecting an increase of more than $700 billion, bringing total data centre spending to more than $830 billion in 2026, then more than $1.5 trillion in 2027.

This spending is led by the big tech companies, whose spending is expected to rise by 85% in 2026, along with the new AI infrastructure operators such as SpaceX, Oracle and CoreWeave, whose spending is expected to jump by 180%.

On capacity, installed capacity is expected to rise from 54 gigawatts in 2025 to around 80 gigawatts in 2026, then around 115 gigawatts in 2027, while capital spending will reach 100% of the companies’ operating cash flow in 2026 and 120% in 2027 — meaning the companies are spending on infrastructure more than they earn from their operations.

Simply put: the race to build AI data centres is speeding up rather than slowing down — companies are betting amounts greater than their income that demand for AI will keep growing.
