💬 ChatGPT Space reaches Plus subscribers! A shared workspace instead of the Library!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The 'Space' feature in ChatGPT has started reaching Plus subscribers around the world, after being limited to the more expensive plans, in an expansion that makes the collaborative workspace available to millions of additional users.

According to release notes issued on November 20, the feature is rolling out gradually to Plus subscribers aged 18 and over, after its launch at the end of September for Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans.

Space lets you build interactive pages and presentations that can be edited with others at the same time, and it replaces the Library section in the sidebar, gathering pages, slides, images, and uploaded files in one place, while Projects remain separate.

Advertisement

There are no public links to pages: opening a shared page requires permission and an account with access to Space — that is, Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans — while free-plan users cannot open shared pages.

But there is an important privacy warning: when Memory is enabled, the assistant may draw on previous conversations when writing or updating a page, and anyone who can view the page may read that information, even if the conversations themselves stay private — so it is advised to review a page's content before sharing it.

Simply put: ChatGPT is turning from a chat box into a shared office... will you use Space for your work and studies?

Will you share your pages with colleagues, or do you prefer your work to stay private?
