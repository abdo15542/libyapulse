🔋 Xiaomi 18 Max.. A Giant Screen and a Battery Over 8,000 mAh!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New leaks suggest Xiaomi is preparing a new phone that may join its flagship line under the name Xiaomi 18 Max — a large-screen phone aimed at people who want a wider display and a bigger battery.

According to the leak, the phone will run on Qualcomm's flagship Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chip, the most powerful chip the company currently offers, meaning performance on par with the most expensive flagship phones.

But the phone's standout feature is its battery, which exceeds 8,000 mAh — a huge capacity compared with the usual batteries of 5,000 to 6,000 mAh, which could mean two full days of use without charging for most users.

Advertisement

These leaks come after the recent launch of the Xiaomi 18 Pro models in China, while the company is also preparing the standard Xiaomi 18 — so the Max looks set to be the third option in the lineup for those who want a bigger screen and longer battery life.

Xiaomi has not confirmed any of these details officially, and the phone is still at the leak stage — the specifications may change before the official announcement.

Simply put: a phone with a giant screen and a battery that lasts two days could end your worry about running out of charge.. Would you rather have a bigger battery or a lighter phone?

If your phone worked for two days without a charger, would you stop carrying your charger with you for good?
