🚁 The Sky Rover V1 drone challenges DJI — 40 minutes of flight for $759!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Sky Rover launched its new flagship drone, the V1, starting at $759 — the same price at which DJI sells its Mini 5 Pro drone — in a direct challenge to its dominance of the small-drone category.

The drone weighs under 249 grams, staying in the lightweight drone category, and flies for up to 40 minutes on a single charge, 25% more than the company's previous X1 drone, which flies for 32 minutes.

It carries a 1/1.28-inch sensor, records 4K video at 60 frames per second and 8K photos, with slow motion at 100 frames per second, and 40 GB of internal storage that removes the need for a memory card.

Advertisement

It features a 360-degree obstacle-sensing system that works even in low light, with smart subject tracking and smart return-path planning, a transmission range of up to 20 kilometres, and a controller with a built-in 5.5-inch screen for those who don't want to use their phone as a screen.

But beware: the advertised flight time is the maximum under ideal conditions — an independent flight test recorded only about 25 minutes per battery, a gap worth keeping in mind before you buy.

Simply put: a small drone with big specs and a competitive price — but the real flight time is less than advertised. Try before you judge.
