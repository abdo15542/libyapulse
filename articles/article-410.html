🚗 'Leapmotor' Builds Its Two Millionth Electric Car — the First Among Chinese Startups!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The two millionth car rolled off the production line of Chinese company "Leapmotor" on November 19, making it the first Chinese electric-car startup to reach this number. The celebrated car was a black, large "D19" sports model — and at the same time the 50,000th car of that same model.

The speed is striking: the company needed just five months to go from 1.5 million cars to two million. It delivered 106,127 cars in October alone, up 51% from last year — the fourth consecutive month exceeding one hundred thousand cars. It exported more than 180,000 cars in the first ten months of the year, about three times its exports in all of 2025, runs more than 1,200 sales and service showrooms in more than 50 markets, and began production of the "B10" model at its Zaragoza plant in Spain in October.

The company says 65% of its cars' cost is components it develops and manufactures itself, giving it a price advantage of about 10%, and that the "D19" is among the four best-selling large sports cars in China this year. The two millionth car will be dedicated to a major support mission for China's national space program. On the same day, the company began pre-orders for the updated "Lafa 5" model, starting at about $15,800, with an 800-volt platform that charges the battery from 30% to 80% in 16 minutes and a range of up to 640 kilometers.
