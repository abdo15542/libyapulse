🎮 Ubisoft Launches a New AI Company — and Game Characters That Remember You!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Gaming company Ubisoft announced the founding of a new subsidiary called Motherbrain, specializing in generative AI inside games, as part of its half-year financial results published on November 19.

The new company brings together the team behind the Teammates experience — the company's first playable generative experience, which appeared in late 2025 — in which the player is accompanied by two AI companions that respond to voice commands in real time and act with two different personalities.

Motherbrain focuses on three things: entirely new gameplay experiences; non-player characters with a memory that remembers their previous encounters with you instead of forgetting you every time; and AI game directors that run vast, complex worlds where events change on their own instead of following a fixed script.

Advertisement

The company says Motherbrain technologies will serve game makers inside Ubisoft and beyond, and that it will collaborate with selected tech companies — but it has not yet announced when these technologies will reach a major game, or whether they will be sold as standalone tools.

The announcement came at a difficult time for the company, which cut 166 jobs at its Montreal and Quebec studios, while its headcount fell by about 1,100 employees over six months, as part of a broad restructuring that began in January with the cancellation of six games.

Simply put: imagine the shopkeeper in a game remembering that you visited him yesterday and greeting you by name, and the world around you living and changing without waiting for you — that is what Motherbrain wants to build.

Would you prefer game characters that remember you, or do you worry the game world might become smarter than the player?
