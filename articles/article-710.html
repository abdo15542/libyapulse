⚡ Reports: Samsung's Exynos 2700 beats the new Snapdragon in performance tests!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new report suggests that Samsung's Exynos 2700 chip — its upcoming flagship phone processor — beat Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chips in internal tests conducted by Samsung itself, covering the central processor, the graphics card and AI tasks. The chip is expected to power the Galaxy S27 series, due to be unveiled at a Galaxy Unpacked event early in 2027.

These reports are backed by a result on the Geekbench benchmarking platform, where a Galaxy S27 Pro phone in its Indian version, model number SM-S957B, was spotted running the S5E9975 chip believed to be the Exynos 2700 — a ten-core chip made with 2-nanometre technology, whose strongest core reaches 4.24 GHz, with 12GB of memory and One UI 9 based on Android 17.

This result matters especially because Samsung has for years relied on Qualcomm chips in many of its flagship phones, while Exynos chips faced criticism over performance and power consumption. If these tests are correct, Samsung may return to relying on its own chip in its most important phones, giving it greater control over cost and performance together.

Simply put: Samsung's own chip may finally beat its strongest rival, but the tests are internal and the company has not confirmed them yet. Do you trust test results before the official launch, or do you wait to try the phone yourself?
