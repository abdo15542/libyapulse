✨ Copilot's big fall update — the "Miko" persona, groups, health and a new mode in Edge!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft revealed on Thursday, November 19, a package of new features for its Copilot assistant as part of the "Fall Update" event, in the biggest development of the assistant since its launch.

The most prominent feature is a new persona called "Miko" that gives the assistant a visual presence, so the robot has a shape and a personality that interacts with you, instead of a rigid text window.

The update also added a "Groups" feature that lets you use the assistant with others, new health features that help you track your health matters, plus a "Copilot mode" in the Edge browser that brings agent capabilities to the browser, so the assistant can carry out multi-step tasks on your behalf inside the web.

Advertisement

An "agent" here means an assistant that doesn't just answer, but acts: it searches, compares, clicks and executes, while you just follow the result.

Simply put: imagine your smart assistant now having a face and a personality, working with your friends, tracking your health, and carrying out your tasks in the browser itself. That is what the fall update brought.
