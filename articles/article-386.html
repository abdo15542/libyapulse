⚖️ A US Agency Freezes the Assets of a $560 Million Fraud Scheme — a Promise of 6% a Month Was a Trap!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US Securities and Exchange Commission announced on November 20 that it obtained an emergency freeze of the assets of "Backward Hat Capital" and its founders, after accusing them of collecting about $560 million from more than 2,000 investors in a pyramid fraud scheme, presented as foreign-exchange trading by an AI algorithm. The emergency measures included a temporary restraining order and expedited discovery to stop what the agency called an ongoing scheme.

The scheme began in July 2022 through a network of promoters who sold stakes in private funds, and investors were promised their money would be converted into crypto and traded to achieve at least 6% a month, with a dashboard showing monthly profits between 6.01% and 7.21%. But the agency says more than $250 million went as payments to earlier investors, while the founders diverted more than $220 million to themselves to buy luxury cars and homes and fund gambling and other businesses. An accountant was also accused of preparing fake financial reports without conducting any real audit.

Authorities moved on every front: the founders were arrested on November 18 in a separate criminal case, an Indiana court froze the assets the same day, and a temporary receiver was appointed on November 20. The Commodity Futures Trading Commission also filed a parallel case saying the amount exceeded $550 million from at least 2,100 participants, while federal prosecutors estimate the total at more than $600 million.
