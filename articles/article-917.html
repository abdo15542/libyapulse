💻 Cognition Is Negotiating New Funding Worth $75 Billion! The Maker of Devin Jumps 56% in 10 Weeks!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Cognition, the maker of the coding agent Devin, is in talks to raise $2 billion or more at a $75 billion valuation, according to Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter, and nothing has been signed yet and the terms could change.

If the deal closes at that price, the company's value will have jumped by $27 billion in just about ten weeks, from $48 billion in its September round — an increase of about 56%.

In September, the company raised more than $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation, and in May it raised more than $1 billion at a $26 billion valuation, bringing its value close to three times higher in under six months, while its annualized revenue reached about $900 million in September.

Most of the interest comes from existing investors, including Avenir, Accel, Lightspeed, Lux Capital, and Bessemer, and continued growth depends on major customers the company named as Devin users, including Nvidia, GE Aerospace, Citi, and Mercedes-Benz.

The talks come after the company launched "Devin Bots", manager agents that lead teams of automated programmers for weeks, in a heated race for coding agents between Anthropic, OpenAI, and Cognition.

Simply put: an automated coding company worth $75 billion without selling a traditional product... do coding agents deserve all this?
