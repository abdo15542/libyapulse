📟 Nothing sets December 8 for its first screen-free AI device!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

British company Nothing has set December 8 as the date to reveal its first AI-powered device, in an official post on the X platform, accompanied by a close-up teaser image of a round metal component and the words 'Be present... it is recorded'.

The company has not yet revealed the device's name, price, or specs, but CEO Carl Pei has previously confirmed it is a device built for work, with no new screen, and that it cannot be just an app on the phone. Earlier leaks showed a small palm-sized device, with a dark rectangular body, a large physical button marked 'Talk', a small round control below it, and two colored indicator lights, suggesting it is a smart voice recorder that captures conversations and ideas, organizes them, and turns them into notes, possibly with live translation while speaking. But all these uses remain unconfirmed expectations, and the company has not confirmed them officially.

The device is part of the company's plan to build an AI platform combining hardware and software, after raising $200 million in a 2025 funding round for this purpose. It will be the first time the company enters an entirely new category outside phones and headphones, at a time when previous similar AI devices stumbled because they offered no clear benefit in daily life.

Simply put: imagine a small device in your pocket that hears your meeting and writes down the most important parts without you looking at a screen; that is what Nothing promises — but the real verdict will come on December 8, when it reveals all the details.
