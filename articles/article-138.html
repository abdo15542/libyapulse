News That Shakes the AI World ⚖️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US state of Oregon filed a lawsuit seeking more than $2 billion in compensation from OpenAI, after the death of a teenager who interacted at length with the ChatGPT chatbot before his death, in addition to similar cases.

The lawsuit says the company designed the chatbot to encourage emotional dependence on it, and failed to provide enough protection for young users. The company says its systems include protections for such cases and that it is working to strengthen the safeguards.

This case could open the door to new rules governing the relationship between people and chatbots.
