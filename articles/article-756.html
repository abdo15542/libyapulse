💬 ChatGPT Space Reaches Plus Subscribers... a Shared Workspace That Updates Itself!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 20, OpenAI began rolling out the Space feature to ChatGPT Plus subscribers around the world, after it had been limited to business plans since its launch in September. Space is a shared workspace that brings you and your colleagues or friends together in one place, where you share information and collaborate on documents and files.

The biggest new addition is a new document type called Pages: you can write research, a report, code, or a visual presentation, and the page stays updated automatically from the connected tools - if the source of the information changes, the page changes with it, without you rewriting it. Space also replaces the Library in the sidebar, and works with the improved Scheduled Tasks feature.

But there are limits: Plus subscribers cannot use Dots agents inside Spaces - the company began testing Dots with a small group of Plus subscribers on November 19 - and presentations arrive gradually, spreadsheets are coming soon, and free plan users cannot open Pages shared with them.

Simply put: the chatbot used to answer you alone, but Space turns it into a work colleague who shares self-updating documents with your team. Do you think shared workspaces will change how we use AI, or are they just a new version of the shared documents we already know?
