🔊 Google is retiring its voice assistant on smart speakers next year!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google has announced major changes in smart-home software: starting next year it will close the 'Nest' app in favor of the 'Google Home' app, and will stop 'Google Assistant' on its smart home devices, to be replaced by 'Gemini for Home'.

The Nest app will close on January 25, 2027, and after that date Nest products, including thermostats, cameras, and 'Nest Protect' alarms, will be managed from the Google Home app instead. Whoever sets up the Google Home app will have Google move their devices to it automatically. Google had already begun replacing the assistant with 'Gemini' on phones and wearables last September, and smart speakers and smart displays remained the last stronghold of the old assistant, until its turn came now. 'Gemini for Home' will work on Google speakers and displays issued since 2016, with advanced conversation features for paid-plan subscribers.

Simply put: imagine the voice assistant you got used to saying 'Hey Google' to for ten years retiring, and a smarter assistant taking its place that understands your natural speech and follows the conversation with you; the devices themselves stay, but the voice that answers you will change.

Do you use a smart speaker at home, and do you trust the new replacement will do the old assistant's job?
