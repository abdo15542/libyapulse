🎓 540 Google Employees Demand Search Without AI for Schools!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

More than 540 Google employees, through the Alphabet Workers Union, signed a petition called “Let Students Search,” addressed to CEO Sundar Pichai and other officials, demanding that schools be able to use Search without AI features. Google replied that “Search and AI are deeply integrated,” and that banning AI would make Search unavailable, while school districts in New York, Los Angeles, and Chicago are considering switching search engines, and Senator Ed Markey gave the company until November 30, 2026 to answer his questions, at a time when the company faces a lawsuit involving a 13-year-old child and the Gemini model.

Simply put: the employees want a button that turns AI off from Search in schools; and Google says the two have become one thing that cannot be separated.
