📱 'Honor Magic 9 Pro Max' Reaches Malaysia on November 24 with Two 200MP Cameras from 'Arri'!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Honor" in Malaysia has confirmed the launch date of its flagship phone, the "Magic 9 Pro Max", on November 24, 2026, in a livestream on its official page — the only phone from the "Magic 9" series to reach the Malaysian market. The local price has not yet been announced and will be revealed on launch day.

The phone features a cinema imaging system developed by "Honor" in collaboration with "Arri", famous for cinema cameras: two 200MP cameras — a main camera and a 3.5x optical telephoto — with the "Log C3" curve and 87 ready-made cinema looks from "Arri", and a dedicated "H1" imaging chip that extends dynamic range to 14 stops. It also has a "Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6" processor and a 6.8-inch screen at 120Hz with brightness reaching 10,000 nits.

The phone carries a huge silicon-carbon battery of 8,000mAh in the Hong Kong version, with 100W wired and 80W wireless charging, and high water and dust resistance ratings. The phone was launched in China in September and then in Hong Kong, where its price starts at about 10,999 Hong Kong dollars.

Simply put: a phone carrying professional cinema tools in your pocket — two 200MP cameras with the "Arri" colors you see in movies, without a huge camera on your shoulder.
