🎮 Nioh 3 Comes to Xbox and Switch 2 at Last! A Complete Edition With All Expansions in January 2027

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nioh 3 Complete Edition is coming to Xbox Series X/S and Nintendo Switch 2 on 28 January 2027.

This is the first time the Nioh series has ever come to Xbox consoles, after being exclusive to other platforms.

The Complete Edition includes the base game together with both of its expansions, so there is no need to buy any extra content later.

Advertisement

The Xbox version supports Play Anywhere, meaning it includes a free PC version along with the console version.

The second expansion, Bloody Insurrection, releases on 17 December 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Steam, and will be included in the Complete Edition.

The game is developed by Team Ninja and published by Koei Tecmo, and the player takes the role of the shogun Takichiyo in a difficult Souls-like action role-playing game.

Nioh 3 scored 86 on Metacritic, a strong score for a game of this type.

The announcement came today through an official press release from Team Ninja.

Simply put: a game that was locked to one platform is finally opening its doors to two new consoles, in a complete edition that includes everything; and Xbox and Switch 2 players can now try one of the toughest role-playing games.

Will you play Nioh 3 on Xbox, or wait for the Switch 2 version?
