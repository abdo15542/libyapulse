🦬 The Bison-1 and Groundhog-1 Models — AI That Decides Instead of Writing!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Baseten launched on November 20 two new open-weight decision models, Bison-1 and Groundhog-1, built on Google's Gemma 4 model, for teams that want an American base model.

Decision models are different from the usual chatbots: they do not write long paragraphs, but evaluate the options presented to them and return probabilities and scores, such as routing a support ticket to the right department, deciding whether content deserves an alert, or approving an invoice.

Bison-1 topped the Decision Index issued by Artificial Analysis with a score of 76, ahead of 19 other models, and achieved the best calibration in the index, meaning its stated confidence is closest to the actual accuracy of its answers.

The two models offer a context window of 128,000 tokens, eight times the company's previous models, and accept text and images together, allowing full policies or long conversation logs to be included without shortening.

As for prices, they are $0.10 per million tokens for the 31-billion-parameter Bison-1, and $0.035 per million tokens for the 26-billion-parameter Groundhog-1. The two models are available today through the company's APIs, and their weights are open on the Hugging Face platform.

Simply put: not every task needs a paragraph; sometimes you need a quick decision and a confidence score, and these two models do that with open weights and a far wider context than their predecessors.

Would you prefer a model that decides and gives you a probability instead of a long paragraph, or do you always want the full explanation?
