🎮 GTA 6 Has an Infinite Money Glitch! And Players Themselves Are Hesitating to Use It!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Just one day after GTA 6 launched, players discovered a glitch that grants unlimited in-game money, in a red warehouse northeast of Hamlet, in South Vice-Dale county.

The trick is that the loot inside the warehouse respawns after saving the game and reloading it, while the bags already collected stay in the car, so the player sells them to a fence and repeats the process all over again.

Reports say each round of this process earns between $8,000 and $11,000 in-game — a large sum at the start of the story.

Advertisement

But the surprise is that many players refuse to use the glitch at all, because the start of the game is built on the feeling of poverty: Jason has almost no money, and Lucia is trying to earn a living for herself and her family — money is what drives the story and the early missions.

There is also a practical reason: some players who collected a lot of money early on later discovered that part of that money becomes restricted in later stages of the story, so the effort goes to waste.

As for the legitimate alternatives, like hunting for treasure and selling cars, they are slower but safer and more stable, and the glitch is expected to be fixed in the first major update, although the company has not officially announced that yet.

Simply put: the glitch exists and is easy, but it could spoil the fun of the story — do you choose quick money or the experience as the developers designed it?

Would you use a glitch like this if you found one in your favorite game, or would you rather live the full experience without shortcuts?
