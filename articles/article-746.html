🛒 Amazon Is Building a Map of Every Product Sold Online... Even Ones It Doesn't Sell!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A press report has revealed an internal Amazon document describing a project called the 'Global Product Map,' aiming to collect data on nearly every product sold online, including products Amazon does not sell at all, in one of the company's broadest plans to expand its influence in shopping.

The document says the project will collect product data from two sources: companies and brands that send their data continuously - Coca-Cola and Bayer have already joined, along with a trial with Procter & Gamble - and automated scanning of rival store websites to collect their product listings. The goal is for shoppers and smart shopping agents to stay inside Amazon instead of going to search engines and review sites, and for its database to become a reference that rival shopping agents themselves rely on, strengthening its Alexa Shopping assistant.

The document carries an ambitious launch date of October 1, 2027, but it is not a confirmed plan: the company responded that old early-stage documents often give an incomplete picture, and that the existence of a brainstorming draft does not necessarily mean it reflects its current plans, so the project could be changed or cancelled.

Simply put: whoever owns the map of all products owns the buying decision. Do you think a single database of this size will make shopping easier, or does it give one company more influence than it should have?
