📱 Samsung warns you during the call: a scammer's voice imitating your relative!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Samsung has announced a new feature on its phones called 'voice phishing suspicion alert', as part of the 'One UI 8' update, to fight a new kind of fraud that relies on AI. Scammers can now imitate the voice of someone you know — a relative, a manager, or a bank employee — then call you and ask for money or sensitive information in a voice that sounds completely real.

The new feature analyzes the call as it happens, and if it detects signs that the voice was generated by AI or that the call is suspicious, it shows you an alert on the screen before you finish the conversation or carry out what is asked of you. It is currently available in South Korea only, and the company has not yet announced when it will reach other countries.

Simply put: imagine someone calling you in your brother's exact voice asking you to urgently transfer money; the new feature is like a guard standing beside you during the call, whispering: 'Careful — this voice may be fake'.

Do you trust a phone alert alone to protect you, or will you verify yourself before transferring any money?
