🚗 NIO Unveils 'World Model 3.0'… Self-Driving With 40% Fewer Interventions, Rollout Starts in December!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Chinese electric car maker NIO has unveiled its new smart driving system 'World Model 3.0', at its 'NIO IN' tech event in Guangzhou on November 25, with a plan to start rolling it out in December.

The system will reach first the users of the Banyan, Cedar, Cedar S, and Coconut Plus systems, after registration and regulatory approvals are completed, and later reach Onvo models with the Coconut and Coconut Plus systems.

The company says the new model cuts driver interventions by 40% compared with its predecessor, and raises users' sense of reassurance by about 60%, thanks to four-dimensional spatiotemporal compression that cuts the computing needed for attention by 36 times.

The company also claims a 78% cut in chassis control response time, and a 54% cut in overall vehicle control time, with five times higher training efficiency, through two reinforcement learning loops: one in observation space and another in latent representation space.

The company also unveiled its in-car assistant 'NOMI Intelligence 4.0', which brings together its models, AI agents, and a comprehensive safety framework, and announced that it has deployed more than 7,500 AI agents in its operations, which have carried out more than 2.9 million tasks so far.

Simply put: a car that understands the road by itself and needs your intervention far less; would you ride in a car that drives itself while you sit without a steering wheel?
