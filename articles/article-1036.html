🔴 Two breakdowns on Mars at once! "Perseverance" loses its radio and "Odyssey" is about to run out of fuel!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The "NASA" agency announced on November 19 that two of its spacecraft at Mars stopped scientific work at the same time because of two separate faults.

The rover "Perseverance"'s problem began on October 30, when its main radio stopped sending its usual periodic signals, so the rover automatically entered safe mode; engineers then discovered that the backup radio was working at only half its expected power.

The rover stopped using both radios and now communicates with Earth directly through its high-gain antenna — a much slower connection — so its scientific instruments and new photo-taking have temporarily stopped.

As for the orbiter "Odyssey", which has circled Mars since 2001, it entered safe mode between November 4 and 6 after one of its steering wheels stopped spinning; it lost another wheel in 2013, and it needs three working wheels to orient itself.

Although the wheel moved when commanded, the remaining fuel is enough for only a month or two if the spacecraft has to steer with its thrusters, and "NASA" hopes both spacecraft will return to scientific work before the end of November.

Simply put: a rover loses its voice and an orbiter loses its balance in the same week... is the elderly "Odyssey" nearing the end of its journey after a quarter of a century?

After 25 years in orbit, is it time for "Odyssey" to retire, or does it deserve one last rescue attempt?
