Instagram finally lets you edit a Story after posting it! ✨

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Instagram announced today that you can now edit a Story after posting it without losing views, likes, or replies. You have a 15-minute window to change the text, music, stickers, and drawings after posting: open the published Story, tap the three-dot menu, then "Edit Story". Edited Stories display an "Edited" label.

Until now, a typo in a Story meant deleting it and reposting — and losing all the views and likes. Now you have a quarter of an hour to fix it and keep everything. But note: after the fifteen minutes are up, the Story locks, and the feature is for Stories only, not Reels or posts. Instagram also added new stickers with organized fonts, colors, and shapes, an updated multi-photo selection, and new backgrounds.

How many times have you deleted a Story because of a typo? 😅
