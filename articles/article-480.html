📉 Advertisers Cut Their Spending on Google Ads — AI Pushes Ads Down!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Long-time advertisers have begun cutting their spending on Google Search ads, after AI summaries came to occupy the top of search results and push traditional ads down or hide them entirely, as costs rise.

A marketer in North Carolina who has managed Google ads for small businesses for more than ten years said he moved spending away from Google over the past six months, and that the spending of one of his clients, a trophy seller, fell 75% from $2,000 a month to $500 over about a year, moving the budget to platforms such as Topi, Bing, Reddit, and Nextdoor.

As for Max Anderson, CEO of OnlyFinder, he said his ads sometimes do not appear at all or appear below the AI summaries, and that his costs tripled or quadrupled over three years, so he cut his spending to about half and is still cutting it as costs keep rising.

Advertisement

Google, for its part, says AI features increase the number of searches, including commercial searches, and that its advertising experiments showed promising results, but it does not guarantee advertisers a specific place inside the AI summaries, making their ads' appearance unpredictable.

The company faces a deeper dilemma: AI Mode ads generate less revenue per search than traditional search, raising questions about Google's ability to profit from AI search as efficiently as the old search, even though its CEO says the priority is the best search experience and then profit.

Simply put: Google changed the shape of search results so the smart answer is at the top and the ad at the bottom, so advertisers began paying less or moving to other platforms, and the company itself earns from smart search less than it used to earn from traditional search.

Have you noticed that search ads appear less often in Google's results? And do you click on the smart summary or skip it to the links?
