🎮 "GTA 6" Crashes on "PlayStation 5" and Halts Players' Progress — and "Rockstar" Responds with an Update!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Several players of "Grand Theft Auto 6" on "PlayStation 5" have complained about repeated game crashes with error code CE-108255-1, a problem that stops some of them from progressing through the story despite every attempted fix.

Players on online forums reported that the game crashes at the same point again and again — right after the intro, when the first autosave is created, or during an early mission — leaving them unable to get past that spot no matter how hard they try.

Those affected tried reinstalling the game, clearing the console's cache and rebuilding its database, and even factory-resetting the entire console, without any of these steps solving the problem.

Advertisement

On Monday, "Rockstar" released a new update for the game on "PlayStation 5" and "Xbox Series", fixing crash and save issues, along with a problem with weather changes stopping, phone calls not triggering as they should, being unable to lose a wanted level, and other mission issues.

But later reports said the same error code still appears for some players even after the first update, meaning the problem may need a deeper fix that has not arrived yet.

The game launched on 19 November and earned $3.79 billion on its first day, becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history and the highest-rated game ever, with an average score of 99.

Simply put: the biggest game in history crashed for some players right at the start — they cannot play, the usual fixes do not help, and the first update did not fully end the problem.
