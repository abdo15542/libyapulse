🎙️ OpenAI Retires ChatGPT's Two Old Voices on December 11… With One New Voice as the Replacement!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI announced it will discontinue ChatGPT's two old voice modes, "Advanced Voice" and "Standard Voice," on December 11, moving everyone still using them to the new "Live" voice mode, known internally as "GPT Live."

The company began notifying affected users on November 10, giving them about a full month before the switch. Accounts will move to the new voice automatically when the date arrives, while conversations held with the old voices will remain available in the chat history.

There are two exceptions that will continue after the date: live video and screen sharing will stay on "Advanced Voice" until the new voice supports them, and custom GPTs will keep Advanced Voice until a separate shutdown date of their own.

Advertisement

This is not the first attempt to retire "Standard Voice": the company announced a similar move in 2025, then backed down after users protested, saying the newer options felt less natural or broke workflows they had built on the simple old voice. This time, the company describes the change as consolidating around a more capable voice with less delay.

Similar reactions appeared on the company's forums this time too, with some users warning that workflows relying specifically on Standard Voice's behavior will break once it disappears. The weeks after the date will show whether the company stands firm this time or is forced into a new exception.

Simply put: ChatGPT's two old voices leave in December… and everyone moves to one voice… Do you prefer one strong voice or several simpler choices?
