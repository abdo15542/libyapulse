📱 Leak: the OnePlus 17 may come with a 240Hz screen — the fastest in a smartphone!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new leak from well-known leaker Digital Chat Station on the Weibo platform revealed details of the screen of a phone expected to launch next year, which followers believe is the upcoming OnePlus 17.

According to the leak, the phone will carry a 6.89-inch 2K display, with a new panel from BOE using LIPO technology for ultra-thin bezels, and very narrow bezels on all four sides.

The screen, according to the leak, supports the BT.2020 colour range and a dedicated display chip, and its refresh rate could reach 240Hz — an unprecedented figure in a smartphone, making motion and scrolling smoother.

Advertisement

OnePlus has not confirmed any of these details yet, so they remain leaks that could change before launch — noting that the OnePlus 16 launched in China on October 12 starting at 5,999 yuan.

Simply put: a faster, sharper screen could be the OnePlus 17's winning card next year — but remember, a leak is not an official announcement.
