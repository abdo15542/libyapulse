📱 Oppo Reno 17 arrives with an 8,000 mAh battery and four 50MP cameras!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Oppo has unveiled the Reno 17 series in China, with two phones going on sale on November 20, featuring huge batteries, powerful cameras and prices starting from about $533.

The standard Reno 17 carries an 8,000 mAh battery, the largest in the series' history, with 80W fast charging, a 6.56-inch OLED screen at 120Hz, and a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chip. The cameras are four, all 50MP: a main camera, an ultra-wide, a 3.5x optical zoom, and a front camera for selfies, with optical stabilisation in both main cameras.

The stronger Reno 17 Pro is smaller, with a 6.32-inch screen, but it carries a 200MP main camera, a Dimensity 8550 Super chip, and a 7,250 mAh battery. Its price starts from about $591. Both phones have a metal frame and top-level water and dust resistance, including withstanding high-pressure hot water.

The company also unveiled a new tablet and a new smartwatch at the same event, but it has not yet announced when the two phones will arrive outside China.

Simply put: a battery that lasts two days and four equal cameras in a mid-priced phone. Has a huge battery become more important than chip power when choosing your next phone?
