🌙 An 8 Million Euro Contract: Danish Satellites Explore the Moon's Riches for Blue Origin!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 19, Danish satellite company GomSpace won an 8 million euro contract for the second phase of a lunar mission, in which it will continue developing two spacecraft orbiting the Moon to explore its resources: water ice, helium-3, rare earth elements, and precious metals, in preparation for future lunar exploration.

The company did not officially name its client, describing it only as a 'leading space company in North America', but its chief executive had previously confirmed that the mission is for Blue Origin, and that the two spacecraft are intended for the Oasis-1 mission, the first mission in the Oasis project that the company revealed in September 2025 in cooperation with Luxembourg.

The first phase of the contract, worth 2.9 million euros in January 2026, had covered the initial design, bringing the total contract now to 10.9 million euros, with delivery expected in late 2027, and the two spacecraft build on the company's experience in the European Space Agency's Hera mission.

Simply put: the Moon is no longer a destination for science only, but a potential mine; so will mining the Moon's riches become a new race between companies?
