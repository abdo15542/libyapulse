🛒 AI agents buy from the Amazon cloud store — and the bill goes on your account!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Amazon Web Services announced that AI agents can now charge their purchases from its online store to users' cloud accounts.

An "agent" here is an AI assistant that doesn't just answer questions, but carries out tasks on your behalf: it searches, compares and buys, within limits you set.

The "AWS Marketplace" is an online market for software and cloud services, where companies buy ready-made tools instead of building them themselves.

Advertisement

What's new is that the agent working for you can now complete the purchase and charge its cost directly to your cloud account, without you paying with a separate card each time.

This opens the door to assistants that complete your shopping and setup tasks entirely, but it also raises an important question: who watches the agent's spending, and what are the limits of what it is allowed to buy?

Simply put: imagine sending your assistant to the market with your wallet, asking him to buy what you need. Convenient — but you want to know exactly what he bought and for how much.

Do you trust an AI agent to buy on your behalf, or do you prefer to be the one pressing the buy button?
