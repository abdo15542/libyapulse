Science & Innovation

England's exams regulator has announced plans to change how some school subjects are assessed, after it became hard to tell who really wrote the coursework: the student or a chatbot. The proposed changes would first cover history and English at secondary level, where grades depend on long written assignments done without supervision. A survey of teachers found that 70% believe unsupervised coursework should not count towards the final grade, and 83% support greater supervision. And the warning is clear: anyone who uses AI to cheat could lose their entire qualification. Do you think in-person exams are the only way to guarantee fair assessment? 🎓
