💰 A Chinese Company Only Months Old Is Worth 5 Billion Dollars... and the Reason Is AI 'Agents'!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Chinese company Pragmatic Labs has closed a new funding round at a valuation of 5 billion dollars, 2.5 times its valuation last August, even though it has not announced any product yet, and even though it is only a few months old.

The company was founded in Shanghai by Lin Junyang, the former head of AI at Alibaba and the leader of the development of the famous Qwen family of models, after he left the company in March 2026. His new company focuses on building a new generation of 'agents' that work in the digital world and the physical world together: general agents for knowledge work and operations, and embodied systems that learn, act, and solve long tasks in the real world.

The new round raised tens of millions of dollars, with new investors joining, among them Hi-Tech Capital and CDH, and carmaker Geely, alongside earlier investors including Tencent. The first round in August had raised hundreds of millions at a valuation of two billion dollars.

The valuation reflects investors' confidence in the race for smart agents, even before the company has proven itself with a single product. Observers believe its real value will be measured when it announces its first model or agent that actually works.

Simply put: 5 billion dollars for a company with no product, only because its founder built Qwen before; so do you buy a promise or a product when you invest in AI?
