🍎 The Smart Siri Is Delayed Until Late 2027! And Apple Is Laying Off the Old Version's Staff!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Journalist Mark Gurman of Bloomberg revealed in his weekly newsletter that Apple recently laid off employees on the Siri team, and that the layoffs hit those working on the assistant's old infrastructure, while those working on the new AI systems were spared.

Gurman does not expect Apple's advanced AI agent — capable of browsing the web, using apps, writing code, and carrying out tasks on the user's behalf — to arrive before late 2027. In the near term, Siri's ability to carry out multi-step commands inside Apple's own apps arrives next December, then inside third-party apps during 2027.

Gurman blames the delay on Apple's internal development calendar, which locks in the features of each major release about a year before it ships, so the company cannot respond quickly to rivals' breakthroughs, as rivals update their models and services continuously. He also criticized Siri's slow responses, weak integration with third-party services, and declining dictation quality.

Gurman warned that rivals' agents could become hard to give up as users' preferences and connected accounts accumulate with them, even though Apple's reputation for privacy and its deep access to its own system could help it compete.

Simply put: while rival assistants carry out whole tasks today, Siri is still waiting its turn until the end of 2027… because Apple's roadmap is drawn a full year before it is carried out.
