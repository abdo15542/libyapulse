🛡️ The US intelligence chief takes on three jobs! 'Clayton' becomes interim cybersecurity director!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

It was revealed on November 23 that the US Director of National Intelligence, Jay Clayton, will also take on the role of Acting National Cyber Director, bringing together three sensitive roles at once: intelligence, coordinating the AI file, and cybersecurity.

Clayton will remain in the interim post until the Senate confirms the appointment of Kirsten Davies, the Defense Department's Chief Information Officer, as permanent director of the Office of the National Cyber Director, and Davies will meanwhile work as a senior adviser in the same office.

The White House had announced on November 20 the nomination of Davies for the permanent post, succeeding Sean Cairncross, after strong support for her persuaded the President to nominate her for the permanent post instead of keeping her as acting director.

Clayton took over as Director of National Intelligence in August 2026, then was later appointed as the government's AI coordinator, a role that had him working closely with the cyber office, the National Security Council and the National Security Agency, paving the way for him to take on the third role.

Clayton said he looks forward to working with the office's team to ensure a smooth transition for Davies to her post once the Senate confirms her, signalling that the arrangement is temporary by nature and not a permanent merger of the posts.

The appointment comes after a series of widespread technical outages affecting companies and government services, with Clayton appearing on television screens on November 22 warning of more outages during the upgrade of legacy systems, and that AI gives malicious actors stronger tools.

Simply put: one man runs intelligence, AI and cybersecurity together... is this a concentration of power or a burden greater than one person's capacity?
