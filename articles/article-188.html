📖 vivo Develops Its Foldable X Fold 7 with 2nm Chips!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new leak reported that vivo is developing its next-generation foldable phone, which may be called the “X Fold 7.” The company is considering two leading chips from Qualcomm and MediaTek, both built on 2-nanometer technology, and the final choice may depend on their price. The company has not revealed any official details about the phone yet.

Simply put: a foldable phone opens up to become a small tablet; and a 2nm chip means stronger performance and a battery that lasts longer.
