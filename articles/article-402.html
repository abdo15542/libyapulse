💳 AI Agents Manage Your Money — and 58% of People Don't Trust Them!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

AI agents have moved from answering financial questions to executing transactions themselves: they pay bills, manage portfolios, and send money without human approval at every step. A survey conducted in November 2026 of two thousand adults in Britain revealed that 58% of them used AI tools like "ChatGPT" and "Claude" for financial tasks over the past twelve months, and that 56% used an AI assistant inside their bank's app — but only 22% are ready to hand daily money management to an AI agent, while 58% said they are not ready.

The distinction is subtle: more than half of participants accept an agent flagging a suspicious transaction or paying a pre-approved recurring bill, but their comfort collapses when it comes to sending money to another person, opening an account, or applying for a loan. In the crypto world, autonomous agents executed more than 138 million transactions via the "x402" payment protocol from "Coinbase", including about 75 million payments worth $24 million in July 2026 alone, and 87% of them were worth ten cents or less.

But the danger is real: an ordinary invoice could contain hidden instructions ordering the agent to change the beneficiary's bank account and bypass verification — known as indirect prompt injection. Spending limits users set also usually live inside the instruction text itself, not as a technical constraint in the payment system, and crypto transactions cannot be reversed once executed.

Simply put: people use AI for financial advice, but they don't want it as a guardian of their money yet — because a single mistake could cost you your entire account, with no undo button.

Would you hand an AI agent the keys to your bank account?
