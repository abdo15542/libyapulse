🧠 Beijing Brain-Computer Interface Conference: 1,175 Companies Worldwide... and the Wireless Beinao 1 Chip Leads!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The 2026 Brain-Computer Interface Conference concluded in Beijing after four days, with more than 400 teams and around a thousand participants from 23 provinces and cities taking part, alongside an exhibition of around a hundred organizations showing the latest brain-signal reading devices and their applications.

At the main forum on the morning of November 21, a new report on the field's development was released, saying the number of brain-computer interface companies worldwide has risen from more than 800 to 1,175, and that more than 190 Chinese companies - around 77% of China's companies in the field - are concentrated in three major regions around Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou.

Topping the list of the 15 most notable achievements in the field was the Chinese device Beinao 1, a fully wireless implant system with up to 128 channels, using ultra-thin flexible electrodes placed on the surface of the brain without penetrating it. It was announced that more than 40 human implant operations have been completed with it, along with around 200,000 hours of safe operation.

Simply put: reading the brain is moving from rare laboratories to an industry with more than a thousand companies. Do you think these devices will one day become an ordinary way to restore movement and communication, or is the road still long?
