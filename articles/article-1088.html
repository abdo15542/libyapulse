🤖 The OpenAI Phone Is Getting Closer.. Foxconn to Handle Assembly, Possible Launch in Late 2027!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A Chinese report on 19 November said Foxconn, through its phone-assembly arm, has won the order to assemble OpenAI's long-awaited phone — a step suggesting the project has moved from idea to real execution.

Another Chinese report added that the launch could come in late 2027 or in 2028, while analyst Ming-Chi Kuo expects mass production to begin in the first half of 2027. Neither company has confirmed any of this information officially.

Reports suggest the phone will run on a custom MediaTek Dimensity 9600 chip built on 2-nanometer technology, with two neural processing units instead of one: one that constantly monitors the camera, audio, and location, and another that handles thinking and decision-making — plus LPDDR6 memory and UFS 5.0 storage, with shipments of around 30 million units expected in 2027 and 2028.

Advertisement

The core idea is that the phone is designed around the AI assistant, not apps: instead of opening an app for every task, the phone watches what is around you and carries out tasks itself — something that requires full control over both the hardware and the operating system.

But the road is not easy: the lawsuit Apple filed against OpenAI back in July, in which Apple won permission for accelerated disclosure of evidence in October, could disrupt the timeline if a court order lands mid-program. The operating system is also still a mystery, because an Android version without Google services means no app store and no essential apps.

Simply put: a phone with no traditional apps, where the AI assistant is the interface itself.. Would you trust a phone that watches everything around you to serve you?

If your AI assistant could book a table and send a message without you opening any app, would you give up the app screen for good?
