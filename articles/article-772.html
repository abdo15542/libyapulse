📱 The Next iPad Pro Could Skip the M6 Chip and Jump Straight to the M7!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A well-known Chinese leaker, known for his leaks about Apple chips, claims the next iPad Pro will run on the M7 chip instead of the recently released M6 chip. The claim came in a reply to a discussion about the difficulty of cooling fanless devices, where the discussion's author argued that these devices cannot sustain the processor's top speed, and that the M6 version of the tablet would run slower than the computer version.

The leaker added that the M7 version in the tablet would be slightly weaker than its version in Mac computers, perhaps because of fewer cores or a lower speed, without offering any evidence to support his words. Earlier reports said Apple plans to update the iPad Pro in spring 2027, and that the base M7 chip could arrive in the first half of the same year, without specifying which chip the tablet would carry.

If the leak is true, it would not be the first time Apple skipped a whole chip generation in the iPad Pro, as it moved from the M2 straight to the M4 in 2024. But for now the news remains just an unconfirmed leak, and Apple's plans could change before launch.

Simply put: a leak says the new iPad Pro could come with the M7 chip directly, without passing through the M6; would you rather wait for the stronger chip, or is the M6 enough for you?
