📝 111 Students Caught with Smart Devices in Korea's Biggest Exam — and AI Is at the Heart of the Scandal!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

South Korea's Ministry of Education announced that 111 candidates were caught with unauthorized electronic devices during the national university entrance exam, known as the Suneung, taken by about half a million students on Thursday. The ministry had tightened procedures this year after a wave of AI-assisted cheating scandals.

Fifty-four of the violators were exposed through emergency alert messages sent to phones during the exam, which rang even on silent phones and revealed those who had not handed in their phones, while 52 others were caught through inspections and Wi-Fi signal detection, and five were caught red-handed using devices during the exam — including a student with smart glasses and another with a miniature camera and wireless earphones. The capital, Seoul, recorded the largest number of violations with 38 cases, followed by Gyeonggi Province with 20 cases.

The crackdown came after a 34-year-old man admitted to cheating on a tax accountant exam with a miniature camera, a phone, and AI, answering 159 of 160 questions correctly. The Korean president called for a comprehensive review of the exam system, and the ministry is considering extending the ban on retaking the exam from one year to three years, while some have called for a ten-year ban. Exam results will be announced on December 11, after penalties are decided.
