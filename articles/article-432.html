🛡️ The 'NextQuantum OS 1.1' Update Gives You a Privacy Dashboard — and Monitors Every Connection!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Indian brand "Ai+" has announced the rollout of the "NextQuantum OS 1.1" update for its phones, with a full focus on privacy, security, and transparency in how apps access device resources and user data.

The update's highlight is the "NextPrivacy" panel, which gathers app permissions in a single interface, plus alerts for background app activity and network diagnostic tools, so you know when an app works without your knowledge and what it's doing.

The company says the system's security framework analyzes source rules across more than 1,000 connections and 172 unique destination addresses, in an attempt to detect suspicious connections before they leak your data.

The update is now available for the brand's phones — a local Indian brand betting on privacy as a competitive advantage against the giants.

Simply put: instead of wondering what your apps do in the background, the update shows you everything in one panel and alerts you the moment any strange activity happens.
