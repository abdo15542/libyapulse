🗄️ Sirius: The DuckDB Database Runs on the Graphics Card! Queries 5 Times Faster!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nvidia, in cooperation with DuckDB Labs, has launched the Sirius extension for the DuckDB database, so analytical queries now run on graphics cards without changing a single line of the query language or the database schema.

Installation is surprisingly simple: just two commands in the query language, "INSTALL sirius" then "LOAD sirius", after which DuckDB analyzes and optimizes the query, while Sirius executes the plan on the card.

In tests on data up to 10 terabytes, queries were between 3.25 and 5.50 times faster than the best CPU setup, a one-terabyte query dropped from 347 seconds to 63 seconds, and computing cost fell by about 79% at the largest size.

Advertisement

The extension needs DuckDB 2.0 and an Nvidia card with a compatible driver, while unsupported queries fall back automatically to the CPU engine without failing.

The extension is open source, developed by the University of Wisconsin-Madison with Nvidia, and its repository published benchmarking tools that let any team re-run the tests on its own hardware before adopting it.

Simply put: the same database, the same queries — but the engine is now a graphics card, so results come faster and cost less.

Would you try running your databases on a graphics card?
