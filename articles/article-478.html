💾 Nvidia Fixes a Driver Bug That Was Writing 30 GB Per Hour to the Disk!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Nvidia released an urgent hotfix driver numbered 621.35 to fix a bug in GeForce card drivers that made the computer write data nonstop to the hard disk even while idle.

The bug appeared in drivers 621.14 and 621.24, where a background process belonging to Nvidia's service kept rewriting a log file called NvLog over and over, and some users recorded up to 30 GB of unnecessary writes in a single hour, while others observed about 5.2 GB per hour on an RTX 4090 card.

The new fix carries just one fix: stopping the excessive writes to the log file, and it is built on the latest official driver, and is available for Windows 10 and Windows 11. The company says hotfix drivers undergo brief testing and are optional, and that the fix will be merged into the next certified official driver.

Advertisement

Nvidia is not the only one to face this problem: AMD also had to release an optional driver on November 12 to address excessive writes to the system disk in some cases, suggesting the problem affects both card manufacturers.

The company advises anyone who notices the problem to download the hotfix driver immediately instead of staying on the affected driver, while those who do not notice it can wait for the next official driver. You can check whether the problem exists by opening Resource Monitor and watching the Nvidia process while the computer is idle.

Simply put: the graphics card driver kept writing a small log file over and over without stopping, until writes reached tens of gigabytes per hour, which could shorten the hard disk's life, and now Nvidia has released a fix that stops this bleeding.
