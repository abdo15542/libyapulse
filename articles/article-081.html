Breach Hits Ethiopian Airlines' Loyalty Program! ✈️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Ethiopian Airlines is investigating a data breach affecting its "ShebaMiles" loyalty program, after a security incident the company told its customers about on Wednesday. The company said the incident was "limited in scope" and that it "may have affected some customer information," adding that there is "currently no evidence of access to your financial information." The size of the breach is not yet clear, and flights were not disrupted.

A loyalty program is like a points card: it holds your name, your email, and your travel history, and "ShebaMiles" has more than 5 million customers. The company says money details appear safe so far — but the investigation is still open, and customers should beware of suspicious messages pretending to be from the company.
