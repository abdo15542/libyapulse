📡 Samsung Competes to Supply Equipment for Starlink Mobile… and SpaceX Is Building a Ground 5G Network!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A press report on November 25 said Samsung has entered the race to become a telecommunications equipment supplier for SpaceX, as the latter accelerates the expansion of its Starlink Mobile direct-to-satellite service.

SpaceX plans to build a terrestrial 5G phone network, deploying tens of thousands of base stations in the United States starting in 2027, combining satellite connectivity with ground networks in a direct challenge to the major telecom carriers.

A financial research firm named Samsung, Ericsson, and Nokia as possible equipment suppliers in a report dated November 20, citing Samsung's earlier experience supplying 5G equipment to US carrier Dish Network, while SpaceX signed a multi-year agreement with Qualcomm on November 4 to supply infrastructure platforms.

Advertisement

The report comes as Samsung's networks unit suffers falling revenue — down to about 400 billion won in the third quarter of the year, compared with about 600 billion in the same period last year — so winning a customer the size of SpaceX could revive its business.

But the news remains an unconfirmed report: neither Samsung nor SpaceX has confirmed it, and SpaceX may choose to manufacture its own base stations and buy only chips and software, while it is also said to be considering acquiring Boost Mobile or a deal to access its network.

Simply put: the maker of your phone might build the base stations for the coming satellite network, but the deal is still an unconfirmed report; will Samsung win it?

Would you rather SpaceX build its network with off-the-shelf equipment, or make everything itself?
