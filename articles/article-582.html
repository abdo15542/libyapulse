🛡️ Sharp AQUOS sense11 — a light phone with a two-day battery and six years of updates!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Sharp officially announced the AQUOS sense11 phone, with sales starting in Japan on November 26, 2026, along with an unlocked version on the same day.

The phone weighs only about 157 grams and is about 7.6 mm thick, yet carries a 5,000 mAh battery lasting about two days of use, based on 10 hours of daily use, with 8 GB of memory — or 12 GB in the unlocked version — and 128 or 256 GB of storage.

The real distinction here is durability and longevity: water and dust resistance rated IPX5, IPX8 and IPX9 — including hot water at 80 degrees Celsius — dust resistance IP6X, and shock resistance to the military MIL-STD-810H standard, with a promise of security updates for 6 years after launch and up to 4 operating-system upgrades.

The phone runs Android 17 with a Snapdragon 7s Gen 3 processor, a 6.2-inch Pro IGZO OLED display with peak brightness of 3,000 nits and a variable refresh rate from 1 to 240 Hz, a dual rear camera of 50.3 megapixels each, and a 32-megapixel front camera.

The camera adds new AI features, including removing unwanted people from the background of a photo, removing shadows from food and text, and reducing reflections when shooting through glass. The phone also comes pre-installed with Sharp's "Niyah" AI assistant, which works with a double press of the power button and helps operate the phone and manage daily tasks.

Simply put: imagine a phone as light as a feather that withstands hot water and drops, and stays updated and secure for six years. The bet here is not on the strongest specs, but on a phone that lives with you for a long time without letting you down.

Would you prefer a light, durable phone that lasts six years, or a stronger-specced phone replaced after two years?
