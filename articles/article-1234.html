▶️ YouTube is testing 7 alternative icons for its Android app… change the app's look however you like!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

YouTube is testing giving Android users more control over how the app icon looks on their home screens.

The latest beta version contains seven alternative icons, including a monochrome version of the familiar play button, and designs inspired by the platform's original logo and the early iPhone app.

Clues point to a new setting in the "General" menu suggesting users may eventually be able to switch between the designs from inside the app itself.

Advertisement

But Google has not confirmed the feature yet, and has not clarified whether it will reach all users or remain a limited test.

The test is part of a wider customization wave across Google's apps, offering more options to change how apps look without third-party apps.

Simply put: an app icon could become a personal choice like a wallpaper; a small detail, but it means the app you open every day might finally look the way you like.
