💬 Apple Closes Its Support Forums After 28 Years… Today Is the Last Day!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Apple is closing Apple Support Communities for good today, Friday, November 20, after a history spanning about 28 years of users helping each other. The forums had stopped accepting new posts since November 10 and became read-only. The forums launched in 1998, then changed names more than once until they became “Apple Support Communities” in 2022. The company said it made the decision after a careful review of its strategy and customer needs, and that users can get support from the official support website.

Simply put: the forums were a place where users asked each other about their device problems and answered them; now support moves to the company’s official channels.
