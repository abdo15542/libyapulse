🤖 Honor Unveils a Robot Phone... a Camera That Pops Out and Moves Like a Smart Assistant!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Honor has unveiled a new experimental phone called the Robot Phone, featuring a pop-up AI-powered camera that emerges from the phone's body and moves, so the phone works as a smart assistant that sees and understands what is around it, not just a device for calling and taking photos.

The company says the phone supports multimodal AI, meaning it understands images, sound, and text together, so it can help you with daily tasks in new ways. The camera also carries a USB-C port that can be used to connect additional accessories, a shift that makes the camera itself an expandable piece.

The phone is scheduled to be shown officially at Mobile World Congress in Barcelona next year, and it is still at the concept stage, meaning it is an idea the company is presenting to test people's reactions before deciding whether to turn it into a real product.

Simply put: a camera that comes out of your phone and moves to see and help you, like a small robot in your pocket; do you think phones will turn into assistants that move, or is this just a technical showpiece?
