🛡️ Korma Raises $125 Million... and Reaches a $1 Billion Valuation in Just 3 Months!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 16, Korma announced it had raised $125 million in a new funding round, reaching a $1 billion valuation just three months after coming out of stealth. The round was led by NEA, with participation from Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures, and Coatue, bringing the company's total raised to $185 million.

Korma was founded in 2025 and operates from Tel Aviv and San Francisco. It is an AI lab that trains foundation models from scratch, specialized in cyber defense, instead of relying on ready-made general models. It deploys these models as independent agents that work inside companies' security teams' tools, alongside their staff, to repel attacks and monitor systems around the clock.

Interest in the company came after a wave of breaches and critical vulnerabilities last October, which pushed security managers to look for automated defense that matches attackers' speed. The company says its specialized models understand the language of attack and defense better than general models, and that demand from major companies is growing fast.

Simply put: when AI attacks, a traditional firewall is not enough; do you think automated defense has become essential for every company, or are humans still the first line of defense?
