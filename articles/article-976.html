🎮 'Nintendo Switch 2' gets its first official discount! The 'Mario Kart' bundle returns to $499!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

'Nintendo's' Black Friday deals have begun, with the first official discount on the 'Switch 2' since its launch in June 2025, as the price of the console bundle with the game 'Mario Kart World' fell from $549.99 to $499.99.

The offer runs from November 22 to December 5 at major American retailers, and the bundle includes the console itself, a full digital copy of the game, and a three-month subscription to the 'Nintendo Switch Online' service.

The total saving is about $88 compared with buying the console, the game and the subscription separately, while the new price equals the current price of the console alone, meaning in practice that the game and the subscription come free during the offer period.

Advertisement

The discount carries extra meaning: 'Nintendo' raised the console's price last September from $449.99 to $499.99, so this offer completely wipes out the effect of that increase for anyone buying the bundle, and returns the price to what it was before the raise.

The offer does not stop at the console: from November 22 to November 29, prices drop by $20 on a group of physical games, including 'Donkey Kong Bananza', 'Kirby Air Riders', 'Metroid Prime 4' and 'Super Mario Party Jamboree', bringing some to $49.99.

As for anyone waiting for bigger discounts on 'PlayStation 5' or 'Xbox' consoles, they will wait a long time, as those consoles will not be in any official offers this discount season, making the 'Switch 2' offer the standout deal of the gaming season.

Simply put: the first official discount on the 'Switch 2' erases September's price rise... is this the right moment to buy?

Will you buy the bundle at this price, or wait for a bigger discount during the holidays?
