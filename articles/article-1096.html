📱 Nothing's screenless AI device appears in a leak: a 'Talk' button and red and green lights!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Nothing is preparing to unveil its first screenless AI device on 8 December, and the company has shared a close-up teaser image of the design, while a source leaked the first clearer image of the device ahead of launch.

The device appears in the leak as a small rectangular metal body, with physical buttons and a dedicated button labelled 'Talk,' and red and green light indicators, inside a rough-textured body, with pill-shaped LED lights as the company confirmed.

The idea itself is striking: a screenless device that relies on voice and artificial intelligence, so you talk to it instead of typing or touching a screen, at a time when companies are competing over who will make the first true personal AI device.

Advertisement

The company has not yet revealed the device's full functions or its price, but the launch in about two weeks will answer the most important question: do people really want a smart device with no screen?

Simply put: a small device with no screen, one button to talk, and coloured lights... Can your voice replace your phone's screen?

If you had a screenless AI device, what is the first thing you would ask it out loud?
