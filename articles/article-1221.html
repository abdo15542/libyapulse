🚀 SpaceX Launches a New Secret Military Mission… And Booster B1081 Flies for the 29th Time!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A Falcon 9 rocket lifted off today at 8:08 AM Pacific Time from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California, on a new secret military mission.

The mission is called USSF-266, for the US Space Force, a secret mission whose payload and nature have not been announced.

The first-stage booster B1081 landed successfully on the drone ship Of Course I Still Love You in the Pacific Ocean, on its 29th flight, a new record for reusing a single booster.

Advertisement

SpaceX ended its live broadcast right after the booster landed, and did not show what the upper stage did on its way to orbit.

The company's public mission page did not identify the spacecraft or its mission, in complete secrecy around the goal.

Booster B1081 previously launched NASA's Crew-7 astronaut mission, a Dragon supply mission, the PACE Earth-observation mission, and 16 batches of Starlink satellites.

The same pad was used for the USSF-317 mission on 20 November and the NROL-136 mission on 22 November, with only 32 hours between them, a record for turning the pad around.

This is the fifth launch from California in November, and the 79th from Vandenberg in 2026.

Simply put: a rocket flying for the 29th time and coming home safely, and a secret mission whose payload nobody knows; reuse has become routine, and secrecy has become part of the scene.
