💬 'WhatsApp' Raises the Group Limit to 1,500 Members — Without Announcing It Officially!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

"WhatsApp" has begun raising the maximum number of members in a single group from 1,024 to 1,500, in a gradual rollout reaching "iOS" and "Android" users in batches. The change was spotted in the latest versions of the app, although the company has not officially announced it yet, and the help center still mentions the old limit of 1,024.

The new limit means the admin of a full group can add 476 more members without creating a new group, and the increase also covers chat groups inside "Communities", while announcement groups in "Communities" stay at the 2,000-member limit as they are.

Admins don't need to do anything: current groups update automatically to support the new limit as soon as the update reaches the admin's account. Those who haven't received the update yet will still see the old limit, and the rollout is expected to widen over the coming weeks.

This expansion follows a series of earlier increases: the limit was 100 members in 2016, then rose to 256, then to 512, then to 1,024 in 2022, and now to 1,500.

Simply put: if your group is full at 1,024 members, it will soon fit 476 more people automatically, without moving anyone or creating a new group.
