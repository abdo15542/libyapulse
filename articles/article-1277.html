📈 Dell raises its forecast for the third time — $202 billion in revenue and record AI server orders!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Dell announced on 24 November the results of the third quarter of its fiscal year 2027, raising its full-year forecast for the third time this year, driven by record demand for AI servers.

The company’s revenue more than doubled to $54.9 billion, up 103% year on year, while AI server revenue jumped 291% to $22 billion, and its infrastructure solutions group achieved record revenue of $42 billion.

The company raised its full-year revenue forecast to $202 billion, from $192 billion, up 78% year on year, raised its AI server revenue forecast to $78 billion, from $74 billion, and raised its adjusted earnings-per-share forecast to $29.68, from $25.50.

The company recorded AI server orders worth $60.2 billion in the quarter alone, ending the quarter with a record backlog of $132.3 billion, with more than 7,500 customers deploying Dell “AI factories”.

Simply put: Dell is no longer primarily a personal computer company — the bulk of its growth and profits now comes from AI servers for large companies.
