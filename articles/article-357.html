💰 'Payhawk' Raises $280 Million to Become an AI Financial Operating System!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Expense-management company "Payhawk" announced on November 18 a Series "C" funding round of $280 million, led by "Goldman Sachs Alternatives", valuing the company at $2 billion and raising its total funding to more than $515 million. The deal is still subject to regulatory approvals.

The company said it will use the money to build what it calls a "financial operating system" of three layers: AI agents that carry out financial work, a payments infrastructure that lets those agents handle money safely, and enterprise controls that define what an agent may do and when a human must decide. According to CEO Hristo Borisov, 77% of customers use the company's agents daily for financial oversight, purchases, travel, and reporting.

The company was founded in Sofia and today operates in 33 countries through 12 offices, and says it achieved positive cash flow in the third quarter, still holds 70% of the capital from its 2022 round, and that its revenue comes from subscriptions, not card commissions.

Simply put: the idea is not for AI to merely suggest, but to execute within approval rules — a financial agent that works like an employee, not a consultant.
