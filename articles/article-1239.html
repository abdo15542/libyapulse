🛡️ DXC launches a service that guards software with Claude Mythos… and humans decide the fix!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

DXC Technology has announced the launch of a new service called Frontier Code Assurance, a managed cybersecurity service that uses Anthropic's Claude Mythos model to detect, verify, and help fix vulnerabilities in critical software.

The model can scan up to 10 million lines of code quickly, searching for vulnerabilities that traditional scanners might miss, and then humans take over: DXC's security specialists verify every finding, rank it by its impact on the business, decide the remediation path, and give the client a documented, auditable roadmap for every step.

The service is available now, but the official announcement came after it had already been selling in practice since late October — the current launch is the formal introduction of an offering that was already on the market.

Advertisement

The service builds on a multi-year alliance between DXC and Anthropic announced last June, which made DXC a first global partner in the Claude partner network, with tens of thousands of certified engineers trained.

The service operates within the company's global cybersecurity practice, which runs 12 security operations centers in more than 40 countries, staffed by thousands of specialists.

CEO Raul Fernandez said the idea is not about the number of alerts, but about fixing them: a scan that produces every alert at once creates a backlog, not security.

Simply put: AI finds vulnerabilities fast, but a human decides which vulnerability deserves fixing first; real security is not a long list of alerts, but completed, documented fixes.

Would you trust an AI model to search your sensitive software for vulnerabilities?
