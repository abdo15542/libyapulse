🎙️ OpenAI launches its new voice model in its API — and silent calls are cheaper!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI has made its new voice model GPT-Live 1.1 available to developers through its API, in an update that makes building voice assistants more accurate and cheaper during silent periods.

The model is fully two-way voice: it listens while it speaks at the same time, so the caller can interrupt it mid-sentence, as happens in human conversations, instead of waiting for the reply to finish.

The new version beats its predecessor in speech-to-text accuracy and in the consistency of accent and voice across different languages, and it had already been powering the voice feature in ChatGPT for Plus and Pro subscribers since October 15.

Advertisement

The standout feature is the new standby mode: when both sides fall silent for a period set by the developer, between 5 and 60 seconds, inference pauses temporarily while the connection stays open, and the cost of those silent minutes drops from $0.05 to $0.02 per minute.

That means a 60% saving on the cost of the minutes users spend reading, thinking or stepping away from the device — minutes that were previously billed at full price despite no conversation taking place.

Standby mode works with the new model and its predecessor, and is limited to WebRTC sessions, while phone calls and other connections continue at the usual pricing.

Switching to the new model requires no more than changing the model name in the session settings, with no change in how the connection works.

Simply put: the voice assistant now understands your accent better — and stops the cost meter when you fall silent to think.
