💻 Windows 11 Version 26H2 Will Install on Your PC Automatically — Even Without You Asking for the Update!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft began this week automatically installing the Windows 11 version 26H2 update on eligible devices, without the user having to search for the update manually.

The company said on the release health page that it began on November 18 gradually expanding the automatic rollout, starting with devices running versions 24H2 and 25H2 that are not managed by IT departments, whether personal devices or unmanaged business devices.

Since late October, the update was available to those who searched for it manually via Windows Update, but now it will arrive on its own to more devices as the rollout advances, according to each device's Windows Update settings, and the restart is usually scheduled outside active working hours.

Advertisement

The version is also known as the 'Windows 11 2026 Update', and was officially released in September 2026 as a small, fast-to-install enablement package, and will remain supported until October 2029.

Whoever wants the update immediately can go to Settings, then Windows Update, and press 'Check for updates', while enabling the option 'Get the latest updates as soon as they're available'.

Simply put: if your computer runs Windows 11 and is not managed by an employer, the big update is coming to you on its own over the coming days or weeks, and all you have to do is restart the device when asked.
