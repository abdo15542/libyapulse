👁️ WhatsApp Launches Read-Once Messages on iPhone! They Vanish After Being Opened... and No Screenshot Can Save You!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

WhatsApp has started rolling out 'View Once' text messages on iPhones, after they were previously available on Android — a new feature for anyone who wants to send sensitive information that does not stay in the chat after it is read.

You send the message by long-pressing the send button and choosing 'View Once'. The recipient reads it once in a separate view, then it disappears permanently from the chat and an 'Opened' indicator appears, leaving no copy with either the sender or the recipient.

Most importantly, WhatsApp blocks screenshots and screen recording while it is being read on iPhone, and also blocks copying, forwarding and saving in any form — there is no way inside the app to keep a copy of it.

Advertisement

This feature differs from the familiar 'disappearing messages': those are deleted after a set time whether they were read or not, while 'View Once' vanishes as soon as it is opened, even if only a few seconds have passed since it was sent.

The feature is currently available to some beta testers via TestFlight in the WhatsApp beta for iOS 26.45.10.73 update, and it is expanding gradually over the coming weeks to reach all users.

Simply put: a message you read once and then it vanishes forever — no screenshot, no copying, no forwarding... would you use it for sensitive information?

Do you trust that the message has really disappeared, or would you rather not send it at all?
