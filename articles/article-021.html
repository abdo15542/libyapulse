Huge Deal: AMD Acquires World Labs for $8.2 Billion! 💻

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

AMD has completed its acquisition of World Labs, a company specializing in spatial AI, and its founder, scientist Fei-Fei Li, has joined the company as Chief Scientist.

The goal? To build models that understand the world in 3D, and to develop stronger AI hardware to compete with Nvidia.
