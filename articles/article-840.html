📱 Motorola Razr Fold Finally Gets Android 17.. Stacked Widgets and Icons in Your System's Colors!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The foldable Motorola Razr Fold phone began receiving the Android 17 update in selected global markets, according to reports from users on 19 November, although the company has not officially announced the start of the wide rollout yet. The update is about 4.15 GB and carries firmware version A171WPB.27-36-2.

The update brings Android 17 features to the foldable phone, most notably stacked widgets on the home screen, which let you place several widgets on top of each other and switch between them with a single swipe, plus matching app icon colors to the system theme's colors, and the ability to hide Quick Settings tile labels by choosing the classic style. These look like small features, but they completely change the home screen's look for anyone who cares about details.

Notably, the Razr Fold is no ordinary phone when it comes to support: when it launched, the company promised up to seven Android upgrades and seven years of security updates, one of the longest support commitments in the foldable phone world. The rollout is expected to widen to more markets in the coming days, as it began with a limited group of users first, as usual.

Simply put: your foldable phone finally speaks the language of Android 17; has the update reached you, or are you still waiting for your turn?
