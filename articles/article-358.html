🤖 'Human Labs' Raises $47 Million to Teach Robots from the Real World!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Human Labs", an embodied-AI research and publishing company founded in 2026, announced a Series "A" funding round of $47 million on November 19. The funding combines a $26 million equity round led by "Accel" and $21 million in venture debt led by "Lighthouse Canton".

The company says it captures real work in factories, warehouses, farms, and homes with its own capture devices, then turns the recordings into training-ready datasets, labeled and source-documented, across vision, sound, motion, and touch. Its customers include leading AI labs, robotics companies, and world-model developers.

The company also says it surpassed $50 million in annual revenue within just six months of starting operations, operates in more than 20 countries, and will direct the funding to infrastructure in Brazil, South Korea, and India, and to capture devices and teams in San Francisco, London, and Seoul. Co-founder Manish Agarwal said the goal is to build a business with $1 billion in annual revenue.
