💰 Nvidia Posts the Biggest Quarter in Its History: $115.8 Billion! And Demand for AI Just Won't Stop!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nvidia announced on November 17 the results of the third quarter of its fiscal year 2027, achieving record revenue of $115.8 billion, more than double the $57 billion in revenue from the same period last year.

Most of the growth came from data centers, whose revenue reached $107.6 billion, up 110% from last year — about 93% of the company's total revenue.

The new Vera Rubin platform contributed about $20 billion in its first quarter of production shipments, the fastest product launch in the company's history, and the systems are now running with partners including CoreWeave, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and Oracle.

Net profit rose to $65.3 billion, up 105%, and earnings per share reached $2.70, while the company forecast revenue of about $130 billion for the fourth quarter, beating analysts' expectations.

Interim CEO Colette Kress raised the revenue growth forecast for fiscal year 2028 from 70% to about 75%, in the first earnings report since the former CEO resigned on November 12.

Simply put: the company is selling chips in numbers never seen before, but the real question is: can the world provide the electricity and cooling for all this computing power?
