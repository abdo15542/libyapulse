🚀 SpaceX Launches a Secret Mission for the US Space Force — and an Unknown Payload into Orbit!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

SpaceX launched a Falcon 9 rocket late Thursday from Vandenberg Base in California, on a secret mission for the US Space Force called USSF-317, sending an unknown payload into orbit. The rocket lifted off at 11:20 p.m. Eastern US time, and its first stage returned to Earth about eight and a half minutes later, landing on a drone ship in the Pacific Ocean.

This was the eleventh flight of this same booster, and the company ended its live broadcast immediately after landing, so no one saw what the upper stage did on its way to orbit. The mission is secret — no one knows what it carries or exactly where it is headed — but some experts suggest it carries a group of the company's Starshield satellites, as part of the Space Force's orbital sensing and targeting program.

This is the 121st Falcon 9 launch in 2026, and the third mission the company has launched for the Space Force in November alone. Most of the rocket's flights this year — about 70% of them — are devoted to building the Starlink satellite internet network, which now includes more than 11,000 satellites.

Simply put: imagine a plane taking off in the dark and no one tells you its destination, but its booster returns safely to a ship at sea — that is how secret military missions in space work: they launch without announcing their payload.
