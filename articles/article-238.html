🚕 Uber tests rides for children as young as 10!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Uber has begun testing a new service called 'Uber Kids', carrying children aged 10 to 12 without an accompanying adult, in three U.S. cities: Austin in Texas, Nashville in Tennessee, and Madison in Wisconsin. The service extends the 'Uber for Teens' service that has existed since 2023 for riders aged 13 to 17, and the company describes it as a limited experiment that may never expand. Only parents or guardians book the ride, after adding the child to the family profile and completing mandatory training on the safety features, and a parent must be present at the pickup point, with a note to the driver explaining the drop-off details. Only highly rated drivers take part, by special invitation, after completing an educational module, with a live video feed parents can watch during the ride, a PIN confirmation for each ride, encrypted recordings the driver cannot access, and sensors and a location system that detect if the ride deviates from its route or stops suddenly. Drivers are paid an extra $7 per kids' ride, and $20 extra for the first ride they complete.

Simply put: the idea is for parents to order a car for their children from school to the club, for example, and watch the ride live on video; but the service is still a small experiment in just three cities.
