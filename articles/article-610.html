🧠 MediaTek reveals the "Dimensity 9600 Pro" with 2-nanometre architecture!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

MediaTek revealed on Friday, November 20, its new chip "Dimensity 9600 Pro" for smartphones — an eight-core chip built on 2-nanometre architecture.

The chip uses a new design called "AI Compute Fusion" that distributes the eight cores across three groups: two main cores, three for performance, and three for efficiency, with a new cache memory structure.

The chip supports the latest LPDDR6 memory and the faster UFS 5 storage, plus a new neural processing unit numbered 1090 with cores dedicated to performance and flexibility.

Graphics are handled by the "Mali-G2 Ultra NX" unit with 10 MB of system memory, while the AI imaging architecture handles scene analysis, true colours and fast focus.

"2-nanometre" architecture means the transistors inside the chip are smaller than ever, so more of them fit in the same space, increasing performance and reducing power consumption.

Simply put: imagine your phone's engine becoming smaller and stronger at the same time. A new chip means faster phones and a battery that lasts longer.
