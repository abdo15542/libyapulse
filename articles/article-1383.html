🎬 Prime Video Revives 60 MGM Films and Series in HD Quality With the Help of AI!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Prime Video has started showing more than 60 films and series from the MGM library in HD quality, after enhancing them with AI-powered technology, whereas these titles were previously available only in standard quality.

The list includes the film 'Stolen Hours' and episodes of the 1950s series 'Highway Patrol', available to subscribers around the world, with a new section on the homepage for viewers in America called 'Digitally Enhanced to HD', and the platform promised more titles next year.

The process runs in four stages: cleaning the old footage of defects, then raising its resolution, then preserving its original look and colors, then adding natural film grain, using multiple AI models, each model responsible for part of the work.

The AI is not left alone with the film: Prime Video's video specialists review every film and series before showing it, to check accuracy, consistency, and fidelity to the original, because old footage looks blurry on today's large modern screens.

This comes after another Prime Video feature that uses AI to sync lip movement with human dubbing, which reached the first and second seasons of 'Maxton Hall', and is due to reach the third season on December 9.

Simply put: old films from the fifties are now shown in high quality without being re-shot; would you watch an old film just because it became clearer?
