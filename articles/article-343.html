⌚ Huawei Unveils the 'Watch Air': Its Thinnest Smartwatch Ever with a 6.9mm Titanium Body!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Huawei has officially announced its new smartwatch, the "Watch Air", which it will reveal in full at its event scheduled for November 24 in China, alongside the foldable phone "Mate X8" and the phone "Mate 90 Air". The company describes the watch as the thinnest smartwatch it has ever made, at just 6.9mm thick, with a unibody metal frame made of shiny titanium alloy, and sapphire glass protecting the screen.

The watch appears in the teaser with a square screen and narrow bezels just 1.4mm wide, in three case colors: silver, light gold, and titanium black, with strap options in silicone, fabric, and Milanese metal. Leaked specs point to a 1.92-inch AMOLED screen with LTPO 2.0 technology and peak brightness of up to 3,500 nits, a screen-to-body ratio of 86%, a weight of only around 27 grams, and a high-density silicon battery that could last up to seven days.

The leaks also suggest the watch's health-tracking features will be similar to the "Watch 6", while its sports-tracking features approach those of the "Watch GT 7 Pro". But Huawei has not yet confirmed these details, nor announced the price or global availability date — all the full details will appear at the November 24 event.
