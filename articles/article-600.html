🛡️ Google patches two critical Android flaws — one hacks your phone without you clicking anything!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google released a new security update on Thursday, November 19, patching two critical "zero-day" flaws in the Android system that attackers were already exploiting.

One of the flaws allows a "zero-click" attack — the attacker can access sensitive data on the phone without the user pressing any link or opening any file.

The second flaw is also critical, and both were known to attackers before Google fixed them, which is what makes the update urgent.

Advertisement

The update reaches Pixel phones immediately, while owners of other phones wait for their phone makers to release the same update in the coming days.

A "zero-day" flaw means the attackers discovered the bug before the company itself, so there was no ready fix on the day it was discovered — as if your house door were open and you didn't know.

Simply put: imagine someone being able to enter your phone while you sleep, without you touching anything. That is what one of the flaws allowed before Google patched it.
