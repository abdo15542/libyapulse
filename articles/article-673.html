📱 Apple is preparing to raise the price of the iPhone 18 Pro next month — after Samsung and Google raised theirs!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple is preparing to launch the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max next month at higher prices than their predecessors, the iPhone 17 Pro and iPhone 17 Pro Max, according to new reports — a step that could make the flagship phone more expensive than buyers are used to.

The company is considering the increase after Samsung and Google raised the prices of their flagship phones by $100, and it is studying how the two companies handled rising costs of essential components, led by memory and storage chips that have become more expensive with growing global demand.

"Memory" is the chips that store your data and run your apps, and "storage" is the space that holds your photos and files. If the price of these two parts rises, the cost of making the whole phone rises, and then the company chooses between absorbing the difference from its profits or passing it on to the selling price.

Apple has not officially announced the new prices yet, and the reports speak of a possible increase of $100, like its rivals — which means anyone planning to buy the new phone may pay more than they paid for its predecessor.

Simply put: the phone's components have become more expensive, so the phone itself may become more expensive. Will you buy the iPhone 17 Pro now before the increase, or wait for the iPhone 18 Pro whatever its price?

Does a $100 increase affect your decision to buy a new phone, or is price no longer the deciding factor?
