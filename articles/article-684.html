🚀 iPhone 17 Pro Max is the first phone NASA has approved for a deep-space mission!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The US space agency NASA has approved the use of the iPhone 17 Pro Max on the Artemis 2 mission, making it the first iPhone in history to receive official approval for a crewed deep-space mission.

The Artemis 2 mission is historic in itself: it is the first crewed mission NASA has sent to deep space in about 50 years, and its first lunar mission in the 21st century, as its astronauts will orbit the Moon and return to Earth aboard the Orion capsule. The agency's approval of the phone means it has passed strict tests proving it works safely inside the capsule, in conditions completely different from everyday use on Earth.

The phone being on the mission does not mean it will be used only for photography: modern phones carry powerful processors, advanced cameras and many sensors, making them practical tools in an enclosed environment like a space capsule. The step also symbolises a bigger shift: ordinary consumer devices have become powerful and reliable enough to enter missions that were once reserved for equipment designed specially for space.

Simply put: the phone in your pocket today may travel around the Moon tomorrow. Can you imagine everyday technology this close to you reaching deep space?
