🚜 'John Deere' Acquires 'Orchard AI' — Smart Cameras That Count the Fruit on Every Tree!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"John Deere" announced on November 19 its acquisition of "Orchard Robotics", known as "Orchard AI", a startup that uses AI to help farmers monitor their crops. The deal's value was not disclosed.

"Orchard" uses cameras mounted on tractors and equipment that capture images while crossing the farm, and AI analyzes hundreds of images per tree to count the fruit, measure its size and color, estimate the yield, and monitor the health of trees and vines throughout the season. Farmers view the results through the "FruitScope" platform, deciding when to thin fruit, spray, and harvest with precision.

The company was founded in San Francisco in 2022 by Charlie Wu, employs 45 people, and has raised more than $25 million from investors, including a $22 million round in September 2025. It is the latest in a series of "John Deere" tech acquisitions, after "Blue River" in 2017, "Bear Flag Robotics" in 2021, and "Sentera" and "GUSS" in 2025.

Simply put: the tractor now carries smart cameras that count the fruit on every single tree, so farmers no longer guess their yield — they know the number and size of the fruit weeks before harvest.
