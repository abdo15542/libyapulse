Phones & Devices

The stable Android 17 update has finally arrived on Motorola's foldable phone, the Razr Fold, after lagging behind the company's other phones. The update has started reaching users in Europe, specifically in Hungary and Spain, with a large download of around four gigabytes. It brings a new design, smarter notifications and faster multitasking, plus a fix for a problem that was affecting some apps. Notably, the company has not officially announced the update yet — users spotted it themselves on their phones. Would you rather receive an update early, or wait for the official announcement? 📱
