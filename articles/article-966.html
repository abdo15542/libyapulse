💰 Lenovo finally makes a billion dollars from data centers! Record revenue after 12 years of losses!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

In its latest quarterly results, 'Lenovo' announced that its Infrastructure Solutions Group made a quarterly profit of one billion dollars, the first real profit for its data center business since the company acquired 'IBM's' server business in 2014.

The group's revenue reached 11.3 billion dollars for the quarter, a record, up 178% from last year, driven by the AI boom that raised demand for servers, storage and networking, making 'Lenovo' the largest seller of 'x86' servers with a market share of 16%.

Since acquiring 'IBM's' business 12 years ago, the company had struggled to turn data center sales into profit, despite expanding into storage, networking and sales to major cloud computing companies, until the AI boom did what successive waves of transformation could not.

The company also announced that it strengthened its position as the world's largest PC provider, with Intelligent Devices Group revenue of 17.4 billion dollars, up 15% from last year, and a market share of 23.8%, ahead of its nearest competitor by seven percentage points, while premium devices represent 63% of its PC business.

The company's total revenue rose 48% from last year to reach 30.24 billion dollars for the quarter, at a time when enterprise hardware supply chains suffer severe tightness, yet the company says it managed to increase shipments of 'x86' servers.

Simply put: after 12 years of attempts, AI achieved what no strategy could: 'Lenovo's' data centers finally make a profit... will the boom continue or is it a passing wave?
