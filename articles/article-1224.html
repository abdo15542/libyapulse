🤖 Embo Raises $110 Million to Build a 'Robot Brain' That Learns Physics From Video!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Embo has raised $110 million in a seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz.

Khosla Ventures, DST Global, Nvidia's investment arm NVentures, and Striker Venture Partners took part in the round, along with other strategic investors.

The company is based in San Francisco and is led by founders Daniar Hafner and Wilson Yan; Hafner is known for the Dreamer research series at Google DeepMind.

Advertisement

The company is building a unified world model that predicts how physical scenes change by analyzing huge amounts of internet video and text.

Robots train inside millions of imagined scenarios through reinforcement learning, before their skills are refined with real data from a fleet of humanoid robots.

The goal is robots that work in environments they have never seen and recover from unexpected mistakes, without training tailored to each task.

The funding will go toward training large-scale world models and developing the virtual simulation and reinforcement learning platforms.

The company has not yet announced a valuation, a product, customers, or a date for its first demo.

Simply put: instead of training a robot on one task in one place, a single 'brain' learns the laws of physics from millions of videos, trains in its imagination first and then applies it in reality; if it works, the robot is ready for any new place.

Would you trust a robot that learned physics from video more than one trained in a real factory?
