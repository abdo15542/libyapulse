🤖 Memory Inside the Model Itself! MemT 1.0 Pro Remembers Your History Without Re-Reading Your Chats!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

MemTensor, in collaboration with NVIDIA, has announced a new AI model called MemT 1.0 Pro, an open-source model that puts memory inside the model itself instead of relying on re-reading old conversations every time.

The idea is simple but deep: current models forget what happened between you and them as soon as the conversation ends, or they have to re-read thousands of words to remember, while the new model stores information, updates it, and retrieves it as a capability learned through training, so it remembers your requirements and past decisions without keeping the original text in context.

The model is based on NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra architecture, with 554 billion parameters in total, 59 billion of them active, and it combines a dynamic local memory that keeps what it learns during interaction with a trainable super memory that learns how to write and read this information.

In tests, the model scored 41.80 points on the BEM 1M long-memory benchmark, compared to 37.04 for the earlier Flash version, and 23.87 points on the MemoryAgentBench benchmark, compared to 17.36, with an average of 53.08 points across 11 memory benchmarks, the highest among the built-in memory methods measured in the report.

The practical goal of all this is AI agents that manage long tasks: a project spanning weeks, or a service request that changes over time, where remembering a single fact is not enough, and you need to keep the original requirements, the revised decisions, and the accumulated information across many conversations.

Simply put: a model that does not forget you after the conversation ends, but remembers you the way a person remembers their friends. Would you trust an AI that keeps your entire history?
