📈 Financial Disclosure: Trump Accounts Made 751 Trades in September — Including Nvidia and SpaceX!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A periodic financial report published on Thursday revealed that the investment accounts of US President Donald Trump made 751 trades last September, covering stocks and bonds worth a total of between $40.9 million and $148.1 million. Trump signed the report on November 1, the Office of Government Ethics received it on November 5, and it was published publicly on November 19.

Among the most notable trades were five transactions in Nvidia shares, including four purchases — the largest worth between $100,000 and $250,000 on September 2, the same day Nvidia announced its deal to buy the Hugging Face platform for $12.9 billion. The accounts also bought Palantir shares before it won two government contracts worth $42.5 million, and shares and bonds in SpaceX before and after NASA announced expanding its contract with the company by $946 million.

The timing of some trades raised questions about conflicts of interest, as they occurred in the same period the government made decisions affecting those companies. The White House responded that the portfolio is managed entirely by independent managers, and that Trump and his family do not direct the investments or intervene in them. The report also noted that some early-September trades were reported after the 45-day deadline imposed by federal law.
