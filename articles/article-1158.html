📱 The Mate 90 Air: Huawei's Thinnest Phone Ever, for $750!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Huawei launched the Mate 90 Air in China today, its thinnest standard phone ever at 6.58mm thick and 208 grams, with a 7-inch 1.5K screen at a 120Hz refresh rate and brightness up to 4,000 nits, and the Kirin 9020 chip.

The company says the new chip is 9% faster in single-core, 36% faster in multi-core, and 18% faster in graphics compared with the previous Mate 70 Air, with gaming performance 30% better. The 6,500mAh battery lasts 23 hours of video watching, with 66W wired charging.

The rear cameras include a 50MP main camera with an f/1.8 aperture and optical stabilization, an 8MP ultra-wide camera, and a 12MP telephoto camera with 3x zoom, plus a multi-spectral Red Maple color camera and a 10.7MP front camera. The phone supports 5G and Wi-Fi 7, and two-way Beidou satellite messaging.

The phone comes in three colors — pink gold, moon silver, and black — priced at 4,999 yuan (about $750) for the 256GB version and 5,999 yuan (about $901) for the 512GB version, with open sales starting on December 4, and no announcement about availability outside China.

Simply put: a very slim phone with a giant screen and a big battery… but it is thinner than its predecessor by barely a hair's width, so is it worth the upgrade?
