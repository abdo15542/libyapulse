💬 'GPT-6.2 Sol' Arrives in Everyday ChatGPT Conversations! Sharper Understanding of Images and Instructions!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

OpenAI began rolling out on Monday a version of its GPT-6.2 model tuned for the regular chat tab in ChatGPT, after the model had only been available since November 17 in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and the developer API.

The new version is built on GPT-6.2 Sol and tuned for everyday conversations. It is available worldwide to subscribers on the Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans, while Enterprise workplace admins decide whether to enable it.

The company says the new model understands images more accurately: in its internal tests, image comprehension accuracy jumped from 47.4% with GPT-6 to 71.7%, and it followed complex instructions in 78.1% of cases, up from 62.5% before — figures from the company itself that no independent party has yet verified.

The update also included safety improvements: the model followed the company's policies in 91% to 94% of difficult conversations about sensitive topics such as eating disorders and emotional dependence, compared with 77% to 80% for the previous model — though that still means 6% to 9% of those conversations fall short of the required standard.

Users on the free and Go plans will stay on the GPT-6 Luna model, with no announced date for an upgrade. The company also announced that the GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-5.6 Terra models will be withdrawn from ChatGPT on December 23, 2026.

Simply put: your everyday chats with ChatGPT have become smarter at reading images and understanding your requests... Have you noticed the difference in its replies?
