📱 The Tensor G6 in the Pixel 11 is built on 3nm — not 2nm as rumoured!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A Google executive confirmed that the Tensor G6 chip, which powers the new Pixel 11 phones, is built on TSMC's 3nm process — not 2nm as previously rumoured.

Earlier reports had predicted the chip would be TSMC's first 2nm processor, but the company officially denied that: the processor runs on the advanced 3nm process.

The Pixel 11 series was unveiled at the "Made by Google" event earlier this week, and the new chip promises on-device AI capabilities and a stronger neural processing unit.

A nanometre is the unit used to measure the size of the tiny circuits inside a chip — the smaller the number, the more circuits fit on the chip, which usually improves energy efficiency and performance.

Simply put: the 2nm rumour was not true, but 3nm is still an advanced process, enough to run AI inside your phone.
