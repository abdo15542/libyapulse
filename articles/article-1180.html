📹 Uber Rolls Out Video-Recorded Rides! The Camera Runs the Whole Trip — in Four Cities First!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Uber has begun trialing a new feature in the United States called “Recorded Rides,” which lets a rider book a trip where the entire journey is recorded on video.

The feature works through “Record My Ride,” which uses the driver’s phone front camera to record the trip from start to finish, and it appears in the app as an option called “Recorded Rides.”

The trial is launching in four cities first: Baltimore, Raleigh, Indianapolis, and St. Louis, with plans to expand to more cities later.

Advertisement

The company is focusing the trial on late-night rides and rides involving teenagers, after launching a live video stream for teen rides this year and testing audio recording for late-night rides in some cities.

The company is also testing a feature that starts recording automatically if the app detects that the car has gone off route, or that an accident may have occurred.

The driver can stop the recording from the settings, and the rider can decline recording through a notification they receive inside the app.

Simply put: your next ride could be documented on video from the first moment until arrival, as evidence that protects both the rider and the driver if something happens.

Would you feel safer if your ride were recorded on video, or would that bother your privacy?
