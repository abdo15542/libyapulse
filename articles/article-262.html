🎬 Meta sells 3D movies on Quest headsets... the cinema in your home!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Meta has announced it has begun selling more than 150 3D movies that can be bought or rented and watched in virtual reality on the 'Quest 3' and 'Quest 3S' headsets, through its own TV app.

The list includes films such as 'Avatar', 'Spider-Man: Far From Home', 'Jurassic World: Rebirth', and 'Wicked: For Good', plus a concert film by singer Billie Eilish directed with her by director James Cameron.

The company suggests watching the films in the new 'Theater' mode, which shows a huge screen and darkens everything around it to create a personal cinema experience, and promised the library will keep growing with thousands of titles and wider international availability when the new virtual reality headsets launch next spring. Disney Plus subscribers can also watch 3D movies through the service's app on Quest devices.

Simply put: instead of going to the cinema, the headset puts a giant screen in front of you in your room, turning your home's walls into a dark hall.
