🚀 Bezos returns as CEO — his secret company Prometheus designs chips and engines with AI!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 17, Jeff Bezos appeared at the Semafor summit and revealed that he is the co-CEO of the company "Prometheus" — his first executive role since stepping down from Amazon in 2021.

His co-founder is physicist Vic Bajaj, a Stanford University professor and former head of scientific operations at Verily, and the company was incubated in 2024 at the "Foresight Labs" lab.

The company is building what it calls a "general artificial engineer": a single multi-physics neural model that designs physical things — chips, radio equipment and valves — aiming to shorten equipment design from many long years to a much shorter time. Developing a jet engine, for example, might require thousands of workers and more than a decade.

Advertisement

The company raised about $6 billion first, then another $12 billion this year, at a $41 billion valuation, and employs about 250 people, including researchers from major AI companies and experts in space, chemistry and energy.

The company secretly tested its tools at Blue Origin, and redesigned the Mars helicopter with its models, and quietly acquired the "General Agents" applied-AI lab.

There is no commercial product or announced revenue yet, and the company's official website shows nothing but the logo. The focus is on design, manufacturing and engineering — not robots.

Simply put: imagine an AI that doesn't write text, but designs an aircraft engine or an electronic chip. That is Bezos's new bet: that AI shortens years of engineers' work into days.

Do you believe AI will one day design a complete aircraft engine, or is the human engineer indispensable?
