💚 Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang as CEO — and the stock jumps 30% in a single day!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Nvidia's board of directors announced on Monday the return of Jensen Huang as CEO and chairman of the company, less than two weeks after he resigned following his admission of two extramarital relationships, one of them with a woman the U.S. government suspects of being a Chinese spy.

The board said it consulted with senior government officials before making the decision, and confirmed that Huang poses no risk to national security. The decision came unanimously, after a series of meetings with him during which the board became convinced of "his candor and ability to lead Nvidia."

The company's stock jumped immediately on the news: it rose 30% in pre-market trading and reached $159.95 at 9:51 a.m. Eastern time, up 27%, in one of the biggest single-day jumps for the stock.

U.S. President Donald Trump had repeatedly expressed his support for Huang's return, writing that the board "made the right and appropriate decision," adding that the Attorney General had confirmed there was no evidence that Huang had engaged in any illegal activity. Top tech executives — including Elon Musk — also signed a petition calling for his return.

Notably, the same board had seemed just a few days earlier determined to keep Huang out, as its chairman, Harvey Jones, said on the quarterly earnings call that the company did not intend to bring him back and had instead hired a firm to search for a new permanent CEO — before completely changing his mind.

Huang thanked the board for its confidence and said he returns "with the greatest humility and renewed commitment," while Colette Kress — who served in the role temporarily — continues in her role as chief financial officer.

Simply put: a dramatic return for the founder of the world's largest AI chip company just two weeks after he left... Can Huang fully win back the market's confidence?
