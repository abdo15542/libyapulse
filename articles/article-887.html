🎮 Ubisoft Confirms: "Assassin's Creed Jade" Still in Development... Despite Losses and 166 Layoffs!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Ubisoft has once again confirmed that its mobile game "Assassin's Creed Jade," set in ancient China, is still in development, according to chief financial officer Frédéric Duguet during the company's earnings call for the first half of the fiscal year.

Duguet said mobile remains "a medium- and long-term opportunity," but he also made clear that consoles and PC remain the focus for the company's biggest franchises, "especially PC, which is a more dynamic platform than ever."

The last time the company spoke about the game was last March, as part of the "Assassin's Creed: Towards 2026" update, and this time it revealed no release date or new details, despite years passing since the announcement amid rounds of layoffs and project cancellations.

Advertisement

In the same context, the company announced the completion of its new operating model, merging its third and fifth "creative houses" under the leadership of Julien Bar, to strengthen integration between live-service experiences across different platforms, and spinning off its generative AI team, "Motherbrain," into an independent unit.

The confirmation came just hours after the company announced another 166 layoffs at its Montreal and Quebec City studios, along with a net loss in the first half of €254.5 million and a 16.5% drop in net bookings.

Simply put: The game is alive despite everything, but the company itself admits mobile is not its priority — so will this game finally see the light of day?
