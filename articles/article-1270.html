📱 Google unveils the Pixel 11 series — four phones, a Tensor G6 chip, and a new watch and tracker!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Google revealed at its annual Made by Google event on Wednesday its new generation of smartphones, the Pixel 11 series, which includes four phones, all powered by the new Tensor G6 chip, alongside the latest Titan M3 security processor.

The event did not stop at phones; the company also announced its new smartwatch, the Pixel Watch 5, and its first-ever Bluetooth tracker, the Pixel Tag, which helps users find their lost belongings, plus a new olive colour for the Pixel Buds Pro 2, with an update that improves active noise cancellation.

These launches arrive amid fierce competition in the flagship phone market, as companies raise their prices with rising memory and component costs, each betting on built-in AI to justify the price.

Simply put: Google is not just selling a phone, but a complete ecosystem: a phone, watch, tracker and earbuds working together on a single chip; the question is no longer about the phone's specs alone, but about the experience of the whole ecosystem.
