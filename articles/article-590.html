🛒 Google links Search and Gemini to grocery stores and banks — ask with your voice and it fills your cart!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google is rolling out a new "connected apps" feature in Search and the Gemini app: you ask with your voice for ideas — such as snacks for a party — and it suggests real products from the store, then fills your cart up to checkout.

The first grocery stores in the feature are Albertsons, Safeway, Shaws and Target, and it works in Search and the Gemini app, for delivery or pickup from the store.

The money apps include Experian, for checking your credit rating and shopping for a personal loan — available in the Gemini app now and coming to Search — and H&R Block, for calculating taxes and estimating the refund amount in Search.

Advertisement

Soon, the Angi and Thumbtack accounts will be linked in Search in the coming weeks to find local tradespeople, such as fixing a leak or hanging decorations.

The shopping apps run on Google's unified commerce protocol, and the feature is US-only for now. Google is marketing it for Thanksgiving shopping and holiday parties.

Simply put: imagine telling your phone "I want snacks for a party", and it suggests the products, fills the cart and pays for you. That is what the new feature does.
