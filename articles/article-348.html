🏠 6 AI Smart Devices That Could Make Your Home Office Smarter!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Tech companies are racing to use AI to make daily work easier, and a new list has gathered six smart devices that could raise the level of any home office. First is the "Flotica Scribe" pen at $159, an ink pen that combines handwriting with voice recording: you write your notes during a meeting, press its side to mark important points, then AI transcribes the recording, summarizes the key points, and turns them into a task list — you can even ask it about meeting details without listening to the whole recording. The pen recognizes up to 15 speakers, supports 92 languages, and has 32GB of memory.

Second is the "Anker Soundcore Work" recording pin at $159 ($99 in Black Friday deals), a coin-sized device that records meetings and voice notes for up to 8 hours on a single charge, and 32 hours with the charging case, from up to 5 meters around the meeting table. Its companion app turns recordings into organized notes and summaries, with 300 free transcription minutes per month, and paid "Pro" plans at $99 a year and "Unlimited" at $239 a year.

Then there's the "Logitech MX" keyboard at $99, which puts AI shortcuts right on your desk: dedicated buttons launch your favorite assistant or productivity tools with a single press, and it works with "GitHub Copilot", "Claude Code", "Codex", "Teams", "PowerPoint", "Excel", "Notion", "Slack", "Photoshop", "Zoom", and "Figma". And Amazon's "Kindle Scribe Colorsoft" e-reader at $479 has a color screen and AI tools that clean up your messy handwriting, evaluate shading, summarize notes, and search through all of them.

Completing the list is the "Insta360 Link 2 Pro" camera at $249 ($199 in deals), a 4K webcam that follows your movement and adjusts the frame automatically during presentations, with group tracking and AI noise cancellation. And finally the "Autonomous Desk Pro" at $499, a sit-stand desk with sensors that monitor your activity and remind you to move and stretch, track your sitting versus standing time, and even tell you the room's carbon dioxide level so you can decide to open the window.

Simply put: a pen that writes your meeting summary itself, a pin that records everything, a keyboard that summons your assistant with one press, and a desk that reminds you to stand — the home office now works with you, not just for you.
