🧠 MediaTek launches the Dimensity 9600M — a super core at 4.21 GHz and two AI units!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

MediaTek has launched its new flagship chip, the Dimensity 9600M — the second addition to the 9600 series of flagship phone chips. The chip works on a "4 + 3 + 1" cluster system: four economical C1 Pro cores at 2.7 GHz for light tasks, three C1 Premium performance cores at 3.5 GHz, and one C1 Ultra super core reaching 4.21 GHz for the heaviest tasks.

The new thing is not only speed: the chip carries a dual AI processing unit design, meaning two neural units working together to speed up AI tasks on the phone, such as improving photos, instant translation and the voice assistant, while consuming less power. The idea is like sharing work in a team: the economical cores handle simple tasks all day, while the powerful cores wake up only when needed.

The new chip is expected to power the standard phone in the upcoming Vivo X500 series, as well as the Oppo Find X10. Full availability details have not yet been revealed, but its entry into flagship phones means the competition for the strongest phone chip is heating up further between companies.

Simply put: a chip that distributes tasks smartly between economical and super cores, with two AI units working together. Does the name of the chip inside your phone matter to you, or is the daily experience alone what decides your choice?
