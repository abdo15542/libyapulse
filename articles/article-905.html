🫁 Your voice reveals asthma! AI diagnoses the disease from a voice recording!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A specialized medical journal published a new study on 22 November proving that AI can tell people with asthma apart from healthy people just by listening to their voices, without complicated devices.

Researchers at the Luxembourg Institute of Health developed a system that combines two simple voice recordings — pronouncing a stretched vowel sound and reading a short text aloud — plus clinical and demographic data such as age, weight, smoking, and medication.

The system was tested on 1,218 participants: 609 people with asthma and 609 healthy people matched to them in age, sex, and recording language, and it achieved a discrimination score of 0.83 out of 1, beating systems that rely on voice alone or on clinical data alone.

What stands out is that the system adapts itself automatically: when a patient's breathing symptoms are more severe, it relies more on the text-reading task, because it reveals how breathing coordinates with speech, while it relies more on clinical data when symptoms are mild.

More than 260 million people around the world suffer from asthma, and its diagnosis still depends on lung function tests and specialists, which makes reaching a diagnosis difficult in primary care and low-resource areas — while a voice recording from a phone could reach anywhere.

But the researchers themselves warn against exaggeration: the study was conducted on cases of asthma reported by the participants themselves, and the system still needs independent future trials before any real clinical use.

Simply put: your phone may one day become an early screening device for asthma just by you speaking into it — would you trust a diagnosis that starts from a voice recording?
