🚀 Varda’s Capsule Returns from Space and Lands in Australia!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The “Varda W-9” capsule returned from orbit and landed at the Koonibba Test Range in South Australia at 5:56 a.m. Pacific time on November 17, after 48 days in space, making it the company’s second successful return this month after the “W-8” return on November 5. Both capsules were launched together on October 1, 2026 on the same rocket — the first time two commercial reentry vehicles were launched on the same rocket. The capsule carried a hypersonic navigation system and heat-shield materials for testing, and the next capsule, “W-7,” is scheduled to return before the end of the year.

Simply put: the capsule is a small lab that flies to space and comes back; new materials and technologies are tested in it in an environment not available on Earth.
