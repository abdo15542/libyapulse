🚀 SpaceX borrows $44 billion to buy Nvidia's strongest chips!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Apollo and Goldman Sachs have announced the pricing of bonds worth about $44 billion to fund the purchase of graphics processing units and equipment for SpaceX, in the largest financing of its kind for buying AI chips to date.

The deal saw investor demand exceed supply, so its size grew beyond the original commitment of about $40 billion announced last October. The money will fund the new generation of Nvidia 'Vera Rubin' chips for SpaceX's ground computing project, with deliveries of the chips and equipment to begin in the first half of 2027, when lease schedules also begin. Key investors in the deal included BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management, and accounts managed by 'KKR'.

Simply put: imagine a company borrowing an amount bigger than the budgets of entire countries just to buy AI computers; that is how the AI race has become: whoever owns the strongest chips owns the future.
