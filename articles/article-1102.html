💰 Amazon Confirms Buying Descartes' AI Technology... and the Deal Is Worth About $7.5 Billion!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Amazon officially confirmed on Monday that it will buy the technology of AI startup Descartes, and that the company's founders and its team of researchers and engineers will join it, in the latest major talent-and-technology acquisition in the AI race.

Reports had spoken days earlier of negotiations in their final stages without official confirmation, but the new announcement settled the matter: the deal covers both the technology and the team, and will be completed after approval from regulators.

Amazon did not reveal the deal's value, but a source familiar with the matter put it at about $7.5 billion — roughly double the company's valuation just six months ago, when it raised $300 million at a valuation of nearly $4 billion.

Advertisement

Descartes, founded in 2023, develops a software engine called the "Descartes Optimization Stack" that helps companies run AI systems faster and switch easily between different chips from Nvidia, Amazon and Google. The company has been working with Amazon since 2025 to improve performance on its custom Trainium chips.

Amazon said the technology will help it improve inference — running models after they have been trained — on its custom chips, lowering costs for its customers and speeding up model training and operation.

Simply put: after weeks of rumors, Amazon officially confirms the deal and brings the whole Descartes team on board... so do big companies buy talent because they cannot build it themselves?

A company just three years old sells for about $7.5 billion... is that a fair price for the technology, or an exaggeration in the AI race?
