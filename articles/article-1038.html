💰 $250 Million for an AI Agent That Does a Banker's Job! Model ML Reaches a $1.5 Billion Valuation

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 17, Model ML announced it had raised $250 million in a new funding round, taking its market valuation to $1.5 billion — just 18 months after emerging from stealth.

The round was led by investors General Atlantic and QED, with other investors taking part, bringing the company's total raised since its founding to $350 million, following an initial $75 million round in November 2025.

The company, founded in London by brothers Chaz and Arni Englander, gives every banker, advisor and investor a personal AI agent that knows their clients, their live deals and their preferred way of working — and works inside the company's systems and data around the clock.

Advertisement

The agent does not just wait for orders: it anticipates what matters to the user, filters out the noise and gets the work done. When news breaks about a company the banker follows, the agent judges its importance, analyses its impact and prepares the analysis in the company's own formats within minutes instead of days.

The company says its annual recurring revenue grew 12-fold in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the previous year, while its daily active users rose 26-fold, and that it will use the funding to expand its research and engineering teams in London and New York.

The company is not tied to a single AI provider: it benchmarks models on real financial tasks and routes each task to the best fit, and runs a growing share of the work on its own fine-tuned models, which it says run at just 2% of the cost of leading models and five times faster on the tasks they handle.

Simply put: a smart agent that knows your deals and prepares your analysis before you even ask for it... Will the junior banker become AI's next victim?

If an AI agent builds financial models and writes due diligence reports, what is left for the human banker?
