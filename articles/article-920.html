🪙 "OKX ICE" Opens Its 24/7 Tokenized Stock Trading Platform Today! Crypto Companies' Shares First, After 67 Companies Objected!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A new platform for trading US stocks around the clock launches today — even on weekends. But the stocks on it are not ordinary shares: they are digital versions of them that live on the blockchain, and can be traded any time from a phone.

The platform is a joint project between crypto exchange "OKX" and "Intercontinental Exchange", owner of the New York Stock Exchange. It is called "OKX ICE", and trading starts today, Monday 23 November, at 9 a.m. New York time.

At launch, the platform lists more than 70 assets, most notably shares of the crypto companies themselves: "Coinbase", "Circle", "Robinhood", "Strategy" and "BitGo", plus 59 exchange-traded funds from "Direxion".

Advertisement

The idea is simple: every digital token on the platform is backed by a real share held with a licensed broker, so holders keep ordinary shareholder rights — such as dividends and voting at company meetings — while buying and selling happens automatically through liquidity pools on the "X Layer" network.

But the launch was not smooth: 67 major companies, including "Apple", "Tesla", "Meta", "Microsoft", "Nvidia" and "Amazon", objected to their shares being listed on the platform, forcing it to start with companies that agreed to take part — most of them from the crypto world.

Access to the platform is initially limited to eligible clients in the United States who have completed identity verification and activated a digital wallet. The platform is not a registered exchange or a licensed broker — it operates under a five-year experimental exemption from the US Securities and Exchange Commission.

Simply put: US stocks trading as digital tokens all day, all week — is the traditional stock market moving closer to the crypto world?

Would you buy a digital share that trades around the clock, or prefer the traditional stock market with its limited hours?
