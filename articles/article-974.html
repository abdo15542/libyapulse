🚗 'Tesla' overtakes 'Waymo' in Texas! 1507 self-driving cars registered vs 1364!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

'Tesla' has overtaken its rival 'Waymo' in the number of self-driving vehicles registered in the state of Texas, with Tesla's registered vehicles reaching 1507 as of November 22, compared with 1364 for 'Waymo', out of about 3700 vehicles registered by 17 companies.

The shift happened on November 10, when Tesla overtook its rival by a margin of just five vehicles, 1302 versus 1297, then widened the gap on the seventeenth of the same month by adding a single batch of 200 'Cybercab' cars to the register.

Tesla's registered vehicles consist of 773 'Cybercab' cars designed specifically for self-driving, and 734 'Model Y' cars, while 'Waymo's' fleet relies on about 881 'Jaguar I-Pace' cars and about 483 'Zoox' minibuses.

Together, the two companies account for about three quarters of all vehicles registered in the state, with a share of about 41% for Tesla and 37% for 'Waymo', after Tesla had only 42 vehicles in late May compared with 619 for 'Waymo'.

But the numbers need careful reading: registration in Texas means the vehicle is authorized to operate, not that it actually operates or carries passengers, as the register does not track how many cars carry passengers or earn revenue on any given day.

The Texas framework is also relatively lenient compared with other states, and registration there relies largely on self-declaration, meaning that adding a vehicle to the list is more about filing the right paperwork than passing a regulated test.

Simply put: 'Tesla' jumped from 42 vehicles to 1507 in six months to top the Texas register... does registration mean its cars are actually operating on the streets?
