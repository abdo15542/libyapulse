🎮 Your in-game companion could become your emotional support!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Today, an AI companion in games helps you track your goals and get past difficult tasks, but a peer-reviewed scientific study from Northeastern University London suggests a bigger role: a companion that cares about your feelings, not just your performance. It might notice your stress after a losing streak, encourage you to take a break, and tell you to stop playing when the night grows long.

The study imagines a companion that talks with you, remembers you between sessions, hears the tension in your voice, and may even read your body's signals: sensors in the controller detecting sweaty hands as a sign of stress, all the way to brain signals, skin conductivity, and eye tracking. But not everyone is convinced: the director of a nonprofit focused on AI risks says game studios are not the right party for mental health — they make games for fun and lack the expertise and readiness for the research and testing needed. Another study also warned that comforting robots may ease loneliness temporarily but create dependence and deepen symptoms in some users.

Simply put: the game character that asks you 'How are you?' may one day mean the question for real — but who takes responsibility for your answer?

Would you trust a virtual companion that watches your emotions while you play?
