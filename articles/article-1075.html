📷 Leak: Galaxy A38 may return to an older sensor — while the A58 keeps its camera!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New import and export records have revealed details about the main cameras of Samsung's upcoming mid-range phones, the Galaxy A38 and Galaxy A58, expected to launch in 2027 — and the surprise is that one of them may go back to an older sensor than its predecessor.

According to the records spotted by a specialist site, the Galaxy A38 may come with a 50-megapixel main sensor, the Sony IMX882, while its predecessor, the A37, carries the IMX906 sensor in some markets. The resolution is the same in both cases, but the new sensor is smaller (1/1.95 inch versus 1/1.56 inch), which could affect low-light photo quality. Notably, the Sony IMX882 was previously used in the Galaxy A36 — an older generation of the series.

The higher-end Galaxy A58, meanwhile, may keep the same IMX906 sensor used in previous generations of the A5x series, with no change to the main camera.

Advertisement

Earlier leaks painted a clearer picture of the two phones: the A38 may get a 6,000 mAh battery, a big jump from the 5,000 mAh battery in the A37, and a 12-megapixel front camera, while the A58 supports 45W charging according to a recently spotted certification, and both phones are expected to launch in March or April 2027, with the A58 starting at around €500.

But Samsung has not confirmed any of these details yet, and the sensors mentioned in the records may only be part of hardware testing. Spec downgrades also remain possible as component and manufacturing costs rise.

Simply put: a bigger battery in exchange for a smaller sensor — would you accept an older camera if you got a battery that lasts longer?

If your next phone sacrificed night photo quality for a bigger battery, which would you choose?
