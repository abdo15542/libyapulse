🎬 Oppo Find X10 Pro Max Shoots Like a Pro — the Blackmagic App Arrives Officially!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The Oppo Find X10 Pro Max has received an enhanced version of the Blackmagic Camera app that unlocks professional video features promised since the phone's launch.

The update enables recording in Dolby Vision 4K HDR at up to 60 frames per second on all three cameras, and at 120 frames per second in 4K and 30 frames per second in 8K on the main and telephoto cameras.

The app also supports the O-Log 2 format, with flat colours that give greater flexibility in colour grading after shooting, and the Open Gate feature, which uses the full 4:3 sensor area for wider coverage when cropping.

Advertisement

The phone carries three rear cameras, each 200 megapixels, developed in collaboration with Hasselblad, making it one of the strongest camera phones on the market.

To use the new features, the phone must be updated to version 17.0.1.203 or later, then install the latest version of the Blackmagic Camera app from Google Play.

Simply put: your phone now shoots to the standard of professional cinema cameras — the difference between it and a cinema camera is now in the hands of whoever holds the phone.
