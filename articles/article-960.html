🚗 XPeng Opens the Streets of Paris to Its Self-Driving Cars! Try Smart Driving from a Store in the Heart of the City!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The Chinese company XPeng announced on 22 November the launch of public test drives for its advanced driver-assistance system at its store in Paris, becoming the first Chinese car company to offer this experience through a retail store in Europe.

Customers can now book a test drive from the store on Rue Gouvion-Saint-Cyr, to try the NGP system on the real streets of Paris, including heavy congestion and complex intersections, instead of watching it in a showroom or a video.

The system works on the VLA 2.0 AI architecture — an advanced driver-assistance system designed to support driving in complex real-world situations — and the company continues to test it and adapt it to Europe's roads and regulations, in preparation for its international launch in 2027 after obtaining the necessary approvals.

Advertisement

XPeng and Tesla were the only two car companies in the world to take part in the official self-driving lab program at the 2026 Paris Motor Show, and the store initiative extends that participation, moving the experience from the showroom into everyday life.

The company's president, He Xiaopeng, said their goal is to bring 'physical AI' into everyday life and make advanced smart driving accessible to everyone, stressing that they listen to European customers' feedback to improve the experience.

Simply put: instead of watching a car drive itself in a video, you can now ride in one on the streets of Paris from an ordinary store... would you try driving without touching the steering wheel?

Would you trust a smart car to drive you through the congestion of a city you do not know?
