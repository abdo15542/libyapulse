🔔 Samsung Copies Apple's Dynamic Island.. Live Notifications Around the Camera!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung is testing a new design for live notifications in One UI 9.5, placing them in the center of the screen around the front camera cutout, much like the Dynamic Island on iPhones. The new design was spotted in early software builds for the upcoming Galaxy S27 series, and it is expected to appear officially with this series early next year.

Today, live notifications on Samsung phones appear as a small pill in the top-left corner of the status bar, showing a timer or a recording indicator, among others. The new design moves the pill to the center, makes it smaller and sharper-cornered, and replaces vertical swiping to switch between activities with horizontal swiping: you swipe left and right to move between the timer, music, and navigation without opening any of them first, while tapping the pill is enough to expand it and show the details.

The same software also revealed a new feature called 'Moment Effects', which shows small celebratory animations around the camera when your favorite team scores a goal or wins a match, relying on the Google Sports service. The design is still being tested and has not been finalized, and the current beta version of One UI 9.5 is only available for Galaxy S26 phones in a limited number of countries. Samsung will not be the first Android company to offer a similar feature, as Chinese companies beat it to it.

Simply put: your phone's notifications are moving to the center of the screen around the camera; do you prefer Apple's look or Samsung's old look?
