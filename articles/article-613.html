📱 OnePlus confirms the "Ace 6T" launch this month — pre-orders have begun!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OnePlus confirmed via an official post the launch of its new phone "Ace 6T" in China during the current month of November, and opened pre-orders for it immediately.

The full official specs of the phone are still secret, but leaks expect it to run on Qualcomm's "Snapdragon 8 Gen 5" chip — a strong chip, but below the flagship "8 Elite Gen 5" chip.

Leaks also point to RAM of up to 16 GB and internal storage of up to 1 terabyte — numbers that compete with flagship phones.

Advertisement

OnePlus's "Ace" series is known for offering strong specs at a lower price than flagship phones, which is why many people who want high performance without paying the price of the most expensive phone are waiting for it.

"Pre-order" means you order the phone and pay in advance before it reaches stores, to guarantee your copy as soon as it is available.

Simply put: imagine a strong phone at a reasonable price arriving this month. OnePlus opened pre-orders before revealing all its secrets.
