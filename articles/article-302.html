💼 Startup raises $48.5 million to connect stores directly to factories in China!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Startup Everful has raised $48.5 million in a new funding round, to connect stores and small brands directly to factories in China and manage the whole process from order to delivery.

The round was led by Vertex Ventures and January Capital, with participation from existing investors. The company was founded in 2024 by Sisi Song, a former Alibaba manager, and Kevin Han, who sold his previous company to Amazon. The company ships more than 5 million items a month to customers in more than 60 countries, works with more than 30,000 factories, and its catalog holds more than 20 million products. It uses AI agents to track consumer trends, find suppliers, coordinate orders across factories, and manage logistics, and plans to expand in the United States and Europe. The company expects to reach $100 million in annual revenue by the end of the year, after only about two and a half years since its launch.

Simply put: imagine a small store ordering its goods from a factory in China at the touch of a button, with no middlemen and no huge minimum order; that is how Everful wants to give small stores the same buying power big stores have.
