The World's Fastest Soft Robot Runs Without Any Electronics! 🤖

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Engineers at the University of California San Diego have built the fastest robot made entirely of soft materials, in a study published yesterday: a six-legged robot, 3D-printed in a single piece from a single material, with no electronics, powered only by compressed air. Its top speed reached 4.72 meters per second — about 17 kilometers per hour — more than a hundred times faster than the team's 2022 version, which moved at just 4 centimeters per second.

Imagine a toy made of flexible rubber with no batteries and no wires — moving on puffs of air alone, like inflating a balloon. The engineers borrowed a trick from a 19th-century steam engine, making the compressed air push twice: first through small high-pressure chambers that operate the valves, then through larger low-pressure chambers that move the legs. The robot runs on sand, gravel, and grass, goes down stairs, swims in water, and walks underwater. It currently works tethered to a compressed-air source, and the next step is a free-standing version with a portable air cartridge.
