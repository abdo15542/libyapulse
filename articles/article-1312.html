📱 Galaxy Z TriFold 2 Appears in Leaks — and Another Limited Release!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new leak has revealed that the successor to Samsung's first tri-fold phone, the Galaxy Z TriFold 2, is in development under the codename Q9m and could reach the market in mid-2027.

The leak suggests the phone may get a limited release once again, like the first generation, which was sold only in South Korea, Singapore, Taiwan, the UAE, and the US.

Reports speak of a thinner, lighter, and more durable design than its predecessor, with an entirely new hinge.

Advertisement

No confirmed specifications have been revealed yet, and Samsung has not announced anything official about the phone, so every detail so far is just leaks that could change.

Simply put: Samsung has not given up on the tri-fold phone idea, but it may keep it restricted to a few markets — the codename alone is enough to confirm the project is alive.
