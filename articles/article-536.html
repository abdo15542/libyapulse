🎬 Runway's Agent Makes a Film from a Sentence — Cinematic Video by Conversation Instead of Buttons!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Runway launched a new agent that creates cinematic video clips through text conversation — instead of adjusting dozens of settings and buttons, the user describes what they want in a sentence, such as turning their notes into a film, and the agent produces the video itself across multiple scenes.

The new interface replaces manual parameter tuning with an ongoing dialogue: the user explains the idea, and the agent takes over building the scenes and linking them, producing a multi-scene video from simple descriptions. The goal is for anyone to make a cinematic video without experience in editing or directing.

Simply put: imagine telling a short story to a digital director, and minutes later he returns with a short film of several scenes — that is what Runway's new agent promises.

Would you try making a video with just a text description, or do you prefer full control over every detail yourself?
