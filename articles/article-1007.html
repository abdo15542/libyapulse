🏠 Google retires the Nest app for good... and Gemini becomes the voice of your smart home!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google announced it is merging Nest devices into the Google Home app, and retiring the separate Nest app, so owners of these devices will have one app instead of two.

The voice side is changing too: the old Google Assistant is being discontinued in the smart home, replaced by Gemini, Google's AI model, as the voice assistant, making the Home app the only place where you control your devices and talk to your home.

The devices themselves will not disappear, but will be merged into the new app, so cameras, thermostats and speakers are supposed to keep working within the new setup, without their owners having to replace them.

Advertisement

The change came because Google was running two apps for overlapping functions, and an old assistant alongside a newer one, and merging them into one app with Gemini is a way to end this split — and the company has not yet announced a precise schedule for each step or which devices the change covers first, so you should check your device and the Home app for notifications.

Simply put: one app and one assistant for your whole home... are you ready to move from Nest to Google Home?
