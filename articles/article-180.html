🎁 Apple Reveals Black Friday Deals: Gift Cards Worth Up to $300!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple revealed the details of its Black Friday 2026 event, which starts on November 27 and runs until November 30, known as Cyber Monday. The company will offer gift cards worth up to $300 with the purchase of selected devices, such as a $100 card with iPhone Air, $75 with iPhone 17 and iPhone 16, up to $125 with iPad Air and iPad Pro, and the highest value of $300 with MacBook Pro and MacBook Air. The offers also include Mac computers, Apple Watch, and AirPods, similar to last year.

Simply put: Apple does not cut the prices of its devices on Black Friday; instead, it gives you a gift card of a certain value with each device, which you can use later in its store.
