🍎 Apple tightens Mac security: AI agents will no longer see all your files!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple has announced new changes to Mac security controls, targeting AI agents that request full-disk access. The changes arrive with the second beta of macOS 27.3, after worrying incidents, including a claim that a smart agent read private messages without permission, and a flaw in another app that could have exposed sensitive data.

The most important change is splitting the 'Device control and data access' setting into two separate options: 'Bypass system and app protections' and 'Accessibility'. The first is for automation tools that control other apps, and the second for assistive technologies such as screen readers. This way, users clearly know when they grant an app permission to bypass the system's built-in protections.

You can now also grant full-disk access to specific folders only instead of the whole disk, specify which apps the agent can control one by one, and request approval every time it needs access. A new transparency indicator will appear in the menu bar when an app controls another app, and previously granted permissions will remain in effect without needing to be set up again. Developers of backup and assistive-technology apps can request special exceptions to keep their functions as they were.

Simply put: Apple is not stopping AI agents from working on the Mac, but it is making it harder to hand them the full keys to your device — and it asks you first about every door you open to them.
