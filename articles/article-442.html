📱 'Claude' Redesigns Its Phone App — a Bottom Bar and Four Tabs!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The "Claude" AI app has begun rolling out a completely new design on phones, with a bottom bar of four tabs: Chat, Projects, Coding, and Products. The design was announced by Daniel DeStefano, who works on consumer products at "Anthropic", in a post on the "X" platform on November 20.

The new design aims to make "Claude" on the phone more than just a quick chat box, designed to support coding, projects, and products on the go, and to make resuming sessions away from the computer easier. The dedicated tab bar also gives the input field greater focus.

The rollout began on iPhone first, reaching Android later. The change comes just one day after "OpenAI" announced a redesign of the "ChatGPT" phone app, which also has a bottom tab bar — a sign of a clear race between the two companies over the shape of the AI app.

Advertisement

"Anthropic" had made major updates to "Projects" in "Claude Code" days earlier, with the coordinator now distributing tasks across parallel tracks and notifying the user of updates itself, in a public beta for "Pro" and "Max" plan subscribers.

Simply put: "Claude" is turning on your phone from a quick chat into a complete workspace, with four tabs putting coding and projects one tap away.
