📱 Huawei Nova 16s Pro arrives worldwide — a 7,000 mAh battery in a body just 7.1 mm thick!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Huawei confirmed the global launch of the Nova 16s and Nova 16s Pro phones, starting in Malaysia on November 19, 2026, after the series was released in China last September. What is new now is its arrival in global markets.

The standout number on the phone is the battery: 7,000 mAh inside a body just 7.1 mm thick and weighing 218 grams, thanks to silicon-carbon battery technology that packs more energy into the same space compared with traditional lithium batteries.

The camera is no less exciting: a 200-megapixel main camera with an RYYB sensor measuring 1/1.28 inches, a 50-megapixel periscope camera for zoom, the company's own Kunlun glass protecting the screen, and Kirin processors developed by Huawei itself.

Advertisement

Comparing with the previous generation makes the picture clear: the Nova 15 Pro had a 6,500 mAh battery, so the jump to 7,000 is real but modest. The bigger change is the thinness — the same capacity in a thinner, lighter body, making two days of use on one charge a realistic expectation.

But there is an important warning before buying: Huawei's global phones do not come with Google Mobile Services, and that still matters for anyone whose daily life depends on Google apps as they work on other Android phones. The hardware is genuinely impressive, but the software needs checking before you buy.

Simply put: imagine a phone thinner than most phones, yet with a bigger battery than phones much larger than it. The idea is that a big battery no longer means a phone heavy as a brick.

Would you buy a phone with a giant battery and a slim body despite the absence of Google services, or are Google services a condition you won't compromise on?
