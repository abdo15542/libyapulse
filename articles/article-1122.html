🔍 Google Search Reads Your Wallet and Files.. Personal Intelligence Comes to "Wallet", "Drive", and "Gemini"!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Google" announced that Search has become more personalized, as users can now connect new apps to the "Personal Intelligence" feature inside AI Mode in Search, so its answers reach directly into the context of their personal life.

The rollout began with "Google Wallet": it can be optionally connected starting today, so Search maps your financial goals, suggests ways to cut daily spending, and benefits from your cards' perks such as cashback and travel points, with confirmation that AI Mode only reads the information and cannot move your money, manage it, or invest it.

"Drive" and the "Gemini" app arrive in the coming weeks: with "Drive" connected, Search takes into account your documents, spreadsheets, and scattered PDF files, suggesting, for example, a personal preparation plan before tax season, and with "Gemini" connected the two apps exchange context, so what you start in one the other continues without you starting over.

Advertisement

These three apps join "Gmail", "Calendar", and "Photos", which were already connected to the feature, and a "Personalize" icon will soon appear below each personalized answer to show the context Search used, while "AI Overviews" and AI Mode answers will become more connected to the context of your latest search.

Connecting is entirely optional: you enable it from the personalization settings in Search, then "Connected apps", and you can turn it off at any time, and you can also correct any piece of information simply by telling AI Mode what has changed for you.

Simply put: your search now knows your spending, your files, and your chats.. to answer you as if it knows you personally.. so will you let it into your wallet and files?

Do you connect your wallet and files to Google Search in exchange for more accurate answers?
