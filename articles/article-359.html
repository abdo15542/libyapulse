💳 Visa Launches 'Recurrent' to Manage Your Subscriptions from Inside Your Bank's App!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Visa has announced a new product called "Visa Recurrent" for subscription management, letting consumers view and act on their eligible recurring payments from inside their bank's digital app, instead of searching merchant sites one by one. The product became available to participating financial institutions in the Asia-Pacific region starting November 19, with "Kiwibank" as the first issuer in the world to adopt it, unveiled on the second day of the Singapore FinTech Festival 2026.

Consumers get a unified view of all their recurring payments and supported actions inside the bank app they already use, while businesses receive instant notifications when customers act, and can use links to engage them directly. The product is Visa's latest subscription-management solution, alongside its payment-stop service and subscription manager.

The announcement was based on Visa research finding that one in three consumers in New Zealand says they kept paying for a service they no longer use, and that 86% want their banks to provide tools to manage recurring payments. The survey covered 400 New Zealand consumers online in October 2026.
