🎮 GTA 6 Breaks Every Record on PlayStation! 39 Million Players and 683 Million Hours Played!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

PlayStation has announced that GTA 6 has become the biggest launch in the entire history of the platform, across three decades and five generations of consoles, after more than 39 million players played it on PlayStation 5 in its first days.

Between November 19 and 22, the game set a new record for the number of simultaneous players for any game on PlayStation, beating every previous multiplayer, free, and single-player game.

Players also clocked a total of 683 million hours inside the game, an average of 17.4 hours per player in the first days, a sign of how deeply the game hooked players from the moment it came out.

The launch also saw the largest number of newly connected PlayStation 5 consoles in a single day since the console came out, outside the holiday season, and the game was the first game played on 70% of those new consoles.

This comes after the publisher announced that the game made $3.79 billion on its first day and sold more than 32.5 million copies, and the new figures confirm that the rush went beyond even those record numbers.

Simply put: 39 million players in four days, 683 million hours played, and the biggest launch in PlayStation history. Have you played GTA 6 yet?
