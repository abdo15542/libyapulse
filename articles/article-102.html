Phones & Devices

A new leak suggests Samsung may soon start charging a subscription for some AI features on Galaxy phones. The details appeared on an updated support page: the free plan will keep only three custom cards in the Daily Brief feature, which summarises your day for you, while anyone who wants more cards will need a paid plan. Creating or editing cards will also use up a balance of credits shared across your devices. The expected subscription price is around five dollars a month. The feature has been free since it launched with the Galaxy S24 phones. Would you pay a monthly subscription for AI features on your phone? 💳
