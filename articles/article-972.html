⚖️ 'Meta' delays its commercial terms to 2027! Three-stage consultations with publishers and regulators!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 18, 'Meta' announced details of its plan to consult on its proposed updates to its commercial terms of service, after halting those updates following objections from news publishers and others about how they would apply to its web crawlers.

The company had halted the proposed updates on November 5, after they were scheduled to take effect on the twentieth of the same month, and removed the preview of the proposed text, saying it would review the changes after deeper consultations.

The consultation plan is divided into three phases: the first is to listen to stakeholders, with the company having started briefing sessions and discussions with publishers in different markets and global industry groups, and these talks are expected to continue over the next few months.

The second phase is dedicated to studying the feedback received and reviewing the originally proposed text in light of it, while the third phase comes in early 2027, when the company expects to reach out again to publishers, stakeholders and regulators about the updated terms before applying them.

The company will also prepare an explanatory document that puts the proposed terms in context and helps partners understand them fully, stressing that it follows the standard 'robots' mechanism in how its crawlers deal with websites.

The company has not set a new date for the terms to take effect, but it confirmed it will notify account holders in advance before any new terms apply, giving them time to review them.

Simply put: 'Meta' halted its new terms after publishers objected, and will listen to them for months before presenting a revised version in 2027... will consultations be enough to protect publishers' content?
