📱 iPhone 18 with bigger memory at last! The base model may come with 12 GB of RAM!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple is working on equipping the base iPhone 18 with 12 GB of RAM, a big jump from the 8 GB in the iPhone 17, according to new press reports.

Apple is in talks with its supplier Samsung to increase supplies of LPDDR5X memory chips, and is also negotiating with SK Hynix and Micron to secure additional quantities of mobile phone memory.

This step comes as memory prices soar worldwide because of huge demand from AI data centers, making securing supplies a real challenge even for the biggest phone makers.

Advertisement

The bigger memory means a faster phone at multitasking, and a better ability to run AI features on the device itself without waiting for the cloud — the direction every phone maker is racing toward today.

The iPhone 18 is expected to arrive next year, and the details are still not officially confirmed, as Apple has announced nothing yet, and the reports rely on sources in the supply chain.

Simply put: your next phone may carry 50% more memory, so AI runs on it more smoothly — is 12 GB enough, or will the memory race never stop?
