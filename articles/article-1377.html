📸 Vivo Officially Launches the X500 Series in India… 2K Display and 144Hz Refresh Rate!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Vivo has officially announced the launch of its flagship X500 series in India, with three phones: the X500, the X500 Pro, and the X500 Pro Max, days after their prices leaked ahead of the official announcement.

The series runs on the flagship MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro processor, while the X500 Pro Max carries a 6.85-inch AMOLED display with 2K resolution, a refresh rate of up to 144 Hz, and a density of 507 pixels per inch.

The phones run OriginOS 7, based on Android 17, and the two Pro versions support dual SIM, while the X500 comes in Ember Red and Titanium Black, and the Pro and Pro Max in Summit Orange and Eternal Black.

Advertisement

The series will go on sale soon in India through Amazon, Flipkart, and the company's online store, in one of the strongest waves of flagship phones to reach the Indian market before the end of the year.

The official launch settles all the earlier leaks about specs and prices, and puts the series in direct competition with Samsung's and Oppo's flagship phones in the same market.

Simply put: three flagship phones with a powerful processor and an ultra-smooth display; is that enough for Vivo to beat its rivals in the premium phone category?
