📱 Security Certificate Reveals an Unannounced Chip in the Foldable Motorola Razr Flex!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A security certification document that surfaced on 19 November has revealed new details about Motorola's foldable Razr Flex phone — most notably that it runs on the Snapdragon SM8845 Pro chip, which Qualcomm has not officially announced yet.

The document comes from a Dutch certification body and concerns Motorola phone model XT2761, codenamed "Parker", while regulatory filings in the UAE link the same model to the Razr Flex 27 — making the connection strong, even though the document does not name the phone explicitly.

The certificate also states that the phone runs Android 17, supports Bluetooth 6.0, and has an under-display fingerprint sensor instead of a side-mounted one, plus face unlock, while earlier filings point to Wi-Fi 7, NFC, and wireless charging.

Earlier leaks point to a 6.92-inch inner display and a 5000 mAh battery, and the phone scored 2669 in single-core and 8837 in multi-core performance tests in October with 12 GB of memory — numbers that match the regular Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chip.

Notably, an unannounced chip appearing in an official certificate usually means the launch is not very close, since companies normally launch a phone after or together with the chip announcement, and expectations point to an announcement in early 2027 and sales in the spring, while a Portuguese store listed the phone at €1510.90 for the 12 GB and 256 GB version.

Simply put: an official document has exposed the chip of a phone that has not been announced yet, and revealed that its launch is further away than many thought.. Would you rather wait for a foldable phone with a newer chip, or buy what is available now?
