📉 Global Phone Sales Drop 16%… Android at Its Lowest Level and iPhone Sets a Record!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new report revealed that global smartphone sales fell by 16% year on year in the third quarter of 2026, a drop that reflects a deep crisis hitting the phone market.

The report blames the decline on the RAM shortage crisis, which hit the budget segment hard and raised prices in the higher segments, making buying a new phone harder for many people.

Android's share fell to 74% of global phone sales in the quarter, down five points from last year, the lowest share the system has ever recorded in a third quarter.

iOS, meanwhile, recorded its highest-ever share in a third quarter at 21%, as iPhone sales grew by 2%, driven by strong demand for the iPhone 18 Pro series, despite Apple raising prices across its entire lineup.

In China, Huawei's HarmonyOS took 22% of phone sales, while Android remained dominant in India at 92%, and iOS led in the United States at 54%.

Simply put: the phone market is shrinking and memory prices are pushing prices up; would you delay buying your new phone because of the high prices?
