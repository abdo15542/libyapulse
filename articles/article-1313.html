🛒 Meta's Muse Agent Takes 51% of AI-Agent Purchases — Beating Grok and ChatGPT!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New data has revealed that Meta's AI agent Muse completed 51% of all orders finished by AI agents on behalf of shoppers during the week of 18 to 24 November, far ahead of its closest competitors.

xAI's Grok agent came second with 22%, followed by OpenAI's ChatGPT with 18%, while Instinct, the standalone ChatGPT agent, and Google shared the remaining share.

Over the last ninety days, Muse took 45% of orders and 43% of total spending, with an average order of $219 — slightly below the $230 average across all agents.

Advertisement

Muse works as a personal assistant inside the Muse app and WhatsApp: it browses the store's own pages, fills in purchase forms, and pays after your approval, using a one-time virtual card so your real card number never appears, and it asks your permission before completing any purchase.

The agent first appeared in September 2026, and since then agent orders have multiplied about 9.5 times. Meta's CEO said shopping accounts for a quarter of Muse usage, and that the company will take a small fee on transactions made through it.

Simply put: AI no longer just recommends products to you — it now buys them and pays for them on your behalf, and Muse is leading this new race unchallenged so far.
