💾 The Regular "Galaxy S27 Ultra" May Miss Out on the Fastest Memory — "Samsung" Reserves the New Tech for the Pricier Tiers!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new leak suggests "Samsung" may reserve its latest memory and storage technologies for only the priciest tiers of the upcoming "Galaxy S27" series, leaving most buyers on the previous generation of memory despite paying flagship prices.

According to the leak, only three of ten variants will get "LPDDR6" memory and "UFS 5.0" storage: the "S27 Pro" with 1 TB of storage and 16 GB of memory, and two "S27 Ultra" variants with 512 GB and 1 TB.

The remaining variants will stay on "LPDDR5X" memory and "UFS 4.1" storage, including the base "Ultra" with 256 GB, all "S27" and "S27 Plus" variants, and the cheaper "Pro" variants.

Advertisement

The reason, according to reports, is the sharp rise in memory chip prices, as AI data centers take most of global production, leaving less available for phones and pushing prices up — and "Samsung" prefers to hold its phone prices down rather than raise them further.

"UFS 5.0" storage reads at up to 10.8 GB per second, and "LPDDR6" memory runs at up to 10.7 gigabits per second — a big leap over the previous generation, but one that will reach only a few buyers if the leak is accurate.

"Samsung" has not announced the series yet, and it is expected to launch in January or February 2027; all of these details remain unconfirmed leaks for now.

Simply put: you pay for "Samsung's" most expensive phone but get last year's memory, unless you pay more for the higher storage capacity; that is how the company splits its new memory by price.

Would you accept a cheaper variant getting older memory in the same phone?
