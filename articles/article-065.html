Allianz warns: AI losses are far bigger than the reported figures suggest! 💰

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Insurance company Allianz warned in a report published today that confirmed AI-related losses are far lower than the true exposure, because most AI claims are recorded under existing categories — such as cybercrime, directors' liability, and products — without being identified as AI losses. The company says it is already tracking claims worth millions of euros, and that individual cases could produce sector-level losses of around $100 million per case.

The numbers tell the story: reported incidents rose from 179 in 2023 to 453 in 2025, more than doubling in two years. US class-action lawsuits linked to AI jumped from 7 in 2023 to 22 by the end of last August. The FBI attributed about $893 million in AI-related cybercrime costs to US citizens in 2025. And AI climbed from tenth to second place in Allianz's 2026 risk barometer.

Do you expect AI mistakes to become the biggest insurance bill of the future? 📈
