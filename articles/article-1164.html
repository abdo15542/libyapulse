💬 GPT-6.2 Sol Reaches ChatGPT Chats… and GPT-5.6 Says Goodbye in December!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

OpenAI has begun rolling out the chat version of its GPT-6.2 Sol model in the chat tab inside ChatGPT worldwide, for subscribers to the Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise plans, less than a week after it appeared in work and coding tools.

The company says this version is tuned for everyday conversations, so it is better at distinguishing image details and following instructions when searching the web, and it can, for example, suggest an alert when the price of a product the user is searching for drops, or weigh a nearer hotel against a cheaper one when planning a trip.

In the same announcement, the company set a retirement date for two older models: GPT-5.6 Sol leaves chat, work, and coding on December 23, and GPT-5.6 Tera leaves work and coding on the same day, less than six months after they were launched.

Simply put: the new model is now the default in your everyday chats, and the old one has only one month left… so if you rely on GPT-5.6 for your work, it is time to move to GPT-6.2.
