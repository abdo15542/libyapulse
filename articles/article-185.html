📱 The 20th Anniversary iPhone: Curved Glass on All Sides and Titanium Returns!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New leaks suggest that Apple is working on a special phone for the iPhone’s 20th anniversary, which could launch next year. According to a leaker, the company is testing several designs, including a front glass that curves around all four edges of the phone, after years of working to perfect this curve. Titanium is also expected to return to the phone’s frame.

Simply put: the anniversary phone could look like a single piece of glass with almost no edges; it is a design that is hard to manufacture, which is why Apple is testing more than one version before settling on it.
