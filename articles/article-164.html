🎨 Canva Lets You Turn Off Generative AI Tools with One Tap!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Canva launched two new settings that give users more control over AI: the first is a switch to turn generative AI tools on or off across the whole platform, and the second is an option to see less AI-generated content when searching the library of templates and elements. Both settings are available free to Free and Pro plan subscribers, and can be reversed at any time. But some editing tools will keep working even with the switch off, such as background removal and moving elements inside a design, because they work on content that already exists and do not generate anything new.

Simply put: generative AI is what creates new images and text from scratch; now you can hide these tools if you prefer to design by yourself.
