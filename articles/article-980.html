📈 Chip stocks bounce back hard! Nvidia jumps 5.8% after its worst week since 2008!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

After a brutal week, tech stocks bounced back sharply in Sunday night trading: Nvidia shares jumped 5.8% after plunging 23% over the week — its worst weekly drop since September 2008.

The rebound was not limited to Nvidia: Intel rose 4.8%, AMD 5.4%, Micron 4.6%, and SanDisk 5.2%, while the semiconductor index climbed 4.2%.

The earlier drop came amid fears that AI valuations were overblown, and after Anthropic's IPO opened 17% higher then lost momentum, on top of Nvidia's crisis after its CEO's departure and the ongoing investigations around it.

Analyst Dan Ives commented that the pessimists will feel they were right, but 'nothing will stop the AI revolution', pointing to energy demand of 160 gigawatts through 2029 against planned capacity of 80 to 100 gigawatts, and naming Apple, SpaceX, cybersecurity firms, and the biggest cloud computing companies among the likely winners.

Retail trader sentiment, meanwhile, was strongly optimistic about Nvidia, optimistic about AMD, Micron, and SanDisk, and pessimistic about Intel and the semiconductor index.

Simply put: a week wipes out gains, and one night brings some of them back... do you see in this rebound the start of a recovery, or just a calm before a new wave?
