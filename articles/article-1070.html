📷 Kodak's 'Slide Tag': Your Photos on Your Keychain — and It Finds Your Keys If They Get Lost!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Rito has launched a new small device under the Kodak brand called the 'Slide Tag', combining a miniature photo frame and a tracker in one. It went on sale on 22 November for $35.

Shaped like a vintage film slide holder, the device carries a 1.54-inch colour e-ink display with a resolution of 240 by 240 pixels in six colours, showing up to 12 photos that change automatically every day or at the press of a button, and the image stays visible without consuming power, since e-ink screens need no electricity to keep the image.

Photos reach the device via Bluetooth from the 'Kodak Memory Link' app on the phone, and the screen can also display a QR code, a name card, a to-do list or a countdown timer. The device also works as a tracker compatible with both Apple's Find My and Google's Find Hub networks, with a range of up to 30 metres and a 75-decibel bell to help you find it nearby.

Advertisement

It runs on a replaceable coin battery lasting around a year, weighs just 16.6 grams, and resists dust and splashes with an IP54 rating, though it is not fully waterproof. It is sold in blind boxes that hide the design until opened: eight standard designs and a rare secret metallic design with a 1-in-64 chance, and the full set of eight designs for $279.92.

Simply put: your favourite photos hanging from your keys, and the keys themselves will never get lost again... would you buy a device that combines memories and tracking in one piece?

If your tiny photo frame can also find your keys, would you give up your traditional tracker?
