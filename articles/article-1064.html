📱 Honor 700 officially launched — a smart rear display, a 9000 battery, and a 200-megapixel camera!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Honor has officially launched its new Honor 700 phone series in China, with three phones — the 700, the 700 Pro, and the 700S — and the highlights are a small screen on the back of the phone, a huge battery, and a high-resolution camera.

The rear display is a square 1.59-inch touchscreen sitting next to the camera unit. It shows the time and notifications, works as a live preview when taking a selfie with the rear camera, and also displays heart rate and blood oxygen levels thanks to a dedicated optical sensor that measures them from the back of the phone.

All three phones carry a 200-megapixel main camera and a 12-megapixel wide camera, while the 700 and 700 Pro add a 50-megapixel periscope camera with 3.5x optical zoom, along with a 50-megapixel front camera and a 6.57-inch AMOLED front display with high resolution, a 120Hz refresh rate, and an in-display fingerprint sensor.

The 700 Pro runs on the Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chip, the 700 on the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4, and the 700S on the Snapdragon 7 Gen 4, with up to 16GB of memory and up to 512GB of storage, and MagicOS 11 based on Android 17.

All three phones share a 9,000mAh battery with 80W wired charging, while only the Pro model supports 50W wireless charging, plus IP69K water and dust resistance, and colors in white, light blue, pink, and dark blue.

Prices in China start at 3,599 yuan (about $528) for the 700S, 3,999 yuan (about $587) for the 700, and 4,599 yuan (about $675) for the Pro, with a 300-yuan launch discount for early buyers, and open sales begin on 27 November. The company has not yet announced when the series will reach the rest of the world.

Simply put: a small screen on the back of the phone that measures your pulse and shows your photo before you take it — has the back of the phone become a second screen?
