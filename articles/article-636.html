💬 Spaces reaches Plus subscribers in ChatGPT — a collaborative workspace instead of a conversation!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI opened its collaborative workspace "Spaces" to Plus plan subscribers on November 20, after it had been restricted to the pricier Pro, Business and Enterprise plans since its appearance at the developers' conference in late September.

Spaces is not a new conversation, but a group workspace: it has "Pages" — editable documents that a whole team can work on at the same time — with ChatGPT inside the space itself as an assistant, not a separate tab.

Pages can update themselves from the tools connected to them, such as a city guide that updates itself or a study guide with 3D images and test questions, and spaces also work with the scheduled-tasks feature.

The space also includes "Slides" — collaborative presentations built and edited directly, which can be exported to PowerPoint, PDF or Google Slides — while the "Sheets" spreadsheets arrive later and have not landed yet.

Spaces replaces the old "Library" section in the sidebar, and works on the web and the desktop app, with page editing also on iPhones.

But not everything arrived with it: the permanent agents "Dots" are not included in the Plus plan yet, as the company began testing them with only a small group of subscribers on November 19.

Sharing also has conditions: to open a shared presentation, the other party must have a paid account with Spaces — free users cannot open it, and there are no public sharing links.

The rollout is gradual for everyone aged 18 and over: if you don't find Spaces in your app, update it first, and if it still hasn't appeared, your turn hasn't come yet.

Simply put: ChatGPT is no longer just a chat box — it has become a shared office where your team works with AI on the same document.
