💻 'Cognition' Negotiates New Funding at a $75 Billion Valuation — Coding Agents Are on Fire!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Startup "Cognition", which builds autonomous coding agents, has entered talks for a new funding round at a $75 billion valuation, in which it could raise $2 billion or more, according to reports citing people familiar with the matter. The talks are still ongoing, and details could change.

The news comes just about two months after the company raised $2 billion in a Series "E" funding round at a $48 billion valuation, led by new investors "Andreessen Horowitz" and "Accel", alongside existing backers "Founders Fund", "General Catalyst", and "Avenir". The company had been valued at $26 billion in a May round, when it raised $1 billion.

"Cognition" builds autonomous software-engineering agents that can plan, write, test, and deploy code with limited human intervention. The company did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

Simply put: a valuation is a company's estimated market value, and jumping from $26 billion to $48 billion and then to $75 billion in a few months means investors are betting heavily that coding agents will transform the software industry.
