📱 Galaxy S27 Prices May Rise More Than Planned… The Memory Crisis Is Pushing Phone Costs Up!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Samsung is considering raising the prices of its upcoming Galaxy S27 phone series above the prices it had originally planned, because of the continuing rise in phone-memory prices, which has eaten into the company's profits throughout 2026.

The company's phone and networks divisions recorded an operating loss of 600 billion Korean won in the third quarter of the year, even though it raised the prices of the Galaxy S26 phones and foldable devices, while the average price it pays for phone memory jumped 263% in the first nine months of the year compared with last year's average, and it spent 7.7 trillion won on memory alone.

Leaks suggest the planned prices are $899 for the base version, $1,099 for the Plus version, $1,299 for the new Pro version and $1,399 for the Ultra version — but the company may raise them further, as research firms expect memory prices to surge again in the first quarter of 2027, the same time mass production of the series peaks.

The company is expected to launch the series in early 2027 with the new Exynos 2700 processor in most versions, alongside a new Pro version without a stylus, and a single 5x optical-zoom telephoto camera in the Ultra version — and the company has not confirmed any official price yet.

Simply put: AI data centres are buying memory in huge quantities, so prices rise, and in the end the phone buyer pays the price... Have AI phones themselves become victims of AI's success?
