🎬 New Shorts and Now Bar in One UI 9.5 — your photos turn into a short video with one tap!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A specialist site has discovered a new hidden feature in the beta version of One UI 9.5 for Galaxy S26 phones, called Shorts, which turns your favorite photos and video clips into short clips ready to share with a single tap.

The feature works inside the Gallery app with Galaxy AI, in two ways: automatic generation, where the phone picks the photos and clips and arranges them, or manual creation with a text description — you type, for example, “photos from my last trip,” and the phone gathers the matching photos and clips and assembles them into a short video. You can customize the clip with fonts and extra downloadable background music, and all clips appear in the Stories section inside the Gallery.

But there is an important note: Shorts may be a paid feature within the Galaxy AI package, it is still under development and hidden in the beta, and it may appear officially in early 2027 with the launch of the Galaxy S27.

Advertisement

The leaks did not stop there: the Now Bar, Samsung's live notifications bar, gets a new design that moves notifications from beside the clock to the middle of the top of the screen, around the selfie camera, resembling the Dynamic Island on the iPhone, with horizontal swipes to move between activities instead of vertical ones. There is also a Moment Effects feature for sports alerts and celebrations of results as they happen, and a Gen Video tool that turns still photos into short clips.

The beta version of One UI 9.5 is available now for the S26 series in India and other countries, and the stable version is expected with the Galaxy S27 — and these features may reach older phones too.

Simply put: your old photos have become a share-ready video, and your notifications have moved to the middle of the screen — will you use Shorts for your clips or do you prefer editing yourself?

If your phone automatically turned your trip photos into a short video, would you share it right away or review it first?
