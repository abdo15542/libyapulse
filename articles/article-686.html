📱 Leaked prototypes: the iPhone Air 2 may get a second camera!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Famous leaker Sonny Dickson has published prototypes of next year's iPhones, and the highlight is that the iPhone Air 2 may come with two cameras instead of one, a clear change from the current generation.

Prototypes are non-working, full-size models made by leakers and accessory makers to prepare cases before launch, and they usually reveal the device's exterior shape and the positions of cameras and buttons. The new models show two adjacent lenses on the camera surface of the iPhone Air 2, after the first generation had only a single lens.

The phone is expected to arrive in March 2027, according to earlier reports, but Apple has not confirmed any details yet, and the specifications may change before the final launch. The second camera could mean better photos in low light, and possibly new photography features, but its exact function is still unknown.

Simply put: the thinnest iPhone may give up its single camera. Was the single camera the biggest flaw of the iPhone Air in your opinion?
