🏢 KKR's Helix in Talks to Buy a Majority of EdgeCore, a Data Center Deal That Could Reach $10 Billion!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Helix Digital Infrastructure, the AI infrastructure investment arm of the KKR group, is negotiating to acquire a majority stake in data center operator EdgeCore, in a deal that could value the company at between $8 billion and $10 billion, according to reports published on November 20.

EdgeCore owns more than 2.27 gigawatts of data center capacity leased or under development in Virginia, Arizona, Nevada, and California, including a huge complex in Louisa County, Virginia, with a planned capacity of more than 1.1 gigawatts. Partners Group bought EdgeCore in 2022 with an initial commitment of $1.2 billion, then later committed an additional $1.9 billion.

Helix is led by Adam Selipsky, the former CEO of Amazon Web Services, and has raised about $11 billion from investors including Nvidia and the Kuwait Investment Authority, and the EdgeCore deal would be its first major deal if it goes through. But talks are still ongoing and could end without an agreement, and all three parties declined to comment on the reports.

Simply put: data centers have become the most expensive assets of the AI era, so much so that a major investment firm is ready to pay $10 billion for a stake in them; so do you think the race to build data centers is still in its early stages, or have prices already reached their peak?
