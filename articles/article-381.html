🏛️ 'Injective' Approves the 'Vera' Upgrade — Real-World Assets Enter the Blockchain for Institutions!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Injective" token holders have approved the "Vera" upgrade, the network's last major upgrade of 2026, with 99.51% of votes in favor. Voting on proposal 717 opened on November 12 and closed on November 16, with participation of 45.4%, exceeding the required quorum. The upgrade is scheduled to activate on November 24 at block number 193,145,000, coinciding with the fifth anniversary of the mainnet launch.

"Vera" targets financial institutions directly, across three pillars: stronger infrastructure for tokenized real-world assets like stocks, bonds, and commodities; "request-for-quote" tools that let large traders request a price for a huge deal from market makers instead of placing it in an open market; and broader connectivity between "Injective"'s Ethereum-compatible environment and other networks via the inter-blockchain communication protocol. The upgrade also paves the way for the planned "Cipher OS" privacy layer.

"Vera" follows an active series: the "Meridian" upgrade on September 24, then the launch of the "Institutional Path" on November 2. The network has recorded more than 3.13 billion transactions and permanently burned more than 7.27 million tokens through monthly buybacks, while perpetual trading volume for real-world assets exceeded $5.3 billion.
