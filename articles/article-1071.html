🏭 Amazon Employees Oppose AI Data Centre Expansion... 'I'm Training My Own Replacement'!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Amazon's expansion into AI data centres is facing opposition from inside the company itself, as a group of more than a thousand employees has moved to slow the building of new centres and open a wider debate about the use of AI, in a report published on 22 November.

The group, known as 'Amazon Employees for Climate Justice', fears AI will replace their jobs, and the company's CEO Andy Jassy has previously said adopting AI could reduce the company's headcount. One employee said: 'I feel like I'm training my own replacement,' while others questioned pouring billions into AI while job cuts continue.

The group is also worried about the environmental impact: running data centres and cooling AI servers consumes huge amounts of electricity and water, putting pressure on local power and water grids and threatening the company's climate goals. Amazon runs more than 900 data centres in around 50 countries through its cloud arm, and its capital spending this year is expected to reach around $220 billion, most of it for data centre infrastructure.

In Ohio, voters approved 21 local measures restricting the building of new data centres, putting projects worth $17 billion at risk. Employees hope to build on the results of the US midterm elections, in which candidates who pledged tighter restrictions on data centres won.

The company responded that it will invest more than $1 billion over five years in the communities where it builds data centres, will not impose confidentiality agreements in its talks with local governments, and will increase its investment in power and water grids and in using them more efficiently.

Simply put: the people building AI fear it will replace them... can companies expand in AI without losing their employees' trust?

If AI threatens your job, would you oppose expanding it or learn to work with it?
