Apple's New Maps Arrive in India: Over 300 3D Landmarks! 🗺️

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple has launched a completely new version of its maps in India for the first time, with faster and more accurate navigation and more detailed images of roads, buildings, and parks.

The new maps include more than 300 3D landmarks — models of famous buildings that appear in their true shape on the map.

The nicest feature is "natural-language guidance": instead of street names nobody knows, the map tells you "turn right after the petrol station" or "at the next traffic light, turn left".

Advertisement

The maps also added bilingual labels (English and the local language) and a "Look Around" feature — interactive street imagery — plus augmented-reality walking directions and public transit in 8 Indian cities, as well as Indian Railways.
