🛍️ Cyberattack Halts Online Orders at The Container Store, While Shops Stayed Open!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 20, parent company Neighborhood Intelligence announced that the American stores of The Container Store had restored their website and systems after a cyber incident over the previous weekend forced them to stop accepting online orders.

As soon as suspicious activity was spotted, the company activated its security protocols and stopped online orders as a precaution to protect its customers' information and systems, while all stores stayed open serving customers through alternative payment-processing procedures. It also brought in independent cybersecurity experts to help with the investigation, containment, and recovery, and the investigation is still ongoing.

The company said it does not believe the incident affected the security of customers' financial data, because the store does not store payment information or consumers' financial information at all. It confirmed that the incident was limited to The Container Store alone, and the company's other brands were not affected, including Bed Bath & Beyond, Overstock, Buy Buy Baby, and Kirkland's, and it expects a limited impact on current-quarter revenue.

The company did not reveal the cause of the incident, whether it was a ransomware attack, or how many days the website stopped accepting orders.

Simply put: a store stopped its own online shop to protect its customers, while its doors stayed open; so do you trust a store that keeps your card details, or do you prefer one that does not store them at all?
