⚡ AI That Writes AI Code!

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Japanese company Sakana announced a new tool called “AI CUDA Engineer,” a framework that speeds up the development and deployment of AI models.

The tool improves model code to make training and inference faster, and the whole process is carried out by AI agents from start to finish with no human intervention.

Simply put, AI has started building itself, and the programmer becomes a supervisor who guides it and reviews it.

Would you trust code written by AI? Or would you review it line by line?
