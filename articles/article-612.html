🎮 The "Red Magic 11 Pro" arrives globally: a gaming beast with 24 GB of memory!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The Nubia brand, owned by ZTE, launched the "Red Magic 11 Pro" gaming phone in global markets outside China, on Friday, November 20.

The phone runs on Qualcomm's most powerful chip, the "Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5", with RAM of up to 24 GB and internal storage of up to 1 terabyte.

The phone carries a 7,500 mAh battery with 80W fast charging — a battery slightly smaller than the Chinese version of the phone, but still huge by phone standards.

Advertisement

"Red Magic" is a series of phones dedicated to gaming lovers, known for an internal cooling fan, distinctive lighting and an aggressive design that withstands long gaming sessions without the device overheating.

As for the 24 GB of memory, it is more than the memory of many laptops, and lets you run the heaviest games with dozens of apps in the background without any slowdown.

Simply put: imagine a phone with more memory than many laptops, designed specifically for gaming lovers.

Would you buy a dedicated gaming phone, or is a strong ordinary phone enough for gaming?
