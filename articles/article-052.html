Over 125 Million Text Messages Exposed — and the Cause Was a Simple Mistake! 📱

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Telecom company CSCTelecom in the Baltic states left a database containing more than 125 million text messages exposed on the internet without protection, and it was being updated in real time.

The messages included verification codes — the code that arrives on your phone to confirm your identity — password reset links and private information, from senders including banks, hospitals, and the 112 emergency line in Latvia.

And the surprise is that the cause was not a sophisticated hack, but a configuration error — someone simply left the door open by mistake. The records span from October 2022 to September 2026.
