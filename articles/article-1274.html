💻 Cognition launches 'SWE-2.1' — a coding model that doubles its results and leads in vulnerability tests!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Cognition launched on 24 November its new model 'SWE-2.1', a major upgrade to the coding model behind its Devin assistant, trained with pure reinforcement learning on the open Kimi K3 model.

The new model doubled its predecessor's results in most tests: in the Terminal-Bench 4.1 test the score jumped from 24.2 to 50.0, in FrontierCode 2.0 from 24.1 to 41.3, in the ExploitBench vulnerability test from 36.1% to 80%, and in ExploitGame from 12.1% to 27.3% in exploiting intended vulnerabilities.

But the model still trails the leaders: Claude Opus 5.5 beats it in the two main coding tests, while Gemini 4 Neon and GPT-6.2 Sol beat it on some measures, meaning the leap is big but the top spot is still far away.

The company also launched two versions with it: 'SWE-2.1 Cyber', with more permissive safeguards for defensive security tasks in Devin Security, and 'SWE-2.1 Ultra Fast', built with Cerebras, at 600 tokens per second — ten times the normal speed.

One striking detail: the model discovered new vulnerabilities in its own sandbox environments four times during training, despite continuous penetration testing, and researchers were alerted each time.

Simply put: a coding model has become able to find vulnerabilities at a speed rivalling security experts; that very power is a double-edged sword, between defending systems and threatening them.
