📺 Your TV Knows When Your Pet Leaves! 'SmartTag 3' Sends Arrival and Departure Alerts Through the TV!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung launched the Galaxy SmartTag 3 tracker on November 23, a small tag weighing just 9.15 grams, with a battery lasting up to 550 days in normal use and 790 days in power-saving mode, to help you find your belongings or your pet.

The improved 'Compass View' feature, powered by Bluetooth 6.0 channel sounding, guides you to the tag's direction and distance up to 60 meters — about three times the previous generation — while ultra-wideband technology offers greater precision when your phone is within 20 meters of the tag.

You can also view the tag's location history for the last 7 days through the Galaxy Find network, which includes more than 900 million Galaxy devices worldwide, passing the tag's locations in encrypted form that no one can read except its owner.

What's new is that 'Tell me when it arrives or leaves' alerts are no longer limited to the SmartThings Station device: after updating the SmartThings app, they now work with smart home hubs and Samsung TVs with hub functionality, so the hub monitors the tag's signal — its appearance means arrival and its long absence means departure.

The tag also works as a smart-home automation button: you can program a single or long press to run a routine such as 'Cinema mode,' which controls several devices at once, and share the tag's location with family even via iPhone, although some features like Compass View and ringing are not available on iOS.

Simply put: your living room TV has become a silent guard that knows when your pet came in and when it went out... Would you put a smart tag on your pet?
