⌚ Apple prepares a new generation of wearables — glasses, a pendant and camera earbuds!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new press report on November 20 said Apple is developing a new wave of AI-based devices that go beyond the iPhone and the Vision Pro headset: smart glasses, AirPods with a camera, and a wearable pendant device.

According to the report, the projects are in active development, and early prototypes are already being tested internally, although the company has not confirmed any of these plans officially so far.

The news comes after earlier reports that Apple stopped developing a cheaper version of the Vision Pro to focus on lightweight smart glasses rivalling Meta's glasses, with expectations they will be unveiled at the 2027 developers' conference.

Advertisement

The pendant is the newest form in this wave: a small device worn around the neck that hears and sees what is around you to answer your questions without holding your phone, while the camera in the earbuds adds the ability to understand what you are looking at.

Simply put: imagine leaving your phone in your pocket all day, and asking your glasses or your pendant about anything you see in front of you, and it answers instantly. That is the new bet: for AI to become something you wear, not something you carry.

Which of these devices would you prefer to wear first: the smart glasses, the camera earbuds, or the pendant?
