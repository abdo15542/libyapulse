✨ OnePlus Nord 7 leak reveals a colorful light strip and a 9000mAh battery!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Leaker Abhishek Yadav published live photos said to show the upcoming OnePlus Nord 7 on 24 November, revealing a design different from its predecessor, most notably a vertical colorful light strip inside the camera unit, shown lit in amber in one photo and off in another.

The photos show a glossy dark glass back with a greenish-blue frame and a rounded rectangular camera unit at the top, housing two vertically stacked lenses and a circular flash, while the light strip sits on the right side of the unit and is believed to work as a notification, charging or smart-assistant indicator, with no official confirmation.

A third photo shows the phone's front with a flat screen, thin bezels and a punch-hole selfie camera in the centre of the screen, with a lock screen dated 23 November, suggesting the photos were taken of a real device under testing rather than imaginary renders.

Advertisement

According to the same leaker, the phone runs on a MediaTek Dimensity 8500 chip with a Super AMOLED screen at a 144Hz refresh rate and a huge 9000mAh battery with 80W wired charging — figures that mean a step down in processor and refresh rate compared with the Nord 6, which came with a Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chip and a 165Hz screen, while keeping the same battery and charging.

The company has confirmed none of these details and has not announced a launch date, but the phone is expected to arrive in India first, one of the series' most important markets, possibly with a Chinese version called the OnePlus Turbo 7.

Simply put: a mid-range phone gets a striking design touch and a giant battery, but steps back on the processor and screen; a distinctive design alone is not enough if performance is lower than the previous generation.

Do you like a colorful light strip on the back of a phone, or do you see it as pointless decoration?
