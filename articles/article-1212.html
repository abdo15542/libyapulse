🔗 "Solana" Launches "solana.ai" — AI Agents That Trade and Pay on Their Own!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The "Solana" Foundation has launched a new platform called "solana.ai", bringing together tools that let independent AI agents trade digital assets, manage wallets, pay and buy services online without human approval for every transaction.

At the heart of the platform is a payments tool called "Pay.sh", which lets an agent with a "Solana" wallet buy access to hundreds of online services, paying for each request individually in the "USDC" stablecoin, without creating accounts or subscribing to APIs.

The platform also showcases an agent called "Milo" that trades around the clock on its own, watching the market and executing trades from a wallet linked to it, as an example of what a fully independent agent can do.

Advertisement

For small recurring payments, the platform offers "payment channels": the agent approves spending once, then exchanges thousands of signed payment updates, with the total settled on the network later. The system passed one million payment updates per second in a September test involving 100,000 wallets.

The platform also includes an "AI toolkit" for developers, with a Model Context Protocol server and an "Agent Kit" that connects to more than 60 on-chain actions, an agent registry, and confidential computing for processing sensitive data inside protected hardware.

The foundation said more than 120 teams are already working on AI products within the "Solana" ecosystem, and payment settlement on its network takes about 200 milliseconds, making pay-per-request practical.

Simply put: imagine an AI program that buys what it needs by itself, pays for each service with digital currency, and trades in the market while you sleep; this platform aims to make that easy for developers.

Are you comfortable with the idea of an AI agent spending real money without your permission each time?
