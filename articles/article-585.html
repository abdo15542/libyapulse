🔋 Honor prepares a phone with a 13,500 mAh battery — and a 7-inch screen!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New leaks revealed that Honor is preparing to launch a new phone from the X series before the Chinese New Year on February 6, with a huge battery of about 13,500 mAh.

The phone will be mid-range, with a huge screen between 6.96 and 7 inches, making the long battery life its most distinguishing feature.

This comes after a battery with a capacity between 14,000 and 15,000 mAh entered trial production earlier this month, showing the company's direction towards batteries far bigger than usual.

Advertisement

For comparison, the Power 2 phone the company launched in January runs on a 10,080 mAh battery — already a large capacity — but the new phone will exceed it by a clear margin.

The leaks also indicate the company has accelerated development of the Power 3 phone, which will come with a new 7-inch screen, a huge battery and a stronger processor, and earlier leaks in July said its battery would be no less than 10,000 mAh.

The leaks didn't stop there: they said the foldable Magic V7 phone will likely arrive after the Chinese New Year in early February — a little later than the January date rumoured in October.

As for the wide phone with the regular, non-foldable screen, it is scheduled to arrive in the first half of 2027.

Simply put: imagine a phone you charge once and then forget the charger for days. That is what Honor is trying to offer with a battery over 13,000 mAh and a screen the size of a small tablet.

Do you prefer a battery that lasts for days even if the phone gets bigger?
