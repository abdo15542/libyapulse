🔧 Apple fixes the iPhone 18 Pro! A new update fixes Face ID, the camera, and the screen!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Apple has released the iOS 27.0.1 update for iPhones, the first quick fix after the launch of iOS 27 at the start of November, to fix problems that bothered users of its newest phones.

The update fixes the sudden restart problem that affected iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max phones after a failed Face ID attempt — one of the most annoying problems, because it suddenly interrupted use of the phone.

It also fixes a camera problem that caused strange color spots to appear in photos in certain conditions — a problem that touches what most sets these phones apart: photo quality.

The update also fixes a problem where the touchscreen stopped responding in some conditions — a problem that makes the phone practically useless until the user restarts it.

The update comes days after user complaints since the launch of the new system, a sign that Apple is moving quickly to fix the flaws that appeared in its most expensive phones.

To get the update, just open "Settings", then "General", then "Software Update", and make sure the new version is installed before the problems get worse.

Simply put: your most expensive phone could break because of your face, but the fix has arrived — did you update your phone right away, or are you waiting?
