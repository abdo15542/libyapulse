🛰️ SpaceX: small rooftop towers to end phone 'dead zones'!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Elon Musk has announced that SpaceX has completed the design of a mini cell tower placed on a home's roof, resembling a Starlink dish, to boost phone signal inside buildings where satellite signals struggle, especially under thick metal roofs. Musk said, during the 'DealBook' summit this week, that these small towers will enter production in 2027, and that the company will launch next year the second generation of 'Direct to Cell' satellites, with 40 times greater capacity. The plan relies on wireless spectrum the company has bought, including a $17 billion deal with EchoStar in 2025, and a low-frequency spectrum portfolio worth $8 billion this year, after the Federal Communications Commission approved the direct-to-phone service in partnership with 'T-Mobile' in 2024. Company president Gwynne Shotwell explained the company will not build huge towers, but small low-power stations that may be sold with Starlink devices. Musk believes traditional telecom companies will not disappear, because AI and robots will need far more bandwidth than humans.

Simply put: satellites reach your phone outdoors, but they cannot pass through your home's roof; the solution is a small tower on the roof that receives the signal from space and rebroadcasts it inside the house.
