📱 Xiaomi Tests a Phone with a Wide Screen and Ultra-Thin Bezels!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New leaks reported that Xiaomi is testing a new flagship phone with a wide 6.3 to 6.4-inch display, a wide aspect ratio, and ultra-thin bezels. It is likely the device is the Xiaomi 18, which has not been announced yet, while earlier reports suggest it could be a budget phone from the Redmi series. The company has not confirmed any details yet.

Simply put: a wide screen means more space for watching videos and reading, and thin bezels mean a bigger screen in a smaller body.
