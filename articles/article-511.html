📱 Huawei Unveils Its Foldable Phone, the Mate X8 — and a Detachable Camera!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Huawei officially announced that its new foldable phone, the Mate X8, will be unveiled on November 24, 2026, in China, after the phone appeared in official photos and a teaser video from the head of the company's device division.

The phone comes with an outer screen of about 6.5 inches and an inner screen of about 8.15 inches — slightly larger than the previous generation — with a hinge said to integrate magnetic technology to reduce the crease, a Kirin 9050 Pro chip, a battery over 6,000 mAh, and a 50-megapixel main camera with a 50-megapixel zoom camera.

The phone will be sold in five colors, including purple-gold, sky blue, black, and snow white, and in four variants: two standard versions with 12 GB of memory and 256 or 512 GB of storage, and two collector's-edition versions with 16 GB of memory and 512 GB of storage or 1 TB. Prices have not been announced yet.

The phone's most distinctive feature is the expected support for the detachable camera unit Roving Z10 — a standalone imaging unit that attaches to the phone, with a 50-megapixel sensor a full inch in size and continuous 10x optical zoom from 24 to 240 mm with a variable aperture. But this support is still not officially confirmed and may be limited to the collector's edition.

Simply put: imagine a phone that opens up into a small tablet, then you attach a real professional camera to it and it becomes a full photography camera — that is what Huawei wants the Mate X8 to be.
