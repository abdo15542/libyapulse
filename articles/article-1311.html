📱 Razr 2027 Arrives Early in January — with a Bigger Outer Screen at Last!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Specifications of Motorola's foldable Razr 2027 have leaked, ahead of its expected launch in January 2027 during the Consumer Electronics Show — months earlier than its usual date.

The biggest change is a larger outer screen: 4 inches at 120Hz, moving from the Plus and Ultra models to the base model for the first time, after it was only 3.6 inches.

The inner screen stays at 6.9 inches, but with a higher refresh rate of up to 144Hz and HDR10+ support.

Advertisement

The phone runs on a Dimensity 7550X processor with 8 GB of memory and 256 GB of storage, two rear cameras of 50 megapixels each, and a 32-megapixel front camera.

The phone has become thinner than its predecessor, at 14.6 mm when closed, but slightly heavier at 195 grams, and it runs Android 17 with three operating system upgrades.

Simply put: Motorola is bringing the best of its expensive phones to its cheapest model and moving the launch earlier — the big outer screen is no longer reserved for the top tier.
