💍 'Aina' Launches a Smart Ring with a Screen and a Joystick — a New Interface for AI Agents!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Startup "Aina" announced on November 5 a new AI-powered smart ring, after fifteen months of working in stealth. The ring carries a small "OLED" screen, a tiny joystick, a microphone, and a speaker, and costs $399 for pre-order, rising to $499 after the early-booking period ends, with shipping starting in January 2027.

The ring runs a custom operating system that learns your habits from your phone, computer, calendar, and email, predicting three things you need before you ask and showing them on the screen. If you're leaving for work at nine in the morning, it prepares a car-booking shortcut, and if you're late for a meeting it suggests sending an apology email. You can also talk to AI agents by voice, or use the ring as a remote control for music, meetings, and smart-home devices, and its battery lasts 18 hours.

The company was founded by Apoorv Shankar, former vice president of hardware at "Ultrahuman", raised $5.5 million in July, and employs a team of forty people who tried dozens of ideas before settling on the ring — because, as its founder says, phones were designed for the browsing era, while the era of autonomous agents needs a faster interface: a button to approve instead of opening a full app.

Simply put: instead of opening your phone every time for an AI agent to approve a task, you glance at its suggestion on a tiny screen on your finger and approve it with one press.

Would you wear a smart ring that decides what you need before you ask?
