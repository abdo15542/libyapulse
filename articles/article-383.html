🗺️ Google Maps Plans Your Trip and Books Your Appointment Itself — Just Ask It!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

"Google" announced on November 16 new features in the "Ask Maps" assistant inside "Google Maps", making it a trip planner and booking assistant at the same time. With a single sentence, it builds you a day-by-day trip itinerary with numbered pins on the map and tips about each stop, plus live information on congestion and opening hours — and you can adjust the itinerary on the way by adding a restaurant or a scenic view.

Even smarter, the assistant now searches for available appointments itself: ask for an appointment at a beauty salon, a vet, a car workshop, or a home repair technician, and it searches the web and even calls the shops on your behalf to show you the available times. It also searches your saved places lists, telling you which saved restaurant has a table for five people within half an hour.

Also added are "Know Before You Go" tips when viewing directions: where to park your car, where to charge it, and the weather, tolls, and possible delays. The features have begun reaching Android and iPhone, with the assistant expanding to 30 new European countries to reach more than 200 countries and regions worldwide, while the appointments feature and route tips reach America first.

Simply put: the map no longer just draws your route; it plans your trip, calls the salon to book for you, and tells you where to park — all with a single sentence.
