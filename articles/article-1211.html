⌚ "Galaxy Fit 4" Price Leaks Ahead of Launch — €79.90, New Colors and More Features!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

A new leak has revealed the price of Samsung's upcoming fitness band, the "Galaxy Fit 4", ahead of its official announcement: it is expected to sell for €79.90 in France, €10 more than its predecessor, the "Fit 3", which launched in 2024 at €69.90.

The leak, shared by well-known leaker "billbil-kun", showed the band will come in three colors — black, green and silver — with a design close to its predecessor's.

The band had already appeared in the companion app "Galaxy Fit 4 Manager", which Samsung itself published in its own store, and a teardown of the app revealed a built-in speaker and vibration motor, plus support for up to 12 tiles on the screen.

Advertisement

Expected features include round-the-clock tracking of steps, heart rate, sleep, active time and calories, with alerts for abnormal readings, a sleep coach that starts after a week of use, and a "Find My Band" feature to locate it via your phone.

Bluetooth 5.4 support and integration with "Samsung Wallet" are also expected, though it is still unclear whether contactless payment will actually work, since the certification file makes no mention of an NFC chip.

Samsung has not yet confirmed any of these details — not the price, the launch date or the full specifications — but the official appearance of the companion app suggests the announcement is near.

Simply put: a new fitness band slightly pricier than its predecessor, with smarter features — from sleep tracking to finding it when lost — and its price leaked before the company announced it.

Would you pay an extra €10 for a speaker and new features in a fitness band?
