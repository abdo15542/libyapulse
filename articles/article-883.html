🎮 The Xbox Version of GTA 6 Has Leaked! And Enthusiasts Claim They Will Run It on PC Within a Week!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Days after GTA 6 launched on consoles only, reports spread of a near-complete leak of the game's Xbox version, published by a well-known leaker called "Cyberleak."

Reports say most of the game's files and assets are now circulating, but the main executable file is still missing, and the leaker promised to release it on Monday, November 23 — or sooner if the value of his cryptocurrency reaches $15 million.

A specialized security group examined the leaked files and said they contain genuine game assets, which suggests the leak is real, while a developer noted that the Xbox version is built on a development platform that shares much of its structure with Windows, which could make running it on PC easier.

The leaker claims players will be running the game on PC "within a week," but so far no one has provided independent proof that the leaked files work as a complete game on PC.

In contrast, the developer warned it will take legal action against any unofficial PC version, while it has not yet announced an official date for the PC version, although expectations point to early 2027, amid rumors that the company may speed up its plans because of these leaks.

Simply put: the leaked files appear to be genuine, but playing on PC is still a promise without proof — will you wait for the official version, or believe the leakers' promises?
