💰 Ransom over $500 million — a suspect who targeted American schools and hospitals is extradited!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Italy has extradited to the United States a 43-year-old man named Roman Khlinovsky, to face charges over his role in a ransomware and extortion campaign that targeted American schools, hospitals and health institutions, with ransom demands totalling more than $500 million.

Authorities say the suspect is a member of a group called "Eight Digits Team", linked to two well-known ransomware strains called "INC" and "Lynx", which targeted victims in at least six states, threatening to publish extremely sensitive information if the ransom was not paid.

"Ransomware" is software that encrypts the victim's files and blocks access to them until money is paid, and attackers often also threaten to publish the stolen data if payment is delayed. The suspect was arrested in Italy in July 2025 while on holiday with his family.

He now faces charges including computer fraud, money laundering, wire fraud, extortion and identity theft, carrying a maximum sentence of 27 years in prison.
