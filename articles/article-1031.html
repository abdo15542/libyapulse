🌊 South Korea's "KT" tests physical AI for water management with "K-Water"!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

South Korean company "KT" announced on November 23 that it has signed a memorandum of understanding with the Korea Water Resources Corporation "K-Water" to develop and test what is known as physical AI in water management.

The signing took place at "K-Water" headquarters in the city of Daejeon, and the agreement combines "KT"'s AI infrastructure, networks and platforms with "K-Water"'s long operational experience in water management.

The cooperation covers four areas: drawing up a roadmap for physical AI dedicated to water management, running field trials at sites including treatment plants and dams, connecting different types of existing devices and facilities in a single operating system, and setting standards, data and operating models for physical AI in this field.

Advertisement

"KT" has been building "K-Water"'s digital platform since 2025, and plans to launch a comprehensive AI service for the corporation during 2026; it has also been preparing, since last August, a long-term master plan for physical AI in water.

A company vice president said water management is core infrastructure that touches daily life directly, and that physical AI could raise the efficiency and safety of field operations, with any commercial or overseas expansion to be built on the trial results first.

Simply put: AI is stepping off the screen into water plants and dams... will smart water soon become part of our daily lives?
