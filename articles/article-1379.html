⚽ FIFA Hacked Before the World Cup… Passports, Visas and Contracts in a Leaked Archive!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

FIFA, the international football federation, confirmed that it suffered a criminal cyberattack that led to unauthorized access to a limited set of personal and commercial information, and that it is working with the authorities and law enforcement to investigate.

A group calling itself the 'Handsome Club' claimed responsibility, after listing FIFA as a victim on November 20, then publishing a 12.2 GB archive the next day on a cybercrime forum, after a failed extortion attempt.

The group claims the files include passport photos and identity documents, financial and legal records, internal governance documents, and sponsorship contracts linked to the 2026 World Cup, while FIFA has not yet confirmed the nature of the files or the number of those affected.

An intelligence firm examined the published archive and found that most of it is presentations and training videos, but it also found around 170 files titled passport and more than 100 files titled visa, in addition to travel bookings, while a source said the breach affected less than 0.1% of FIFA's data.

Reports indicate that the attackers got in by impersonating FIFA staff rather than through a technical flaw, and this is the second security issue to affect FIFA's Microsoft systems this year, after a researcher revealed in June that he had access to World Cup broadcast systems.

Simply put: officials' passports and World Cup contracts in a leaked archive months before the tournament; can you imagine the chaos if these documents were used in fraud?
