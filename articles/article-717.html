🎮 The iQOO 16R appears on Geekbench with the Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chip and Android 17!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The upcoming iQOO phone has appeared for the first time on the Geekbench benchmarking platform under the model number Vivo I2606, scoring 1,775 points in the single-core test and 6,680 points in the multi-core test, according to leaker Abhishek Yadav on November 21.

The test reveals that the phone runs on the eight-core Snapdragon 8s Gen 4 chip, with an Adreno 825 graphics processor, 12GB of memory and Android 17 out of the box. A certificate from the Indian Bureau of Standards (BIS) issued on November 11, plus certificates in Indonesia and Malaysia, suggests the launch in India is near.

The phone's name is still undecided: it may arrive in India as the iQOO 16R, while a Malaysian certificate carries it as the iQOO 16s, suggesting they are two names for the same phone in different markets. Earlier rumours expected a higher-tier chip, but the Geekbench result points to targeting the upper mid-range at a competitive price.

The phone is expected to arrive in the first quarter of 2027, after the launch of the standard iQOO 16 expected in India in December 2026. Early test results remain only an initial indicator, as the final version's performance may differ after software optimisation.
