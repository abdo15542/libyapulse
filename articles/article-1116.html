📺 YouTube Lets You Build Your Own Feed by Talking… 'I Want Videos About This'!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

YouTube has launched a "Custom Feeds" feature that lets you describe in your own words the kind of content you want to watch, and the platform builds you a personal feed that appears at the top of your homepage and is easy to reach.

To create a feed, tap the "+" at the top of the homepage, then type what interests you, such as "I want tech nostalgia: 1980s ads and old computers," or even a general request like "I want to explore something completely different from my usual feed." You can edit the description later from the same prompt box.

Free users can save three feeds, while YouTube Premium subscribers can save eight. Feeds sync between phone and computer as long as you are signed in, but they expire if you do not use them for thirty days — after which you can create a new feed with a new description.

The feature is currently available in English in the United States only, and requires search history to be turned on to work. Feeds are also subject to the platform's community guidelines, and the feature is part of wider updates to the "Home" page, including moving the subscriptions feed to the top bar.

The feature arrived in October 2026, at a time when the homepage relies almost entirely on the algorithm, finally giving viewers some control over what the system suggests, instead of leaving the choice to the algorithm alone.

Simply put: instead of the algorithm deciding what you watch… you tell it what you want in your own words… Would you build your own feed if the feature reached you?
