🔐 Staples Canada's Printing Site Hacked — Customer Data Exposed, Including Encrypted Passwords!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Staples Canada revealed a breach of its printing site, Staples Print, after an unknown party gained unauthorized access to the site between November 1 and 5.

During that period, the attacker viewed personal data of some customers including name, email, shipping and billing addresses, order summary, and encrypted password, according to a message the company sent to affected customers.

The company confirmed that no financial information was exposed, that it removed the attacker from its systems as soon as the breach was discovered, secured all accounts, and began a forced password reset on the next login for potentially affected customers.

Advertisement

Although the company believes the risk to individuals is low, it advised customers to change passwords regularly, use a different password for every site, and beware of unsolicited emails asking for personal information, since attackers exploit such incidents in phishing messages.

The disclosure comes about two weeks after the breach period itself, while the company promised to update customers as soon as it knows more details about the incident.

Simply put: a printing site belonging to a famous store was hacked for five days, and names, addresses, and encrypted passwords were stolen, and the company is asking its customers to change their passwords immediately.
