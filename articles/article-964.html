🚁 Dubai completes the world's first permanent electric air taxi route! From the Marina to the airport in under 10 minutes!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

On November 18, 'Skyports' and Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority announced the completion of the 'Dubai Marina Vertiport' and its official certification for commercial flights, making it the second certified air taxi station in Dubai's network, after Dubai International Airport station.

With this certification, Dubai now has the world's first permanent route linking two certified electric vertical take-off and landing stations, which 'Joby' Aviation will fly with its electric aircraft carrying four passengers, taking off and landing vertically like a helicopter, at speeds of up to 200 miles per hour.

The flight between the two stations, coded 'VMR' and 'VDX', is expected to take less than 10 minutes in the air, compared with about 35 minutes by car, and passengers can book a seat just as they would on any means of transport, while the new station can handle up to 21,000 landings and 84,000 passengers a year.

The new station sits next to the American University in Dubai, a short walk from the 'Al Fardan Exchange' metro station, connecting it easily to the rest of the city's transport network, and it includes a single elevated landing and take-off area, a charger and a passenger lounge.

The certification was celebrated at an official ceremony held at the station on November 3, the second certification issued under the UAE General Civil Aviation Authority's new regulation for vertical flight infrastructure.

Commercial flights have not started yet, however, as they still await operational approvals and final preparations, while 'Joby' works on equipping and testing both stations with its aircraft, after carrying out crewed test flights in Dubai since June 2025, and completing in November last year the first direct air taxi flight between two points in the UAE, from the Margham facility to Al Maktoum International Airport.

Work is now under way to develop two additional stations in downtown Dubai and Palm Jumeirah, to complete an initial network linking the city's most prominent destinations, while the two companies stress that the electric aircraft runs with zero operating emissions and low noise, making it suitable for residential areas like Dubai Marina.

Simply put: Dubai has become the first city in the world with a permanent air taxi route between two certified stations... would you ride a flying taxi instead of a car?
