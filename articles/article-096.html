ChatGPT Now Types Your Words Moment by Moment.. With No Time Limit! 🎤

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

OpenAI announced yesterday an update called "Live Dictation" in ChatGPT: your speech turns into text as you talk, moment by moment, instead of waiting until the recording ends, with higher speed and reliability. And the previous 10-minute recording limit has been removed, so you can now talk for longer without interruption. You can tap the sound wave to show or hide the text while speaking.

Instead of typing a long question by hand, you talk and the app writes it in front of you straight away — useful for lectures, interviews, and long messages, though you need to review the text because accuracy is not fully guaranteed. The feature is rolling out globally to all ChatGPT plans, including the free one, on iOS, Android, the web, and the desktop app. It is different from the voice mode for conversation: dictation writes your message in the chat box.

Would you rather talk to ChatGPT than type? 💬
