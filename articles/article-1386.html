📞 Samsung Exposes AI Call Deception… New Alert in One UI 8!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung has announced a new feature to confront the danger of AI-powered voice fraud, at a time when fake voices have become so convincing that it is hard to tell them from real ones.

The new feature is called 'Suspected Voice Phishing Alert', and it works within the One UI 8 update on the company's phones, alerting the user when it suspects that an incoming call is a voice fraud attempt.

The idea is that a call may look like it comes from a familiar number, or sound like the voice of someone you know, while in reality it is an AI-generated voice aiming to steal your data or your money.

Advertisement

The feature is available in South Korea only for now, with no official announcement about when it will reach other countries, but its launch signals that phone makers have started treating voice fraud as a core threat, not an exception.

This is no longer just suspicious text messages, but calls speaking in a familiar voice; an early warning could be the difference between an ordinary call and a real loss.

Simply put: the caller's voice is no longer enough proof of who they are; would you answer a call from an unknown number without caution?

Do you trust the caller's voice, or do you verify first before sharing any information?
