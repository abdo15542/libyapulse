💻 Lenovo prepares the 'Googbook Duet 13': a tablet that detaches from its keyboard... in January!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Leaked images have revealed that Lenovo is preparing to launch the first detachable 'Googbook', called the 'Googbook Duet 13', in selected markets during January 2027, which could mean unveiling it at the Consumer Electronics Show in Las Vegas. The device appears with a flat back panel, a single rear camera in the top-left corner, and a colored light bar called the 'Globar' that lights up when the 'Gemini' assistant is used.

The device comes with a white leather cover containing a built-in keyboard, attached magnetically and detachable as needed, plus stylus support. Earlier leaks and documents point to a 13-inch screen, a MediaTek processor, 16 gigabytes of memory, and the same 'Googbook OS' used in the rest of the category. Neither Lenovo nor Google has confirmed the device, its price, or its date officially yet.

'Duet 13' is the first 'Googbook' whose keyboard detaches from it, carrying a name Lenovo previously used in its 'Chromebook Duet' tablets. The device first appeared in a leak last July, and that leak has proven accurate at least once — when the 'Googbook 15' reached stores in October.

Simply put: a computer that turns into a tablet by detaching its keyboard, with an AI assistant lighting a colored bar when you talk to it — but so far this is all leaks, not an official announcement.
