👁️ Google Brings 'Guided Vision' to iPhone… Gemini Live Sees What Your Camera Sees!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The 'Guided Vision' feature in Gemini Live has reached iPhone users, after first appearing on Android last October, letting the smart assistant see what your phone's camera sees and understand it with you moment by moment.

The feature works by pointing the camera at anything around you, and Gemini tells you what it sees, answers your questions about it, and asks you to adjust the camera angle or move closer when the object is out of frame or unclear.

Google says the feature helps with simple everyday tasks, such as reading small print, checking a home device's settings, or finding a lost item in the room, without needing to take a photo or write a description.

Advertisement

The idea is that the conversation becomes fully visual: you talk to the assistant while looking at the same thing, and it follows the live image and explains it, instead of you describing what you see in words.

Google has not yet clarified all the compatibility details with iPhone OS versions, nor the list of countries that got the feature first, but its arrival on iPhone means millions of new users will try camera search for the first time.

Simply put: instead of typing your question, point the camera and let Gemini see, understand and answer; would you try asking your phone about what is in front of it?

Do you trust an assistant that sees your home and your belongings through the camera, or do you prefer to keep the camera off?
