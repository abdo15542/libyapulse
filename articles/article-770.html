📱 HMD Touch AI: A Simple Phone with a Small Touch Screen and AI... at a Tiny Price!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

According to recent reports, a new phone from HMD has appeared in China called Touch AI, a simple phone with a small 3.2-inch touch screen, in a colorful design reminiscent of classic Nokia Lumia phones. What sets it apart is that it runs the Doubao AI model from ByteDance, offering AI features despite its simplicity.

Its AI features include instant translation, help with learning, photography, and image generation. The phone also supports 4G networks, Wi-Fi, sharing the internet as a hotspot, and payment via QR code. It has a 2-megapixel rear camera, a 1,950 mAh battery, parental control tools, and an emergency button.

The phone is now available for pre-order in China for 469 yuan, about $65, making it a cheap option for anyone who wants a simple phone with a touch of AI without paying for a full smartphone.

Simply put: a simple, cheap phone, but one that understands your language, translates it, and helps you with AI; do you think smart feature phones could make a strong comeback, or is a full smartphone irreplaceable?
