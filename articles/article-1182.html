🚗 New York Bans Car Subscriptions! No More Paying Monthly for Heated Seats!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed a law on Monday, November 23, banning car companies from charging monthly subscriptions for features already installed in the car.

The ban covers features like heated seats, remote start, enhanced navigation, hands-free driving, and entertainment options, as long as they run on components already in the car and do not require ongoing support from the company.

Hochul said: “Someone who spends their hard-earned money on a car should not have to pay more for features that should come with the car automatically,” describing this model as “deceptive and it will not fly in New York.”

Advertisement

The law did not come out of nowhere: BMW faced widespread criticism when it charged $18 a month in Europe to unlock heated seats, while Mercedes sells a paid subscription to unlock extra acceleration power.

But the law exempts genuinely connected services: navigation updates, the entertainment system, satellite radio, in-car Wi-Fi, roadside assistance, and driver-assistance and automated driving systems that rely on software and networks.

That means Tesla, for example, will keep charging $99 a month for its advanced self-driving features, while General Motors and Ford charge around $40 and $50 a month for similar services.

The law takes effect on February 27, 2027, with a fine of $250 for each point of sale for each violation, enforced by the state’s Department of State.

Simply put: you bought the car with everything in it, so the company cannot sell you twice what you already paid for once.
