🇬🇧 “Muse” From Meta Arrives in the UK! An AI Agent That Shops and Books on Your Behalf!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Meta launched its personal AI agent “Muse” in the United Kingdom on Monday, November 23, after launching it in the United States last September.

Muse is not just a chat that answers questions, but an agent that carries out tasks on your behalf: it sends email, books travel, shops, compares energy offers, and organizes the calendar, and it builds plans, schedules, and tools from your description.

It runs on the “Muse Spark” model, the first model from Meta’s Superintelligence Labs, and every user gets their own private virtual computer in the cloud, where their information and login details are stored away from Meta’s advertising systems, and no other user’s agent can access them.

Advertisement

The user stays in control: they decide what the agent can access, they approve important actions before they are carried out, such as sending an email or completing a purchase, and they can ask it to remember something or forget it at any time.

When paying, Muse uses the “Link” wallet from Stripe, which creates a unique digital copy of your card made for agents only, or “Shop Pay” from Shopify in supported stores.

Muse is available for free for most uses to those over 18, through the iOS and Android apps, the Muse website, and WhatsApp, with paid plans for those who want wider use, and it will later come to Meta’s smart glasses.

Fourteen British partners joined the launch, including Marks & Spencer, Sainsbury’s, Currys, Ocado, ASOS, easyJet, Jet2, and Trainline, as well as global brands like Spotify and OpenTable, while Amazon blocked the agent from shopping on its site in September.

Simply put: an AI assistant that does not just talk, but actually gets the work done — it buys, books, and follows up, and you approve the important steps before they happen.

Would you trust an AI agent to buy and book on your behalf with your card?
