🛡️ Microsoft launches "Perception" officially: a team of AI agents guarding cybersecurity!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft announced at the "Ignite 2026" conference on Tuesday, November 17, the general availability of the "Perception" platform — a platform that coordinates teams of AI agents to protect cybersecurity.

The platform first appeared in July as "Project Perception", then entered public preview inside Microsoft Defender in August, before becoming officially available now.

The agents' teams are divided into three groups: red teams that attack to test vulnerabilities, blue teams to detect attacks and sort and investigate alerts, and green teams to fix problems.

The alert-triage agent can handle about 80% of typical alerts in Defender, based on analysing about 8 million alerts across the company's security products, while the red reconnaissance agent has become generally available, and the web-application penetration-testing agent entered preview.

The company also added two new green playbooks working across Microsoft, Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud, plus integrations in preview with security-management systems via the MCP protocol and APIs.

The platform also deals with the risk of the agents themselves, as the company's tools can detect 60 types of local agents, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot.

An "AI agent" is a smart program that carries out whole tasks on its own, and "alert triage" means deciding which security alert is genuinely dangerous and which is just noise.

Simply put: imagine a digital guard team of smart robots: some attack to reveal vulnerabilities, and some fix them before hackers exploit them.
