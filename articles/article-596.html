🎮 "Copilot for Gaming" enters public testing — an AI assistant that joins you inside your games!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft began public testing of its new assistant "Copilot for Gaming" on Wednesday, November 18 — an AI chatbot dedicated to players on the Xbox platform.

The assistant is currently available in the beta version of the Xbox app on phones, whether iPhone or Android, and any player can try it directly from the app.

The assistant can answer your questions about games, offer tips to help you get past a difficult stage in a game, and even suggest new games to you based on the games you have played before.

Advertisement

The idea is that the assistant knows your play history and what you have achieved, so it becomes more like an expert friend sitting beside you as you play, instead of you leaving the game to search for the solution online.

Simply put: imagine being stuck on a difficult stage, and asking a smart assistant on your phone that guides you step by step until you get past it. That is what "Copilot for Gaming" promises.
