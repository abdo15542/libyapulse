💬 WhatsApp Disguises Itself as 'WhatUp' — a Joke from the GTA 6 World That Confused Followers!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

WhatsApp surprised its followers on November 19 — the launch day of Grand Theft Auto 6 — by changing its official account on the X platform: it replaced its famous green picture with a blue icon bearing three dots, and changed its bio to 'WhatUp everyone?', a reference to 'WhatUp', the fictional messaging app inside the game.

The sudden change sparked a wave of questions: some users thought the account had been hacked, while others immediately connected it to the game. WhatsApp then confirmed the joke with a post saying: 'If you see us in Vice City, you didn't see us,' referring to the fictional city where the game's events take place. CRED founder Kunal Shah joined in the joke, asking the account playfully: 'WhatsUp?'.

And WhatUp is not alone: the game parodies real tech companies through fictional copies, such as 'Sahara' instead of Amazon, 'Ride Me' instead of Uber and Lyft, 'Pac Me' instead of money-transfer apps, and 'Snapmatic' instead of Instagram. Coverage confirmed that it is a temporary joke, not a real change to WhatsApp's identity — its green logo remains.

Simply put: imagine your favorite app dressing up as a character from a game for one day, and some of your friends think its account was stolen — that is exactly WhatsApp's joke with GTA 6.
