🏢 Protests Storm Meta's Offices in India! Deleted Accounts Ignite the Anger!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Protesters from the youth wing of India's opposition Congress Party stormed Meta's offices in Hyderabad and clashed with police, protesting the company's deletion of Instagram accounts belonging to party leaders.

The protests spread to Mumbai and Gurugram, where protesters painted slogans on the walls of the company's office building and raised banners demanding the deleted accounts be restored, threatening to storm the company's offices across the country unless the accounts are restored within seven days.

Meta had restricted the accounts of the youth wing's president and its communications official, and after the protests it restored two accounts, but thousands of accounts belonging to journalists, content creators, and influencers critical of the government remain restricted, according to a party official.

Advertisement

Meta declined to comment on the protests, which come in its largest market by number of users, amid a growing debate in India over freedom of expression and repeated government requests to delete content it objects to.

The company's data shows it restricted more than 5.4 million posts or accounts in India under local laws in the first half of 2026, compared with only about 70,000 in the whole of 2025.

Simply put: the deletion of politicians' accounts sparked protests inside the offices of the world's biggest social media company... do platforms protect freedom of expression, or do they carry out governments' orders?
