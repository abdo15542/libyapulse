🎙️ ChatGPT writes your words moment by moment... with no time limit!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

OpenAI has launched the 'Live Dictation' feature in ChatGPT, so your speech turns into text on the screen as you talk, instead of waiting until you finish recording. The company also removed the ten-minute limit that used to cut off long recordings, and the feature is now available on all plans including the free one, on iPhone, Android, the web, and the desktop app. The text appears while you speak, and can be hidden or shown by tapping the sound-wave icon, then edited before being sent as a normal message. The audio recording is saved with the conversation and deleted within 30 days of deleting it.

Simply put: instead of recording your whole message and then waiting for it to become text, you watch the words being written in front of you as you say them, so you can correct a mistake the moment it happens.
