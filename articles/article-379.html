📊 A New Index Reveals: The Cheapest Coding AI Model Does the Same Job at 32x Less Cost!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Databricks" has launched the AI Index, a public dashboard updated weekly that measures real enterprise adoption of AI models and spending patterns, based on data from more than 10,000 companies passing through its "Unity Gateway", plus a coding-agent benchmark built on real engineering tasks from inside the company.

The benchmark revealed a surprise: the models "GPT-6 Luna" and "Claude Opus 5.5" achieved the same success rate on coding tasks at 89.1%, but "Luna" cost on average just $0.05 per task, versus $1.62 for "Opus 5.5" — about 32 times less. The top performer was "Claude Fable 5.1" with a 96% success rate, but it costs $7.47 per task, while "Muse Wonder" achieves 94.9% for just $1.53. The index also showed that token price alone isn't enough: "Gemini 3.8 Flash" has cheap tokens, but needed 140 conversation turns on average per task, reaching a cost of $11.97 — among the highest in the test.

The index also showed companies moving away from relying on a single provider: those relying on one provider fell from 64% in July to 28% in November, while the share of open-weights models jumped from 7.3% to 30.9% of token traffic, and "Anthropic" advanced to 68.8% of total share.

Simply put: the most expensive isn't always the best; the cheapest model may do the same job for a fraction of the cost — and the real price is measured by the completed task, not the token price.
