🔍 Google puts AI chats on its homepage!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Google has begun updating its famous homepage by adding a new sidebar showing AI chats, in the biggest change to the page in many years.

With a click on a grey bar on the side of the screen, the sidebar opens to show a list of searches you made through 'AI Mode', with the ability to start a new chat, search your past chats, or link a 'Gemini' notebook.

The company's vice president of products said people come to search with multi-step questions that do not end with a single answer, and that tests showed the sidebar helps continue exploration. The new update is reaching users gradually on computers around the world.

Simply put: the homepage used to be a door you entered with a single question; now it has become a desk where all your chats with AI are kept, to continue whenever you like.
