💬 'Genesys' Launches AI Agents for Customer Service Without Changing the Contact Center!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"Genesys" announced on November 17 standalone AI agents for customer service, which any company can run with the contact center, phone numbers, and telecom carrier it already uses, without needing to move its entire contact center to the "Genesys Cloud" platform. The service is available now to everyone.

Alongside the agents, the company launched "Genesys Arena", a trial ground with ready-made models for more than 50 use cases across different sectors, so a team can try a ready agent in just ten minutes and customize it in natural language before deploying it. The company also changed its pricing: instead of paying per message, the price is now based on effort — a simple task needing one to three tools costs less than a complex task needing seven tools or more, with consumption measured in units called "experience units".

The agents run on large action models that don't just understand the customer's request, but actually carry out the work: changing an account, following up a payment, or booking an appointment, within tools and permissions the company approves. From the announced results: "Electrolux" supports more than 65% of service requests with agents, and "Riachuelo" raised its self-service rate from 30% to 84%.

Advertisement

Simply put: companies used to have to tear down their entire contact center to try AI; now they can add a smart agent on top of what they already have, and pay according to how hard the task is, not how many messages there are.

Would you rather talk to an AI agent that completes your request immediately, or to a human employee?
