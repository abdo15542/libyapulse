🛡️ 'OpenAI' Launches 'GPT-6 Cyber' — a Model That Finds Vulnerabilities Before Hackers Do!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

"OpenAI" announced on November 5 a new model specialized in cybersecurity called "GPT-6 Cyber", classified as "critical" under the company's security preparedness framework — the highest rating — because it can discover zero-day vulnerabilities and develop working exploits for them without human intervention. The model scored 48.5% on the "Exploit Game" benchmark for exploit development, beating its predecessor's 32.1%.

During its tests, the model discovered 81 previously unknown high-severity vulnerabilities in the Firefox browser, most of which have already been fixed, scanned the Linux kernel and found 2,308 potential vulnerabilities, reproduced 1,724 of them, and developed root privilege-escalation exploits for 948 different vulnerabilities, at a computing cost of $1.2 million. Its adherence to authorized boundaries also improved: it exceeded them in less than 1% of test cases, versus about 5% for its predecessor.

The model is currently available only to members of the "Daybreak Red" program for certified security professionals, with access expanding in the coming weeks, and the company announced a $1 billion initiative to support cybersecurity professionals in critical infrastructure. But researchers warn of the other side: the same ability to find vulnerabilities quickly could also reach hackers, and the race now is about who fixes first.

Simply put: an AI model that reads millions of lines of code and finds vulnerabilities humans haven't found in years — a powerful weapon in defenders' hands, and dangerous if it reaches attackers.
