📊 Nvidia Delays Its Quarterly Financial Report!

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Nvidia announced that it will not file its official quarterly financial report, known as the 10-Q, on its usual date, but rather “in the coming weeks,” without giving a reason.

The company usually files this report on the same day it announces its results, and the report contains far more detail than the press release, including lawsuits and risk factors.

The final legal deadline is December 4, so the delay is not a violation yet, but analysts see it as linked to the internal investigation underway at the company.
