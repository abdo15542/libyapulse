💰 Databricks negotiates new funding at a $225 billion valuation!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Data and AI company Databricks has entered early talks to raise several billion dollars in a new funding round that could lift its value to around $225 billion, according to the Wall Street Journal.

The talks are still in their early stages, and the terms or size could change before agreement. The company has not commented on the report, but the new valuation is about 18% higher than last August's valuation, when the company raised $5 billion at a $190 billion valuation.

Databricks was founded in 2013 and offers software that helps companies store, manage and analyse their data and build AI applications on it. It competes with Snowflake and is considered a strong candidate for a future IPO.

The company says its Lakebase database product has passed $100 million in expected annual revenue, while its Lakehouse data-warehouse business has passed $1.5 billion.

Simply put: a data company that has not yet gone public is approaching a quarter of a trillion dollars in value — a sign that investors are still betting heavily on AI companies. Will this momentum continue, or is it a bubble?
