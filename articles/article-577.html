👓 Samsung's smart glasses are getting closer — Korea and America first, with AI inside!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New reports on November 20 say Samsung is preparing to launch its first AI-powered smart glasses, with a limited rollout in South Korea and the United States before other markets.

The glasses will run on Google's Android XR platform and use the Gemini assistant for instant translation, navigation and other tasks, while prices have not been decided yet, and the date they reach Japan, China and Europe is still unconfirmed.

The company partnered with the Gentle Monster and Warby Parker brands on the glasses' design, in a move resembling Meta's collaboration with Ray-Ban, which made smart glasses a popular product.

Advertisement

Samsung has not confirmed any of these details officially so far — everything published is press reports — but they come at a time when major companies are racing over lightweight glasses that put AI in front of your eyes all day.

Simply put: imagine ordinary-looking glasses through which you hear an instant translation of what is said in front of you, and ask them for directions and they guide you. The idea is for the smart assistant to be with you wherever you go, without holding your phone.

Would you buy smart glasses if they reached your country, or do you prefer AI to stay inside your phone only?
