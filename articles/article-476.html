🇸🇬 Mistral Opens Its Asia Headquarters in Singapore — 200 Employees and Sovereign AI Models!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

French AI company Mistral opened its Asia-Pacific headquarters in Singapore on November 20, to act as an extension of its science center and focus on developing sovereign AI models for governments and companies in the region.

The company plans to double its staff in the region to more than 200 employees by 2027, after their number was about 100, including about 40 in Singapore alone until last May, with hiring focused on science, engineering, applied AI, and robotics.

The new center will work on custom models for three areas: government services, high-tech industries, and robotics, letting institutions build and run their models inside their own environments, while keeping control of their data and institutional knowledge.

Advertisement

Founder and CEO Arthur Mensch said opening the headquarters reflects a long-term commitment to building sovereign AI capabilities 'in the region and for the region', and that the goal is for Singapore to become a strategic location enabling governments and companies to own their infrastructure and intelligence.

The company builds on earlier partnerships in Singapore, including a collaboration with the national DSO Labs and with HTX, the agency under the Ministry of Home Affairs, among the company's first partnerships worldwide in model customization.

Simply put: the French company competing with the biggest AI companies is opening a large headquarters in Asia, to sell governments AI models that run on their own servers instead of sending their data abroad.
