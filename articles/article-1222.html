💻 AMD Expands Its Instinct MI400 Cards… A New Card and Speed That Beats Nvidia in AI Benchmarks!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

AMD has expanded its Instinct MI400 series of cards for AI and scientific computing, with a new card, the MI450X.

The series now includes three cards: the MI430X, the MI455X, and the MI450X.

The MI430X card is dedicated to scientific computing, offering native double-precision computing, 432 GB of HBM4 memory, and bandwidth of up to 23.3 TB per second.

Advertisement

The company published research examining 47 work configurations across 11 applications and standard benchmarks, comparing native computing with simulation in scientific applications.

The MI455X card, on the Helios system, achieved 21,333 tokens per second per card on the DeepSeek-R1 model in the MLPerf inference benchmark.

The result is up to 30% higher than Nvidia's latest published results for the Vera Rubin platform, according to AMD's tests in October 2026.

AMD also claims up to 20% higher memory bandwidth and higher scale-out bandwidth than Nvidia's platform.

The new MI450X card carries 288 GB of HBM4 memory, with the same computing power, bandwidth, and connectivity as the MI455X, and shares its Helios architecture and ROCm software.

Simply put: AMD says its new cards are faster than Nvidia's newest cards in AI benchmarks; the race for AI hardware is heating up, and data centers and developers stand to benefit.
