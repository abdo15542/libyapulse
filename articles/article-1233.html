🎯 Call of Duty loses 42% of its players after the launch of GTA 6!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Call of Duty lost about half of its daily players during the launch weekend of Grand Theft Auto 6, according to estimates from Alinea Analytics.

Daily players of the Call of Duty HQ app, which includes Modern Warfare 4, Black Ops 7, and Warzone, fell 42% across PlayStation, Xbox, and Steam, from an average of 7.5 million players to 4.4 million.

PlayStation, the game's biggest platform, was hit hardest with a 44% drop, and four days after launch the number was still 41% below normal.

Advertisement

Fortnite was not spared either: its daily players fell 28%, from 17.8 million to 12.8 million, before partly recovering to 19% below normal by the end of the launch weekend.

EA Sports FC 27 also fell by about 27%, from 5.9 million to 4.3 million players, then returned to around 20% below normal after its price was cut in half.

The same firm estimates that GTA 6 sold 44.9 million copies during the launch weekend, earning more than $4 billion, and was played by 40.8 million people on Sunday alone.

But these figures are third-party estimates, not official numbers from Activision, Sony, or Microsoft, and the firm did not disclose the baseline window it measured against; Modern Warfare 4 also launched just 27 days earlier, so part of the drop may be a natural post-launch decline.

Simply put: when a game the size of GTA 6 arrives, players abandon everything else for a while; the question is not whether they will return, but when — and Call of Duty had not recovered even after four days.
