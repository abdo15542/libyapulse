💬 Meta's Muse Enters Instagram DMs… Ask About Any Post Inside the Conversation!

26 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Meta has integrated its smart assistant Muse into Instagram DMs, so you can share posts with it and ask it about their content while staying inside the same conversation, without leaving the app.

Muse works inside DMs as a party in the conversation: you send it a post and it explains it to you, or you ask it about the details of a photo or a clip, and it replies instantly, in a step that makes AI part of daily chatting rather than a separate app.

The integration is reaching users gradually in five countries first, while Meta has also started bringing Muse to the Apple Watch, so the assistant is on your wrist too.

Advertisement

The update was not limited to DMs: Meta also added new tools for organizing Saved items in Instagram, including creating 'Collections' for saved posts and clips, shopping lists, and travel plans, turning the Saved page into an organized space instead of a random pile.

The new organization tools are available first in the United States and Canada, while Muse in DMs reaches other countries in turn, in a clear race between the big companies to put a smart assistant inside every app you use daily.

Simply put: a smart assistant now lives inside your DMs, explaining any post and tidying your saved items; do you want it in your conversations, or do you prefer DMs without AI?

Would you ask Muse about your friends' posts inside DMs, or does that feel like an intrusion into the privacy of the conversation?
