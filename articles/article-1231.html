🎮 A week before GTA 6… PlayStation 5 and Xbox sales more than double!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Console sales in the United States jumped sharply in the week before the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6, according to market research firm Circana.

In the week ending November 14, PlayStation 5 sales rose 2.4 times compared with the same week last year, and Xbox Series X/S sales rose 2.3 times.

PlayStation recently confirmed that more than 39 million unique players have played on PlayStation 5, and recorded the highest number of new device activations for a single game since the console launched in 2020.

Advertisement

Nintendo Switch 2 sales also rose by more than 40% year on year over the same period, likely driven by The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, released on November 5 and selling 5.2 million copies in just four days.

Analysts believe the long-awaited game lifted sales of all consoles, not just the game itself; many people bought a new console specifically to play it.

Full November sales details are expected next month.

Simply put: one game moved the whole console market; people don't buy a new console for its specs, they buy it for the game they want to play on it.
