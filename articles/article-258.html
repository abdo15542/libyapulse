🎙️ Transcription company leaves police and court recordings exposed on the internet!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

American transcription company 'Tigerfish' has closed a serious security hole, after leaving a large amount of its internal data exposed on the internet, including recordings belonging to police departments, prosecutors' offices, and major tech companies.

The exposed data included transcripts of police interviews, body-camera footage, raw audio and video recordings for clients, plus their invoices. Security researcher Anurag Sen found the files on an Amazon cloud server that was open to everyone through an easy-to-guess link, without any password. Sen discovered the leak in October and reported it to the company, but his message landed in the spam folder, so it did not reply; after press intervention, the company shut down the server immediately and confirmed it was checking its other servers.

Simply put: imagine leaving your filing cabinet's door open in the street; that is how the cloud server was — available to anyone who knew the link, without any lock.
