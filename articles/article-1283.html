🤖 ByteDance Launches Xiaodu — A Personal Assistant That Remembers You and Gets Things Done in the Background!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Chinese company ByteDance opened the public beta of its new personal assistant, Xiaodu, on November 24. It is a standalone, invite-only app that aims to be a true personal assistant — not just answering questions, but managing your tasks in the background and reminding you of what you need before you ask.

Xiaodu stands out because it remembers your context and tasks over the long term: it follows up on your orders, reminds you of appointments, tracks parcels, searches for nearby places and guides, follows up on purchases, returns, and after-sales service, reminds you to drink water, take your medicine, and log your workouts, and helps with office tasks like drafting texts, organizing ideas, and writing summaries.

With your permission, the assistant can connect to ByteDance's own apps such as Douyin and Feishu, as well as your calendar, health, and maps — alerting you, for example, when the price of a product you follow drops or a discount coupon is about to expire — and it can run up to 10 scheduled tasks in parallel.

Advertisement

The app works by invitation: a user needs an invite code to get in, receives a free usage allowance, and can earn more by inviting friends, while paid plans have already appeared with monthly prices starting at 68 Chinese yuan.

The company said the features that succeed in Xiaodu will gradually move into its main app, Doubao, which is used by hundreds of millions, in a clear step to compete with new personal assistants such as Meta's Muse.

Simply put: the competition has moved from 'who answers best' to 'who gets the most done for you' — and an assistant that remembers your habits may become closer to you than any other app.

Would you want an assistant that knows your habits and handles your tasks on its own, or does it worry you that it knows so much about you?
