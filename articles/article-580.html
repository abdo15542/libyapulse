📷 After years away, the legendary PureView brand returns in a new HMD phone!

21 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

HMD published a teaser video for a new photography-focused phone, starting with the "PureView" logo, then showing a large circular rear camera unit — a sign of the return of one of the most famous photography brands in phone history.

The phone appears with four rear cameras, including what looks like a periscope lens for long-distance zoom, with a capsule-shaped LED flash beside the camera unit, which carries the logo of the German optics company Schneider Kreuznach.

What is striking is that the German company's logo points to a possible collaboration in the imaging system, but the two companies have not yet explained the nature of the partnership or the role it will play in the final camera, and it is not clear whether the name "PureView" will cover a complete imaging system or is just a familiar name on the back of the phone.

Advertisement

The PureView brand has a long history with Nokia: it first appeared in the Nokia 808 PureView in 2012 with a 41-megapixel camera, then HMD bought the brand from Microsoft in 2018, using it last in the Nokia 9 PureView in 2019 with five rear cameras developed with Zeiss lenses.

Now the new partner is Schneider Kreuznach instead of Zeiss, and the teaser coincides with the company's tenth-anniversary celebration on December 1. No specs, price or launch date have been revealed yet — everything shown so far is a short teaser video.

Simply put: imagine a name that meant "the best phone camera in the world" more than ten years ago returning again. The question now is: does it return with the same quality, or just the name?

Do you remember the old PureView phones, and do you trust that the return of the name means the return of quality?
