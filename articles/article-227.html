📱 OpenAI's phone gets closer: Foxconn will assemble it!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Supply-chain reports say 'FIH Mobile', a Foxconn subsidiary, has won the order to assemble OpenAI's first smartphone, in a new step toward a phone built around an AI agent instead of traditional apps. The phone is expected to arrive in late 2027 or 2028, with custom chips built on MediaTek's Dimensity 9600 architecture at 2 nanometers, according to analyst Ming-Chi Kuo, and companies from Apple's supply chain such as Luxshare and Lingyi iTech may supply its components. Neither OpenAI nor Foxconn has confirmed the phone exists at all, despite an existing partnership between them in AI data-center equipment.

Simply put: OpenAI's phone will not be an ordinary phone with an assistant app, but a device designed from the ground up to understand your requests and carry them out itself — as if apps turn into tasks done by voice.
