🍎 A wide outage hits Apple services — iCloud, the App Store and Apple Pay stop working!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Users in the United States observed on Wednesday, November 18, a wide wave of outages hitting Apple services, with a big jump in outage reports.

The outage covered essential services millions of users rely on daily: iCloud for storing photos and files, the App Store, Apple Pay for payments, and Apple Music.

The outages mean a user might fail to download an app, or complete a payment, or access his files stored in the cloud, even if his phone and internet are working normally.

Advertisement

Large cloud services run from huge data centres, so if a small part of them breaks down, millions of users may be affected at the same time — which is what makes such outages so widespread.

Not all the details of the cause of the outage are known yet, but such waves usually resolve within hours as services gradually return.

Simply put: imagine all your bank's branches closing their doors at the same time, even though your card is fine. That is what Apple users felt during the outage.
