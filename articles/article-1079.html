☁️ Anthropic commits to paying Microsoft $47 billion — for cloud computing until 2033!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Official documents have revealed that Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI models, has committed to paying at least $47.2 billion to Microsoft for Azure cloud computing services, in a deal running until June 2033.

The disclosure came in an amended registration statement the company filed with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 17 November, with the commitment ranging between $47.2 billion as a minimum and $49.2 billion as a maximum.

Notably, the deal cannot be cancelled: it is a “take or pay” agreement, meaning Anthropic owes the amount whether it uses the computing capacity or not, and it cannot back out except in the case of an uncorrected material breach by Microsoft or a failure to deliver agreed chips.

Advertisement

Over six years and eight months, the commitment equals about $7.1 to $7.4 billion a year — an amount guaranteed on Microsoft's books, not just a forecast, which makes it highly valuable for the cloud computing business.

But Microsoft remains the smallest of Anthropic's three computing suppliers: its minimum commitment with Google is about $121.3 billion from April 2026 to July 2033, and with Amazon $110 billion from May 2026 to April 2036.

In the same deal, Microsoft agreed to buy about 12 million Anthropic shares for about $1 billion at the IPO price of $83 per share, a sign of the depth of the partnership between the two companies.

The deal comes at a time when AI companies are spending hundreds of billions on computing capacity: Anthropic's total purchase and lease commitments stand at about $895 billion, mostly for computing and infrastructure.

Simply put: Anthropic has secured computing power for years ahead, and Microsoft has secured guaranteed billions — does the AI race justify contracts of this size?

If a single AI company commits hundreds of billions to computing, do you think these investments will pay off, or are they a bubble that will burst?
