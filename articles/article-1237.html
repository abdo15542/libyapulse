🤖 X Square robots enter Malaysian post offices… sorting parcels and learning on their own!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

X Square Robot, a company specializing in robotics and embodied AI, has signed a cooperation memorandum with Pos Malaysia, the Malaysian national postal service, to explore using intelligent robots to automate logistics services.

And Pos Malaysia is no small company: its history spans more than 200 years, its network includes more than 3,000 service points, and it reaches more than 11 million addresses across Malaysia, as it gradually transforms from a postal company into an integrated parcels and logistics company.

Under the agreement, X Square provides robotic equipment for evaluation, while Pos Malaysia contributes its real-world operational expertise and needs, to test robots on tasks such as parcel handling and sorting.

Advertisement

The idea here runs deeper than a robotic arm repeating a single motion: embodied AI combines perception, reasoning, and physical handling, so a robot can deal with parcels of different sizes, shapes, and weights, adapt to a changing environment, and carry out multi-step tasks.

X Square develops foundational models for embodied intelligence, robotic platforms, and dexterous manipulation systems, aiming to move its technology from the lab to real applications in homes, logistics, and industrial environments.

The announcement comes shortly after a meeting of Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation leaders in Shenzhen, pointing to cross-border technical cooperation opportunities in the region.

Simply put: the post office that once relied on hands and eyes is now testing robots that see a parcel, understand it, and carry it themselves; if the trial succeeds, your parcels may arrive faster and with fewer errors.

Would you be comfortable with a robot sorting your parcel instead of a person?
