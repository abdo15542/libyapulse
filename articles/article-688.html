💻 A new chip for Googbooks — AI with no fan and no internet!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

MediaTek has launched the first chip in a new family called "Dimensity CX": the Dimensity CX C10 Max chip, aimed at premium, lightweight Googbook devices — a step that moves the company from the world of phones into the world of laptops.

The chip is made with 3-nanometre technology, and carries an eight-core central processor, an 11-core graphics processor, and an NPU 890 AI unit with a power of 55 trillion operations per second. The company says it supports fanless designs and a battery that lasts all day, while the built-in AI engine runs the Gemini assistant and smart agent tasks on the device itself, without needing the internet.

This step comes after many companies revealed their Googbook devices — laptops built around Google's smart assistant — making AI-dedicated chips a new arena of competition between chip companies. The company has not yet announced when the first devices with this chip will reach the market.

Simply put: AI that works on your computer with no fan and no internet. Would you prefer a computer that thinks locally, or one that depends on the cloud?
