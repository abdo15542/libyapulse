💥 New report: Nvidia's former CEO 'terrorized' his employees with public humiliation!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

A press investigation published today has revealed that Jensen Huang, Nvidia's former CEO, ran the company through public humiliation and fear, according to testimony from more than a dozen current and former employees and hundreds of pages of documents and court records.

According to the investigation, Huang told employees that the company's wealth made money insufficient to motivate them, leaving only humiliation as a way to pressure them. He was reported to have told one executive that his mother should have aborted him, and threatened another employee that he would tear off his arms and beat him to death with them. Some incidents go back to 2005, when then HR director Michael Winston threatened to sue the company, describing Huang's behavior as the most abusive of his professional life, before Nvidia paid him more than $1 million in a confidential settlement. Huang's spokesperson described these allegations as fabricated.

In contrast, thousands of employees signed a letter demanding Huang's return, while Elon Musk compared his dismissal to removing Michael Jordan from his team at the height of his brilliance. The company had announced Huang's resignation last week amid investigations into chip exports.

Simply put: imagine a manager who believes fear is stronger than money in making people work; that is how the investigation described Huang's style of running Nvidia for more than twenty years.

Can a harsh style build a great company, or does success not justify humiliation?
