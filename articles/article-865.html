🚀 German company Isar starts building a launch base in Canada.. first orbital flight in 2028!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

German company Isar Aerospace announced on 21 November that it has officially taken over a dedicated launch complex at Spaceport Nova Scotia in Canada, and has begun building its own base there, making Canada the company's second launch site in the world and its first in North America.

Under the agreement with the Canadian company Maritime Launch Services, Isar will build and operate the entire complex, including a final assembly line for its Spectrum rocket and a facility for preparing satellites before flight, and it is targeting its first orbital launch from the site in the first half of 2028. The spaceport sits near the town of Canso on the Atlantic coast, allowing launch paths over the sea and access to polar and inclined orbits, serving Earth-observation, communications and defence missions.

Last September the Spectrum rocket reached orbit on its second flight, making Isar the first European commercial space company to deliver satellites to orbit. The company manufactures its rockets at a factory near Munich with a capacity of up to 40 rockets a year, and the Canadian project has the support of the Canadian government, because it gives Canada a national ability to launch its own satellites instead of relying on other countries.

Simply put: instead of sending its satellites to launch bases in other countries, Canada now has its own spaceport on its own soil — so will small countries succeed in joining the space race with their own launch bases, or will launching remain the preserve of the great powers?
