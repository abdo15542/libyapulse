🚗 Waymo Halts Its Self-Driving Cars in San Francisco Over Rain and High Tides!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie announced that Waymo will suspend its self-driving taxi service in the city starting at 4 a.m. Monday, because of expected rain and the "king tide" phenomenon, which raises sea levels and floods low-lying streets.

The decision came a week after heavy rain and flooding left several Waymo cars stuck in flooded intersections, blocking traffic and leaving passengers stranded. The company had already suspended the service last Wednesday for the same reason, then resumed it Thursday.

The mayor did not specify when the service would resume, but heavy rain is expected to start in the Bay Area on Tuesday. He advised residents to finish their errands and holiday travel on Monday, and to avoid parking their cars in flood-prone areas.

The mayor said city departments are coordinating their preparations for the coming storms, while Waymo's cars will stay parked until the weather improves, because their safety system prefers stopping over risking a drive through floodwater.

The suspension also comes after California banned Waymo cars from highways following incidents in the Bay Area, adding to the pressure on the company in its most important city.

Simply put: driverless cars stop when water floods the streets, because safety matters more than finishing the trip... so would you trust a driverless car in bad weather?

Your self-driving car stops working every time it rains... would you still trust it as a daily means of transport?
