🛰️ Europe invests more than €240 million in a satellite that watches oceans and floods moment by moment!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Thales Alenia Space signed a contract worth more than €240 million with OHB System to develop altimetry instruments for the new Sentinel-3 NG topography satellite, and the contract was announced in the French city of Toulouse on Monday.

The satellite is part of Copernicus, the European Earth observation program, the most advanced environmental monitoring program in the world, run by the European Commission with funding from the European Union and the European Space Agency.

The company will develop two instruments: the first is a radar altimeter that continues the data of the current satellite, and the second is a wide-swath interferometric altimeter that scans the surface of oceans and waterways across a 100-kilometre-wide strip, producing two-dimensional images of water terrain.

Data will arrive in near real time, in less than six hours, helping manage floods and droughts and respond to emergencies. The satellite will cover almost all of the oceans in just five days, and will be able to spot small ocean eddies as small as 50 kilometres.

Altimetry satellites had previously enabled scientists to measure the global average sea level rising by about 3.3 millimetres per year, meaning the new satellite will continue a vital measurement of climate change.

Simply put: a satellite that maps Earth's waters every five days and sends the data within hours... Can this data help us predict floods before they happen?
