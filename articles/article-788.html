🛰️ Curiosity Captures Glowing Clouds Over Mars After Sunset... a Sight That Appears Every Autumn!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 20, NASA published a new image captured by the Curiosity rover on the surface of Mars, showing thin glowing clouds drifting across the twilight sky above Gale Crater, in a sight that appears only for a short period each autumn.

The rover captured the image on October 31, 2026, on Martian day number 5061 of its mission, using its navigation cameras, just after sunset, when these clouds, known as twilight clouds, shimmer above the dark horizon.

Curiosity has been exploring Gale Crater for more than 14 years, and the mission team now knows when these clouds appear each year, capturing them at their expected time, though the agency's post did not specify the clouds' composition or altitude.

Simply put: clouds glowing in the Martian sky after sunset, returning every autumn as if on schedule; so have you ever imagined that Mars has a beautiful sunset like Earth's?
