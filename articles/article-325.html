🗓️ Microsoft is shutting down the 'To Do' app in June 2027!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Microsoft will shut down the 'To Do' task-management app on June 30, 2027, according to an official support document spotted by a tech site. Worse, your tasks will not move automatically to any other app — not to 'Planner' or anything else — and if you forget to move them manually before the deadline, you could lose your whole list.

The company offers no replacement app for free personal account holders, while paid work and school accounts will see their tasks appear in 'Planner', though the move remains manual there too. Microsoft promised an export tool to help you move your tasks to another app, without setting a date for its launch. The decision recalls the story of the famous 'Wunderlist' app, which Microsoft bought in 2015 for $200 million, then never carried its features into 'To Do' as users had hoped — and now 'To Do' itself is closing. Suggested alternatives include 'Superlist', made by the creators of 'Wunderlist' themselves, alongside 'Todoist' and 'TickTick'.

Simply put: a free app you rely on every day may disappear, and your list will not move on its own — move it yourself before it is too late.
