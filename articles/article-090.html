China Launches a Mysterious Satellite Into Space.. and Won't Reveal Its Mission! 🚀

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

China launched a new satellite today called "Shijian 24" aboard a Long March 2D rocket from the Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center, at 6:51 a.m. China time, and the satellite successfully entered its sun-synchronous orbit. China did not reveal the satellite's specific purpose or who built it.

The word "Shijian" means "practice" or "training"; the name is used for satellites that test new technologies and establish operating procedures. A sun-synchronous orbit is an orbit that passes over each point at the same time of day, so the light stays constant — which is useful for Earth observation. And a "mysterious" satellite is not necessarily a secret military one; China simply does not announce the mission of these test satellites. This launch is the 678th flight of the Long March rocket family, the 108th flight of the Long March 2D rocket, and China's 82nd orbital attempt in 2026. The cold weather in Taiyuan required extra thermal insulation and heating of critical parts of the rocket before liftoff.
