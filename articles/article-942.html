❤️ Apple rebuilds its Health app with AI! Your 'Health Age' tells you how old your body really is!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Apple announced on November 3 that it is completely rebuilding its Health app on the basis of artificial intelligence, launching it in the beta version of 'iOS 27.3', and it will reach all users as a free update before the end of the year, after the company pulled the new design from earlier tests and delayed it for weeks.

The new app is organized around two main tabs: the 'Insights' tab, which generates smart summaries throughout the day from heart, sleep, activity, fitness, and vital-sign data, and offers personalized recommendations such as improving a sleep habit, and the 'Longevity' tab, which tracks health over the long term across seven areas: heart health, sleep, mental health, movement, metabolic health, hearing, and nutrition.

At the heart of the 'Longevity' tab is the 'Health Edge' feature, or 'Health Age': a single number that summarizes how your body is aging compared to your real age. The app calculates it from Apple Watch data, including maximum oxygen consumption, resting heart rate, sleep duration, and heart rate variability, so if you sleep well and move a lot, your health age may appear younger than your actual age, and vice versa.

The app also offers new at-home movement assessments: using the iPhone camera with the Apple Watch, it can measure your flexibility, strength, balance, movement style, and maximum oxygen consumption, with instant voice and visual guidance. Apple stresses that these assessments run on on-device vision models, without recording, storing, or sharing any video.

There is one important condition: the new smart features only work on phones that support 'Apple Intelligence', meaning the 'iPhone 15 Pro' and 'iPhone 15 Pro Max' and the 'iPhone 16', '17', and '18' lines, while older phones will not get these features even after the update. 'Health Edge' also needs an Apple Watch to collect its data, and it is an indicator for tracking the general trend of your health, not a medical diagnosis.

Simply put: a health app that reads your body's data and tells you honestly how old your body really is... Are you ready to hear the number?
