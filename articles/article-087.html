New mRNA Vaccine Protects Against the Deadly Nipah and Hendra Viruses! 💉

19 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Researchers have designed a dual-antigen mRNA vaccine against the Nipah and Hendra viruses — two viruses transmitted from animals, especially bats, with high fatality rates and pandemic potential, and for which there is no licensed vaccine for human use. The study was published today. In rigorous tests on golden hamsters, every vaccinated hamster survived lethal doses of both viruses, and no viral genetic material was detected in the lung, spleen, or brain after challenge with the Nipah virus.

Nipah virus is rare but extremely deadly, and there is still no vaccine for it — this is an early laboratory success: it fully protected the hamsters. mRNA technology is the same technology used in the COVID vaccines — it teaches the body to recognize the virus without using the live virus. An important note: this is a vaccine at the animal-testing stage only, not a vaccine people can take, and human trials are still a long way off.
