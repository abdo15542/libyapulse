📝 A blank white window crashes Word, Excel, and Outlook — and Microsoft finally fixes the bug!

25 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Microsoft fixed this week a strange bug that prevented some Microsoft 365 users from opening Office apps: when the app opens, a completely empty dialog window appears, with no text and no buttons, which cannot be closed, blocking the app behind it.

The bug hit six apps: Word, Excel, Outlook, PowerPoint, OneNote, and Access, and users complained about it on social media and in the company's support forums, before Microsoft confirmed in a support document updated on Monday that the fix is ready.

The fix is available in Microsoft 365 apps version 2611, build 16.0.20625.20142, released last week, and is usually installed from inside the app via the Account menu — but the problem is that the empty window itself may block access to the update menu in the first place.

Advertisement

That is why the company published an alternative route: save your work and close all Office apps, then open the Command Prompt as an administrator and run the update command that points the installer directly to the fixed build, and users confirmed that this solution worked for them.

The company has not yet explained why the empty window appears, which earlier versions were affected, or how many people were hit, and confirmed that it is a bug in the app installed on the device, not a security incident or a cloud outage, and different from the cloud email outage of 12 and 13 November.

Simply put: a window that says nothing and will not close stopped work in the world's most popular apps, and the fix may need just one line of commands. Have you ever faced a blank window that stopped you opening your program?

Do you update your apps as soon as a fix is released, or do you postpone updates until something breaks?
