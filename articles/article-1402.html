🇯🇵 Japan Launches Its "AI Vision for Defense" — The Human Has the Final Say!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Japan's Ministry of Defense has issued its "AI Vision for Defense," new principles governing the use of AI in the Self-Defense Forces, placing the human as the decision-maker and the one responsible in all cases.

The vision rests on five core principles: keeping judgment and responsibility in human hands, complying with domestic and international law, ensuring safety, reliability, and transparency, avoiding overreliance on a single technology or company, and starting from operational needs rather than from the technology itself.

The principles also state explicitly that Japan does not intend to develop fully autonomous lethal weapons that operate without human intervention.

The vision calls for faster analysis of radar data, satellite imagery, and intelligence, using physical AI in tasks that do not put personnel at risk, and automating logistics services.

This vision replaces the basic policy the ministry adopted in July 2024, at a time when major countries are racing to bring AI into their armies.
