📱 OnePlus 17 May Come With a Wide 240 Hz Display.. The Fastest in the Company's History!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

New leaks suggest that the upcoming flagship OnePlus 17 could carry a major display upgrade, with a wide panel expected to offer a refresh rate of up to 240 Hz — a figure never seen before in the company's phones.

The refresh rate is the number of times the display redraws the image per second — the higher the number, the smoother the motion, whether you are scrolling through apps or playing fast-paced games, and 240 Hz means the image is redrawn 240 times every second.

This leak follows an earlier claim by the well-known leaker Digital Chat Station that an upcoming OnePlus phone would feature a 2K display with a 240 Hz refresh rate, strengthening the chance that the new display will be the standout feature of the next phone.

Advertisement

It is worth noting, though, that such a high refresh rate could drain the battery faster, which is why companies usually rely on variable refresh rate technology that automatically lowers the rate when you are reading still text and raises it during gaming — and the leaks have not yet revealed whether the phone will use this technology.

OnePlus has not confirmed any of these details officially, and the phone is still in the leaks stage, with more information expected to emerge as the launch date approaches next year.

Simply put: a display that redraws itself 240 times a second could make your next phone the smoothest you have ever tried.. Can your eyes actually tell the difference between 120 and 240 Hz?

If your phone displays everything with superb smoothness, would you pay more for that?
