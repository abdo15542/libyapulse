⚖️ The state of Wisconsin sues TikTok — accusing it of deliberately addicting children and comparing it to a slot machine!

22 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

The US state of Wisconsin has filed a lawsuit against the TikTok platform, accusing it of deliberately spreading features that cause addiction and harm children's mental health, while misleading parents and the public about the app's risks, in a case announced by the state's attorney general, Josh Kaul, on November 19.

The lawsuit, filed in Dane County Court on November 18, targets features such as the recommendation engine, infinite scrolling, autoplay, notifications and live streaming, which the attorney general compares to a slot machine because they are designed to keep users on the app as long as possible. The state is asking the court to force the platform to change its design, in addition to financial compensation whose amount has not been announced.

The lawsuit relies on the company's internal documents admitting that minors do not have the ability to control their screen time, and that the app describes mature topics such as sexual content and drugs as "light" and "rare", even though they appear to young users constantly. The lawsuit notes that 63% of American teenagers aged 13 to 17 use the app, and 57% of them use it daily.

Simply put: an American state says the app's design itself is the problem, not children's misuse of it. Will this lawsuit become a precedent that forces social media platforms to change their design to protect young people?
