📱 Galaxy Z Fold 9 appears in the IMEI database before launch!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Samsung's foldable phones, the 'Galaxy Z Fold 9' and 'Galaxy Z Fold 9 Ultra', have appeared in the IMEI database, an early sign that the next generation of foldable phones is heading toward its expected launch in July 2027.

According to the report, the two phones were registered under model numbers 'SM-F986U' for the 'Ultra' version and 'SM-F981U' for the wide foldable version — the American versions — under the codenames 'Q9' and 'H9'. The 'Q9' codename may split into two versions: the new generation of the tri-fold phone, and the traditional book-style version of the 'Fold 9 Ultra'. Earlier reports suggested the 'Fold 9' will bring back the under-display camera in the inner screen, a technology Samsung abandoned after the 'Fold 7' due to poor image quality, while the 'Fold 9 Ultra' is expected to keep the same rear camera system: a 200-megapixel main camera, a 50-megapixel wide camera, and a 10-megapixel telephoto camera.

Although appearing in the IMEI database is usually an early sign that a launch is near, the July 2027 date is still far away, and all these details are based on unofficial reports — Samsung has not confirmed any of them yet.
