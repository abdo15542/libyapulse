🔀 Perplexity Launches 'Orchestrator'... One Interface for Every AI Model!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

On November 19, Perplexity launched a new service called Orchestrator, a single API that lets developers reach dozens of models from different companies — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI — with one bill and one key, instead of contracting with each provider separately, and its catalog includes more than 45 models in private preview.

The service's most notable feature is 'automatic failover': if your request fails with one provider, the service automatically resends it to another copy of the same model with a different provider, without changing the model or the price. It also offers an 'auto' mode that picks the most suitable model for each request based on a description of the task in plain words.

The company's measurements between October 4 and November 2 showed that Claude and GPT requests through Orchestrator succeeded at a higher rate than through any single provider, and the service works with the same current Perplexity keys, with no new registration needed.

Simply put: one provider goes down? No problem, your request moves on its own to another provider; so are the days of depending on a single AI provider over?
