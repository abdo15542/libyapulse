🚀 Shenzhou 24 Ready for Launch: First Joint Chinese-Foreign Crew Prepares for Space!

22 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The Jiuquan launch site has announced that all preparations for the crewed Shenzhou 24 mission are complete, after the mission finished its final full joint rehearsal on Saturday, ahead of its flight to the Chinese space station.

Shenzhou 24 was moved with its Long March-2F carrier rocket to the launch area on November 17, and is now undergoing functional checks, joint tests, and fuel loading, while the site confirmed that the rocket, the spacecraft, and all systems are in good condition.

The mission carries a historic distinction: it is the first Chinese mission to include a joint Chinese-foreign crew, as a Pakistani astronaut joins it, in a first-of-its-kind cooperation that opens China's crewed space program to astronauts from other countries.

Simply put: space is no longer the preserve of two or three countries; it has become a field of international cooperation, even among nations that were outside the race.
