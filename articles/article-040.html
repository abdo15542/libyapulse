Motorola Edge 70 Neo: 7000mAh Battery and a Bright Screen — Launching in India on November 24! 🔋

19 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Motorola confirmed the launch date of its new Edge 70 Neo phone in India: November 24, 2026.

The phone comes with a huge 7000mAh battery that the company says lasts up to 53 hours of use, with 45W fast charging.

Its screen is AMOLED with 1.5K resolution — sharper than a standard Full HD screen — and reaches a peak brightness of 5,200 nits, meaning excellent clarity under sunlight.

Its main camera is 50 megapixels with a Sony LYT-710 sensor and optical stabilization that reduces shake in photos and videos, and it comes in four Pantone colors. The price has not been announced yet.
