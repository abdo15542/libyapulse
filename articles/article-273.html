🦋 Bluesky lets you see how many views your posts get... in complete privacy!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Platform 'Bluesky' has begun testing a new feature showing post view counts, in version 1.138 of the app, but in a different way from other platforms: the numbers are private to you alone.

No one will see the view counts of your posts but you; the number appears only on your own posts, not to visitors or followers, so views do not become a public popularity race. The feature requires enabling the experimental features option from the account settings, after which the numbers appear on your posts directly.

The platform announced the feature on November 20, inviting users to try it and send feedback, without setting a date for making it available to everyone or adding further analytics later.

Simply put: imagine a counter in your pocket counting who read your post, without anyone else knowing the number; that is how Bluesky designed its new view counter.
