🎮 Intel Is Preparing the "Arc G3 Play" Chip for Cheap Handheld Gaming Devices! Just 4 Graphics Cores and a Price Under $800!

23 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

New reports say Intel is preparing a cheaper version of its handheld gaming processors, called "Arc G3 Play", to open the door to lower-priced handheld gaming devices — although the company has not officially announced the chip yet.

The new version carries only 4 "Xe3" graphics cores, compared with 10 cores in the standard version and 12 cores in the "Extreme" version — roughly a third of the power of the strongest version.

The chip is built on the "Panther Lake" platform and packs 8 power-efficient processor cores, with no high-performance cores, drawing between 8 and 25 watts — a good fit for lightweight handheld devices.

Advertisement

The aim of the new version is clear: handheld gaming devices under $800, to compete with cheap "AMD" devices in the same category — though the final specs may change, since the company's plan has not been finalised yet.

Simply put: a weaker gaming chip, but a much cheaper one — will it be enough for light and medium games?

Would you buy a cheaper handheld gaming device with less power, or pay more for full performance?
