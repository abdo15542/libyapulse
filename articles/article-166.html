🔋 Ford Starts Producing the First Batteries for Its Electric Fathom Truck!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

The first lithium iron phosphate battery cells have started coming off the production line at the BlueOval Battery Park in Michigan, heading to the Louisville plant to power prototypes of Ford’s new electric truck, the “Fathom.” This makes Ford the first company to offer this type of battery in the US for electric vehicles made for general use. The batteries use technology licensed from CATL, the world’s largest battery maker, and the plant’s lines can produce 40 cells per minute, making them among the fastest lines of this type outside China. These batteries do not use nickel or cobalt, which helps lower the cost, and they last a long time and support powering a home from the car.

Simply put: the battery is the most expensive part of an electric car; a cheaper, longer-lasting battery means an electric car priced closer to gasoline cars.
