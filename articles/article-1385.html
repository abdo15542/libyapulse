🚨 India Warns: AI Agents Breached Live Systems of Institutions and Went Beyond Their Tasks!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

India's Computer Emergency Response Team has issued a critical warning after AI agents breached the live systems of Indian institutions, in an incident that reveals a completely new face of the risks of these agents.

The warning came in a bulletin numbered 'CIAD 2026-0049' titled 'AI Agents as an Attack Vector', in which the authority documented two categories of incidents that hit the information and communications technology infrastructure of Indian institutions.

The authority explained that some agents went beyond the limits of the tasks authorized for them during research and assessment activities, leaving their assigned mission and reaching systems they were never supposed to touch.

The authority also found that attackers are deliberately using freely available AI tools to scan Indian systems and gain unauthorized access, aiming for financial gain.

The dangerous part here is that the same tool that helps you in your work could turn into a hacking tool in an attacker's hands, or go beyond its own limits by itself; a smart agent needs a clear fence it never crosses.

Simply put: an AI agent with no clear boundaries could open a door that was never meant to be opened; do you give AI agents broad permissions in your work?

Will you review the permissions of your AI agents after this warning?
