📸 WhatsApp Tests Shooting With Both Cameras at Once… Your Face and the Moment in One Frame!

24 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

WhatsApp is testing a dual-camera mode on Android, according to a report from WABetaInfo, which tracks the app's features before they launch.

The feature lets you shoot with the front and rear cameras at the same time, in a single frame, so your face and the scene in front of you appear together in the same photo or video.

The feature is reaching some beta testers through WhatsApp version 2.26.46.9 on Google Play.

Advertisement

The rear camera is the main view by default, and the front camera appears in a small window that can be moved to another spot on the screen.

The two views can be swapped with a rotate button, making the front camera the main view and the rear camera the small window.

Photos and videos taken this way can be shared in chats, groups, channels, and Status, with a caption or edits added before sending.

The rollout is gradual: even those who install the latest beta may not see the feature right away, as it arrives in waves.

There is no official date for the public launch yet, and no confirmation that the feature is coming to iPhones so far.

Simply put: instead of flipping the camera between your face and the scene, WhatsApp captures both together in one shot; handy at parties, on trips, and for instant reactions.
