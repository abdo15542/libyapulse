🏛️ Prominent US Senator Accuses Commerce Secretary of Ignoring Warnings About AI Chip Smuggling to China!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Senator Tom Cotton, chairman of the US Senate Intelligence Committee, accused Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick of ignoring repeated warnings about smuggling American AI chips to China, before the Nvidia scandal broke publicly. Cotton said in an interview published Friday that he sent many letters to the secretary but received no reply.

Cotton revealed that in October 2025 he sent Lutnick a letter asking for an investigation into Megaspeed in Singapore, suspected of diverting American chips to China — the company where Alice Huang, suspected of working for Chinese intelligence, was a senior executive. The Commerce Department refused to place the company on the trade blacklist, although it has been investigating it since 2024, while the Justice Department is now leading several investigations related to the case.

The accusation came a day after House data revealed that the Commerce Department had approved the sale of more than 800,000 Nvidia and AMD AI chips to China up to May 11 of this year, with more than a third going to companies the Pentagon has classified as Chinese military companies. President Trump defended Lutnick on Friday, saying he had not lost confidence in him, while Cotton expected Congress to pass tougher chip-export restrictions.
