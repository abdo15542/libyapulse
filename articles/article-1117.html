⚛️ Quantum Company Q-CTRL Raises $290 Million… and Joins the 'Unicorn' Club!

24 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Q-CTRL, a company specializing in quantum infrastructure software, has raised $290 million in a new funding round, lifting its valuation above $1 billion for the first time and entering the club of "unicorn" companies.

The round was led by Salesforce Ventures, with other investors taking part. The company said the round was expanded twice to meet investor demand, a sign of the strong appetite for the quantum computing sector.

The company describes itself as a global leader in quantum infrastructure software, designing software that helps quantum computers produce reliable and useful results — which its backers describe as the central obstacle in the sector, more than building the machines themselves.

Advertisement

A partner at Salesforce Ventures said "the core challenge is getting useful output from quantum machines, not just building them," and the company will direct the new funding toward accelerating its global growth and expanding its AI-powered quantum software.

The company will also expand navigation products that do not rely on GPS, using quantum sensing to help vehicles and aircraft determine their position without satellite signals — a technology that could become vital when satellite signals fail or are jammed.

Q-CTRL thus joins a small, growing list of quantum companies that have passed the $1 billion mark this year, at a time when investor interest has shifted from the companies making the quantum processors themselves to the software companies that make these machines usable for real problems.

Simply put: quantum computers exist… but they need software that makes them genuinely useful… Will the quantum computer one day become an everyday tool like the ordinary computer?
