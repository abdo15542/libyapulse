🔌 Google Forces AI Agents to Go Through Its Servers — Deadline: February 2027!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

Google has announced that its Model Context Protocol servers for Google Workspace and its official command-line tool are now available to everyone, in a move that redraws the rules for how AI agents access email, files, and calendars.

Under the new rules, any AI agent that wants to access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and other services must go through these official servers, instead of connecting directly to the old APIs.

Technical enforcement of this requirement begins on February 1, 2027, when any agent that ignores it will be limited to low usage quotas, whatever its subscription.

The company also introduced new usage tiers, with a free limit of 5,000 requests per day per product, while billing for the expanded tier starts in April 2027.

The package also includes new data-protection controls, including a ban on storing raw data or training models on it, deleting derived data within a set period, and deleting all data when the connection is cut off.

Simply put: Google is building a single monitored gateway for AI agents — do you think these rules protect your data or restrict developers?
