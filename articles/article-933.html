💶 100 million euros from Deutsche Telekom into Mistral! Europe is building its own sovereign AI!

23 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

German company Deutsche Telekom announced on November 19 that its investment fund T.Capital invested 100 million euros in French AI company Mistral, as part of its Series D funding round, in a first step toward a strategic partnership between the two companies.

The partnership aims to bring trusted European AI to German companies and public institutions, especially in industrial and regulated sectors, by combining Deutsche Telekom's sovereign telecom infrastructure, customer relationships, and integration expertise with Mistral's models, enterprise software, and deployment capabilities.

A Deutsche Telekom board member said Europe's competitiveness depends on its ability to develop strong AI and use it, and that companies must trust this technology while keeping control of their data, while Mistral's CFO described the investment as a strong signal that the two companies together can bring sovereign, trusted AI to German industry at scale.

The investment comes after Mistral recently opened its research and operations center in Munich, as part of its expansion in the German market, alongside Deutsche Telekom's investments in industrial AI cloud infrastructure inside Germany.

T.Capital manages assets of more than 2 billion euros on behalf of the Deutsche Telekom group, investing in companies that support its strategic priorities, while Mistral aims to become the first European choice for companies that want AI models running on their own data and infrastructure, without relying on American or Chinese companies.

Simply put: a German telecom giant puts 100 million euros into a French AI company to keep European AI in Europe... will Europe succeed in competing with America and China?
