🛡️ X Finally Launches Parental Controls — Daily Limits and Follower Approval!

27 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

X has launched the first parental controls in its history, aimed at teens aged 13 to 17, giving parents practical ways to follow their children's use without taking over their accounts.

The new tools let parents see how much time their teen spent on the platform over the past week, and set a daily usage limit in 15-minute steps, after which the platform locks until midnight.

Parents can also approve or reject new follow requests, restrict the "For You" feed to accounts the teen already follows, filter specific words, and turn off sensitive media.

In direct messages, parents decide who can message the teen and see the accounts they talked to, without reading the messages themselves or viewing the search history.

The system requires both sides to agree to link the accounts, one parent can supervise up to five accounts, and supervision ends automatically when the teen turns 18.
