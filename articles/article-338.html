💚 Huang Offers to Return as Nvidia CEO — and the Board Says No!

20 November 2026 · Website · TechPulse

In his first public statement since resigning, Jensen Huang, the founder and former CEO of Nvidia, said he would be "proud" to return to his post if the board decided his return was in the interest of shareholders, employees, the company, and America. He added that employees, investors, partners, and customers are still reaching out to him directly asking him to come back, apologizing for mistakes he described as serious in his personal life, and confirming his full cooperation with an independent investigation the board is conducting.

But the board shut the door quickly: board chairman Harvey Jones, the first chairman in the company's history, said during the quarterly earnings call that the board had unanimously accepted Huang's resignation and does not intend to bring him back. CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO, while the board has hired an executive search firm to find a permanent chief.

Huang had resigned on November 12 from all his positions, after the board revealed personal conduct inconsistent with company standards, ending a 33-year career in which he led the company he founded in 1993 from a graphics-card maker to the world's most important supplier of AI hardware. Huang is scheduled to appear before Congress on December 1, followed by the company's general counsel the next day.
