🦾 Gemini 4 Hydrogen: A Faster Robotics Model from Google!

20 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Google announced its new “Gemini 4 Hydrogen” robotics model, which is faster than its predecessor “Gemini Robotics ER 2” and replaces it, priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, with a context of up to one million tokens. It also launched a robotics extension for the “Antigravity” tool, covering device setup, MuJoCo simulation, and robot control, so that a “robot in a box turns into a working app in a single afternoon,” and a robotic arm was calibrated in only about 10 minutes. The extension is open source under the Apache 2.0 license, but it requires a paid Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription.

Simply put: the new model is a faster brain for robots; and with the new development tool, programming a real robot becomes much easier than before.
