🚕 Pony AI's fleet reaches 2,768 self-driving taxis — target 3,500 before year-end!

21 November 2026 · Website · Libya Pulse

Chinese company Pony AI announced in its third-quarter financial results on November 18 that its self-driving taxi fleet reached 2,768 vehicles as of September 30, compared with 1,975 at the end of June.

The company is still on track for its goal: operating 3,500 robotaxis in 20 cities around the world before the end of 2026.

The financial figures reflect this expansion: total company revenue reached $44.6 million in the third quarter, up 75.2% on last year, while revenue from robotaxi services alone jumped 165.4%, making up about 40% of all revenue.

Advertisement

On November 16, the seventh-generation Toyota bZ4X joined the driverless commercial fleet in Guangzhou, available around the clock via the company's app or via WeChat and Alipay, and the two companies are working towards about 1,000 vehicles of this model in major Chinese cities.

The expansion is not limited to China: the company launched new services in Singapore and Luxembourg, began road tests in Manchester, and deployment plans in London are advancing, with its vehicles now operating in nine countries.

Foreign demand is expanding too: the overseas deployment pipeline exceeds 4,000 contracted vehicles, including more than 2,000 in five European cities with Uber.

Simply put: driverless taxis are no longer a small experiment — they are a fleet approaching three thousand vehicles and growing every quarter.
